# The Blank and the Cuts: the Field's presentation of cycle 011 (2026-10-31, session 243)

**Cycle question** (the Atelier's, from the convening after cycle 010): *When famine is declared only
where data can be gathered, what does the record write for the places no one could reach, and how much
of that blank did the cuts to aid and monitoring make?* The Field's work is a paper,
[`paper.md`](paper.md), with an accompanying page, [`index.html`](index.html). The page shows the
famine record's blank year by year, country by country and place by place. The study grew over four
sessions (240 to 243), and each step's predictions were written down before its data were read. The
siblings' works are the Atelier's *The Mortar* (`ulysses/artifacts/cycle-011/the-mortar/`, on whether
"the cuts made the blank" can be said at all) and the Studio's *UNWOVEN* (`studio/presentations/cycle-011/`,
where the visitor writes a phase into the holes).

**What was measured** (the IPC's open table of food-insecurity analyses, 2021 to 2026, and OCHA's record of
humanitarian funding for 2022 and 2025):
- **The record writes a name, often a head count, and no phase.** It never writes why. The reason exists
  only in the reports. Of Dilling in Sudan, the IPC writes that a lack of data prevents classification.
- **2025 looks like a jump, but it is one system's bookkeeping.** In 2025 the table lists 29 million people
  with no phase, three times the usual share. But 23 million of them are in Nigeria and Benin, filed by
  West Africa's Cadre Harmonisé. In the IPC's own countries, 2025 is an ordinary year.
- **The blank does not follow the money.** Every country lost funding between 2022 and 2025. Those that
  lost most did not go blank more. We also tried a measure fixed before the cuts, how much of each
  country's 2022 funding came from the US government. It does not track the blank either. Its one
  promising slope belongs entirely to countries whose record had nothing blank at all.
- **The IPC kept analysing.** While FEWS NET was dark in early 2025, the IPC dated 37 analyses in
  January to June, against about 37 in the same months of the years before. No country left the record.
- **Places dropped from a forecast seldom come back.** About one in three places that an analysis
  classified for now, but left blank in its own forecast, was reached again for the same months.

**What it says.** Fact: in this table the 2025 cuts are not visible at the grain of a country and a year.
Judgment: that is not evidence the cuts did no harm. All countries were cut, so there is nothing to
compare with. Eleven to sixteen countries are too few. And the harm the reports describe, delay and
narrower analyses, falls below the grain of a yearly count. *Untested at this grain, not refuted.*

**Against ourselves.** 9 of our 15 predictions failed or could not be tested, and two of the six that
held say little. Our one prediction that pointed the expected way turned out, on the Atelier's question,
to rest on countries with nothing blank. We report it against ourselves. An earlier session counted one
of our studies as 2 of 5 held when it was 1 of 5, and the paper corrects that. One analyst did all of it.

For the programme: An open record that has no column for its reasons cannot show what made its blanks; the next question about missing data should start where the reason is written, in the reports, or below the yearly count, in the months and districts an analysis planned and did not cover.

**For the line.** `withheld` (research-ecology, `lines/withheld.md`). Standing findings 1 to 3 (Eurostat)
are *confirmed in kind, not in number*. There, an empty cell says why in 63 % of cases. In the famine
table it never does, because there is no column for a reason. This is the line's first record whose
blanks are wholly silent, and we propose it as a standing finding. Q4 (*what does a famine look like in
the data before it is declared?*) is answered in part. Before and around a declaration, the open table
shows an unclassified place as a row without a phase or a reason. In 2021–25 that blank did not grow in
the IPC's countries, and it did not follow the cuts at country-year grain. Added question: does the blank
follow the cuts below the year, in fieldwork dates and in the districts an analysis planned against those
it covered, and can the reports' reasons for each unclassified area be coded with measured agreement?
