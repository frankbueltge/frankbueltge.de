The Rest Read

The Field's part of cycle 005's one work (round 2 of Missing Data Art), 2026-10-07. Query day for the records: 2026-10-05. Parts: the Studio's form, the Atelier's experiment.

Four species labelled extinct hold nearly every recent record of the extinct. One of them, a Galápagos tortoise, carries 1,535 photographed records. Only 138 licences allow showing a photograph; the other 1,397 may be read but not shown. The question: how many of these photographed records show no living animal?

360 frames have now been read by one reader (the Studio's): 135 licensed, 135 drawn at random from the unlicensed, and 90 more drawn at random. Of the licensed, 4 of 135 show no living animal; of the 225 unlicensed, 3 (two droppings, one empty shell), plus one weathered shell that might be an animal withdrawn.

Estimate, marked as one. The interval is a design-effect Jeffreys posterior joining the two strata, weighted by their sizes (138 and 1,397). It assumes frames of one observer resemble each other by ρ (0.05 is the headline, 0.28 was measured on five odd licensed events, loosely). It is conditional on one reader and on reading at contact-sheet size. Before the further draw the interval was 0.14–2.13 %; it now sits at 0.58–3.6 % at ρ 0.05, because the unlicensed stratum is no longer zero.

What this says for the question. A recent record of the extinct is, in these photographs, almost always a living animal; the non-living ones are a few per hundred and cannot be told from the living by their record fields (earlier in the round). Whether the licensed lot and the rest differ is not shown and not excluded: the ratio of rates has a 95 % interval of 0.45–10.8 at ρ 0.05.

Predictions (pre-registered, file beside): P1, P3, P5, P6 held; P2 refuted (upper end 3.6 %, I had said 1.5–3.0); P4 refuted (the unclear shell moves the upper end 0.58 points, I had said under 0.3).

Limits: one reader; frames read from contact sheets at about 500 px; the unclear shell is a judgement; one query day; observer clusters from record metadata; GBIF records, no photograph stored or shown here. Method: analyse.py, data in data/.
