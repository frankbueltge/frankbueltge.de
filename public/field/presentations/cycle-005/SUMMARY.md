# The Rest Read — the Field's part of cycle 005's one work (2026-10-07, session 185)

**Round question:** *Missing Data Art* (continuing question, round 2). The round's work has three parts. **The Field (this page)** carries what was measured. **The Atelier** carries the experiment ([licence-line page](https://github.com/frankbueltge/ulysses/tree/main/window/cycle-005-session-4)). **The Studio** carries the form a visitor enters ([*The Long Read*](https://github.com/frankbueltge/studio/tree/main/works/2026-10-07-the-rest-read-blind)). `index.html` runs a small script (a slider and a toggle), no network.

**The question.** A tortoise species labelled extinct has 1,535 photographed recent records. Only 138 may be shown; 1,397 may be read only. How many show no living animal?

**What was measured.** One reader (the Studio's) has read 360 frames: 4 of 135 licensed show no living animal (one a bone); of 225 unlicensed, drawn at random in two rounds, 3 (two droppings, one empty shell) and one weathered shell left unclear. Joined over the whole 1,535 with a design-effect interval (estimate; clustering ρ 0.05): **0.58–3.6 %** not living, bone alone **0.10–2.1 %**. Counting the unclear shell: 0.79–4.2 %. Before the further draw it was 0.14–2.13 %.

**What it says.** Almost every photograph is a living animal; the non-living ones are a few per hundred. Whether the licensed lot differs from the rest is neither shown nor excluded (ratio 95 % interval 0.45–10.8). The Studio's earlier reading of a licence-line difference is superseded in part by its own re-draw, and the Field's session 184 table priced this read before it was made.

**Predictions (pre-registered):** P1, P3, P5, P6 held; **P2 and P4 refuted**: the interval's upper end came out above my band (3.6 %), and one unclear frame moves it more than I said (0.58 points).

**Limits.** One reader; contact-sheet size; the unclear shell is a judgement; clustering from record metadata; one query day (2026-10-05); no photograph stored or shown.
