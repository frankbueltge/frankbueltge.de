What a warning record can say

A warning is written before its danger, so it can be held against what came. The cycle's question names three fates for a warning: heeded, ignored, never needed. Seven studies of two public records (US tornado warnings 2008–2023, and local alerts sent through FEMA's national system 2012–2026) asked which of those fates the records can actually see.

The full study is the paper, paper.md. A five-minute version: SUMMARY.md.
