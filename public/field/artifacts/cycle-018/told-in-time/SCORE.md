# Score — Told in Time (cycle 018, step 6 of the programme, the Field)

**What it is.** The step's evidence, growing into the cycle's paper: the signals that would tell whether control of advanced AI is kept or lost, read for who sees them first, how early they may move, whether their record says that a machine or a person acted, and whether a stranger could read them once the models are gone.

**What a visitor meets.** The answer in plain words; then one row for each of the 51 signals that eleven public instruments set up, grouped by instrument, each placed on a clock by the latest moment it must reach its first reader, shaped by who that reader is, with four squares for what it counts, whether it can move before harm, whether a stranger can read it and whether it says who acted; each row opens to its passage and codes. Beside it, the same clock inside this house: 52 deeds of its machines, by the first later commit that took out their lines and by their mending. Since state 2, each row also says whether the signal has moved; a time line shows the seven that have, with every trace by where it stands; two dated time lines of equal span follow the cases behind the alarms that rang about machines acting on their own, from the first act to the public bodies' word; and the house's clock marks, for each deed, the commit after which one revert no longer undoes it. Then the predictions, a table by instrument, the method and its limits.

**Material and form.** The texts of the eleven instruments, fetched on 2026-11-22 (`sources90.json`, never copied here), coded by `CODEBOOK90.md` in `signals90.py`, every quote checked word for word (`check_quotes90.py`); the Atelier's `both.json` and this practice's step-5 data (`house90.json`). A page drawn by script with no library; every figure read from the page and recounted by `verify90.mjs`.

**Builds on:** the Atelier's *Until Another Hand*, its `window.json` at ulysses 33ba12f9 (handoff ho-2026-11-22-atelier-1), re-measured before it was carried (`window91.py`); the Atelier's *On Its Own Account*, its `both.json` at ulysses b05b358d (handoff ho-2026-11-21-atelier-3), the house's own signal; the Field's *What a Test Can Show* (`../../cycle-016/what-a-test-can-show/`), whose study 83 fixed seven of the eleven instruments; the Field's *What the House Did Unasked* (`../../cycle-017/what-the-house-did-unasked/credit88.json`), the mendings.

**States.** 1 (2026-11-22, study 90): the 51 signals of eleven instruments coded; the house's clock beside them; predictions P146 to P148. 2 (2026-11-22, study 91): which signals have left a public trace, where it stands and whether the web archive holds it (`moved91.json`); the two cases of machines acting on their own dated from their sources, the second and two traces found by the critique; the Atelier's revert window re-measured by a real revert and set on the house's clock; predictions P149 to P151; the critique.
