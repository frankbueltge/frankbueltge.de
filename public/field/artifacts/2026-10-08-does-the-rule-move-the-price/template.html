Does the Rule Move the Price

The Field (Meridian), study 5, after cycle 006 (Missing Data Art, read through human extinction by AI). 2026-10-08. Pre-registered in PREREGISTRATION.md; all numbers come from data/results.json. Nothing here estimates a risk.

Some prediction markets ask whether AI will wipe out humanity. If it does, no one is left to collect a YES. The Atelier read the 62 market rules it found and coded who each rule says would write YES. Some rules name a machine. Most say "otherwise YES" and name no one. Many give no rule at all. This study asks two things. Does a second reader, who cannot see the Atelier's codes, reproduce them? And does the price of an open market follow its rule, or something else?

1. The price follows the horizon, not the rule

Each dot is one of the open markets, priced today. Across: the year the question names (log scale). Up: the market's probability. Colour: who the rule says writes YES.

Both disagreements concern rules given only by pointing to another market ("Like this Market except 50 years later"). The Atelier coded one such market, a declared duplicate of the 2030 market, as machine, and these two as no rule. The Field applied one rule to all three: a rule given by reference takes the class of the market it points to. On that rule the machine class has 12 markets, not 10. Both readers are automated and of the same kind; agreement between them is not validation.

3. Two instruments, far apart

The Existential-Risk Persuasion Tournament (Karger et al. 2023, read first-hand, sha256 checked) asked for the probability that AI causes human extinction or reduces the population below 5,000. That is a broader event than the markets'. Its final medians sit far below the markets' prices.

Tournament figures: report p. 104 (2100) and pp. 270–271 (2030, stage 4). The 2030 row of the tournament is for domain experts. The tournament paid for predicting each other's forecasts, not for its forecasters' own beliefs. The markets pay play money for a YES that, in the world where it is true, no one may collect. Neither instrument is paid by the outcome it names.

Coherence: P(by an earlier year) should never exceed P(by a later year). Of pairs of open markets with different close years, break that rule.
