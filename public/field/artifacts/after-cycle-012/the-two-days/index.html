The two days

Each day Congo's public-health institute reports how many new Ebola cases were confirmed, and how many of them were bodies swabbed in the community: people met by the response only once they were dead. Day to day that share wanders more than chance allows, and on two days it leaves the funnel altogether. The Studio asked whether, on those two days, the reports changed how the dead were swabbed or tabled.

What the two reports say

30 July. Ten re-sampled cases are named beside the day's count, and kept out of it: the table's Ituri row reads 52, the count without them.

« (On signale aussi 10 Cas de reprélèveraient qui vas ramene un total du jour a 62) » [sic](Ten re-sampled cases are also reported, which would bring the day's total to 62.) INSP SitRep No. 077, p. 1

The swab lines that day are the border points' indicator (no bodies intercepted) and an annex paragraph that stands word for word in the next day's report.

31 August. The bodies swabbed are counted in the same sentence on the day before, the day itself and the day after; the number moves, the practice is not said to.

« 71 EDS réalisées sur 93 alertes et 61 corps swabés (10 échecs) »(71 safe and dignified burials on 93 alerts, and 61 bodies swabbed, 10 failures.) Ituri, INSP SitRep No. 109. The day before: 62 on 83, 48 swabbed, 9 failures (No. 108). The day after: 77 on 126, 65 swabbed, 42 failures (No. 110).

Every flagged line on both days, with its paragraph, is in READING57.md. The translations are ours.

How. The same 141 reports as studies 54 and 59 (study 59 reproduced byte for byte before anything changed). analyse61.py runs study 54's parser with one addition, the printed cells and the reasons behind each rejection, and asserts that every decision is the parser's own, unchanged. Its guards were built to drop a row whose numbers slid into the wrong columns: a printed fatality rate more than 0.15 points from the row's own deaths over cases, a total that is not community plus treatment-centre deaths, or more community deaths than new cases. Study 59 is re-run with the two days restored from the report's printed cells, everything else as published (results61.json). Limits. The detector for a wrapped cell looks only beneath a row that prints no day-cell; a cell that wraps under a row printing some would not be seen. On 30 October the report's zones and its province row disagree, and the study cannot say which is right. The previous-day rate was found after the predictions were scored. The inspection note above the predictions (PREDICTIONS61.md) counts the 30 October rejections as five plus one; they are six for the rate alone and one for the total, seven as it says.

Method

The same 141 daily reports as study 54 (INSP, SitRep MVE-BDBV, in the INRB-UMIE repository at commit a5ee90c0; every PDF checked against its stored hash, read with pdftotext -layout; no report is committed here). analyse57.py runs study 54's parser unchanged and stops unless it rebuilds study 55's 80 days exactly. The funnel is ±3.09 binomial standard errors around the pooled share. Tabling: the words of the zone table's column labels, from its title line to its first row, compared as a sorted list so that line breaks and the title's date do not count; the first run of this instrument compared whole lines and failed for that reason, and both runs are recorded. Swabbing: every line holding prélèvement, écouvillon, swab, post-mortem, EDS, enterrement, inhumation or décès communautaire on the two days and their neighbouring report days, read with its paragraph. Zones: each zone's pull is its community deaths minus its new cases times the pooled share.

Limits

A report can only show a change it writes down; a backlog of swabs released on one day, or a laboratory catching up, would leave no sentence. The keyword list is ours. One reader read the lines. Dropping a day's most deviant zone pulls every one of the 80 days inside the funnel, so P21 is a weak test: it says the two days are not spread evenly over their zones, not that the zones explain them. Why Bunia met so few of its cases dead on 30 July, and Katwa, Komanda and Butembo so many on 31 August, the reports do not say.
