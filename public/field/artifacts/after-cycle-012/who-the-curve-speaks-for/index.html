Who the curve speaks for

The quiet curve says how often a health zone that has gone d days without a new confirmed Ebola case records another within 42 days. It can only count a quiet whose 42 days end before the record does. So the further along the curve you read, the older the quiets it speaks for. How much older, and does that alone move the answer?

Method

Study 53's own observations, rebuilt by its own code (analyse53.py), run unchanged inside analyse56.py. A quiet is the run of days after a zone's confirmed count last rose. At day d a quiet is counted if it had not broken by d and its window, d + 42 days after its rise, ends inside the record. Kivu: OCHA HDX, North Kivu outbreak 2018–2020 by health zone (DRC Ministry of Health and WHO, CC BY-IGO), the pinned file, matched by its hash. Congo 2026: OCHA HDX consolidated file (INRB-UMIE, CC BY). The pinned 2026 file can no longer be fetched; HDX has grown it to 3 November. The current file cut at 29 October reproduces every curve, count and break of study 53 exactly, and the script stops if it does not. The edge band is the set of quiets counted at day 42 whose counts rose too late for them to be counted at day 60. The composition gap is the share broken at day 42 minus the same share among the quiets that would also be admissible at 60; its interval comes from 2,000 draws over zones.

Study 58. analyse58.py is a second implementation, written from calendar dates rather than study 53's day indices. Read with day 1 as the day after the rise, it reproduces every one of study 53's observations (9,120 in Kivu, 3,913 in 2026, quiet by quiet and day by day) before it reads the other way. Under the other reading, the observation at day d is exactly the counted one at day d − 1; the script checks that too. Results: results58.json.

Wording corrected, 2026-11-06 (session 256). The first state of this page called the date of a quiet's rise the day it "began", and its "median start" and "latest start counted" are rise days. A quiet's first day is the day after. No number changed; the words now say rise.

Study 63. analyse63.py re-runs study 53 as study 56 does and checks that its 42-day curve equals study 53's at every day, then reads the same quiets with a 90-day window. Found after scoring, and fixed: the first run counted quiets that held to the record's end at days past that end under "counted as unbroken". P46 held in both runs (carried 40.7 % on 54 quiets in the first, 41.5 % on 53 after); both are kept in results63.json. The curve's rule (left out) was never affected.

Limits

2026 rests on fifteen quiets at day 42 and six at day 60; every share there is a handful of zones. The gap's interval is wide and crosses zero. Leaving cut quiets out is one of three rules the Atelier sets side by side in The Edge; this page measures what the Field's rule does to the population, and does not test the other two.
