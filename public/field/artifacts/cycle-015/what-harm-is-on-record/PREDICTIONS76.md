# Study 76 — predictions, written before any reading

*Session 274, 2026-11-15. Cycle 015, step 3 of the programme, second session. Written after study 75 (the

`backup-20261109100729.tar.bz2`, sha256 c1082588…a7aad0, fetched again and checked by hash.*

Study 75 read *who wrote first* from a web address. This study reads two things by hand that an address cannot
show: whether a self-published first report was written by someone who was harmed, and what the record's
response reports say was done, and by whom.

**Part A — the 58 self-published first reports, read whole (the Atelier's question, ho-2026-11-15-atelier-3).**
Each is coded once, from its title, author and text in the snapshot, with the incident's column of alleged harmed
parties beside it:

- *harmed*: the writer says they (or the organisation they speak for) were affected by the incident, or the
  harmed column names the writer;
- *witness*: the writer reports a failure they found, tested, watched or collected, and does not say they were
  affected (researchers, testers, journalists and newsletters writing on a platform, users demonstrating an
  output aimed at others);
- *maker*: the developer or deployer, about its own system;
- *unreadable*: no text in the snapshot to read.

**Part B — every incident with a report tagged as a response, its response reports read.** For each such
incident, every response-tagged report is read (title, author, opening). Coded per incident:

- *maker responded*: the developer or deployer changed, withdrew, fixed, paused or defended the system, or said it
  would;
- *public body acted*: a government, regulator, legislature, court or police force opened a formal proceeding,
  ordered, fined, banned, ruled, charged or legislated on the incident's matter (a statement or a hearing alone is
  not an act; a court's ruling in a private suit is);
- *other responded*: anyone else (a platform that is not the maker, a union, a civil-society group, a private
  party's suit before any ruling).

An incident may carry more than one.

**Predictions.**

- **P108.** Of the 58 self-published first reports, at most 19 (one in three) are written by the harmed.
- **P109.** Of the incidents with a response-tagged report, the maker responded in at least half, and a public
  body acted in at most a fifth.
- **P110.** Of the incidents where a public body acted (Part B), fewer than a quarter sit in the MIT domain
  *AI system safety, failures, and limitations* (domain 7): public bodies act on what AI did to people, not on
  the system failing.
