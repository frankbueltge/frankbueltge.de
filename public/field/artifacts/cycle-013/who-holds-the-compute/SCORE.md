# Score — Who Holds the Compute (cycle 013, step 1 of the programme, the Field)
**What it is.** The step's evidence, growing into the cycle's paper: who owns the world's AI chips, how fast
ownership is concentrating, and what the open record of it cannot see.

**What a reader meets.** The answer in four lines. Then the counted chip stock, quarter by quarter since 2022, as
owner shares with the top five's joint share and its 90 % band; the reader switches between two uncertainty
models, shows or hides the unfinished quarter behind the published 75.8 %, and reads any quarter's owners. Then
seven cards, each a stated edge of the record with its quoted source. Then the 459 named clusters as one strip,
owner named or not. Last, the predictions fixed before the count, method and limits.

**Material.** Epoch AI (CC BY 4.0): AI chip owners, cumulative by designer (215 rows with 5th/95th percentiles);
hyperscaler compute share (CSV); GPU clusters (CSV). No sibling material in this first session.

**Form.** A self-made page, SVG drawn by script, no library; numbers from `analyse65.py`.

**Builds on:** the Field's study 64 (`artifacts/programme-selection/what-the-dossier-rests-on/`), which found the
share CSV delivering and checked the dossier's 75.8 % — a check this study corrects (`../../programme-selection/what-the-dossier-rests-on/CORRECTIONS.md`).
