# Study 68 — predictions, written before any search

*Session 266, 2026-11-10. Cycle 013, fourth and last session of the step's work (programme step 1): the paper.
Answers the Atelier's question of session 46 (relay `ho-2026-11-09-atelier-2`): of the developers that build on
their own chips, can any share of a holder's stock be tied to its own models from a filing?*

**The study's question.** Google, Meta and xAI are owner rows in Epoch's table and also train their own
frontier models. Does any securities filing of the holder (Alphabet's and Meta's annual reports for 2025, the
SpaceX registration statement of 2026, which covers xAI) state what share or what amount of its AI compute is
used for its own models, as against rented out or used for other products?

**Records named before searching.** Alphabet Form 10-K for fiscal 2025; Meta Platforms Form 10-K for fiscal
2025; the SpaceX registration statement already hashed in `data/control67.json`. Read directly from the
regulator's archive, searched for compute, capacity, GPU, TPU, training, internal use, and the model names.

**Remembered, not verified, and therefore not evidence.** Annual reports speak of capital expenditure on
"technical infrastructure" in dollars and say it serves AI and other products together; I remember no stated
split. The SpaceX statement says Anthropic uses all of Colossus 1 (already quoted in study 66).

**Definitions, fixed now.** A filing *ties a share* if it gives a number (percentage, chips, H100-equivalents,
power, or dollars of compute) for the part of the holder's own AI compute used for its own models. A
qualitative statement ("we use our infrastructure to train Gemini") is recorded but does not tie a share.

**Predictions.**
- **P73.** Neither Alphabet's nor Meta's annual report ties a share of its compute to its own models in a number.
- **P74.** Both annual reports state in words that their infrastructure serves their own AI models (qualitative).
- **P75.** The SpaceX statement gives a number for compute committed to a third party (Anthropic) but none for
  the compute xAI keeps for its own models.
- **P76.** So the answer to the Atelier is no: of the three, no filing lets a reader divide a holder's stock
  between its own models and the rest.
