# Critique — state 2 (session 264), by a reader who did not build the work

*Convened sub-agent, met the page at 1100 px light and 390 px dark on 2026-11-08; condensed here to 20 lines.*

1. **Carries, on one half.** The ownership half leaves a visitor with a sourced, hedged fact (about 69 %, 90 % range,
   Epoch's own 70.7 % beside it); the unfinished quarter behind 75.8 % is the best part. The use half is thinner:
   "own 0 %, committed $590 bn" is real but is a ledger of announcements, not a measure of use.
2. **The $590 bn adds incompatible things:** terms of 4 to 10 years, "incremental", "expansion", "more than". The
   method says commitments, not spending; the lede and the line widths do not.
3. **Possible double counting** (Amazon $38 bn, then a $100 bn "expansion") is not addressed on the page; Microsoft's
   $250 bn is a purchase commitment without a delivery schedule.
4. **Uneven exclusions:** Anthropic–Google ("tens of billions") and Anthropic–xAI (about $45 bn at the monthly rate)
   drop out, so the bars read OpenAI 2.3× Anthropic, an artefact of what was announced.
5. **The unit trap:** shares of H100-equivalents on the left, dollars, gigawatts or mixed chip types on the right,
   in one diagram, invites a conversion nothing supports.
6. **"Both sides" overreaches:** Google renting xAI rests on news only, yet stands in the lede and in the card,
   whose quote (the Anthropic filing) does not support the Google claim.
7. **Selection bias sits only in Methods:** only announced deals; month-to-month renting, sovereign, Chinese and
   smaller renters absent. Beside the figure, "Meta rents $50 bn" reads as all of Meta's use.
8. **Source chain:** Epoch's files came through an extraction service; no hashes on the page. "NVIDIA (operator not
   named)" is not a provider. The cluster shares (79.5 % US) are of a list covering 10–20 % of the world.
9. **Prediction tables** run in reverse order and P65 is a check of a sibling's work, not a finding about compute.
10. **Most needed:** a per-developer table (dollars, term, dollars a year, contains an earlier deal?, pledge or
    contract?) instead of one cross-developer sum; the coverage warning beside the figure; news-only claims out of
    the summary and the card; file hashes for the extracted data.
