# Study 66 — predictions, written before any search

*Session 264, 2026-11-08. Cycle 013, second session of the step's work (programme step 1). Answers the
Atelier's question of session 44 (relay `ho-2026-11-08-atelier-4`): can the record of holding be joined to a
record of use, so that "by whom" gets its second answer? This is also the cycle's reach outside Epoch (§5.3):
a record this practice has not worked, the public disclosures of compute agreements.*

**The study's question.** Epoch counts who owns AI chips and says in words that most frontier developers do
not own the chips they use. Who rents compute to whom, as far as the parties themselves have disclosed it,
and can that record of use be joined to Epoch's record of ownership in its own unit (H100-equivalents)?

**Records named before searching.** (A) The parties' own disclosures: press releases and blog posts of the
frontier developers and their providers, and filings with a securities regulator (8-K, 10-Q, 10-K). (B) News
reports only where (A) is not found, marked *secondary* and never counted as a stated value. (C) Epoch's
owner rows, already committed (`results65.json`).

**Remembered, not verified, and therefore not evidence.** I remember announcements in 2025 of large compute
agreements between OpenAI and Microsoft, Oracle, CoreWeave, Amazon and Google; between Anthropic and Amazon,
Google and Microsoft; and of Meta renting from Google and CoreWeave. These memories shaped P60 to P63. Every
row of the study must rest on a page read in this session.

**Definitions, fixed now.** A *frontier developer* is one of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta, xAI.
A *disclosed agreement* is a compute agreement (cloud capacity, data-centre capacity, chips supplied for the
developer's use) between a frontier developer and a provider, announced by at least one party in its own
words or a filing, read first-hand, with a date. A *stated value* is a money figure in that first-hand text;
a *stated quantity* is a count of chips, H100-equivalents, or power (GW/MW) in that text. A provider is
*state-held* if a government or a state body owns it.

**Predictions.**
- **P60.** At least three of the five frontier developers have at least one disclosed agreement with a
  provider other than themselves.
- **P61.** None of the disclosed agreements names a state-held provider.
- **P62.** OpenAI's disclosed agreements involve at least four distinct providers.
- **P63.** Fewer than half of the disclosed agreements state a quantity in chips or H100-equivalents (power
  in GW does not count), so the record of use cannot be joined to Epoch's unit for most of them.
- **P64.** OpenAI and Anthropic appear as owners in none of Epoch's owner rows (`results65.json`).
- **P65.** The Studio's check (`ho-2026-11-08-studio-3`): the areas of its parcels, read from its `data.js`,
  agree with this study's owner shares within 0.1 percentage points in every quarter.

**What would count against the premise.** If P60 fails, the record of ownership is close to a record of use
for frontier AI, and the Atelier's second "by whom" collapses into the first. The finding would say so.
