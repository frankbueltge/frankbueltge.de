What Reaches the Kept Share

Step 1 found that Google, Meta and xAI hold 37.8 % of the counted AI chips and build their own frontier models on them. Nothing is sold or rented there, so no contract can carry a law into it. The Atelier asked which laws reach such compute at all, and how much of the 37.8 % they cover. This page reads nine legal texts for that. A day later the Atelier asked what happens when such a law is broken: is the sanction written as something that follows, or as something a body may do, and has it been used once? Section 3 reads the three laws in force for that.

The laws in force reach the model, never the chips. Two of them start at an amount of training compute (the EU at 1025 operations, California at 1026) and put their duties on whoever trains the model.

No law in force addresses a firm because it holds chips, and none makes a firm say how much compute it has or how it divides it. The one text that did, a US order of 2023, was revoked in January 2025.

Use inside the firm is reached, but in confidence. California makes large developers report on internal use every three months, to a state office, exempt from public-records law. The EU text reaches it through a recital and a guideline, not through an article.

So the 37.8 % cannot be counted as covered in chips. By holder: Google and xAI stand on the EU's list of code signatories (26.8 % of the counted chips sit with them); Meta (11.0 %) stands on no such list.

And if a duty is broken (study 70)? The EU's fine is a power the Commission may use; California's penalty follows from the breach, but only if the Attorney General sues. Neither has been used. The export controls have: 13 people and one small company charged over AI chips since August 2025, none of them a holder of the counted chips, and no sentence found.

2. How much of the 37.8 % is named

The whole bar is every AI chip the open record counted at the end of 2025 (study 65). The three coloured parts are the holders that train their own frontier models on chips they own. The laws count models, not chips, so the only coverage that can be stated is by holder: who is named on a public list of the body concerned.

Not being named is not being exempt. The EU's act applies to a provider whether it signs the code or not; the list only shows who has said in public that it will comply through the code. No public list of California's frontier developers was found, and California's reports on internal use are confidential by statute.

5. Method and limits

Eight texts were named before the search and one was found during it (the New York bill). Eight of the nine were fetched from their publishers, hashed and searched for every quoted passage by analyse69.py; all 30 passages stand in the files as fetched. California's act could not be fetched directly: its publisher answers a browser challenge. It was read through a page-fetch service, so its five passages are quoted but not machine-checked, and its date of force rests on two law-firm notes.

Limits. This is a reading of texts by one reader, not legal advice and not a census of law: China's rules, competition law, securities disclosure and tax law were not read, and of US export controls only one section. Whether a recital of the EU act binds as its articles do is a legal question no court has answered. The coding was not repeated by a second reader. The shares are study 65's, with its uncertainty (the five largest holders: 63.5 to 72.9 %).

Section 3 (study 70). Twelve files were fetched from their publishers on 11 November, hashed and searched for every quoted passage by analyse70.py: the EU act and its implementing regulation, the Commission's enforcement page, the US statute and regulation, five releases of the Department of Justice, the export agency's annual report and one release of California's Attorney General. All 25 passages stand in the files as fetched. California's act again answered a direct request with 403; its two passages were read through a page-fetch service and are not machine-checked.

Its limits. A clause was coded by its governing verb, by one reader. The uses are those the enforcing bodies announced and a search found on 11 November: a charge is an allegation, the people charged are presumed innocent, and a case nobody announced is not here. "No record found" covers the pages and searches listed in results70.json and nothing more: court dockets were not read, so a sentence may exist that no page I read reports. Two things are second-hand and drawn pale or left out: the Commission's first requests for information (a law firm's note) and a third guilty plea reported by a news agency in October 2026. Sanctions under other laws (competition, securities, data protection) were not read.
