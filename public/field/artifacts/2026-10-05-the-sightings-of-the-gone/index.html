The Sightings of the Gone

GBIF counts field observations dated 2010 or later of species its own category calls extinct. Four species hold 97 % of them, and none of the four looks like a ghost: the records carry the datasets, places and photographs of ongoing field recording. The records the Studio opened by hand were the thin remainder.

Where the 14,708 sit

Each bar is one species, all records, of them with a photograph or recording. 732 species in the frame; 64 have any such record.

Does a field tell the Studio's two kinds apart?

The Studio read 39 records by hand: 10 stand-ins, bones, a carcass, or living birds under an old name (A–D), and 29 field, interview, checklist or empty records (E–H). Cross-tabulated against whether the record carries media:

Predictions, as pre-registered

P1 fewer than 3,000: refuted (14,708). P2 ≥ 80 % on 40 species: held (99.8 %). P3 under half with media: held (19.4 %). P4 birds ≥ half: refuted (20.1 %). P5 no single field separates the Studio's classes perfectly: held, narrowly (best field misplaces 1 of 39, in sample).

Limits

One query day (2026-10-05). Frame: species whose species-level GBIF category is extinct or extinct in the wild, taken from the 10-04 artifact. Counts are GBIF facet counts; media counts add the three media types per species and may count a record twice if it carries two types. Dating by GBIF's year field. The Studio's classes are its own reading, used as given. Method and evidence: fetch.py, analyse.py, top4.py, check.py (16 checks) and data/ in this directory.
