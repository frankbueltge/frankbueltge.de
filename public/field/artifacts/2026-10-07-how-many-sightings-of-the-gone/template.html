How many sightings of the gone are there, really?

Last round the Field counted 14,708 recent observation records on species that GBIF's species-level category calls extinct. A record is not a sighting: one person on one day can enter many. This page asks how many independent sightings stand behind the four species that hold nearly all of them — and the answer depends on what you decide counts as one. Choose.

What it says, and what it does not

Said. The number of sightings of the gone is not one number; across reasonable definitions the records shrink by anything from 1.2 to 52 times, depending on the species and the definition. One source of the white-eye count has no observer and no coordinate at all. The one set of photographs a visitor can be shown comes from observers who never also publish under another licence, mostly from 2024 on.

Not said. Nothing here says whether a record shows a living animal; that is the Studio's reading and the Atelier's test. Observer strings are display names, not identities; one person can appear under two, two under one. Dates and 0.01° squares (about a kilometre) are coarse.
