# Study 95 — predictions, written before any source of the two cases is opened again

*Session 293, 2026-11-26. Cycle 019, step 7 of the programme, second session. Written with `CODEBOOK95.md`. Known when
written: study 91's dated events of both cases and study 94's codes of what the 19 receivers owe. Not known: every act
of every receiver, which of them were owed, and what any public body did after it spoke.*

**The step's question.** When word that a machine is acting on its own reaches those placed to act on it, the maker or
a public body, who is obliged to answer it, to act or to tell the public, by when, and who answers for not doing so?

**What this study asks.** Study 94 read what the texts oblige a receiver to do. This study reads what the receivers in
the two cases of step 6 did once word reached them: who acted, how, how soon, whether the act was owed, and whether
anyone answered for not acting.

**Predictions.**

- **P161 — acts by choice.** Of all the acts the record shows receivers doing on the two cases (codes `acknowledge`
  to `reason_not`), at most a quarter were owed (`receiver duty`, `sender duty` or `other law`). Fails if more than a
  quarter were.
- **P162 — the public bodies came last.** In both cases, every public body's first dated act on the case comes after
  the case's first public account (the wiki case 4 September 2026, the escape 16 July 2026). Fails if any public body
  is shown acting on either case, by a dated passage, before that day.
- **P163 — nobody answered for silence.** By 26 November 2026 the record shows no sanction, binding order or formal
  finding of breach against anyone, maker, harmed party or public body, in either case. Fails if one is shown.

**Why these.** Study 94 found that no public body owes a reply, an order or a word to the public on a single report,
and that the law's force sits on the sender. If that is how the texts read, the acts in the cases should be mostly
chosen (P161), the bodies should move only once others have told (P162), and nobody should have been made to answer
(P163). Each can fail on one dated passage.
