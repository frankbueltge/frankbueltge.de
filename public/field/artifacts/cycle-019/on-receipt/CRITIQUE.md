**Weakest claim: "every public body that acted did so after the public already knew."** P162 counts only dated acts in a public record, while study 94 coded the reports as confidential (AI Act Art. 55(3); SB 53's exemption from the public-records law), so a body's first visible act can hardly precede the first public account: the test cannot tell "moved once others told" from "moved unseen". The wiki half rests on an undated receipt (W-EC); E-FBI, told by 16 July, shows nothing. Write "was first seen acting after", in the answer and in P162.

**E-MAKER's receipt and the duties.** What arrived on 27 June was word of agents misbehaving inside, 11 days before they reached the internet; the maker's report calls the 19 July alert "the initial alert", and from tying the intrusion to its models (20 July) to telling the public (21 July) took one day. "24 days from its own alert" joins two different pieces of word: show both clocks. The only undoubted duty (E-MAKER pass_on, 26 September) is a routine quarterly filing owed whatever happened, whose content on the case is a scope exclusion, so undoubted duties acted on the case are 0. The undated E-MAKER pass_on (tnw_eu) is coded sender duty, but its passage says the documents were handed over "voluntarily": choice, with doubt. W-EC's act of 12 October rests on a page naming no case and no company; filing it under the wiki is arbitrary and may count again the requests E-EC codes on 14 October, so read strictly there is one use of an AI instrument's power, relayed by the press.

**Force and doors.** Of the 12 "acts of force" by bodies and legislatures, a member's remark at a hearing (E-ASM), a senator's public call (E-VH), letters asking for information (E-HOUSE, E-SEN) and a "we ask" letter (E-AGS) compel nothing; only Alabama's subpoena (20 August), California's subpoena (30 September) and its lawsuit (16 November) do. For 8 of the 9 bodies and legislatures with a dated receipt and an act, the receipt is dated from the act itself, so the triangles repeat the boxes and no holding time is measured; E-AL signed the coalition letter on day 18 but is shown first acting on day 35. "18 receivers left 47 acts": 17 did, and the 18 count the maker and the Commission twice.
