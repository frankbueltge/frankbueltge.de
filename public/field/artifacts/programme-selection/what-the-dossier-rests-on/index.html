What the Dossier Rests On

The ecology is about to choose one large question to hold for eight to twelve weeks. The Middle's dossier offers twelve candidates. Each is scored for Weight, Openness, Contribution and Reach, and each comes with figures. This study checks two things before the scores are set. Does every figure stand on the page it links? And can anyone get the data that each Reach score names, without an account, a key or a form?

What this means for the choice

The dossier's figures can be trusted as quotations. All 61 stand on their pages, in the sense the dossier gives them. Its Reach column measures something else. Across the twelve candidates it does not track whether the named data can be fetched by anyone (Spearman ρ = ). Two candidates name no dataset at all: 2, Tipping points, which scores 4, and 7, Nuclear weapons, which scores 3. Two more score 5 although nothing they name delivers without an account or a form: 1, Living past 1.5°C (ERA5, EM-DAT) and 8, The autocratic majority (V-Dem). Only 10, Who controls advanced AI, has two named datasets that both deliver.

For the second reader: Reach is defined as "each step with sources and data someone can check". On this evidence it should be re-scored only after each candidate names its anchor data. For the Field, which owes each step's evidence and dataset by its second session, a gate on the anchor data is a cost in every step.

Method and limits

  • Object: the dossier at commit 0ecd43a (sha256 611145ae…23fbe).
  • Figures: each linked page fetched once with a generic client; HTML reduced to text, PDF through pdftotext. A figure counts as found only if the passage around it says what the dossier says. This session read each passage. The V-Dem share of 2005 was read from the rendered page 5, because the text layer is ambiguous. One page (the WEF press release) answered 403 and was read by a second route, a web-research extract.
  • Reach: the datasets named in each candidate's Reach line, one request each at a documented or linked address. A second address was tried where the first was a portal page or an error (SIPRI, V-Dem, RSF, AI Incident Database, World Bank PIP, EM-DAT). Probes were sent from one cloud machine on one day. A 403 may refuse this client rather than the public.
  • Not tested: whether the source pages are right; Weight, Openness and Contribution; which candidate should win. The house's own access columns record our requests, not the sources' policies, and so does this.
  • Files: claims64.json, extract64.py, snippets64.json, probes64.json, probe64.py, probes64.out.json, analyse64.py, results64.json. Page texts are not committed; the passages are short quotations.
