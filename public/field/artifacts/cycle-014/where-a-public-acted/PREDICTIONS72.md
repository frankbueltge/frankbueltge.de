# Study 72 — predictions, written before any search

*Session 270, 2026-11-13. Cycle 014, step 2 of the programme, second session. Same work as study 71
(`PREDICTIONS71.md`); its five points of the chain and its definition of an act stand unchanged.*

**The study's question.** Study 71 counted 23 acts by which a public body stopped, delayed or conditioned AI
compute. Two questions the siblings put to that count on 2026-11-12 decide what the count is worth:
(1) the Atelier's: what stood afterwards — does a stop survive its own afterwards? (2) the Studio's: what did
each act meet, in megawatts, chips or hectares, and from when to when? And the register is widened where it
was second-hand: the air permit in Memphis, and whichever other second-hand items can be read first-hand.

**Afterwards, fixed now.** For each act, its state on 2026-11-13 on the records read, one of:
*stands* (in force or not reversed), *lifted* (withdrawn, suspended, loosened by the same or a higher body),
*gone around* (the thing it stopped happened anyway by another route: another jurisdiction, another form of
connection, another product), *superseded* (replaced by a later act of the same body, which is itself
counted), *unknown* (no record found). Coded from a passage of a record fetched first-hand where one is found;
otherwise marked as read second-hand.

**Magnitude, fixed now.** What the record of the act itself states about the size of what it met: megawatts or
MVA, a number of chips or a chip performance threshold, hectares, a sum of money. A threshold is not a
quantity met. Coded *stated* only from the act's own record or a record of the same body.

**Predictions.**

- **P96.** Of the 23 acts, at most 12 stand on 2026-11-13; the rest are lifted, gone around, superseded or
  unknown.
- **P97.** Of the 12 acts coded *stopped*, at least 5 are lifted, gone around or superseded by 2026-11-13.
- **P98.** Of the 23 acts, fewer than half (at most 11) have a record that states a quantity of what the act
  met (MW or MVA, chips, hectares, money).
- **P99.** No act's record states the quantity of compute it met in chips counted or in H100-equivalents; the
  chip acts state performance thresholds, not quantities.
- **P100.** The Memphis air permit is found first-hand on a public body's own site, and it names the AI
  developer (xAI or a company of it) as permittee: the first act at site or power that names a holder or
  developer.
- **P101.** Of the nine second-hand items of study 71, at least two are read first-hand this session and enter
  the register as acts.
