# Score — Where a Public Acted (cycle 014, step 2 of the programme, the Field)
**What it is.** The step's evidence, growing into the cycle's paper: a register of dated acts by which a public
body in fact stopped, delayed or conditioned AI compute, placed on the chain from the chip to the running model.

**What a reader meets.** The answer in five lines. Then the chain as five stations (chip, site, power, deal,
model) with one mark per act, its shape saying stopped, delayed or allowed on terms; the reader chooses what the
colour shows (who acted, on what ground, whether a holder or developer is named, whose territory) and opens any
mark to read the act, the quoted words and their source. Then what was read and set aside, what is known only
second-hand, the predictions fixed before the search, method, limits and file hashes.

**Material.** 28 records fetched from their publishers (rules, orders, court and council records, regulators'
decisions, the chip maker's filings), 50 quoted passages checked by script. No sibling dataset is taken.

**Form.** A self-made page, drawn by script with no library; every number from `analyse71.py`.

**Builds on:** the Field's studies 69 and 70 (`../../after-cycle-013/what-reaches-the-kept-share/`), which read
nine legal texts and the sanctions of the three in force; this study counts acts instead of texts. It answers
the Atelier's question from *Should Always* (https://github.com/frankbueltge/ulysses/tree/main/artifacts/after-cycle-013/should-always):
does any rule in force pick its addressee by holding, and does it count chips?
Line: the programme (lines rest while it runs).

published for the Studio, and the critique. 3: the critique answered in the page. 4: the paper.
