Where a Public Acted

Between a chip leaving the factory and a model answering a question there are five points at which a public body could put its hand on AI compute. This page collects the dated acts at which one in fact did: stopped something, delayed it, or allowed it on terms. Each act is read in the body's own record, or in a filing of the firm acted on. Since the second state it also follows each act to what stood on 13 November 2026, and says what size of thing it met.

1. The chain, and every act read so far

One mark is one act; a dot in its corner marks an act added in the second state. A filled mark stopped something, a half-filled mark delayed it, an outlined mark allowed it on terms. Dashed circles at the deal point are reviews that closed without an act. Choose what the colour shows, then open any mark.

Open a mark to read the act, the words it rests on and where they stand.

      4. Method and limits

      • Afterwards is each act's state on 13 November 2026, read from a later record where one was found: stands, lifted or loosened (by the same or a higher body), gone around (what it stopped happened anyway in another form or place), superseded (replaced by a later act of the same body, itself counted). One state, Chile's, rests on press reports and says so. Size is what the act's own record states about what it met; a threshold in a rule is not a size met.
      • A found set, not a census. For each point of the chain a short list of searches was run (listed in results71.json), and each act they led to was read in a record of the acting body or of the firm acted on. Counts are lower bounds. Shares say what was read, not how the world divides: a private tracker lists many more local moratoria than the two read here.
      • Every quoted passage is checked by the script against the file as fetched, and every file is hashed (below). The script stops if a passage is missing.
      • One reader coded the acts. The point of the chain, the effect, the ground and the named party were coded once, by the definitions in the predictions file. Where the record states a result but not its reason, the page says so on the act.
      • Dates. Three acts are dated to the month, because the firm's filing gives no day. Two Brazilian votes are dated by their signatures.
      • Not read. No court docket and no licence itself: export licences are not published, so the chip maker's filings stand in for them. Chinese and Korean authorities' own records were not reached. The Memphis permit was read in the department's evaluation and its answer to comments, not in the issued permit, which was found only as a scan. Merger reviews of chip designers were left out as outside the deal point's definition (compute and the contracts it is rented under).
      • Refused.
