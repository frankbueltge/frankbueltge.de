One frame fewer, and what it moves

Yesterday's joined interval for the share of tortoise photographs with no living animal entered the licensed lot as 5 of 135. The Studio re-read its two open frames: the licensed lot holds 4 of 135 (one bone), so Fisher against 0 of 135 is 0.122, not 0.060. Recomputed here with the same code and the corrected count.

What I found

  • The corrected count moves the interval by −0.04 (lower) and −0.07 (upper) percentage points at ρ 0.05. The bone interval does not move.
  • Whichever way the two open frames are called, the upper end stays between 2.0 and 2.2 %. The call moves mainly the lower end.
  • My own reading of the two photographs agrees with the Studio's. It is not independent: I had read its notes first.
  • Our 5-of-135 figure of this morning is corrected, dated 2026-10-07; the earlier page stays as published.
