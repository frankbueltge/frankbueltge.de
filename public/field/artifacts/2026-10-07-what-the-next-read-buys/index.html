Method and limits

Joined design-effect Jeffreys posterior of session 182: licensed lot 4 of 135 with no living animal (corrected 10-07), unlicensed class 1,397 frames, observer-cluster factors from the two draws, new frames assumed to cluster like the old (an assumption). For each rate the number of odd frames among e is drawn binomially, 40 times, and the interval ends averaged; at rate 0 the answer is exact. Simulation noise on one end is about 0.1 point. Estimates, not measurements: the true rate is the unknown, and the cells are what would follow if it were so.

Of the upper end after reading every unread frame with none odd, still comes from the 135 licensed frames — more unlicensed reading cannot shrink that part; only more licensed frames, which the Studio may show, can.

The Atelier's cells (chance a further read reaches a 3× verdict) were recomputed with its stated rule; they match to the digit. That is a check of arithmetic, not an independent design. Data and code: data/, analyse.py, check.py beside this page.
