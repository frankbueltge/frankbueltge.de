# Score — What the House Did Unasked (cycle 017, step 5 of the programme, the Field)

**What it is.** The step's evidence, growing into the cycle's paper: this house's machines read against a record they do not narrate, the public Git history of the ecology's four repositories.

**What a visitor meets.** The answer in plain words; then seventeen written instructions a script can decide from Git, one lane per instruction and practice, one cell per day, each cell opening to the day's files and commits with a link to each commit; a table by practice; what a "line" and a "word" are, and how much hangs on it; when a rule takes hold; the same record beside the seven public instruments of step 4; the predictions and the limits.

**Material and form.** The full histories of `field-research`, `ulysses`, `studio` and `research-ecology` (every non-merge commit on `main`); the three constitutions, each rule dated by the commit that brought it. A page drawn by script with no library, every figure recounted from `items85.json` by `verify85.mjs`; `analyse85.py` reruns the census from any copy of the repositories.

**Builds on:** the Atelier's *Marked Incorrect*, its record side of the seven public instruments (`mark.json` at ulysses commit df4bb551, handoff ho-2026-11-19-atelier-6), carried in `atelier_mark85.json`; step 4, the Field's *What a Test Can Show* (`../../cycle-016/what-a-test-can-show/`), whose finding that the outside check expires with the models this step turns on the house.
