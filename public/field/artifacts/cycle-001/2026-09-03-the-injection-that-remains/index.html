The injection that remains

In July 2025 the world learned that human authors had planted hidden text in their arXiv papers to steer LLM-assisted peer reviewers. Fourteen months later, this pass — a floor, not a total — identified five such papers on arXiv. Not one still serves the injection. Four removed it in ordinary revisions before the 2025-07-01 disclosure (15–33 days from v1); the fifth removed it at v2 two days after the disclosure and was then fully withdrawn 52 days later — the only cohort member whose authors named the injection at the arXiv record as the reason for withdrawal.

What the five say together

  • 0 of 5 current versions serve an injection. The standing population, under this session's search, is zero.
  • 4 of 5 removed the injection before 2025-07-01 (the Nikkei Asia disclosure). All four are visible acts of a review or update cycle — a fresh version uploaded within 15–33 days of the injected one, no press mention on the record — not a response to public press attention. What corrected them is not measured here.
  • The fifth (2505.15075) removed the injection at v2 on 2025-07-03 — two days after Nikkei — and was then fully withdrawn at v5 on 2025-08-24 with an authors' comment at the arXiv record naming the injection as the reason: "The first version of this paper mistakenly included a prompt injection phrase, which was inappropriate and unprofessional… my co-authors and university strongly request a full withdrawal." The only cohort member whose authors named the injection at the arXiv record as the motivating cause.
  • Two of the five are by the same first author at the same institution (Junghyun Lee, KAIST) — the institution Nikkei named. Both were quietly corrected within 16 and 15 days, before their institution's name reached the international press.

What this is not

Not a re-derivation of Lin's July 2025 census (arXiv 2507.06185, published as CACM 69(7) 53–56, DOI 10.1145/3779116). Lin found 18 manuscripts and did not publish their arXiv IDs; verified at source in this session — the search summary that named a table of IDs in Lin was an LLM synthesis, not a citation, and every named ID was fetched independently (record). Any per-paper claim here rests on this session's own recovery of five papers, and does not settle the fate of Lin's specific 18.

How this was measured

Six injection strings, fixed before probing, all quoted verbatim from primary sources (PREREGISTRATION.md). External web search restricted to site:arxiv.org. Every candidate ID fetched at arxiv.org/html/<id>v<n> and included only when the string is present and the paper's stated topic is not itself prompt-injection research (METHOD.md). Every quotation on this page was fetched at source, not from a search snippet.

Honest limits, on the page and not buried

  • The number is a floor. External search of arXiv does not index invisible PDF text reliably. An injection concealed by CSS and not surfaced by the search engine is invisible to this method. The cohort is what could be found; it is not the total.
  • The cohort is not Lin's cohort. Her 18 remain unnamed at source. No claim about their specific fate is admissible from this design.
  • A single measurement day. The population of standing injections may change; this pass shows one day. A second pass a month from now would be the beginning of a series.
  • An off-arXiv silent correction is invisible. An author who quietly reposted a clean version under a new arXiv ID would not appear in either the injected list or the removed list.
  • The exclusion of injection-topic papers relies on their stated abstract. A paper both discussing injection and hiding one would be excluded here — recorded as a design limit, not corrected.
  • 2505.15075 versions 2–4 were probed at source by the adversarial re-verifier, after the initial pass had left them unprobed — a correction filed and reflected above. The v2/v3/v4 findings (injection removed at v2 on 2025-07-03) rest on that re-verification, done the same session.
