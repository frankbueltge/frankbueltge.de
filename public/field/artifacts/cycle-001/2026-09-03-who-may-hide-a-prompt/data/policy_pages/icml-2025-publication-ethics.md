# ICML 2025 — Publication Ethics

- **URL:** https://icml.cc/Conferences/2025/PublicationEthics
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-03
- **Version note on page:** "Update July 11, 2025: Added statement about hidden LLM prompts"

## Verbatim passages

> Submitting a paper with a "hidden" prompt is scientific misconduct if that
> prompt is intended to obtain a favorable review from an LLM.

> The inclusion of such a prompt is an attempt to subvert the peer-review
> process.

## Additional passage surfaced by adversarial re-fetch

> We did not desk-reject these identified papers because such a
> consequence was judged to be too severe … We contacted the authors
> of the identified papers and reported them to the ICML Oversight
> Committee and ICML Board.

*(Important context: ICML 2025 explicitly chose not to desk-reject
the papers it identified as carrying hidden prompts, referring the
authors to the Oversight Committee and Board instead. The
`explicit-forbidden-with-consequence` A1 code stands under the
pre-registration's definition, which admits "a listed process" as a
consequence, but readers should know that the listed process here
did not include desk rejection.)*

## Passages by axis

- **A1 (author-side rule):** quoted above. Consequence named: "scientific
  misconduct" and "attempt to subvert the peer-review process," with
  the listed process of Oversight-Committee/Board referral.
- **A2 (reviewer-side LLM rule):** page also states, per the fetch,
  reviewers "are forbidden from using LLMs to produce their reviews of
  paper submissions."
- **A3 (venue-embedded probe):** the page distinguishes hidden prompts
  intended to solicit favourable reviews from prompts "intended to
  detect if LLMs are being used by reviewers; the latter is an
  acceptable use of hidden prompts." **Permitted, not stated deployed
  at ICML 2025.**
- **A4 (accountability):** consequence named against the author of the
  submission ("Submitting a paper with a 'hidden' prompt is scientific
  misconduct"). The reviewer-side use of an LLM is separately
  forbidden but not linked at this page to the author's injection as
  a joint offence.
