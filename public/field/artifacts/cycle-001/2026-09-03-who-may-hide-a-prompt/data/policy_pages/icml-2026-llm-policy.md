# ICML 2026 — Policy for LLM use in reviewing

- **URL:** https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/LLM-Policy
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-03

## Verbatim passages

> Use of LLMs to help understand the paper and related works, and polish
> reviews.

> Ask clarifying questions about the submission. Ask about background
> concepts or concepts introduced earlier in the paper.

> Use LLM to help polish the final review.

> Ask LLMs about strengths/weaknesses, ask to suggest key points for the
> review, suggest an outline for the review, or write the full review.

> Ask LLM to summarize the paper. Ask LLM to evaluate strengths and
> weaknesses. Ask LLM to write a review.

> Any attempt by a reviewer to deviate from their assigned policy
> constitutes a violation of our academic integrity policy, and may
> result in actions by the program chairs (including the desk rejection
> of their own submissions).

## Passages by axis

- **A2 (reviewer-side LLM rule): `restricted-permitted`.** The policy
  explicitly separates permitted uses (understand the paper, polish the
  review) from forbidden ones (summarise, evaluate, draft, outline). A
  parallel Reviewer Instructions page adds: "Reviewers must follow
  their assigned actual policy for LLM use in reviewing, displayed on
  their Reviewer Console" — ICML 2026 runs an assigned-policy split.
- **A4:** reviewer consequence named for LLM-policy violation:
  "including the desk rejection of their own submissions."
