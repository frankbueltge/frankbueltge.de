# EMNLP 2025 — Reviewer Policies

- **URL:** https://2025.emnlp.org/reviewer-policies/
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-03

## Verbatim passages

> it is acceptable to use LLMs for paraphrasing, grammatical checks and
> proof-reading, but not for the content of the (meta-)reviews.

> the content of both the submission and (meta-)review is confidential.
> Therefore, even for acceptable purposes such as proofreading, it must
> not be passed on to non-privacy-preserving third parties, such as
> commercial LLM services.

> While there is no definitive way of determining whether a review was
> (entirely) generated by an LLM, the Program Chairs will review the
> evidence and only proceed in cases where there is no reasonable
> doubt.
