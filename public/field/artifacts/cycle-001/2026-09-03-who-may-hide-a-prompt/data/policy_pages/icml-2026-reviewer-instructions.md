# ICML 2026 — Reviewer Instructions

- **URL:** https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerInstructions
- **Fetched:** 2026-09-03

## Verbatim passages

> Prompt injection by authors is forbidden. ICML integrity chairs and
> program chairs will use prompt-injection detectors to ensure
> compliance.

> While prompt injection by authors is disallowed, we will not penalize
> papers with prompts that merely seek to detect the use of LLMs by
> reviewers.

> Reviewers must follow their assigned actual policy for LLM use in
> reviewing, displayed on their Reviewer Console.

> Any deviation from their assigned policy [...] constitutes a violation
> of peer-review ethics, and may result in the desk rejection of their
> own submissions.

## Passages by axis

- **A1:** author-side prompt injection forbidden; enforcement is by
  "prompt-injection detectors" run by the integrity and program chairs.
  This is the venue's own tooling reading the venue's own submissions.
- **A3:** the sanction line above authorises **author-embedded** probes
  whose intent is to detect an LLM-using reviewer — `permitted-not-deployed`
  at this URL; `deployed` at the Peer Review FAQ URL (separate file).
