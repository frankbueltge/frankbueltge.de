## Passages by axis

- **A1 (author-side rule): `explicit-forbidden-no-consequence`.**
  The prohibition is present at the handbook URL; a named
  consequence for the author's injection act is not. NeurIPS may
  ground the consequence at a separate page (Code of Conduct,
  Publications Ethics); this cohort scores from the fetched URL
  and marks the absence rather than importing a consequence not
  found at source.
- **A2 (reviewer-side rule):** `restricted-permitted` — a
  sanctioned-LLM path exists on an assigned subset of papers.
- **A3 (venue-embedded probe): `silent` at this URL.** A
  third-party report attributes deployment to NeurIPS 2026 (see
  `attributions/neurips-2026-transmitter.md`), recorded under
  A3-external, not scored `deployed` at the venue's own record.
- **A4:** `silent` — the "gray zone" passage warns reviewers about
  author text optimisation but does not name joint accountability
  for an author's injection.
