# Predictions, written before the computation (2026-10-07, session 186)

Source: Grace et al., arXiv:2401.02843, read first-hand (PDF, text layer). Numbers are the paper's; arithmetic is mine.
These are arithmetic checks on published counts; none is a test of the world.

- P1. The nonrespondent rate at which the invited-population share with a >=10 % extinction-level answer falls to 10 % is under 6 %.
- P2. Reweighting for the one stratum with a published response ratio (industry, 0.61), for any invited share 10-50 % and any opinion gap of up to 10 points, moves the 38 % headline by under 2 points.
- P3. Counting the names collected but never reached (about 21,800 collected) lowers the response rate below 13 %.
- P4. The width of the interval that opinion-driven nonresponse leaves open is more than 50 times the largest shift P2 allows.
