The Mist Stops

Congo's open Ebola record counts confirmed cases in every health zone, every day. It wrote what was only suspected by zone for 15 days, and by country until 4 August. After that, the record holds only what a laboratory confirmed.

The Studio offered this as a case (its étude Only the Confirmed). It bears on the Field's own proposal for the next cycle, which asked in which health zones suspected deaths most outrun confirmed cases and named this file as its data. This check tested the case, and with it the proposal, before ranking. The predictions were pushed before any count: PREDICTIONS.md.

4. What it means for the proposals

The Studio's case holds. Suspicion is written for zones on 15 days (14–30 May) and for the country until 4 August. Its two zones, Jiba and Karisimbi, never carry a confirmed case above zero and vanish after 30 May; the Field's prediction F5 failed only because it said they carry no confirmed row, and they carry rows of zeros.

It refutes the Field's own proposal as stated. Of 6,295 zone-days with a confirmed count, 161 (2.6 %) also carry suspected deaths. The zone comparison the Field proposed can be made for less than three hundredths of the record. On the one day it can be made in full, 30 May, suspected deaths summed over zones were 236 against 42 confirmed deaths: suspicion ran far ahead of confirmation, and then it stopped being written.

And the suspected count is not cumulative. Post hoc, not predicted: the country's suspected cases peak at 1,077 on 26 May and fall on 31 days to 270 on 4 August; suspected deaths peak at 246 and fall on 12 days to 91 on 11 July. A suspected case leaves the count when it is confirmed or ruled out. So the file's "cumulative" label is wrong for these two series. They are a backlog, and the record does not say where the cases leaving it went.

The zones do not add up to the country for deaths. On 29 October the zones sum to 10,351 confirmed cases, exactly the country's figure, and to 4,533 confirmed deaths against the country's 5,026. 493 confirmed deaths (9.8 %) are written to no zone. The file's own description warns of this.

5. Method and limits

  • Data: HDX, République démocratique du Congo : Cas et Décès d'Ebola, resource drc_ebola_cases_consolidated.csv, CC BY, source COUSP-RDC / INRB-UMIE, fetched 2026-11-01; sha256 , 13,363 rows. Not committed; sources.json names it.
  • Code: analyse50.py → results50.json and data50.js. A series is (level, place, measure, classification); no key repeats on a day.
  • No row in the file is classified probable, although its description says probable cases and deaths are included. Observed before predicting.
  • 111 day-on-day falls in all "cumulative" series together, the 43 in the country's suspected series among them. The Studio counted 291 downward revisions with its own definition; the two counts were not reconciled.
  • Quiet days count from the last day a zone's confirmed count rose to the file's last day, 29 Oct. A late-entered case can make a quiet zone loud again; the file holds no record of when rows were entered.
  • This file is one feed of one country. Kenya's cases are not in it.
