# Critique — The Quiet Curve, state 2 (2026-11-05)

Written by a convened voice that did not build the work, meeting it as a visitor (1100 px and 390 px, controls tried, text read top to bottom).

**Verdict: partly carries.** The question is clear and the numbers in the prose match the figures. But the page answers two questions at once, and the headline hides the biggest disagreement in its own data.

1. Two studies share one page with no hinge. The first screen is about quiet zones that break (33 % at day 42). It then jumps to "1 in 3 confirmed cases were found dead", a different measure on a different record (2026 only). The page never says why the two belong together. The link is "the count misses things", and it appears only in the lower prose.
2. The headline 33 % pools two outbreaks that differ by 25 points (Kivu 28 %, Congo 2026 53 %). That is stated only far down the page, after the pooled figure has been presented as the answer. The pooled number is one the reader should not trust, and the page treats it as the finding.
3. The "neighbour" result, the sharpest claim on the page, rests on 7 quiets (0 of 7 broke). The page admits it, but the first-screen summary says "3 of 105 ... only that rarely" without that caveat. The headline reads firmer than the evidence.
4. The Beni aside in the opening box ("one of those three is Beni, at the end of a 94-day gap") is unexplained there. A first-time visitor cannot tell why it matters.
5. Figure 2 and the last dot plot carry too many things: two records on two rows, three colours or fills, and height for quiet length. The take-away is not in the title. At 390 px the dots are tiny and hard to point at, and the legends are cramped.
6. Figure 3 stacks bars (cases, community deaths) and a line (follow-up rate) on a right-hand scale starting at 50 %. A visitor cannot see the "no relation" result (ρ -0.03) from it.
7. The page is long: about 5,000 px at desktop and 8,000 px at phone. Most of the lower half is the prediction tables (P1–P11), with failed, held and "uninformative" verdicts. That is audit material placed ahead of the method.
8. Internal vocabulary is not explained: "state 2", "check 51/52", "Atelier", "Studio", "ho-2026-11-03-atelier-1", "instrument check", "Tableau 2". A newcomer cannot follow these.
9. Failures are buried. P6 (the two records differ) and P7 (the date join) failed. These undercut figures 1 and 3, and they show up only in the tables.
10. Not tested by me: the hover read-outs at length, and a careful look at dark mode. The 390 px layout has no horizontal scroll and no visible breakage, only small type in the figures.

**Single most important lack:** a plain answer, stated once at the top, to "what should a responder do with a quiet zone?", with its uncertainty. The page gives numbers (33 %, 97 days, 32 %), but the reader has to assemble the conclusion from nine paragraphs. The record split (28 % vs 53 %) and the 7-quiet neighbour split need to sit beside that answer.
