The Quiet Curve

WHO declares an Ebola outbreak over once 42 days pass without a new case, "twice the maximum incubation period". That rule speaks of a whole outbreak. But a case count also goes quiet zone by zone, and responders read those quiets. This study asks of two records how often a health zone that has been quiet for d days records another confirmed case within the next six weeks, and whether anything outside the zone can tell the quiets that break from those that hold.

On these two records: of the health zones that had gone 42 days without a new confirmed case, of (, interval ) recorded another within the next 42 days.

What this state shows, and what it does not yet

  • From which day is a zone quiet? Not from day 42. At zone level, while an outbreak is running, the 42-day mark leaves about a one-in-three chance of a new case within six weeks. Within three weeks it is about one in five (switch the horizon).
  • The neighbourhood tells what the zone's own record does not. The Atelier found that the first 42 days of a quiet sound the same whether it later broke or held (The Last Case: 0.54 and 0.44 against a coin's 0.50). Split by a neighbour's case: at day 42, of quiets with a case elsewhere in the province in the last 21 days broke, against without one. But only quiets at day 42 lacked such a neighbour. The split rests on them. Read as a test, "no case in the province for 21 days" is close to "the outbreak has ended here".
  • How much the count does not see. On counts alone, the count admits a chain it missed only when a case comes from nowhere visible: of . Every other break could be an import from a known focus or a chain nobody followed. A count cannot tell those apart. State 2 measures one part of what it misses. The daily reports say how many of the day's confirmed cases were found dead in the community: over the readable days, and about the same in the first half as in the second ( and ). That share did not move with the day's contact follow-up rate (Spearman's ρ over days; predicted at most −0.20, failed). A body is a case the count saw only once nobody could be followed from it any more.
  • Are breaks met dead more often? This could not be tested. Only of the 34 breaks of 2026 can be read in a report of the same date (the instrument check needed 31). Of those, were met dead, against of all zone days with a new case. That is no sign that the case ending a quiet is found dead more often. It is post hoc and on breaks. In breaks the report of the day shows no new case in the zone: the rise in the open file comes from a corrected cumulative figure, not from a reported case.
  • The two records differ. At day 42, Kivu , Congo 2026 . The 2026 outbreak is still running at the record's end and gives only quiets a full six-week window.
  • Kenya is not in this state either. No zone-level Kenyan record is in hand, and the work's one sibling material is already chosen.

Method

Records: OCHA HDX, North Kivu outbreak by health zone, 2018–2020 (ministry and WHO, CC BY-IGO, sha256 76c7e999…86cb), North Kivu, Ituri and South Kivu rows; OCHA HDX, Congo 2026 consolidated (INRB-UMIE, CC BY, sha256 761e1ed4…2dbf), 14 May – 29 October 2026. Quiet rule as in checks 51 and 52: a rise is a report on which a zone's cumulative confirmed count exceeds every earlier value, and a quiet runs from one rise to the next. A neighbour is another zone of the same province (Kivu: the file's province; 2026: the first four characters of the pcode). Intervals: 2,000 bootstrap draws over zones, seed 53. Everything is computed by analyse53.py into results53.json; this page reads data53.js and computes no number of its own.

State 2: INSP, Rapport de situation SitRep MVE-BDBV, the 141 PDFs in INRB-UMIE/BDBV2026-Data (commit a5ee90c0, 4 November 2026; 74 of them from its large-file store, each matching its pointer's sha256), read as text (pdftotext -layout). Each zone row is checked against itself (its printed fatality must equal deaths over cases, and community plus intra-CTE deaths must equal its total), and a report is used if at least 80 % of its zone rows pass. Blank cells are placed by the column positions of the full rows on the same page. A break is joined to the report whose reporting date is the break's date. Everything is computed by analyse54.py into results54.json. No PDF or text is committed: the repository asks for contact before use of its epidemiological data, and this page publishes counts read from the public reports, not their files.

Limits. A community death is counted on the day it was confirmed, and the reports do not say how long the chain behind it ran. It measures one kind of unseen case: those who died before anyone followed them. Unseen cases who survived are not in it. The reports do not say, in a table that can be read, whether each new case was a listed contact. A rise in a cumulative count dates a case by its report, not its onset. Kivu's record has one 94-day hole (8 March – 9 June 2020, check 52), in which no rise can be written; quiets across it are counted as calendar days. A province is a coarse neighbourhood, and 21 days is one incubation period, chosen before the count. Two outbreaks are two cases, not a sample of Ebola. This rule is not WHO's end-of-outbreak rule, which counts from the last patient.
