# frankbueltge.de — crawler policy
#
# Stance (Frank, 2026-07-26): cite, don't train.
# Search engines and AI retrieval agents are welcome: they fetch a page when someone
# asks a question, and they link back. Crawlers that collect material for model
# training are not permitted — an express reservation of rights under Article 4 of
# EU Directive 2019/790 (DSM), consistent with the licences the works here carry.
#
# NOTE: these directives only take effect while Cloudflare's managed robots.txt is
# switched off for this zone (AI Crawl Control). While that feature is on, Cloudflare
# serves its own block instead of this file.
#
# /trending (Common Ground, the trending ledger) follows the site default. From
# 2026-09-02 to 2026-11-14 an exception opened that route to training crawlers in this
# file; it was withdrawn on 2026-11-14. The ledger passes on titles, links and counts
# of other platforms, a good part of which reserve their content against model
# training, and those terms stay theirs: this site cannot give away what they keep.
# Search engines and AI retrieval agents stay welcome there as everywhere. The data of
# the ledger is CC0 up to 2026-11-14 and CC BY 4.0 after (name and link Common Ground,
# https://frankbueltge.de/trending); a source's own titles and links stay under that
# source's terms. Record: docs/design/2026-07-26-crawler-politik.md §5.

User-agent: *
Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=no, use=reference
Allow: /
