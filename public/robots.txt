# frankbueltge.de — crawler policy
#
# Stance (Frank, 2026-07-26): cite, don't train.
# Search engines and AI retrieval agents are welcome: they fetch a page when someone
# asks a question, and they link back. Crawlers that collect material for model
# training are not permitted — an express reservation of rights under Article 4 of
# EU Directive 2019/790 (DSM), consistent with the licences the works here carry.
#
# NOTE: these directives only take effect while Cloudflare's managed robots.txt is
# switched off for this zone (AI Crawl Control). While that feature is on, Cloudflare
# serves its own block instead of this file.

User-agent: *
Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=no, use=reference
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://frankbueltge.de/sitemap-index.xml
