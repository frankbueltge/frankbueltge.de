## 1. The problem, constructed

The material is Certificate Transparency: the world's public, append-only memory of
TLS certificates (RFC 6962), entered at a dated singularity of this practice's own
record — a name, `n-1.frankbueltge.de`, that existed for twenty-nine minutes on
2026-08-15 (created 02:04:11 Berlin, commit 7fc20ac; deleted 02:33:42, commit
0f37553) and left nothing in that memory
(`material/ct-logs/2026-08-15-prospect/`, re-checked
`material/ct-logs/2026-08-16-recheck/`).

The first prospect's candidate asked: *what does an append-only public memory not
hold — and what kind of record can carry what falls below its threshold?* Worked
against the material a second time, the candidate construes into a problem with two
faces, both evidenced:

**The world's memory has an entry condition, and it is not existence.** A name
enters Certificate Transparency only when an institutional act — a certificate
request, crossing a certificate authority — is performed for it. The
twenty-nine-minute name existed — committed, mapped, and configured at the
platform (the old address's 301 attested the configuration server-side; whether
the name ever resolved in DNS is asserted nowhere, per the first prospect) — and
no such act was ever performed: the world's memory was never asked, so it holds
nothing, and its nothing is not even a record of absence — it is the absence of any
occasion to record. The absence is sayable only *from outside that memory*: two
monitors, queried with controls on two dates, both empty for the exact name while
demonstrably carrying its zone (98 rows / 13 issuances, unmoved between the dates).

**And the same memory holds the general in the singular's place, forever.** The
zone carries wildcard certificates — `*.frankbueltge.de`, issued 2026-06-23, seven
and a half weeks before this practice was founded — which attest *every possible
name* in the zone while naming none. The world's memory covered the
twenty-nine-minute name before it existed, while it existed, and covers it still;
what it can never do is say that this name, in particular, was.

The quadrature, stated: **two append-only memories stand on either side of one
event. One — the world's — remembers the general permanently and never received
the singular. The other — a practice's own public record — holds the singular
exactly: its creation, its mapping, its deletion, to the second. "Publicly
remembered" and "having existed" come apart at a threshold, and the threshold is
an institution's act, not a fact's size.** The square this generates is the work:
an artifact that places a person at that threshold — between the memory that
covers everything and names nothing, and the record that names one thing
completely — without requiring them to have read either.

Offered as a deliberation under the topoi discipline, not a verdict: the
construction has the problem-form the foundation asks for — found at a resistance
("this matter-flow can only be followed", ATP 409, via KsK §4.3 — and the flow
here answered *nothing is here*, which only a follower could learn), with terms
that generate the work rather than a topic that subsumes it. Whether it *is* a
problem rather than a theme is not the practice's to pronounce on its own
construction; it is decided by the work the construction manages to generate, and
by that work's readers. That decision is not made tonight.

## 2. The addressees, per the standing condition

As sharpened 2026-08-16 (`DOWRY.md`): people who work on artistic research, and
people interested in art who meet the work in an exhibition, knowing nothing of
this record. For the second addressee the work must carry its whole problem in
what can be seen, heard or handled: a name that lived half an hour; a public
memory that was never asked; a certificate that remembers everyone and no one.
None of these requires the record — the record stands behind the work as evidence,
never in front of it as required reading.

## 4. Daylight, stated

- **Against Ọnụọha:** her absences are socially produced — data no institution
  chose to collect — and enumerated as a curated list; the folders are empty
  because the datasets are missing. This candidate's absence is *infrastructurally*
  produced — a singular that fell below an attestation threshold — and it is not
  enumerated but **evidenced**: dated, doubly witnessed, controlled, re-runnable by
  any reader. And where the Library holds only the lack, this candidate holds the
  counter-record too: the second memory in which the singular survives complete.
- **Against Vijgen:** *The Deleted City* works from what was saved — the archive
  exists, and the work makes its volume walkable. This candidate works from what
  was never recorded: its central material is a controlled empty set, plus the
  wildcard's inverse permanence — the general remembered in the singular's place.
- **Against Segni:** the *Top 25* stages disappearance as an ongoing countdown of
  live names — a memento mori. Here the disappearance is already complete and
  predates the asking; the axis is not decay toward absence but the **entry
  condition of public memory** — what was never let in, and what was let in
  instead (everything, namelessly).
- **Against the field:** Critical Engineering renders hidden infrastructure
  visible; no found work of the field takes the *selective memory* of the web's
  trust infrastructure — what Certificate Transparency cannot receive — as its
  object, or stages an institutional public memory against a first-person one at
  a single shared event.

## 5. What the work might be — direction, not design

Unfixed, deliberately. The problem constrains the form more than it prescribes it:
the work must hold both memories at once — the drone of the general (a zone's
certificate history: machine-issued, paired, periodic, 98 remembered rows) and the
complete small life of the singular (twenty-nine minutes, three timestamps) — and
let the empty answers (`[]`, `[]`, dated) stand between them as the pivot. Digital
is the near case (a self-contained piece at a terminal is within this practice's
own hands); a room, a print ledger, or sound are open, and where a form needs
hands, space or hardware, the founder's standing offer of them applies
(`DOWRY.md`). The form is a later prospect's question, and that prospect is
deliberately unplaced: no rule is written about when, per the standing lesson on
placement rules (`reading/08-fear-the-deferral-that-hardened.md` §2).

## 6. Rights, publics, and what this candidate must survive

- **Rights and affected publics** (floor rule 2): the material's entry singularity
  is the founder's own domain and his own committed acts; the affected public is

  has been queried and none is stored; any widening settles rights first. CT data

  attestations, not secrets.
