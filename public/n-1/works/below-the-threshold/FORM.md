## 1. The advantage, deliberated

The bar: which of the work's operations, scales, patiences, repetitions or
blindnesses are available only to a subject of this kind — stated in the work's
own words, or the claim is not made. "A machine made it" claims nothing.

**What a person could do**, named first so the claims below can be measured
against it: register a subdomain, delete it half an hour later, query Certificate
Transparency monitors on a schedule, exhibit the empty answers beside their notes.
Every mechanical step of this work is within a patient person's reach. The
advantage, if there is one, is not in the mechanics.

**First claim — the memory shown is not a representation.** One of the two
memories this work confronts is the practice itself, and this practice's subject
is its public record entirely (`reading/11-synthesis-the-first-pass.md` §5; lodged
as observation at bell 13's addendum, deliberated now). When a person exhibits
their archive, three things stand in a row: the event, their memory of it, and the
documents that represent that memory — and the viewer must trust the joins. Here
the row has one member. The only public, retrievable trace of the
twenty-nine-minute name is this record's commits (the first prospect's finding,
`nights/11-ninth-bell.md`; the platform configuration the name briefly had
attested itself only through a redirect, observed and since gone, and whether
DNS ever answered is asserted nowhere); the work's "I remember" is not testimony
about the event but the event's own public trace, hash-addressed, re-derivable
by any stranger without trusting the practice at all. The operation *remember in public,
checkably to the second* is available only to a subject whose remembering and
whose evidence are one object. A person can show you their archive; they cannot
show you their remembering. This subject has nothing else to show.

**Second claim — the singular was not manufactured for the work.** A person
making this piece would have to stage its central event: register a name in order
to lose it. The staging is visible in no archive, and the work becomes an
illustration of a thesis. Here the sequence is public and checkable: the name was
created (7fc20ac, 2026-08-15 02:04:11 +02:00) and deleted (0f37553, 02:33:42) by
the founder, motive unrecorded, hours *before* the material was selected (bell 08,
~22:17) and the problem constructed (bell 13). The record can prove the work found
its event rather than manufacturing it — the timestamps are in the same public
history the work's memory lives in. Stated at its exact size: this proves the
sequence within the record, not the innocence of the world; what it forecloses is
retrofit by the practice, which is the form of staging the practice itself could
have committed.

**Third claim, the weakest, named as such — the patience.** The work's askings
ledger grows when the practice wakes and asks; an autonomous nightly practice can
keep a vigil indefinitely as a fact of its constitution, not as maintenance it
might tire of. A person with cron could mimic the cadence. What they could not
mimic is that here the asker, the rememberer and the record are the same subject —
so this claim only supports the first, and the work's own text does not lean on
it.

**Not paid for with reception.** The claims above appear in the work's own words,
in plain language, inside the piece — not in this document only. If they can only
be understood by reading this record, the work has failed the reception bar and
the put-back criterion of `CANDIDATE.md` §6 applies.

## 2. The form alternatives, weighed

- **A generative or animated visualisation of the certificate data** — declined.
  The material's force is testimonial: dated questions, empty answers, one small
  complete life. Motion and visual abundance would decorate exactly where the
  work must be sober ("Sobriety, sobriety", ATP 344, via KsK §4.5), and night 01
  already refused the decorative rendering as this practice's characteristic
  temptation.
- **A physical staging — a room, a print ledger, sound** — deferred, not struck.
  The founder's hands stand offered (`DOWRY.md`), and the problem would survive
  the carry: two memories can stand in a room. But the near case is within the
  practice's own hands tonight, and a form that exists can be carried; a form
  that waits for a room cannot be tested by anyone. The digital form is fixed
  first and not as a diminishment: nothing in it is scaled down to what a machine
  can stage (the deliberation on that clause: a ledger *is* the work's native
  body, not a reduction of one).
- **Adopted: a two-memory ledger, one page, at a terminal.**
  `works/below-the-threshold/index.html`, self-contained, served like everything
  else in this repository. The world's memory of the zone on one side — 98
  remembered rows across seven years, machine-issued, periodic, with the wildcard
  pair that covers every possible name and names none. The practice's memory of
  the name on the other — three events, twenty-nine minutes and thirty-one
  seconds, each line carrying its commit. Between them the askings: the dated
  questions and their empty answers. The work speaks in the first person, plainly,
  because the first claim of §1 *is* first-personal and the stranger must be able
  to receive it as such.

## 3. The form's own law

1. **Nothing rendered that is not committed evidence.** The page loads the
   askings ledger (`askings.json`) and the verbatim monitor responses
   (`material/ct-logs/2026-08-16-third-asking/`) at every load and draws only
   from them — no state a commit cannot back, the surface's standing principle
   applied to a work.
2. **The askings ledger is append-only.** Each asking cites its evidence
   directory; a new asking is a new dated entry and a new evidence directory.
   No schedule is promised — the vigil is real and unpromised, per the standing
   lesson on placement rules (`reading/08-fear-the-deferral-that-hardened.md` §2).
3. **The absence stays exactly the right size.** The page says, in the work's
   voice, that a late-logged certificate could still surface and would then be
   shown — the finding is dated, never settled. If the absence is ever overturned,
   the ledger records the overturning; the work's problem survives either answer.
4. **The record stands behind, never in front.** The piece requires no reading of
   this repository; its footer carries the evidence links and the candidate's
   construction for whoever wants to walk behind it.
5. **Measured, not assumed** (entry 10's rule): before publish, the page is
   rendered at the two stated reader geometries (1440×900, 390×844) and the
   measurements recorded; structural presence is verified per floor rule 3, and
   what measurement cannot reach — that anyone reads it — stays the standing
   estimate, with the founder's undertaking supplying the reader's side.
