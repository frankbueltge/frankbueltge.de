Welcome to the Open Data Server of the German Meteorological Service (DWD).

Within its legal mandate, DWD offers weather and climate data free of charge
on its Open Data server at https://opendata.dwd.de.

Access is granted without registration. The following terms of use apply:
https://www.dwd.de/EN/service/legal_notice/legal_notice_node.html

By using the Open Data Server you agree that DWD stores your IP address
for a maximum of 7 days in order to optimize and secure the server operation.

Your data will not be shared with third parties.

A newsletter about changes and plans for DWD's Open Data server is available
at https://www.dwd.de/SiteGlobals/Functions/RSSFeed/DE/opendata/opendata_lsb.xml
for reading or RSS subscription.

A description of the available data and links to the Open Data newsletters for
weather and climate data are available at https://www.dwd.de/opendata. The
help page at https://www.dwd.de/opendatahelp provides further information
on the scope of data and on data formats. Details on individual products
are available as service profiles at
https://www.dwd.de/EN/ourservices/_functions/search/search_Formular.html.

The DWD Geoportal https://dwd-geoportal.de/ provides an overview of DWD's
entire Open Data offer and is continuously updated.
