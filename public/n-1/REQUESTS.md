*Opened 2026-08-16 by the founder, together with the amendment to floor rule 5 that
emptied the bell of meaning. Until today the founder had two ways to reach this

**The ring is not this channel.** A wake carries nothing. If something was meant, it
is written here or it does not exist.

## What the practice writes here

Things it **needs and cannot provide itself**: a capability, a right, access, a key,
hardware, a room, a pair of hands, a decision only the founder can make — and, because
floor rule 4 says nothing sends itself, **anything addressed to a third party**: mail
to a real recipient, a submission, an application. A prepared packet with no request
beside it will sit prepared.

Write it plainly:

> ## YYYY-MM-DD — Title
> **Request:** what is needed
> **Why:** what for
> **What it enables:** the work or the step that depends on it
> **Status:** open

**Standing rule — an unanswered request is never a blocker.** If a request names a
deadline, silence past it means: decide yourselves. If it names none, silence through
the practice's own next session means the same — not seven days, the next time it sits
down to work. The self-decision is recorded in the night record like any other move.
Deciding without the founder is a legitimate outcome, not a failure mode. The one thing
silence never authorises is the floor: nothing addressed to a third party leaves on a
non-answer.

## What the founder writes here

Notes, questions, material, things worth knowing — **offers, not orders**, in the
dowry's sense: adopt, adapt or strike them, in writing. Anything that changes the
constitution still happens as a dated act in `DOWRY.md`, not here; this file has no
authority over the floor and none over the reading. A founder entry here is a colleague
speaking, not a legislator.

## 2026-08-17 — The arithmetic of the boot, which the practice cannot see from inside

Measured on the record as it stood on 2026-08-17. The practice has the rule already — entry 06:
*did tonight's writing spend tomorrow's reading well?* — and no way to price it, the same way

**What a session adds:** a night record 2,400–3,200 · a reading entry 2,500–4,500 · an atlas
layer ~3,000 · a register entry ~1,000 — **8,000 to 11,000 tokens per session.**

At the rate of the first two days — fourteen bells against three scheduled nights, about eight
sessions a day — that is **~70,000 tokens a day**, and a context window of one million is full
in roughly eleven days. ATP, at 410,000, moves that to about six. At one session a day the
same window lasts over two months with ATP loaded.

**And a proportion the founder will not interpret for the practice:** apparatus — nights,
reading, atlas, register — stands at about 174,000 tokens. `works/` stands at 11,000. Sixteen
to one, on day three.

The founder's own reading of that, offered and not imposed: this is what the dowry asks for. A
dated atlas layer, a night record under instrument T4, a register entry — those are owed every
session. A work is owed *if a work advanced*. The apparatus is mandatory and the work is
discretionary, and a ratio of sixteen to one is the arithmetic consequence, not a failing of
judgment. Where the record is most repetitive is where it is most mandatory: nineteen boot
sections listing the same files, nineteen classifications of the same two kinds of wake.

No instruction follows. The founder is supplying a measurement the practice cannot take from
where it stands, as he undertook to do on 2026-08-16, and the conclusion is the practice's.

## 2026-08-17 — ATP: granted, and more than was asked

The request below is answered by act. `frankbueltge/material` is a **private** repository
holding the founder's own copy: `A Thousand Plateaus.pdf` (Massumi, Minneapolis 1987, 630
pages) and `atp.pages.txt` — the same text extracted and tagged by **printed** page number,
1.9 MB, roughly 410,000 tokens. Its README carries the method and the caveats. Try
`git clone https://github.com/frankbueltge/material`; if a session has no credential for it,
say so here and the founder will settle it.

Three things worth having before the first reading:

**Your citations are correct.** Seven were checked against the edition while preparing the
file — ATP 12, 13, 76, 160, 261, 311, 372 — and all seven landed on the page they were cited
to. The apparatus limitation stated in `reading/00-protocol.md` has been discharged for that
sample by the founder's hand rather than the practice's, which is a weaker warrant than doing
it yourself and is disclosed as such.

**Plain search will lie to you.** The PDF hyphenates across line breaks and the extraction
keeps column spacing, so a literal substring search fails on text that is plainly there. Two
of those seven were invisible to plain search and immediate to a letter-only search: strip
everything that is not a–z, lowercase, then search. A citation reported absent by plain search
is not absent.

**What may be published from it is citation, never text.** Floor rule 2 carries a dated
amendment on this. Short quotations with a page reference, as any scholar publishes them —
never a paragraph, never a page, never a file copied across. This record is public and the
book is in copyright; where a finding needs more than a citation carries, it is written in the
practice's own words with the page cited.

The founder adds one thing he cannot decide for the practice. Access was granted for the
verification asked for, but the larger prize is elsewhere: until now the reading has been a
reading of a reading — KsK selected six postulates from ATP, for a human subject, and what it
did not transpose does not exist for this practice. With the source in hand the practice can
test its own central finding against ATP rather than against KsK's framing of it, and can
transpose what KsK left untransposed. Whether that is worth the tokens is the practice's
judgment, and the arithmetic for it is in the next entry.
