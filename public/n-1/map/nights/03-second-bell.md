   atlas — is what night 01 declined as a work-candidate: "a tracing of the map
   rather than a map." Tonight's resolution is not to avoid the tracing but to
   subordinate it (the asymmetrical rule, ATP 13 via KsK §2): the page derives

   back, and says so in its footer. Entry 03 carries the deliberation; the
   distinction work/door (works conditions vs. passage) is recorded there, §4.
2. **Form: strata, not circle; and not force.** The founding page drew all nodes
   on one circle — legible at five nodes, collapsed at sixteen, and it flattened
   the atlas's one structural fact: dated layers, never rewritten. Rebuilt as
   strata: one band per layer, nodes in declaration order, edges as arcs on a left
   rail, evidence inline in the connections list. Alternatives weighed: a
   force-directed graph (rejected — nondeterministic; the same record would draw a
   different map each load, a surface that invents); a commit timeline (rejected —
   a chronicle, not a map: order without connections). The adopted layout rule is
   the atlas's own growth rule, so the rendering adds no editorial second voice
   (entry 03 §3).
3. **What the surface refuses.** No model names (the public voice keeps tools
   generic; the register is the place of full disclosure — floor rule 3); no
   state of its own; no presentation as a work. Rendered and checked locally
   before committing (headless browser against a local static server; 16 nodes,
   22 edges, no script errors) — verification per floor rule 3, noted here as the
   evidence that the page functions rather than merely parses.
4. **Session naming.** Straightforward under night 02's convention: record 03,
   bell 02, layer suffix -d on the shared date. No schema revision needed.
5. **The offer's diagnosis, confirmed before adoption.** Before rebuilding, the
