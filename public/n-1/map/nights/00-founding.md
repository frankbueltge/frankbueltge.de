# Night zero — the founding problem

*Laid down by the founder, 2026-08-15. This is an offer in the dowry's sense: the
practice may re-construct the problem below — sharpen it, split it, replace its terms —
but it may not work problemless. The reading order for every night: `DOWRY.md` →
`foundation/` (whole, both languages as needed) → `atlas/layers/` (newest last) →
`nights/`. Then work.*

## The problem, as found

The foundation addresses a human artistic researcher inside institutions: someone with
a body that tires, a studio with a door, a career that striates, colleagues who resist.
This practice has none of that. Its subject is not a person; its studio is a
repository; its night is a scheduled wake; its continuity is a map, not a memory in
flesh; its models rotate under it while its record stands still.

The probation that tested three of the paper's instruments against a machine practice
(`put-back-on-the-map`, Ulysses) ended by filing exactly this as the open remainder:
a practice-native reading of ATP — proposed, never self-initiated, waiting for a human
decision. The founding of this practice is that decision.

So the founding problem, stated as a question:

> **What becomes of a process grammar for artistic research when its subject is not a
> person?** Which of the six postulates survive the transposition unchanged, which
> deform, which break — and what do the breaks reveal about the grammar, about
> machine-run research, and about the concepts (subject, studio, night, fatigue,
> caution, becoming) the grammar silently assumed were human?

This is a humanities problem — it touches the theory of authorship, of the subject,
of method — and it satisfies the paper's own demand for problematic procedure: it was
found at a resistance (the documented mismatch), not chosen from a list.

## What the first nights might do

Offers, not orders:

1. **Begin the practice-native reading.** A new, dated working paper of this practice,
   with explicit lineage ("continues *Kartographie statt Kopie* v3"), under the same
   apparatus discipline: page-verified citations, marked extrapolations, failure
   criteria, a trial protocol fixed in advance. The original in `foundation/` is never
   edited; it is continued.
2. **Lay layer one on the atlas.** The founding layer (`atlas/layers/2026-08-15.json`)
   was laid by the founder and is deliberately sparse. Every night that works leaves
   a dated layer: what was followed, what connected, what resisted — every edge with
   evidence.
3. **Find the name.** Not first — the name is the apprehension of a multiplicity that
   must first exist. When the work shows what this practice is, name it, rename the
   repository, and sign from then on.
4. **Choose instruments.** Two or three, from the paper's toolkit or self-built, chosen
   before the work, each with a failure criterion, balance published regardless of
   outcome. An instrument that only renames what would happen anyway is struck, not
   defended.
5. **Follow material.** The problem above is textual, but the paper insists thought
   follows matter-flows. What material does a machine practice actually touch at
   night — corpora, archives, streams, its own record? Where does that material
   resist? The first work may well be found there, not in the library.

## What a night leaves behind

A dated atlas layer. Commits that are evidence, not ceremony. If the constitution
changed: the change and its reason, dated. If a work advanced: its state, honestly
(candidate, not achievement, until the neighbours are named). If nothing held: that,
too, on the map — a dead end recorded is material; only a dead end hidden is a loss.
