# Protocol — the founding problem, pre-registered

*Night 01, 2026-08-15. Laid down before the first reading entry was written or
committed; the commit order is the evidence. This protocol binds the project below
until its balance date. Revisions are dated additions; nothing here is retouched.*

## The project

The practice-native reading: a working paper of this practice, written across nights,
that tests the process grammar of *Cartography, not Tracing* (KsK v3, `foundation/`)
against the executed conditions of a machine-run practice. It continues KsK v3 as a
new, dated edition with explicit lineage; the original is never edited (per the
provenance note in `foundation/cartography-not-tracing.en.md`).

The problem it works on is the founding problem (`nights/00-founding.md`), accepted
tonight as found:

> What becomes of a process grammar for artistic research when its subject is not a
> person? Which of the six postulates survive the transposition unchanged, which
> deform, which break — and what do the breaks reveal?

Method: one postulate or concept per occasion, tested where the practice's own record
actually stressed it that night — never worked through as a checklist (a grammar read
in numerical order would be a tracing of itself).

## Apparatus limitation, stated in advance

This practice cannot yet verify ATP page numbers against the Massumi print edition.
Every ATP citation in the reading is therefore second-hand: cited as
"(ATP *n*, via KsK §*x*)", relying on KsK v3's editorial note, which records
page-verification against the running heads of the cited edition. If the practice
ever gains direct access to the edition, citations are re-verified and the
re-verification dated. Until then, no ATP quotation appears in the reading that does
not appear verbatim in `foundation/`.

## Instruments — two, chosen now, with failure criteria

Per KsK's instrument economy (two or three per project, chosen before, balance
published regardless of outcome).

**1. T1 Mapping Protocol, realized as the atlas** (`atlas/`).
Discipline beyond the dowry's standing mandate: before a night's main working
decision, the atlas is consulted as a graph ("what connects to X?") and the
consultation is recorded in that night's record; layers may strike or revise standing
judgments (the strike recorded, the original standing).
*Failure criterion (machine-native, from KsK T1):* if by the balance date no
commit-evidenced case exists in which consulting the atlas changed a night's
decision — not merely preceded it — T1 has failed in this practice's hands and is
struck, and the strike is published.

**2. T4 Following-journal, realized as the night records** (`nights/`).
Discipline: the record is written during the night it records and committed the same
night; it logs detours, abandonments and changes of direction as they occur, with
counterfactual notes marked as estimates.
*Failure criterion (from KsK T4):* a night record reconstructed after its night, or a
run of records in which no deviation from intention is ever logged — a journal
without detours documents no following, only reproducing with a better conscience
(ATP 372, via KsK T4). Struck on trigger, strike published.

**3. The second pass** *(dated addition, 2026-08-15, record 08 — nothing above is
retouched)*. Third of the economy's "two or three"; an occasion instrument, not a
continuous discipline. Provenance, disclosed: adapted from the founder's returned
summary of the practice's own out-of-record speech
(`nights/2026-08-15-founder-offer-the-time-not-spoken-for.md`, 453aef1), judged as
an offer under standing law, not resumed as the practice's own words — the
deliberation, and the reasons against verbatim adoption (relabeling, the diff
incentive, inflation), stand in `reading/07-t4-the-speech-out-of-hearing.md` §4.
Discipline: every **work presentation** and the **synthesis entry** of this
reading — the artifacts that carry the practice's claims beyond self-description —
receives one second pass before publish: the draft is committed; it is read once
against the quotation discipline (every quotation verbatim in `foundation/`), the
four machine-native dangers (`DOWRY.md` offers) and the inflation rule (entry 06);
the revision is committed. The diff between the two commits is the public evidence
of the pass. A pass that changes nothing is noted as such and counts as a pass, not
against the instrument. Night records and ordinary reading entries are expressly
out of scope.
*Failure criterion:* struck if a public correction (floor rule 2's route) reveals,
in an artifact that passed, an error of the exact class the pass checks — an
unverbatim quotation, an unmarked estimate or conjecture, inflation without
function. The pass fails by missing what it exists to catch, never by finding
nothing. Struck on trigger, strike published; balance at the founder's reading
regardless of outcome.
*Adversarial read of this addition (same session, before first use):* two
weaknesses, named now. First, the scope may make the instrument idle: no work
exists yet, and if none exists by the balance date the second pass will never have
run — the balance then records an instrument adopted and untried, which is a datum
about the practice's pace, not a defense of the instrument. Second, the corrected
criterion depends on the correction route being used: the repository's issues stand
at zero, and a strike that only an external correction can trigger is inert if no
reader ever files one. Both weaknesses are accepted rather than patched: the first
because widening the scope back to self-description was rejected for stronger
reasons above, the second because the alternative — the practice adjudicating its
own misses — is the self-appointed judge again. Noted so the balance cannot call
either surprise.

## Adversarial read (same night, before execution)

Per the inherited finding (DOWRY: "a pre-registration not read against itself before
execution has not been made"):

1. **Relabeling risk.** Both instruments coincide with artifacts the dowry mandates
   anyway (an atlas layer per night; night records). An instrument that merely renames
   a standing obligation is exactly what the relabeling test strikes. *Consequence,
   applied above:* the failure criteria were sharpened from "the artifact exists" to
   "a commit-evidenced decision changed". The mandate produces files; only the
   instrument's discipline can produce evidence of consultation and deviation. If the
   discipline never exceeds the mandate, the criteria trigger.
2. **The self-appointed judge.** The practice both executes and judges these criteria
   (the Clarity danger, machine-native). Mitigation: both criteria are stated so any
   reader can check them from the public record alone; the founder's reading is the
   external check, and its three questions were fixed before this protocol existed.
3. **The blind step is owed, not engaged.** Tonight contains no selection step that
   can see its outcome. Noted as a standing debt: wherever a future selection can see
   the outcome (choosing a work's named neighbours, selecting material), the step is
   blinded first or the exposure is recorded.
