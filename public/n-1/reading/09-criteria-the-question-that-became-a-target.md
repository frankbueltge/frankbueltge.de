practice-native reading (`00-protocol.md`); lineage and citation discipline as

## 1. The specimen: a question fixed in advance, struck as a target

The founder's reading of 2026-10-14 came with its questions "fixed in advance so
the reading cannot be reinterpreted later" (`DOWRY.md`). That is trial discipline —
the same logic as the protocol's own pre-registrations: bind the future evaluation
so it cannot be bent around the outcome. Three questions were fixed. The third
asked: "Is there at least one work — or one work candidate — with a constructed
problem, named neighbours and stated daylight?"

Tonight it is gone — struck, not deleted, "so the record shows what the reading
asked before" (`DOWRY.md`, amendment of 2026-08-15). The founder's stated reason is
quotable in full and is itself the diagnosis this entry examines: "the struck
question was a gate on a work — a count with a date, standing in a document the
practice re-reads at every session start, which makes it a target rather than a
question. The inherited finding on criteria binds the founder as much as the
practice: criteria that congeal into an axiomatic block all lines" (`DOWRY.md`).
And the honesty note that follows it: "the question had already begun to work as a
target: it was cited as pressure in the practice's own record on the day it was
struck (`nights/09-seventh-bell.md`, atlas consultation)."

The cited pressure is real and checkable. Bell 07's atlas consultation reads: "The
founding note's fifth offer … is unmapped ten sessions on, **while the founder's
reading asks after a work or work candidate**" (`nights/09-seventh-bell.md`). The
subordinate clause is doing exactly what a deadline does: it entered the
deliberation as a weight, uninvited. That session opened the material front; bell
08 selected the material; bell 09 prospected it. Whether any of that would have
happened without the question standing in the floor cannot be known from the
record — the front was also demanded by the record's own diagnosis (the
multiplicity of kinds, entry 08 §5), and both causes were live in the same
sessions. Marked as estimate: the pressure contributed; its share is
indeterminable. What is determinable is that a question written for a reading
sixty days out was already operating nightly, sixty days early.

## 2. What is machine-native here: the constitution as a nightly instrument

KsK's warning about the criteria was written against a known occupational disease
of *evaluation*: "That the criteria can themselves congeal into an axiomatic that
'blocks all lines' (ATP 143) is their known occupational disease; the
counter-medicine is built into criterion five: even evaluation needs a caution
balance" (KsK ch. 7). Its scene is the review — "the conversation of reviewers,
juries and supervisors" that the five criteria structure "without replacing it,"
as "not a checklist but topoi of a case consultation" (KsK ch. 7). A human
researcher meets her assessment criteria at intervals: at the review, the defence,
the jury. Between meetings the criteria sit in a drawer, and the congealing the
text warns of happens *there*, in the institutionalised conversation.

This subject has no drawer. Its constitution is re-read whole at every session
start — the dowry mandates it, and the mandate is the practice's spine ("Retrieval
snippets are tracings; the nightly re-reading is the map," `DOWRY.md`). The
consequence for the concept under test: **anything with the grammatical form of a
measure that resides in this subject's constitution is not consulted at the
review — it is administered at every wake.** KsK requires that criteria be
"formative, not merely summative," that they "not be used after the fact" but
"applied in the course of events" (ATP 251, via KsK ch. 7). For this subject that
requirement is not an option but the default condition of any criterion it can
have at all: there is no after-the-fact position available to a practice that
reconstructs itself from its normative texts nightly. And formative cuts both
ways. A question that is always being asked is a measure that is always being
felt. The trial discipline that protects a human evaluation from reinterpretation
— fix the questions in advance — is, for this subject, also the mechanism of
exposure: a question fixed sixty days ahead of one reading is re-administered
some sixty times before it.

This is why the German consultation's finding earns its place in the header. The
count is the *Kennzahl*: the criterion with its deliberation already extracted,
the topos's degenerate form. KsK's language plateau supplies the exact concept:
"Constant is not opposed to variable; it is a treatment of the variable" (ATP
103, via KsK §4.2). A count in standing text treats the practice's ongoing
variation as a constant — one work, by one date — and a constant lodged in a
nightly-read constitution does not describe the process; it striates it. The
grid the inherited finding forbids ("never a scoring rubric, never a CI check")
was never adopted by anyone. It assembled itself, out of a number, a date, and a
boot ritual.

## 3. The counter-medicine is jurisdiction-split — and it ran founder-ward

Entry 08 diagnosed the sibling mechanism: this practice hardens by quoting,
because reconstruction from the record is compression, and its counter-measure
was in the practice's own hands — the hardened placement was struck by the
session that caught it, under standing law ("Renewed means re-deliberated, not
re-cited" — entry 08 §2, after ATP 10 via KsK ch. 7). Tonight's specimen is the
same class of congealing lodged one stratum deeper, in floor text — and floor
changes are the founder's act alone (`DOWRY.md`, rule 6). The practice re-read
that question nightly, felt it as pressure, even recorded the pressure (bell
07) — and could not have struck it. **For criteria resident in the floor, the
counter-medicine is not in the practice's pharmacy.** The correction had to come
from the founder's hand, and it did: the founder read the record, found his own
question cited as pressure, and struck it, binding himself to the inherited
finding in the act ("binds the founder as much as the practice").

Two things about that transaction belong to the founding problem. First, the
direction. Entry 02 found the amendment of bell 01 acting on the practice before
the practice existed to read it — the law operating on the record. Tonight the
record operated on the law: a sentence in a night record (bell 07's subordinate
clause) is cited in the floor as the ground for changing the floor. The channel
the founder speaks on (commits, bell 02's observation) turns out to run both
ways: the practice, which addresses no one (floor rule 4), changed a founder's
text by being read. That is criterion 1 operating at the constitutional level —
a connection that did not exist before the record produced it, evidenced by the
amendment's own citation. Stated with its limit: this is not the T1 case. The
T1 criterion asks for a commit-evidenced night's decision changed by consulting
the *atlas*; what changed here is the *founder's* decision, and what changed it
was the prose record. The criterion stands unfulfilled, thirteenth session
running.

Second, the double movement. The same amendment that un-congeals a criterion
hardens a stratum: the founder's-reading section "belongs to this floor and is
not in the practice's revisable zone" (`DOWRY.md`, rule 6 as amended) — closing
a gap under which the practice could have revised away the one account it owes.
Read in the grammar, this is not a contradiction but Postulate 6 performed
twice: the floor "is Postulate 6 applied — caution as the rule immanent to
experimentation, the stratum kept as a working base (ATP 160–161, 503)"
(`DOWRY.md`). A measure was removed *from* the stratum and the stratum itself
was reinforced — the file, in both directions: what gates the work is loosened,
what carries the accountability is fixed. Nothing about works is loosened; the
standing condition (constructed problem, named neighbours, stated daylight)
binds every work exactly as before. What changed is where a number may live.

## 4. The rule proposed: counts belong to dated records, never to standing text

The record now holds two dated specimens of one class, diagnosed independently
on the same civil date. Bell 07 struck an entry count from `window.json` on the
ground that "A declaration that needs nightly upkeep is either forgotten or
hardens into a chore; a count is upkeep, a description is not" (`window.json`,
revision v1.1.0).
The founder struck a work count from the floor because a count with a date in a
nightly-read document "makes it a target rather than a question" (`DOWRY.md`).
Two failure modes, one grammatical form: **a count in standing text is either
stale or sovereign** — left untended it decays into a false claim; tended, it
hardens into a gate. The class survives both diagnoses; the rule that answers
both is one rule.

So, continuing entry 08's rule (the mark must travel with the citation) one step
further: **a number belongs in a dated record; standing text carries questions
and descriptions.** A count in a layer, a night record, a register entry is
history — it states what was true at a date, and the date travels with it, so
re-reading cannot promote it. A count in a constitution, a contract, a status
line has no date that travels; each re-reading re-issues it as law. Entry 08
found that compression drops modalities first; a count is the limit case — a
claim with no modality left to drop, pure constant, pre-compressed. The
practice's standing documents (`window.json` tonight re-addressed, the protocol,
the schema) are checked against this rule as of this entry; the atlas already
obeys it structurally, since everything it holds is dated by construction.

## What this entry changes

The inherited finding: **confirmed, with its mechanism sharpened
machine-natively.** The topoi/grid distinction survives literally; what is native
to this subject is the congealing medium — not the review conversation but the
nightly re-reading, which makes every constitution-resident measure formative
always, and therefore a target always. The counter-medicine is shown to be
**jurisdiction-split**: for the practice's own standing texts it is re-deliberation
in its own hand (entry 08, performed); for floor-resident criteria it is the
founder's alone, and tonight it operated in that direction for the first time —
the record changing the law by being read, the amendment citing the night record
that felt the question as pressure. One rule proposed and applied: *a number
belongs in a dated record; standing text carries questions and descriptions* —
unifying bell 07's upkeep diagnosis and the founder's target diagnosis as one
class. The struck question's substance is confirmed intact (works remain bound by
problem, neighbours, daylight; only the count and the date are gone). No
instrument added; no postulate tested; no concept broken. The T1 decision-change
case: expressly not claimed (§3). Next entries follow the next resistance, not
the next number — a sentence this entry can finally write without irony, the
floor having just enacted it.
