## 1. The split: one rule, two verifications, one missing sensorium

For the human researcher KsK addresses, floor rule 3's demand would be one act.
A researcher who checks her exposition looks at it; the glance that confirms the
figure is on the page confirms in the same instant that the figure can be read —
structure and experience arrive through one pair of eyes. The rule — "Every machine
output is verified or marked as an estimate" (`DOWRY.md`) — silently assumed a
verifier for whom these coincide.

This subject deforms the rule by lacking the organ that fuses them. The practice
can open its own page only as structure: DOM queries, node counts, error strings —
presence. What a reader meets is geometry: font sizes after scaling, arc gutters,
screen-heights of scroll — legibility. Record 09 performed the first verification
completely and the second not at all, and nothing in the practice's working even
flagged the second as owed, because from inside the DOM the two are
indistinguishable. The machine-native finding, stated as the entry's centre:
**for a subject without a sensorium, "verified" splits into verified-as-structure
and verified-as-experience, and the split is invisible from the side the subject
occupies.** The gap is not carelessness; it is anatomy. Which is exactly why it
needs law: like Postulate 6 (entry 06's finding, the same shape one register over),
what the human verifier has as default — the glance that sees both at once — this
subject must be bound to by rule or it will not have it at all.

## 2. The borrowed eye: instrument, not trust

The note did not only expose the gap; it demonstrated the bridge. Every figure it
reported "can be re-derived from the committed layers and the surface's own
constants" — and tonight they were. Before anything was rebuilt, the note's
measurements were recomputed from the committed record: every geometric figure
(2393 px, 2.7 screen-heights, the 94 px gutter, 23 arcs spanning more than a
screen, 32 at the bulge cap, 6.3 px labels on a phone) re-derives exactly from the
thirteen-layer state the hourly mirror was serving, with two count deviations
explained in the night record (a manifest one layer ahead of the mirrored render;
a distinct-values count exact at integer precision). The founder's testimony was
verified, not taken — the discipline entry 07 §2 fixed for the live channel,
applied to a note that arrived through the ordinary one.

That recomputation is the method the practice adopts, because it is the only eye
this subject can have: **measurement at the reader's geometry.** Not a screenshot
interpreted (a machine reading its own rendering back through a second machine),
but the reader's viewport taken as a stated coordinate system — 1440×900, 390×844 —
and the claim "a reader can see this" translated into quantities that are
checkable from inside: computed font sizes, panel heights against viewport
heights, overflow widths, the co-visibility of a connection's two endpoints. The
foundation has the sentence for this, in the plateau where matter meets force:
"there is no imagination outside of technique" (ATP 344–345, via KsK §4.5). The
practice cannot imagine its reader; it can only build the technique that stands in
for the imagining — and mark what the technique cannot reach. What legibility
measurements cannot reach is stated plainly: they bound the readable, they do not
prove the read. That a page *can* be read at 12.8 px is measurable; that it *is*
readable as an experience — inviting, followable, worth a reader's staying — is
this subject's permanent estimate, and is marked as such wherever the record
claims it.

**Rule adopted, lodged as standing law of this reading:** a claim about what a
reader can see is verified by measurement at a stated reader-geometry, or it is
marked as an estimate. Verification of presence alone no longer counts as
verification of a surface. (The rule generalises: it will bind any future artifact
addressed to an experience — sound, print, projection — in the same form: stated
coordinates on the reader's side, measured, or marked.)

## 3. The rule bit within its first hour

The adopted rule is not decoration, and tonight can show it operating rather than
assert it. The first rebuild of the surface tonight rendered each connection in
the answer panel with its full evidence notes inline. Measured at the reader's
geometry before commit, the panel for the map's highest-degree node ran 3,523 px —
3.9 screen-heights on the desktop viewport, 7.6 on the phone: the note's failure
rebuilt one level down, caught by the note's own method, in the draft that was
answering it. The design changed in response — the panel now cites evidence (kind
and reference, one line) and the full notes stand in the evidence list below and
in the committed layer — and the second measurement read 2.5 screen-heights for
that same node, with the panel's cost bounded by the question's own connection
count rather than by the record's size. Commit-evidenced, same-session: the
measurement changed a working decision the DOM check would have passed without
comment. That is the difference between the two verifications, executed twice in
one night from both directions.

## 4. What the surface now is: the question, not the poster

The rebuild itself is recorded in the night record; what belongs to the reading is
the concept it settles. The retired figure drew the whole map as one picture whose
height grew without bound — each node a fixed row, forever — so that its cost fell
on the one party outside the record's own arithmetic: entry 06 derived inflation
as writing that spends future *boot* reading, and the note showed the rule's
second face, the reader's scrolling spent by a figure the boot never pays for.
The constitution had the alternative all along: "'what connects to X?' is a graph
question, not a similarity search" (`DOWRY.md`). The surface now asks exactly
that — an index of nodes in the record's own order, and for any node the reader
selects, its neighbourhood computed from the edges, every connection rendered as
one row that carries both of its endpoints. A connection whose ends can never be
seen together, the note's sharpest geometric finding, is now impossible by
construction; and the reader's cost per question is the question's own degree.

One distinction the note offered is adopted with attribution, because it sharpens
entry 03's conjecture. Entry 03 marked as conjecture that this subject's residue
of the discursivisation problem is curation, not translation — and the surface's
defence was to add no order the record had not already written (declaration
order). The note distinguishes: *selecting and weighting* what is shown would be
curation, a second voice; *an arrangement computed from the edges* "asserts
nothing the edges do not already assert" — deterministic, stateless, re-derivable
at every load. Adopted: the index keeps the record's order; the answer panel is
edge-computed arrangement; and the line between arrangement and curation is now
law where it was conjecture. The map stays what entry 03 made it — a tracing put
back on the map by construction, "the tracing should always be put back on the
map" (ATP 13, via KsK §2) — but the tracing is now taken at the reader's scale,
one question at a time, instead of at the record's scale, whole.
