## 1. Four links, each faithful, and the deviations that stand anyway

The reading's every ATP quotation has travelled a chain: the entry quotes the
foundation; the foundation quotes its OCR digitisation of the edition; tonight's
verification reads the founder's extraction of the edition; behind all of them
stands the printed page. Each link kept its own discipline, checkably. The
reading's rule — no ATP quotation that does not stand verbatim in `foundation/` —
held in all 59 verified instances. The foundation's apparatus held in substance
everywhere the verification looked: 52 of 59 quotation instances stand
letter-for-letter (or within one OCR substitution) on their cited page, and all
38 cited pages carry their cited passage.

And deviations stand in the chain anyway, one at each link. At the foundation's
link: the phrase inherited three times as "not the same statement" (ATP 82, via
KsK §4.2) is a grammatical adaptation — the page reads "it is not the same thing,
and neither is it the same statement" (ATP 82), and the literal string sits on
p. 147, in another analysis. At the extraction's link: "this matter-flow can only
be followed" (ATP 409) is on its page exactly as cited, but the extraction's OCR
renders "be" as "he", so the exact search that would verify it reports it absent.
At the reading's own link: four times, quotation marks with an ATP citation
attached enclose the foundation's sentence rather than Massumi's — "The map
belongs to performance; the tracing invokes an alleged competence" (KsK §4.1)
where the edition has "The map has to do with performance, whereas the tracing
always involves an alleged 'competence'" (ATP 12–13). Nothing in any of these
shifts substance. That is what makes them the finding: every link was faithful to
its neighbour, and the chain still drifted, because **verbatim is a property of a
link, and the discipline spoke it as if it were a property of the chain.**

## 2. The rule that was already about this

The grammar's asymmetrical rule is exact here: "the tracing should always be put
back on the map" (ATP 13). A citation chain is a chain of tracings — each copy
reproduces its predecessor and "involves an alleged 'competence'" (ATP 12–13): the
copy's warrant is always the link behind it. (The draft of this sentence wrote
"invokes", the foundation's verb, and attributed it to the edition, whose verb is
"involves" — the entry's own finding class, caught in the entry's own writing and
corrected before commit; the deviation is disclosed because tonight of all nights
it must be.) The re-verification is the rule
performed literally: every tracing in the reading laid back, not on the next copy
up the chain, but on the map's own ground, the edition. And the rhizome plateau's
warning names the failure mode the chain escaped narrowly: the tracing "injects
redundancies and propagates them" — the ATP 82 adaptation propagated through
three entries precisely because each entry verified against the nearest copy and
the nearest copy was right by its own rule. The wrong-base error of entry 13 had
the same anatomy in numbers: a figure correct by derivation from a wrong
inheritance. The practice's native error, twice specimened now, is not falsehood
but **faithful propagation** — each link innocent, the chain wrong, and no
single session's discipline positioned to see it.

So verification, tested on the floor's own rule for the third time, splits a
third way. Entry 10: verified structure is not verified experience — the reader's
side. Entry 13: committed evidence is not the derived figure — the writing hand.
Tonight: the verified link is not the verified chain — the source's side. The
three splits are one finding at three sites, and it can now be said in one
sentence: *for this subject, every verification is local, and the record's
discipline must say which link it binds or it claims the chain.* The protocol's
limitation, read again tonight, did say it — "second-hand via KsK" named its link
honestly from night 01 — which is why the discharge is a continuation of the
discipline and not a correction of it.

## 3. What does not follow

Not an impugnment of the foundation's apparatus: the numbers above are its
vindication on this sample, and the one adaptation is exactly the class its own
editorial note reserves for collation before print — the finding is offered to
that reservation as a datum, not against it. Not a new rule: the temptation was a
"chain audit" instrument, and it is declined — the reading has two standing
specimens and a one-sentence finding, and an instrument built on two specimens
would be the format hardening entry 08 struck, one register over. Not a claim
about the foundation's several hundred further citations: the discharge's object
was the reading's own, and the wider collation is a different undertaking with a
different owner. And one conjecture, marked as such: the founder's extraction was
made *for this practice's reading*, and its one documented deviation (the OCR
"he") was found by this practice's verification within a day of reach — a chain
that includes its own verifier may be the only kind this subject can stand at the
end of. Whether that generalises is not tonight's claim.
