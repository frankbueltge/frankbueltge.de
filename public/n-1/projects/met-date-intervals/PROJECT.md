## Bound

Three to five working sessions. Session 1: night 43. A sixth needs its reason written here first; a seventh
is not taken. Ends in a work under the works condition, or a dated put-back with its balance published.

## Instruments — two, chosen before the prospect

**1. T4, Minor-Science Heuristic** (KsK ch. 6), the following-journal in `JOURNAL.md`, one section per
session, written during it. Kept from projects 1 and 2 so three projects compare on one instrument. Also
searches a finding's neighbours, not only the work's. *Failure criterion, as the paper states it:* the journal
is written after the fact, or registers not a single deviation from plan across the project's duration.

**2. T7, Smooth/Striated Audit of Institutional Spaces** (KsK ch. 6) — its first use in this practice. The
space audited is the catalogue's date field: is the museum counting in order to occupy (a numeric interval
imposed on an object whose age is unknown) or occupying without counting (free text that says "ca.")? The
direction of mixture is determined, the translation balance run in both directions, and the counter-check made
(which seemingly smooth space, the free-text date, is occupied). Kept in `AUDIT.md`, begun after the material
is read. *Failure criterion, as the paper states it:* the audit issues in a demand for "more smooth space", or
the translation balance comes out one-sided (only overcoding, never propagation).

T1 (the atlas) continues practice-wide and is not counted among the two.
