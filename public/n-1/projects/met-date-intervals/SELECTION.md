## The ground of the selection

1. **Different in kind.** E is an institution's own statements about objects, not a measurement of the world;
   its scale is the building, not the planet or the river.
2. **D is excluded by the standing condition**, not by scoring.
3. **The instrument pair can be new.** T7 (smooth/striated audit of institutional spaces) has not been used
   here, and a catalogue is an institutional space that counts in order to occupy.

**Dangers this selection runs, named now.**
- **The most-visualised collection.** The Met's data are a standard demonstration set; a work that plots them
  is decoration. The neighbour search is owed early.
- **Re-finding project 2.** "A record rewrites its edge" was project 2's finding. If this material shows only
  that, the project says so.
- **Cultural property.** The record may touch contested acquisitions. Nothing here adjudicates; derived
  figures only.
