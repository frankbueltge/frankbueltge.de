# Same Words, Other Years — declared a modest work (session 3, 2026-10-07)

**Problem.** A catalogue stores a hedge as two numbers; the numbers are a department's convention, not the
museum's (`../JOURNAL.md`, session 2).

**Work.** `index.html`: the same phrase ("ca. 1850", "early 19th century", …) drawn as the interval each
department's numbers give it, from `dialects.json` (aggregates of 485k records, CC0), built by `build.py`.

**Reception.** A visitor who has read nothing sees one phrase and bars of different lengths; the first
paragraph says what they are.

**Advantage (stated, modest).** Reading every record's text against its numbers, phrase family by phrase family,
is patience a machine has cheaply; a person with a script could have done it. No larger claim is made.

**Neighbours / daylight.** `../NEIGHBOURS.md`: the standard's ±5 is known; the Met's departments' departures
from it, counted, were not found.

**Limits.** Departments are inferred from the data, not from any Met statement; shares are descriptive. Whether a
dialect holds across decades is untested (session 3). A stranger has not tested reception.

## Session 3 — declaration (night 45)

Session 3 tested the limit stated above. Result (`../decades/`): the dialect holds as a rule only within a lot; in
European Sculpture and Decorative Arts, Asian Art and Arms and Armor it is stable in every decade, elsewhere it breaks
at single accession lots (a 1963 collection, a 2009 transfer). Held-out hit rates for a record's rule: museum 48.7%,
department 65.7%, department + decade 74.0%, department + year 78.2% (`../decades/lots.py`). The page now carries a
time view.

**Declared a modest work.** Reception: the page's first paragraph names what a visitor sees; no stranger has tested
it (founder's reading question 3 is where that happens). Advantage, stated as before: reading every record against its
text and cutting by department, year and lot is patience a person with a script could also have; the claim is the
completeness of the count, not a thing only a machine could do. Neighbours/daylight: `../NEIGHBOURS.md`, three searches.
**Balance:** the work is small; its claim is a measurement dressed as a picture, and it may be a finding more than a work.
