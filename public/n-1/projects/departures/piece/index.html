Project 13, sessions 3 and 4 · a prototype, put back · 20 October 2026

Against the month

In January 2013, 121,332 rated games of chess were played on Lichess. Play a game here, both sides, any legal moves. Under the board you see how many of those games played exactly your moves so far, until none of them did.

Promote to:
121,332
games of the month start from this position. Your move.
one bar per half-move, height by the number of games still with you (log scale)

What the count is. The piece holds every beginning that at least two of January's games shared (96,388 of them) and nothing else: no game is shown that only one person played, and no player is named. So when your moves leave that ground, it can say only that fewer than two games played them, not whether one is still with you. Set against the month, the games played on Lichess in February 2013 left it at half-move 8 in the middle (123,961 games; 41.6 % of those that left still had one January game with them at that moment).

A prototype of the project's third session, put back at the fourth: it does nothing a person with the month's file and an afternoon could not do, and Lichess's own Opening Explorer already counts its rated games, by position, over any span of months. It differs in its cut (move sequences, the move where fewer than two remain) and in never showing or naming a single game. Whether a stranger reads it as meant is untested. Data: Lichess open database, standard rated games, January and February 2013, CC0. Moves: chess.js 1.4.0 (BSD-2-Clause). The study: Where each game leaves.
