# T2 assemblage analysis — session 3

Field 1, bodies: languages, their last letters, and now papers — a request filed, dated, numbered — and the people who write them (not named).
Field 2, statements: the register's titles; "proposal", "revised proposal", "ballot", "draft chart" as genres of paper; the final character name.
Field 3, territorialisation: the register fixes what counts as asked (filed with this committee, in this register); the final name fixes what
counts as the same letter, so an asking under another name falls outside.
Field 4, cutting edge: the paper. Between a language's writers and the ledger there is one document, and the years sit on the writers' side of it.
Movements: 4 -> 2: the claim moved from "the language waited" to "the language waited for a paper" — the page's lede with it. 3 -> 4: the final-name
rule (territorialising) made three ballot and chart stages look like askings; reading them reclassified them as later stages and turned their
intervals into floors. Failure criterion not triggered; moderate.
