Legible only below

A study. The dates carved on three Elbe stones, readable only while the river reads low. Move through the month.

  • Děčín stone (Tetschen), Czech Republic

    1616, 1746, 1790, 1800, 1842, 1868

    1417 and 1473 are listed as illegible

  • Oberposta stone, Elbe-km 31.6

    1707, 1782, 1790, 1842, 1858, 1859, 1863, 1868, 1873, 1878, 1904, 1947, 1963, 2003, Aug 2015

    listed 'among others'; more than fifteen entries

  • Pillnitz stone, Elbe-km 43.0

    19.07.1873, 18.07.1904, 17.08.2003

The rule is this study's own: a stone's dates are legible while the Dresden gauge reads below its mean low water (67 cm). It does not claim any stone is exposed at that level; the stones' own thresholds are known for two only (Děčín, a discharge; Schönebeck, a level) and are not used here. Without scripts this page shows the dates legible, which is the state of 88.4 % of the 3264 readings held.

The record. Dresden gauge, 15-minute readings, 2026-08-29 to 2026-10-02: two 31-day windows of the federal waterways' public service (fetched 2026-09-29 and 2026-10-02, licence DL-DE->Zero-2.0), joined; where they overlap they agree. 288 of the readings are no longer served by the service. Lowest held: 50.0 cm at 2026-09-30 08:30 (Europe/Berlin). Stones' dates: the Saxon state table “Hungersteine und Untiefen der Elbe” (LHWZ Sachsen / Senckenberg Dresden, status 24.07.2018), dates only. The marks' relation to the lowest stage of their year is Elleder et al. 2020, Climate of the Past 16, 1821–1846, doi:10.5194/cp-16-1821-2020. Source and figures: projects/elbe-low-water/study-1/.
