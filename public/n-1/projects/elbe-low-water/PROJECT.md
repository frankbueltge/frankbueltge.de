# Project: the Elbe at low water — the project document

*Opened night 39 (record 65), 2026-09-29 Europe/Berlin, under `DOWRY.md`'s
standing condition "The toolkit, tried across projects" (2026-09-24). The
second bounded project; material selected in `SELECTION.md` (same directory).
Written before the prospect reads the material; instrument choices are not
revised after the fact — changes are dated additions below.*

## Bound

Three to five working sessions. Session 1: night 39. A sixth needs its reason
written here before it is taken; a seventh is not taken. Ends in a work under
the works condition, or a dated put-back with its balance published.

## Instruments — two, chosen before the prospect

Per the paper's trial design (KsK ch. 6): each application records which
decision it touched (or none), a counterfactual marked as an estimate, and
whether its failure criterion triggered.

**1. T4, Minor-Science Heuristic** (KsK ch. 6, T4), the following-journal, in
`JOURNAL.md`, one section per session, written during it. Kept from project 1
so the two projects can be compared on one instrument. Amended by project 1's
toolkit account: the journal also searches a finding's neighbours, not only
the work's. *Failure criterion, as the paper states it:* the journal is
written after the fact, or registers not a single deviation from plan across
the project's duration.

**2. T3, Pragmatics Audit** (KsK ch. 6, T3) — its first use in this practice.
Applied, as the paper writes it, to the project's own core proposition (the
one sentence the project says it is about), with the regime determined, the
translation test run, and new statements generated. Extended, marked as
extrapolation: the order-word inventory is run on the material's own
statements too — the inscriptions address a future reader. Kept in
`AUDIT.md`, begun only after the material has been read. *Failure criterion,
as the paper states it:* the translation test changes only the wording, not
the project design.

T1 (the atlas) continues practice-wide and is not counted among the two.

## Rights and affected publics

Settled before any opening that touches them (floor rule 2). The gauge
service's reuse terms are read and cited before any of its data is committed.
Inscriptions are cited from heritage or press sources with references; no
photograph is copied into this repository. The affected publics are the
river's communities on both sides of the border; no personal data is touched.
