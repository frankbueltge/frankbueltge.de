## Found

1. **Jodi Le Bigre, *Hunger Stones* / *Moveable Hunger Stone and Shifting
   Baselines Tool*** — two lithographic limestone pieces: a cylinder that is "a
   rolling matrix to lithographically print this message where needed" and a
   flat stone "light enough to be moved into place and shifted as necessary",
   carrying the line "We have wept, we weep, and you will weep", taken from a
   Czech hunger stone, for "the continually shifting environmental baselines of
   our current era". Sources: <https://www.jodilebigre.com/hunger-stones>,
   <https://glasgowartclub.co.uk/exhibiton/moveable-hunger-stone-and-shifting-baselines-tool/>
   (both read 2026-09-29). Year not stated on either page. Uses no gauge data.
   **Consequence:** it occupies the moved stone and the shifting threshold —
   the prospect's finding 5 (Schönebeck, moved 2011). That finding leaves the
   centre of this project, as the vote did in project 1.
2. **Sophy King, *Drought Stones*** (<https://www.sophyking.com/drought-stones>)
   and **Stone Lane Gardens, *16: The Hunger Stone***
   (<https://stonelanegardens.com/sculpture-catalog/16-the-hunger-stone/>) —
   named by the search result as site-specific stones carrying "If you see me…
   weep". **Not read tonight**; to be verified before any daylight is stated.
3. **Live river-gauge data art** — IDIA Lab, *oneWAPIHANNE* (White River,
   Indiana; <https://idialab.org/waterviz/>); +POOL, *+POOL Light* (East River,
   New York, water quality). Named by the search; not read tonight.

## Session 2, night 40, 2026-10-02

Read: pages fetched through a summarising service, then Elleder's abstract and
the Euronews sentence checked against the raw page (curl, HTTP 200).

- **Sophy King, *Drought Stones*** (<https://www.sophyking.com/drought-stones>):
  sandstone, Rudyard Lake, Staffordshire, 2022 with additions planned, a
  five-minute video 2023; "if we see them again we will know that this is a
  pattern rather than an event" (as reported by the fetch service; not checked
  against raw HTML). Marks water levels; no gauge data. Not the same.
- **Dawn Ensor, *The Hunger Stone*, Stone Lane Gardens, 2023**
  (<https://stonelanegardens.com/sculpture-catalog/16-the-hunger-stone/>):
  local stone sited in a stream, inscription "If you see me… weep". Not the same.
- **Elleder, Kašpárek, Šírová, Kabelka, "Low water stage marks on hunger stones:
  verification for the Elbe from 1616 to 2015", *Climate of the Past* 16 (2020),
  1821–1846, doi:10.5194/cp-16-1821-2020, CC BY 4.0.** Compares marks on Elbe
  stones to systematic gauge records (Magdeburg 1727–1880, Dresden 1801–1829,
  Prague 1825–1890, Děčín 1851–2019), 3D-scans the Děčín stone. Not an artwork;
  a scientific neighbour that **occupies the comparison of stones and gauges**.
  Daylight for this project is therefore not the comparison.
- **Euronews, 2026-08-10**
  (<https://www.euronews.com/2026/08/10/historic-drought-rivers-reveal-hunger-stones-warships-and-mammoth-remains>):
  the Oberposta stone exposed by the persistently low Elbe. No gauge figure given.
  Cited as the reason the project's condition is not conjecture this season.
