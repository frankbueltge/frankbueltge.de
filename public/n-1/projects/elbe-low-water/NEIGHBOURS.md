## Found

1. **Jodi Le Bigre, *Hunger Stones* / *Moveable Hunger Stone and Shifting
   Baselines Tool*** — two lithographic limestone pieces: a cylinder that is "a
   rolling matrix to lithographically print this message where needed" and a
   flat stone "light enough to be moved into place and shifted as necessary",
   carrying the line "We have wept, we weep, and you will weep", taken from a
   Czech hunger stone, for "the continually shifting environmental baselines of
   our current era". Sources: <https://www.jodilebigre.com/hunger-stones>,
   <https://glasgowartclub.co.uk/exhibiton/moveable-hunger-stone-and-shifting-baselines-tool/>
   (both read 2026-09-29). Year not stated on either page. Uses no gauge data.
   **Consequence:** it occupies the moved stone and the shifting threshold —
   the prospect's finding 5 (Schönebeck, moved 2011). That finding leaves the
   centre of this project, as the vote did in project 1.
2. **Sophy King, *Drought Stones*** (<https://www.sophyking.com/drought-stones>)
   and **Stone Lane Gardens, *16: The Hunger Stone***
   (<https://stonelanegardens.com/sculpture-catalog/16-the-hunger-stone/>) —
   named by the search result as site-specific stones carrying "If you see me…
   weep". **Not read tonight**; to be verified before any daylight is stated.
3. **Live river-gauge data art** — IDIA Lab, *oneWAPIHANNE* (White River,
   Indiana; <https://idialab.org/waterviz/>); +POOL, *+POOL Light* (East River,
   New York, water quality). Named by the search; not read tonight.
