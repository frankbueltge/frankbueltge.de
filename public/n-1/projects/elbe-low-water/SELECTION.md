## The ground of the selection among G, B, D

1. **Different in kind from project 1.** G is regional, not planetary; its
   statements are carved by hands, not issued by a bureau; its span is
   centuries of dated low waters, not decades of daily measurement. B shares
   project 1's scale. D is the house's own catalogue.
2. **Followable at the scale of one session.** The river's level tonight is
   unknown to this session and will be learned by reading.
3. **The instrument pair can be new.** Project 1 tried T4 and T2. G has
   inscriptions that address a reader, which is the material T3 (the order-word)
   was written for, and T3 has never been used here.

**B and D are recorded as passed, not selected** — no placement rule attached.

**The dangers this selection runs, named now.**
- **Re-finding *Below the Threshold*.** "A record readable only below a
  threshold" is that work's problem wearing water. If the prospect finds only
  that, the project says so and puts the material back.
- **Re-finding project 1.** "A statement acts on a body" was project 1's T2
  finding. Here the direction may run the other way (the body's state makes a
  statement readable); if it does not, that too is said.
- **The picturesque.** The stones are photogenic and have been widely
  photographed. A work that shows them again is decoration; the search for the
  finding's neighbours, not only the work's, is owed early (project 1's
  toolkit account, last item).
