# Project 9 — neighbours (session 1)

*Searched 2026-10-15, before the citations were parsed. Begins in the Atlas of Data Art, as the founder's offer puts it.*

**The Atlas of Data Art** (`https://frankbueltge.de/atlas/werke.json`, 523 records, CC0). A grep of every record for asteroid,
minor planet, naming, celestial, sky, stars, obituary and biography found no work that takes minor planet names or their citations as
material. The nearest by subject are works on the sky as data (Playmodes Studio, *ASTRES: Mapping the Firmament*, 2025; Scott
Hessels, *Celestial Mechanics*, 2007; Adriana Knouf, *On your wrist is the universe*, 2016), none of them on who is named in it.

**Outside it, found by search and read where marked:**

- **Scott Kildall, *Celebrity Asteroid Journeys*** (shown in *Machine Data Dreams*, Black & White Projects, 2016; page read,
  `https://blackandwhiteprojects.com/2016/10/14/kildall-2016/`): silkscreen prints charting impossible journeys between asteroids
  named after celebrities, using orbital data. It takes the name as a destination; it does not read the citations or the honourees'
  lives.
- **zooofzane, *5000 Names of Minor Planets*** (undated data-story site; page read, `https://zooofzane.cargo.site/5000-Names-of-Minor-Planets`):
  numbers 1-5000 from the Minor Planet Center as stories of astronomers and of observing activity. No ages or birth years.
- **Jan Holeš, "People Love to Name Things: Current Trends in Naming Minor Planets"**, *Voprosy onomastiki* 22 (1), 2025, pp. 220-235,
  doi:10.15826/vopr_onom.2025.22.1.009 (bibliography verified on Crossref; the text answered 403 and was **not read**; a search
  summary says it classifies the 648 names published in 2022, about 90 % after persons). The nearest scholarly neighbour: names by
  kind, in one year.
- **Lutz D. Schmadel, *Dictionary of Minor Planet Names*** (Springer, several editions): the reference work that gathers the
  citations. Not read here; it holds the texts, not a measurement of them.

**Daylight (conjecture until the data stands):** none of these draws the age at which a person is written into the sky, or how
long after death the dead are named. If the distribution holds a shape (the predictions in `SELECTION.md`), that shape is what this
project would add. Not yet claimed.
