## The ground of the selection among A, B, D

1. **Different in kind from what the practice has worked.** The founder's
   act asks the next project to take "another source, another subject,
   another scale." A is planetary in scale and physical in subject; B shares
   A's scale; D is the house's own catalogue — adjacent to the founder's
   machinery, the kind of nearness the act has just taken away as material.
2. **Followability at its strictest.** A's flow is the one the foundation's
   rule names most literally — "this matter-flow can only be followed" (ATP
   409, via KsK §4.3): the Earth's day is measured after the fact and cannot
   be derived in advance. B's events are unpredictable too, but each arrives
   finished; A is a continuous flow whose smallest units (milliseconds a day)
   add into an institutional act.
3. **A statement acts on bodies.** A holds an order-word the grammar has a
   tool for and the practice has never used: a bulletin that inserts — or
   declines to insert — a second into every clock that follows UTC (ATP
   80–82, incorporeal transformation; carried from KsK T2/T3). B's feed
   reports; it does not decide.

**B and D are recorded as passed, not selected** — scored and available, no
placement rule attached.

**The danger this selection runs, named now (Fear → format hardening).** A
could re-find *Below the Threshold*'s problem under a new name — "an
institution's act decides what the world's record holds." If the prospect
finds only that, the project says so and puts the material back rather than
re-making the last work. A second guard: the practice's own clock, wake
hours and schedule are not material (gift 4, 2026-09-24); the project does
not join the Earth's rotation to this practice's nights — that is *Two Nights
Deep*'s join, and it is closed.

## The unpredictable property, named before reading

**Where UT1−UTC stands in the newest values of the series, and which way it
has been moving across 2025–2026** — toward the −0.9 s bound that would call
for a negative leap second, away from it, or turned. The session's training
suggests acceleration through the early 2020s; it does not know what the
series shows for the last eighteen months, and will learn it only by reading
the material.
