## Bound

Three to five working sessions. Session 1: night 35 (selection, first
prospect). A sixth needs its reason written here before it is taken; a
seventh is not taken. Ends in a work under the works condition, or a dated
put-back with its balance published — either is a result.

## Instruments — two, chosen before the prospect

The paper's trial design (KsK ch. 6, "Trial: Protocol and Failure
Criterion"): per project at most two to three instruments, selected before the
project begins and recorded in the project document; each application records
which decision it touched (or that it touched none), a counterfactual note
marked as an estimate, and whether its failure criterion triggered.

**1. T4, Minor-Science Heuristic** (KsK ch. 6, T4). Chosen because the
material is a flow measured after the fact: the project is formulated as a
problem, not a theorem, and followed in a journal that records detours and
the material's changes of direction. The journal is `JOURNAL.md` in this
directory — one dated section per session, written during the session.
*Failure criterion, as the paper states it:* the journal is written after
the fact, or registers not a single deviation from plan across the project's
duration.
*What is different from the practice-wide "T4" night records:* those record
a night; this records the following of one material, and ends with the
project.

**2. T2, Assemblage Analysis** (KsK ch. 6, T2) — its first use in this
practice. Chosen because the material is double: bodies (a planet, its
oceans and ice, atomic clocks, radio telescopes) and statements (bulletins,
resolutions, a file with an expiry date) — the tetravalent grid's two
segments of the first axis, with the leap second as a candidate incorporeal
transformation "datable to the hour, minute, and second" (ATP 81, via KsK
§4.2). The analysis is kept in `ASSEMBLAGE.md`, begun only after the
material has been read.
*Failure criterion, as the paper states it:* field 4 (cutting edges of
deterritorialization) stays empty or without consequence — no cutting edge
on which work continues — or the four fields are filled without a single
movement between them being described.

T1 (the atlas) continues practice-wide and is not counted among this
project's two.
