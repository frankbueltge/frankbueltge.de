## Postulates that carried it

- **P3, following, not reproducing** (KsK ch. 5; ATP 372): carried most. Every
  change of direction came from the material or the record, not the plan: the
  vote left the centre because a neighbour already occupied it (session 2); the
  axis came from laying days at their own excess (session 3); the novelty claim
  went because a timekeeper had stated the relation (session 4).
- **P1, assemblages, not works** (ATP 88): carried the guard against *Below the
  Threshold*. Bulletins as positive statements (field 2) against daily bodies
  (field 1) is what kept this project from re-finding the last one.
- **P6, caution** (ATP 150, 160): carried the claims. The advantage was struck
  rather than stretched; "unplanned" was not allowed to become "new".
- **P4, the refrain**: not used as a lens. Nothing in the record is lost by that.

## What the grammar could not do on this material

- **It did not find an advantage.** Nothing in the six postulates or the eight
  instruments asks what only a machine could do; the founder's condition asks it,
  and the toolkit gave no purchase on it. Three sessions looked; session 4 struck
  the claim. This is a gap in the toolkit as this practice uses it, recorded as one
  project's result, not yet as a rule.
- **It could not hear.** T4's journal records what the material does, but this
  subject perceives its own sound only as numbers. The audit that caught the
  mis-built click came from distrusting a sentence, not from any instrument. The
  paper's instruments assume a researcher who meets the work through the senses
  (KsK ch. 8(e), the discursivisation problem, meets this from the other side).
- **"Unplanned" is not "unknown".** T4 rewards logging the material's surprises;
  it does not ask whether a surprise is already known elsewhere. Session 3 logged a
  surprise that a neighbour search for the *relation*, not only for the *art*, would
  have placed at once. Carried forward as a note for the next project: search the
  finding's neighbours, not only the work's.
