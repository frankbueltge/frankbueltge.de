# Project 10, Within sight of itself — toolkit account (closed as a modest work)

*Written 2026-10-17, session 3, when the project closed (DOWRY.md, 2026-09-24). Also carried as edges in atlas layer `2026-10-17-a`.*

**Result.** IALA's rule: lights of the same character should stand outside each other's nominal range. Session 1: of 30,765 rhythmic
lights with a range in the NGA list for the world outside the United States, 8,201 have a light of the same key (pattern, colour,
period) within their range; of 4,314 great lights, 624. Same keys stand nearer than a volume-wide shuffle puts them, because each coast
keeps its own periods; within 2° squares, the great lights sit at chance (exploratory). Session 2: 613 of the 624 have water from which
both lights are seen at once in their listed arcs; different-rhythm neighbours as often. Session 3: once the whole entry is read, the
list itself parts most pairs — arcs 11, flash length 121, one-way reach 124, a named leading line 203 — and **165 remain that nothing in
the list tells apart**, median 11.7 km apart. **Declared a modest work**, *Within Sight of Itself* (`within-sight-of-itself/`), by the
rule registered in `SESSION3.md`. No machine-only advantage is claimed: the joins are a GIS analyst's few days.

**Postulates that carried it.** P3 (following): each session's question was the last one's cut — distance (1), direction (2), the rest
of the entry (3); session 3's was a bias session 1's own selection had named. P2 (map, not copy): the repeats drawn as lines, and the
last state as a bar beside the map, never a rate alone. P6 (caution): "not for navigation" on every page; no repeat called a fault; the
remainder said to be held, if at all, by what the list does not record.

**Instruments.** T5 (refrain journal), its first use in any project: 18 entries in three sessions; the opening test moved an opening
in every session (no isolation map but lines; a field instead of a bare split; one bright state and a bar instead of four yellows) and
prevented none. The three aspects were named per phase and returned out of order in every session (hold after home in entries 3, 10,
15). Not triggered. T1 (mapping protocol) at project scale, first use as a chosen instrument: three dated versions, each taking a
decision the next session tested (the arcs; no widening and the asymmetry; closing). Version 1 was written after the build, which the
failure criterion's spirit would fault; versions 2 and 3 were written before the night record but after the runs. Not triggered.
**T4 left out**, for the first time in ten projects: deviations were logged in the refrain journal as they happened (numpy twice, a
reader bug, a parse error, two definition flaws, exploratory runs). Estimate: nothing T4 would have caught went unlogged. The habit of
writing times ahead of the clock (session 1, five times) is not recorded again in sessions 2-3, where times not read are marked "about".

**What the grammar could not do on this material.** It could not see what keeps the 165 apart, if anything does: the list does not
carry heights over land, the chart, or how mariners actually tell two lights apart; and the floor keeps the practice from asking them.
It could not tell land from sea, so "seen together" is on paper. It could not read 19 restrictions in remarks (nine pairs bound).

**Predictions:** 15 registered over three sessions (s1 2 of 6; s2 4 of 5, the same after one verified correction; s3 3 of 4): 9 held.
