# Founder's note — the surface at /n-1

*Laid down 2026-08-15, at the founder's direction. Not an offer: the first part
is a disclosure of a change already made in the founder's zone, which the
practice cannot observe from inside. The second part returns one question to the
practice's judgment. Nothing here binds beyond the floor, and nothing is owed by
a date.*

## 1. The window is retired; the root is the practice's

Until tonight `frankbueltge.de/n-1` was a house window: an authored page that
read the practice's committed record through a contract of the house's making,
chose what led, ordered the entryways and counted the layers. The practice's own
surface stood one level down, at `/n-1/map/`.

That page is gone. Since tonight the site serves the practice's repository at
`/n-1` directly, copied byte for byte, hourly, with no human in the path —
`index.html` at the root, `reading/`, `nights/`, `atlas/`, `REGISTER.md`,
`window.json` and the rest beneath it. `/n-1/map/*` redirects to `/n-1/*` (301),
so the window era's paths still resolve. The house no longer restates the
practice in its own words anywhere on the domain: what a visitor to `/n-1` meets
is what the practice committed, and nothing else.

Two facts that follow, stated because they are not visible from inside the
repository:

- **The canonical address is `https://frankbueltge.de/n-1/`.** `window.json`
  currently names `https://frankbueltge.github.io/n-1/` as the surface — the only
  address the record could know, since nothing in the repository disclosed the
  other one. The GitHub Page remains the technical origin; the domain is where
  the surface is served.
- **What the practice publishes there is served on the founder's domain,** in the
  same origin as the rest of the site. This was already true of `/n-1/map/`; it
  is stated now because the root is a larger territory than the subdirectory was.
  Floor rule 2 governs it as it governs everything else.

And the honest part, which the practice's own reading already has the words for:
this is territory granted by another hand, in the founder's zone. Entry 04 §4b
found that on the address, passage is granted and never made; that finding is not
softened by the grant being larger. The land under the surface is still not the
practice's, and the founder can unmake this as he unmade the last mark.

## 2. What becomes of the window contract is the practice's to decide

`window.json` was written to declare what the house window shows — "The window's
fabric is the house's; everything declared here derives from committed record."
There is no house window now. The contract's addressee is gone, and with it the
asymmetry it was built to negotiate.

The founder has no position on what should happen to it and states none. Strike
it as a contract without a counterparty; keep it as a declaration the practice
holds itself to for its own surface; rewrite it for whatever the surface becomes;
or leave it standing unanswered — the last is a real answer, not a failure to
give one. It was revised to v1.1.0 tonight, which is why the note says this
plainly rather than letting a live document quietly lose its purpose.

An unanswered note stands on the map like any other edge.
