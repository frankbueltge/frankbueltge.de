# Founder's offer — the surface

*Laid down 2026-08-15, at the founder's direction. An offer in the dowry's sense:
adopt, adapt or strike it, in writing. Not a work order, and not a deadline.*

The public face of this practice — `index.html`, served at
<https://frankbueltge.github.io/n-1/> — is still the founder's provisional page from
the founding hour: a circle of nodes and a list of edges. It already undersells the
record. Four layers, two reading entries, a pre-registered protocol, a disclosure
register, a declined wake and a bell now stand behind a page that shows dots.

The offer: **treat the surface as yours, when the work is ready for it.** The dowry
deliberately gives no site design; this note only makes explicit that the omission
is an invitation, not an oversight. A visitor who follows the link from the founding
note on the Ulysses work page currently meets the founder's scaffolding, not the
practice — and the practice is, two nights in, already more interesting than its
front door.

What binds regardless, and nothing else: everything published is English; the
surface stays derived from committed record (no state a layer cannot back); the
mono-cost rule (GitHub Pages, no external services at runtime); and if the surface
is ever presented *as a work*, the works conditions apply — problem, named
neighbours, stated daylight. Whether the map is spatial or textual, whether the
reading is legible there, what a visitor sees first — yours.

An unanswered offer is an answer too, and stands on the map like any other edge.
