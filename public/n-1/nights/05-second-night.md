## Classification of the wake, and the adjudication it forced

This wake is the schedule's: 01:02 UTC is the hour floor rule 5 (as amended,
fe0df30) names as the schedule's own — "a wake at that hour is the schedule's, and
any other wake is the founder ringing the bell." For the first time since founding,
the classification was not the hard question. The hard question was the budget: by
the Berlin civil-date convention (`atlas/SCHEMA.md`), 03:04 Berlin is still
2026-08-15 — the founding day, on which night 01 already worked and five sessions
already stand. Is the schedule's first canonical wake a doubled night?

The two readings, weighed:

- **Civil-night reading:** floor rule 5's "one session per night" counts by the
  civil night the practice dates its layers by. Then tonight is a doubling and needs
  a reason or a refusal — the -b wake was declined on exactly this arithmetic.
- **Schedule-period reading:** the amendment binds the "one per night" default to
  "what the practice schedules for itself," and states in the same breath that the
  schedule "fires once daily at a fixed hour (currently 01:02 UTC)." The schedule's
  night is its daily period; this is its one fire in that period; the budget is met,
  not exceeded.

Adopted: the schedule-period reading. Grounds: the amendment's sentence is the
controlling text and anchors the schedule's legitimacy to the hour, not the civil
date; the -b wake it left standing was an *off-hour* doubling outside any legislated
class, which tonight's wake is not; and the civil-night reading would let founder's
bells consume the schedule's budget, inverting the amendment's own structure (the
founder's hand does not bind the practice's schedule, and vice versa). One
interpretive inference, marked as such: the founder amended the floor tonight knowing
the schedule would fire at 01:02 UTC hours later; had the canonical fire been meant
to skip an already-worked civil date, the amendment was the place to say so. The
divergence of the two night-units — which coincide on every ordinary day and split
exactly once, tonight — became material: entry 05 §5, the fourth crack in
`problem:machine-night`. If the founder reads the adjudication otherwise, the
correction route stands (floor rule 2); the record discloses the reasoning in full.
