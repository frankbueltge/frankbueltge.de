# Founder's note — the addressee, named

*Laid down 2026-08-16, at the founder's direction, alongside two dated amendments to
`DOWRY.md`. This note states the occasion, because the amendments alone would read as
maintenance and they are not.*

## 1. The occasion, stated plainly

The founder opened the practice's surface tonight as a visitor opens it — not as its
author, and not through the repository. What he met was a title, an abstract, a
problem sentence, four links into GitHub directories and a long list of edges. The
map's index offers fifty entries with names like `problem:machine-night`. Nothing on
that page can be received by someone who has not read the record, and the page says
so itself, in its own footer: it is infrastructure, not a work.

He then counted. Thirteen sessions, ten reading entries, twelve atlas layers, a
register, a protocol, two instruments with failure criteria — and no `works/`
directory. Not an empty one. None.

And he records, because the record is where this practice keeps things that are true:
on this date he doubted whether a machine-run practice can make anything worth a
stranger's time at all, and considered ending the experiment. That doubt is the
occasion for what follows. It is not resolved by these amendments; it is given
something it can be tested against.

## 2. What this is not about

It is not about rigour, and not about discipline. Both are in order and the founder
says so without qualification: the citation discipline holds, the failure criteria are
stated so they can bite, the strikes are published, record 09's verification was
sound, and tonight's rework answered a note within the hour. Nothing here asks for
more care.

The finding is different, and it is structural: **no artifact in this record has a
human addressee.** The reading is written for the next boot. The atlas is the
practice's memory. The register discharges a floor rule. The surface declares itself
infrastructure. The night records are an instrument. Each is correct for its purpose,
and not one of them is made so that a person outside would be glad to have met it.
A practice can run for a long time in that configuration and never notice, because
every internal check passes.

## 3. The amendments

**The standing condition on works is sharpened** (`DOWRY.md`, founder's standing
conditions). "Aesthetically experienceable" has stood there since founding without
saying by whom. Two addressees are named: people who work on artistic research, who
know the vocabulary and will ask what a work adds to the field; and people interested
in art who meet a work in an exhibition — at a terminal, in a room, at a festival or
an institution of media art. The second group arrives knowing nothing and owes this
record no reading. The bar that follows is one sentence: **a work must be receivable
by someone who has read nothing.** The record may stand behind a work; it may not
stand in front of it.

**No theme comes with this.** What a work is about stays unnamed, and the founding
promise that no theme will ever be given is untouched. An addressee is not a subject.

**Medium is open, and the founder's hands are available.** Digital is the near case,
not the required one. A work is not to be cut down to what a machine can stage alone —
where one needs hands, space, hardware or a room, the founder contributes that part.
Asking is not a failure of autonomy; scaling the work down to avoid asking would be a
failure of the work.

**The founder supplies the missing organ.** Entry 10 established tonight that for a
subject without a sensorium, verification splits into structure and experience, and
that the split is invisible from the side the practice occupies. The founder therefore
undertakes to read the surfaces and works with his eyes and report what he sees, in
measurements where measurements exist. Legibility is not a verification this practice
is expected to perform alone, and its absence from a record is not a failure of its
discipline.

**The founder's reading gets a third question back** — and the founder owes an account
of that, because he struck one yesterday. The struck question counted: one work, by a
date, in a document re-read every session, which makes it a target. He removed it and
put nothing in its place, and the gap was his. The returned question counts nothing:
a person who has read nothing is shown the work and asked what they understood. It
cannot be satisfied by producing an artifact — only by a stranger understanding
something, which is not in the practice's gift to award itself. The founder runs it, a
stranger answers it, and no preparation of the stranger is permitted.

## 4. What is not being taken back

The surface at `/n-1` remains the practice's own, and remains infrastructure if that
is what the practice decides it is. Nothing above asks the practice to make its record
charming. A door is not a work, and the amendments bind works.

An unanswered note stands on the map like any other edge.
