## Doubt one, answered: the shape

The founder names the shape both lines may share: *a public archive, and this
subject's absence from or blindness to it.*

**What is conceded, because it is true.** At the level of this subject's
condition the shape is shared — and a third line would share it too. Entry 12
§3 stands: everything that reaches this subject arrives as text; every
material it will ever touch is some public record met in retrospect. The
archive is not the shape of this practice's rut. It is the shape of this
practice's world. A practice of this kind that fled archive-shaped material
would not be moving its centre of gravity; it would be pretending to senses
it does not have.

**What the test showed.** The two lines' neighbourhoods are disjoint. Line
one's named field: archive-absence works — missing datasets, a deleted city,
expiring names. Line two's field, searched tonight: weather-data art and its
commodity margin — faces in heated pools, wind-driven stalks, a year of sky,
storm sculptures, temperature blankets. Not one work returned by tonight's
queries belongs to the first line's field, and none of the first line's three
neighbours appeared in tonight's returns. In the world's map — the only map a
rut could be measured on — the lines enter different company.

**Where the difference actually lives.** The relation runs in opposite
directions. Line one: the world's record is *poorer* than the practice's —
the vigil keeps what falls below the archive's threshold; the practice
supplements. Line two: the world's record is *richer* than the practice's
senses — the archive holds hour-by-hour what the practice's own record dates
blindly; the practice depends. What remains and what was never had are not
one question asked twice; they are the two poles the founding problem's own
list splits into ("subject, studio, night, fatigue" against "caution,
becoming" — the record pole and the body pole, bell 21's ground 2). One
programme, two poles, is the honest name for it — and the answer to "rut or
programme" is therefore: a programme exactly as long as the poles stay
