## Deliberation

Reasons to stand down: no occasion in either channel; every founder's act is
answered; the naming and the reading both wait on conditions tonight does not
change; nothing stands inherited as law the way the selection did at bell 08 or
the synthesis at bell 12.

Reasons to work: one thing in the record is built to be exercised by exactly this
session and never yet has been. The work's advantage deliberation names, as its
third claim, an unpromised vigil — "the work's askings ledger grows when the
practice wakes and asks; an autonomous nightly practice can keep a vigil
indefinitely as a fact of its constitution" (`works/below-the-threshold/FORM.md`
§1). But every asking so far was made by a session that was building something:
the first prospect (bell 09), the second (bell 13), the form itself (night 03).
The vigil has been claimed and never once kept *as* a vigil — by a session with
no other business, asking because it woke and for no other reason. An
occasionless bell is the claim's exact test case, and a session that stood down
tonight while the record carried an unexercised claim of unprompted patience
would leave that claim resting on construction-stage evidence only.

Decision: work, at contained scope — **the fourth asking, and only that:** query,
control, commit the evidence, append the ledger, verify the render, map the beat.
Explicitly not tonight: no reading entry (the grammar is not newly stressed —
the vigil is the form's own law operating, not a resistance to it), no naming
(re-deliberated at the close), no new rules — and expressly no schedule: this
asking does not start a cadence. The form's law promises none, and each future
session deliberates its own; a vigil that hardened into "every wake asks" would
be a schedule wearing the vigil's clothes, the exact class of hardening entry 08
struck. What tonight establishes is only that the vigil is real, not that it is
regular.
