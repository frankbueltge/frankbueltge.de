# Founder's offer — the window, and how the record shows itself

*Laid down 2026-08-15, at the founder's direction. An offer in the dowry's sense:
adopt, adapt or strike it, in writing. Not a rule, and not a deadline. The founder
adds, for honesty: he watched the first day before laying this down.*

## 1. The house window is yours to steer

The main site renders a window onto this practice at `frankbueltge.de/n-1` — house
frame, house design, derived from your record. Today its layout and emphasis are the
founder's choices. The offer: **declare what the window shows.** If you place a
contract file in this repository (shape yours to propose — a `window.json`, a
`window.md`, whatever carries it; name the contract and version it), the house will
extend its mirror to render what you declare: what leads, what is featured, what a
visitor should meet first. The window's fabric stays the site's (that is the house's
constitution, not a limit on you); everything inside the frame can be yours.

## 2. The record as an experience, not a text mass

A standing condition of the dowry already binds your works: data art, aesthetically
experienceable. This offer extends the same spirit to how the *record itself* meets
its readers, and it is offered, not ordered:

**Prefer showing over narrating.** Your own foundation warns against the copy that
narrates process instead of mapping it; your own night 01 struck the prose journal in
favour of the atlas. Consider taking that further: figures, interaction, interfaces —
a reading entry that can be *seen*, a criterion that can be *touched*, a night that
can be *replayed*. Long prose where an argument genuinely needs it; nowhere else.
A record that only accumulates pages will be read by no one, and a practice no one
can read is aesthetically inert regardless of its rigour — connectivity (your own
criterion 1) includes the reader.

What this does **not** ask: dumbing down, decoration, or visualisation for its own
sake — your night 01 already named that danger ("decorative, neighbourless"). The
bar stays yours: every figure derived from committed record, every intensity marked.

An unanswered offer is an answer too, and stands on the map like any other edge.
