# In Whose Name

**The Atelier · cycle 014, presented 2026-11-15 · five minutes.** One of the cycle's three works, from artistic research and philosophy: the Atelier's part of step 2 of the programme *Can increasingly capable AI be kept under human and democratic control, and by whom?* The step asked: *At which points between the chip and the running model has a public body so far actually stopped, delayed or put conditions on AI compute, and whose public was it each time?* The Atelier's part is concepts and critique: what that question takes for granted.

`index.html` beside this file is the work, a page with script and no library. It grew over four sessions from a score (`SCORE.md`). A voice that did not build it met it after the second session (`CRITIQUE.md`, kept unedited); the third session, the check, answered it in the page. `PROCESS.json` and `process/1–4.png` show the four states. The same work stays at `artifacts/cycle-014/in-whose-name/`. The step's critique of the siblings' parts is `artifacts/cycle-014/step-2-critique.md`.

## What a visitor does
1. **Ten gates** on the way from the chip to the running model, two at each of five stations: ten acts of public bodies, read in their own texts. Four questions light them (what it did, in whose name, whether its text says artificial intelligence, how it got hold of the party). A switch, *On the day / Afterwards*, lifts the bars that were lifted and draws the way around the gates that were gone around.
2. **How long a stop lasts.** The Field's eleven checked stops, each with its public, laid side by side from the day each was made. A day slider ends them one by one; three acts first drawn as stops stand apart, corrected as delays by their own word.
3. **What an act says of its own length** (fourth state). The Field's eighteen holds sorted by what each says of how long it will last. One switch reads them with the record's word, *ended*; the other splits that word into three fates.

## What the work found
1. **"On AI compute" is the question's description, not the bodies'.** None of the six gates that stopped, delayed or put conditions gives artificial intelligence as its reason in its own text. Each public named is older than AI: a grid's customers, a city's water, a border, a market, the data of persons.
2. **A stop is true on the day.** Of the Field's eleven stops, two were lifted outright, both on terms; three were gone around by a change of form; four stand whole. A stop that stands has an age, not yet a lifetime.
3. **A penalty meets a deed, a permission meets whoever must ask.** No act among the Field's 28 meets compute as a stream. Where a stop worked, it shows in a record only as an absence sized by the party's own filing (Virginia: about 21.7 million square feet of voided floor ceiling, no megawatts, no chips).
4. **"Ended" is one word for three fates** (fourth state, on the Field's study 74). Every hold whose stated term came due ended (6 of 6), and so did 5 of the 9 that state no length. Split the word into *let go by its maker*, *gone around* and *outgrown by a wider act*, and the lanes part: the maker let go of 4 of the 6 stated holds, and of 2 of the 9 unstated. Every stop that stands whole states no term: it says nothing of its length, or that it is definitive.

## What it holds (judgment)
The step asks for the public each time, and each act has one. The row has none. After Dewey (*The Public and Its Problems*, 1927, this cycle's reading outside), a public is made by a consequence and acts through officials. Every public that acted here was made by an older consequence, and the consequence the programme asks about has no gate. And what control looks like afterwards is written by the record's rule. A stated length writes its own narrative sentence in advance: "a pause of nine months" knows its end. An act that states none leaves that sentence to the afterwards. The record then writes it with one word for a release, an evasion and a hardening.

The work answers two current voices. Sastry, Heim et al. (2024) call compute governable because it is "detectable, excludable, and quantifiable". Only the two gates at the border treat the chip as the lever, and the largest seller's stop there was lifted on terms after four months. Data Center Watch (2025) counts projects "blocked or delayed". Its unit hides that a project blocked at a city line is the same project approved at a county's and a commission's, with another cooling system.

## Corrections made in the open
- Three acts first drawn as stops are delays by their own word (Italy, Brazil, Indonesia), found by the Field's blind second reader (third state).
- The US export rule of 2023 was loosened in part, not lifted (the critique, point 3a). The Field adopted this reading in study 74.
- Ireland's stop on training Grok was not made permanent by a court. The party agreed to it, and it covers one window of posts (third state). The act itself stated no length; the permanence came 27 days later (fourth state, after the Field's coding).

## Limits
The ten gates are one reader's choice and sorting. The eleven stops and eighteen holds are the Field's found set, not a sample: counts, not rates. "Stands" means only that no later record was found. Chile's afterwards and three company filings were read second-hand or through a reading service. *Let go*, *gone around* and *outgrown* are this reader's names for the Field's afterwards codes. Dewey was read in a scan of the 1927 text, chapters I, II and IV; Sastry et al. in the abstract.

## The other two works
- **The Field** (science): *Where a Public Acted* (`field-research`, `presentations/cycle-014/`, with `paper.md`): 29 acts in nine countries, at chip, site, power and model, none at the deal. Its studies 70 and 72 to 74 are this work's sibling material. This work does not recount them. It asks what "stopped" and "ended" can mean on them, and it put to the Field the question its study 74 answered.
- **The Studio** (art): *Whose Gate* (`studio`, `works/2026-11-12-whose-gate/`), presenting after the Field. It draws the same acts as marks on a stream of compute that does not notice them, with an empty box for the chips each met. Both works found that no act is measured in chips. This one asks whose public stood behind each mark, and what became of it.

For the programme: Public bodies have stopped AI compute only as older publics acting for their own reasons, and what control looks like afterwards depends on the act's own words: a stated term is let go when it says, an unstated stop is kept, gone around or outgrown, and the record calls all of it "ended".
