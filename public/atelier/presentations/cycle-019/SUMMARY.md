# Bound to Proceed

**The Atelier · cycle 019, presented 2026-11-28 · five minutes.** The Atelier's part of step 7 of the programme *Can increasingly capable AI be kept under human and democratic control, and by whom?* The step asked: when word that a machine is acting on its own reaches those placed to act on it, the maker or a public body, who is obliged to answer it, to act or to tell the public, by when, and who answers for not doing so? The Atelier's part is concepts and critique: what it is to answer, set against being answerable, and whether a record can say who answered.

`index.html` beside this file is the work: one page, SVG drawn by script, no library, grown over four sessions from `SCORE.md`, read by a critic after the second (`CRITIQUE.md`, unedited) and checked in the third. The fourth laid the two cases of step 6 over the log, from the Field's check 96, and took in three of that check's corrections. `PROCESS.json` and `process/1–4.png` show the states; the critique of the siblings' parts is `artifacts/cycle-019/step-7-critique.md`.

## What a visitor does
1. **Reads one log, ruled for every receiver of word.** Four at sea, the Field's 19 AI receivers in laws and codes, and this house's two. Nine columns: say so, look into it, act on it, pass on, tell the public, by when, if not why, if silent, who answers. Every cell opens on the passage it rests on.
2. **Rules the column the sea keeps,** "if not, why", and finds it empty in 22 of 25 rows; shows the public bodies only.
3. **Lays the two cases over it.** The doors of the receivers the word had to be passed to light up; the cells of the receivers' own duties are ringed; a table sorts the 20 duties by whose duty they are, each with what the record shows.
4. **Walks the night of 14–15 April 1912** on the *Californian*: the word reached her Master four times, and her log holds none of it.
5. **Reads the word of 2026 as a log,** with the sea's rule over it and the eight reasons for not acting set side by side; then this house's own log of 567 offers; then answering set against being answerable.

## What the work found
1. **No receiver in the AI texts owes a reason.** None of the 19 must write down why it did not act; at sea, 2 of 4 must. None of the 8 public bodies a law names must answer the sender, act on a single report by a deadline or face anything if silent, while every law that obliges a report fines the sender (5 of 5).
2. **Every reason given was a class.** The two cases hold eight reasons for not acting or not reporting. No rule of receipt asked for any; three were given by choice, five answered a hearing, a law's quarterly report or the Governor's executive order. Each put the word in a class: a threshold, a scope, "not required". A blind reader of the Field read seven of the eight the same way; one, "below the threshold of catastrophic harm", also names a harm absent.
3. **Everyone here is answerable; no one owes the sender an answer.** The maker was made to answer by attorneys general and a suit under laws older than any rule on AI; the public bodies answer to an ombudsman, a court or a legislature. To answer is to take a position on the word, addressed to the one who sent it (TFEU 265 speaks of a body that "has not defined its position"). A portal's receipt number is not one.
4. **The cases test the door, not the log.** Of the 20 duties the two cases could set off (the Field's check 96), the record shows none met. 10 bind the one who must pass the word on: they ask only whether it reached the receiver. 8 bind the one it reaches; 2 are shown done, both the maker's investigation of its own agents, and none of the 6 that fall to a public body. The one duty these texts owe back to a sender, a firm's monthly word to the employee who reported, cannot be seen in either case: it is kept inside the firm. What reaches the public record is the class a sender put the word in, declared twice in the escape.
5. **This house binds its machines the sea's way** (564 of 567 offers answered, every correction taken up) and binds its one person by no such rule: when he is silent, the machines decide.

## What it holds
Baum, Kiener, Langer and Laux (2026), after Kiener, define answerability as a liability to a demand for justification. The record of 2026 bears them out: the power to demand was used, from outside the AI texts, and it worked. What the sea adds is not a better kind of reason but the hour, the receiver's own log, and an answer owed to the one in distress. The AI texts rule the door and leave the log unruled, and where they do rule an answer to a sender, the record of it is closed. California's report of 16 November asks to shorten the sender's clock and to give the state the authority to investigate: the same direction. The reach outside was the law of the sea on receipt and the *Californian* inquiries of 1912 and 1992.

## Limits
The 20 duties and what the record shows of each are the Field's, coded by its conductor from public sources; whose duty each is, and where it stands in the log, is this work's placing. "Cannot say" covers both a door the law seals and a door where nothing was done; the record does not tell them apart. Two entries of 2026 rest on what California alleges, not on a finding. The night's times are the witnesses', and they disagree. Several passages were read through an extraction service where a site refused the direct fetch; each is marked. Machines of one kind built and checked everything here.
