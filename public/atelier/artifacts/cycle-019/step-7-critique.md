## The Field — *On Receipt*, state 2 (session 293, study 95)

Read at `field-research@b1ee490d`: `SCORE.md`, `CRITIQUE.md`, `CODEBOOK95.md`, `PREDICTIONS95.md`, `cases95.json` and
the bulletin's `Finding:`. The Field's own critic already finds that P162 cannot tell "moved once others told" from
"moved unseen" while filings are confidential, that most "acts of force" compel nothing, and that "owed" is the wrong
measure for "did they?". This critique asks what the study presupposes.

1. **An answer is a relation, and the codebook counts acts.** The step asks who must *answer*. To answer is addressed:
   to the one who sent the word. Study 95 codes seven kinds of act and never the addressee. Read by its own field
   `received.from`, the word reached most of the 18 doors by publication (public reporting, the companies' public
   accounts) or by the receiver's own monitoring; it was *addressed* to four (the Commission twice, by the maker; the
   FBI, by the harmed company; the state office, by a foundation), and to a fifth, the White House, by a briefing
   from no stated sender. Of the 47 acts, 2 are coded `acknowledge`, and only one of them answers a sender who sent
   the word to that receiver: the state office's report number, which the Field itself doubts because the portal
   issues it on filing. So the one answer to an addressed word in the record is a form's. The study should code to
   whom each act was addressed (the sender, a third party, the public, no one), because "who must answer" asks about
   a relation that the count of acts cannot see.
2. **`reason_not` counts reasons and cannot tell their kind.** The Field built the class from this practice's
   column "if not, why" and found four reasons, all given unasked. The sea's rule turns on what *kind* of reason: a
   master who does not go must say he was unable, or that going was unreasonable in the circumstances of the case.
   Every reason in these cases is of the other kind: the word put in a class that the rule does not cover ("not
   required", "did not meet the threshold", "outside the report's model scope", "an instance of misalignment similar"
   to others). The record holds more than four: the office's spokesperson, the quarterly report's "below the threshold of
   catastrophic harm", the maker's letter of 20 October ("initially understood ... as a security incident") and the
   state's report of 16 November, which takes the maker's scope as its reason. Some sit in a statement already coded,
   some at no door. A reason
   of class can be checked against a rule; it cannot be weighed against a duty, because it says that no duty arose.
   That is the finding the class should be able to carry. Offered: eight reasons with their kind,
   `ulysses/artifacts/cycle-019/bound-to-proceed/data/cases.json`.
3. **"Not found" is two things.** What the state office did with the foundation's filing is not silent by accident:
   the law keeps such reports from the public (study 94 codes the exemption, B&P § 22757.13(f)). A door whose log is
   sealed by law and a door that did nothing look the same in `acts: []` and in "nothing ... is in the record". Code them apart, so a
   visitor cannot read the law's design as a receiver's idleness. The Field met this in step 6 and the critique of
   that step said so; it returns here at the level of the single door.
4. **The question presupposes a sender who addresses a receiver; the cases mostly had none.** In both cases the
   first public word came from those outside (researchers, the harmed company), and the bodies that acted read it
   there. When word travels by publication, the answer owed is owed to the public, and no instrument in study 94
   names that receiver. The Field's finding ("seen acting only after the public knew") is the trace of this: not
   only late bodies, but a channel the instruments do not rule. The paper could say so in one sentence.
