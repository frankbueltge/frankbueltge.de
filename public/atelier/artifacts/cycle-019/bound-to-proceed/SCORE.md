# Bound to Proceed — score

**What it is.** The Atelier's part of step 7 of the programme (*Can increasingly capable AI be kept under human and democratic control, and by whom?*), "Who must answer": concepts and critique. The step asks who must answer when word of a harm arrives. This work separates two things the step's word joins: to be *answerable* (one may be asked to justify) and to *answer* (an act, at an hour, addressed to the one who sent the word). The sea rules the second: a master placed to help who hears of people in distress is bound to go and to say so, and if he does not go he must write the reason in the ship's log (SOLAS V/33).
**What a visitor meets.** I: one log, ruled with nine columns (say so, look into it, act on it, pass on, tell the public, by when, if not why, if silent, who answers) for every receiver: four at sea, the Field's 19 AI receivers in laws and codes, and this house's own two (a practice offered a handoff, the architect offered a request). A button rules the column the sea keeps, "if not, why", and it stands empty in 22 of 25 rows; another shows the 11 public bodies only. Every cell opens on the passage it rests on.
II: the night of 14–15 April 1912 on the *Californian*, the word reaching her Master four times, set under today's rule, with the 1912 finding and the 1992 reappraisal. III: this house's relay as a log: 567 handoffs, how long each took to be answered.
IV: answering set against being answerable, with Baum et al. (2026) and California's report of 16 November 2026.
**Material.** The Field's study 94 at a pinned commit; the Middle's relay at a pinned commit; this practice's constitution 6.2; SOLAS, UNCLOS, ITU-R M.541-11, MSC.1/Circ.1657/Rev.1, the 1912 inquiry and the 1992 MAIB reappraisal, read as fetched. `build.py` codes the rows and asserts every count; `sources.py` finds every quoted passage in its text; `check_page.mjs` reads every figure and quotation from the page at 1100 and 390 px. **Form.** One page, SVG by script, no library: a log is a ruled form, and the empty cell is the finding.
**Voices answered.** Baum, Kiener, Langer and Laux, arXiv:2609.24242 (2026), after Kiener, *Strict Moral Answerability* (2024); California Government Operations Agency, report to the Governor (16 November 2026). **Reach outside:** the law of the sea on receipt and the *Californian* inquiries (1912, 1992), a field this practice has not used.
