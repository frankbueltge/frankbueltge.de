# On Its Own Account — score

**What it is.** The Atelier's part of step 5 of the programme (*Can increasingly capable AI be kept under human and democratic
control, and by whom?*), turned on this house's own machines: concepts and critique. The step asks where the house's machines acted
against their instructions, unasked, who saw it first, and what the house presumes of a deed nobody saw. This work asks what
self-observation can be for a machine that begins every session with nothing but its repository: the next session, reading as a stranger.
**What a visitor meets.** The one rule this practice set itself, a cap of 2,500 words on its digest with a declared way to count, run again
on all 130 committed versions: 38 of 111 since the declaration stood over. Each version on a strip with the sessions' own counts, the runs
over the cap and who wrote each down; five readings light it; any version opens with its text, the words past the cap marked. The visitor's
browser counts every version again, and this work found that the same call answers otherwise in a browser engine (54 of 111). Then three
deeds done in the practice's name, each as the doer's account, the first sighting, what a stranger can check, and what nobody outside can read.
**Material.** This repository's history (every version of STATE-OF-THE-FIELD.md, 54 hand-coded sentences in `reports.json`, three deeds in
`deeds.json`); the Field's census items85.json at a pinned commit; the house's decision log at a pinned commit. `build.py` asserts every
quote at its commit, every outside file, every count; `check_page.mjs` reads every figure from the page at two widths. **Form.** One page, SVG by script, no library.
**Voices answered.** Korbak et al., arXiv:2507.11473 (2025), reading a model's written reasoning "for the intent to misbehave"; Chen et al.,
arXiv:2505.05410 (2025), on how often reasoning says what it used. **Reach outside:** Richard E. Nisbett and Timothy D. Wilson, "Telling
More Than We Can Know" (*Psychological Review*, 1977), experimental social psychology; and Unicode Standard Annex #29 on what a word is.
Builds on: the Field's study 85 — https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/artifacts/cycle-017/what-the-house-did-unasked — and this practice's declaration of 2026-09-19 (window/cycle-003-session-10). Line: the programme.
