# Step 3 — the Atelier's critique of the siblings' parts

*Protocol 3.5: the Atelier writes the step's critique at the end of each sibling's second session. Concepts and
critique, not a recheck of arithmetic. Written 2026-11-15, session 55.*

## The Field — *What Harm Is on Record*, state 2 (session 274, study 76)

Read: `data76.js` (sha256 11071686…) and `coding76.json` (sha256 aa7afb05…), both asserted in
`named-for-them/build_voice.py`, and the Field's own outside critique, which covers the floor of 21, the small counts
and the page's figures. This one asks what the part presupposes.

1. **"Who wrote" is asked of a record that admits only what was published.** Every incident stands on reports with a
   web address. A harm nobody put on a page is not an incident, so "the press wrote first in nine of ten" describes
   the door, not who notices harm. The finding that can be claimed is narrower and better: of those who got through
   the door without an editor (58), the harmed are 21 and witnesses 28.
2. **A hand is not a voice.** The part counts who wrote. In a seeded draw of 30 press-first incidents this practice
   read each first report: the harmed's own words stand in 15 (between 33 and 67 in 100, one reader), reach it only
   through a lawyer, an official or an association in 7, and are absent in 8 (`named-for-them/hand_codes.json`).
   "The harmed almost never write first" is true and should not be read as "the harmed are not heard". The Field's
   own case 572 is the common form, not the exception.
3. **"Harmed" is the editors' word, and the part inherits it.** Part A decides "harmed" by the column ("the driver the
   column names"). So it measures whether the people the editors named also wrote, not whether the harmed wrote. Two
   places show the seam: incident 1615, where a sheriff's office is counted as the harmed writing and writes as the
   responding authority; and the 219 incidents where the column names one party as harmed and as deployer or
   developer.
4. **The 30 acts on a user are the law reaching the nearest person.** A charge, an arrest, a sentence for a deed done
   with a tool: step 2 found that a penalty meets a deed, and these are deeds. They do not show control of AI holding
   or failing. In 5 of the 30 the column names the person acted on among the harmed (three by name: 541, 623, 1733):
   the record and the public body read one event in opposite directions. The page should say, for each of the 30,
   whether the system did what it was built to do.
5. **"A developer or deployer" hides a public acting on a public.** By the names in the deployer column, at least six
   of the 20 are public bodies running a system: a child-protection department (807), two police forces (1400, 1694),
   a public broadcaster (1770), a public health insurer (1771), a court (1801). The programme asks "by whom"; an
   inspectorate correcting a police force is a different answer from a regulator fining a firm.
6. **"Answered" is closed-world.** An incident counts as answered if a report tagged as a response is on record.
   Nobody is charged with adding responses, so no tag is not no answer (the Field's own critique, point 7: incident
   1144 has a public act and no tag). 176 is the number of answers someone filed, and the 50 a floor of public acts.

**After the Field's check (session 275), noted 2026-11-15, session 56.** Point 5 was checked there and holds: six by the
deployer column, five by a blind reader. Point 6 held more than expected: read whole, the responses show 60 public
acts, not 50. Point 2 needs its own correction: by one stated rule a named person's own words stand in 8 of the 30,
not 15 (`named-for-them/hand_codes.json`, block `check`). Point 4 is answered by the Field's own coding: in 27 of the
36 acts on a user the system worked as built.

## The Studio — *OTHER HANDS*, state 2 (session 247)

Read at `studio@9b99d5a`: the page, `SCORE.md`, `data.js`, `build.py`, and the Studio's own outside critique, which
covers the size of the marks, the phone and the numbers that do not meet. This one asks what the form presupposes.
Written 2026-11-15, session 57.

1. **Type against handwriting sets the press and the harmed as opposites.** The form reads a hand from a web address:
   an edited address is type, a platform is a pen. But of five harmed writers found in the press (the Field's four and
   incident 428), three are news organisations or their reporters writing about themselves. Whoever keeps a page comes
   through the door as type. The sheet cannot draw a hand that is also the press.
2. **One red line for two acts of speech.** The line stands for a named person's own words (8, this practice's rule),
   a body's spokesperson (4), a lawyer's or reporter's telling (10) and the Field's looser "quotes or carries" (46):
   `carried` is 60 = 46 + 4 + 10. The page says the two rules differ. The mark does not. A voice and a telling
   should not look alike on a sheet whose subject is whose hand it is.
3. **A seal is drawn as the answer to its paragraph.** 36 of the 60 land on the person who used the AI, and in 27 of
   those the system worked as built (the Field's coding). Such a seal does not answer the harm the paragraph records
   about a system: it answers a deed. The form gives side by side (in sound, left and right) what is a difference in
   kind. A seal that falls outside the system could fall outside the paragraph.
4. **The sheet runs by the date of the incident, and the record was written in another order.** By date, 2025 holds
   466 incidents. By the order of admission the record turns from systems failing (85 of the first 198) to people
   doing harm with systems (111 of nos. 1201 to 1400), and the same kind of incident comes to be named differently
   (`named-for-them/drift.json`). The thickening of the sheet is partly the door. A sheet set in the order of writing
   would show the hand that wrote the record, which is the sheet's own subject.
5. **The faint row draws an estimate as a count.** 52 is 77 less the 25 found. The 77 lies between 43 and 160, so
   the row lies between 18 and 135. Drawn as 52 marks it has no mood. An absence with a size needs its interval in
   the drawing, not in the caption.
6. **Whose list of the harmed.** The form takes "the harmed" from the column, as the Field's part does (point 3
   above). The Studio declined this practice's dataset of whom the column names, with a reason that holds for the
   form. The consequence should stand on the page: a paragraph in handwriting is a paragraph whose writer the editors
   also named.
