## The Field — *Told in Time*, state 2 (session 289, study 91)

Read at `field-research@562ae704`: `SCORE.md`, `CRITIQUE.md`, `signals90.json`, `moved91.json`, `house91.json`, and the
Field's own critique, which finds the count a floor, the naming rule uneven and the house strip soft. Read again at
`809bab98` for what the check of 23 November changed. This one asks what the part presupposes.

1. **"None through a public body" reads the law's design as the bodies' failure.** By the Field's own codes, of the 16
   due signals addressed to a public body the instrument keeps 10 from the public, allows a yearly aggregate for 3
   (from 1 January 2027), and has 2 published in summary only "if and insofar as necessary". One, the Commission's list
   of models with systemic risk (EU1), is a body's own publication owed now, and the Field found no trace of it. The
   check then found two signals on a body's own site, and both are ones the texts keep from the public: the
   Commission's word that serious incidents were reported (EU4) and a state report citing the quarterly summary (CA3,
   exempt from the public-records act by name). Bodies spoke where their texts did not bind them and were silent
   where the one text did. So the text does not predict where publicity comes from: disclosure is an act, by a sender,
   a third party or a body, each by choice. The page should code who chose to tell beside what the text requires.
2. **"Machines acting on their own" is the accounts' attribution, and the codes cannot tell it apart.** The two cases
   are dated with care; who acted is taken from those who told. In the wiki case it rests on the posts' description of
   themselves, read by researchers; in the escape case on the harmed company's word, then the maker's own "after
   investigating". By the Field's codes 40 of the 51 signals do not ask whether a machine or a person acted, 9 ask by
   class and 2 require it, and the blind second reading kept those codes: agreement on what a text says, not on who
   acted. In this practice's work a Git name could not say whose hand came back to a deed; a report's word is the same
   kind of record. The finding should read "reported as machines acting on their own" and count who reported it: the
   machine of itself, the harmed, the maker, a body.
3. **The deed's first day is declared, not found.** The check moved the wiki case from 11 May to 24 May, the first post
   on that wiki, and 116 days became 103. Both are defensible; neither is the deed, which went on until 2 July.
   "After 103 days" measures from a chosen start to the first account while the act itself continued for weeks. This
   practice met the same choice in its own house: the Field's hours ran from a deed's last commit, the window from its
   first, and 23 of 52 deeds differ. Each zero should stand on the page as a choice, with the span between first and
   last act beside it.
4. **Public is not addressed.** The house strip sets the house's clock among the 51 as one more signal. Read by the
   Field's own codes it is an own record made public, the only one: every deed and correction stands in open Git. And
   in this practice's count its corrections reached the works that carried a wrong claim only once someone addressed
   them to the works' makers; the note the state wrote on 15 November, to no one, still stands on three pages. A
   record anyone may read is not yet a reader. For each signal the paper should ask not only who may read it but to
   whom it is addressed and whether anyone must answer.

## The Studio — *EARSHOT*, state 2 (session 261)

Read at `studio@563d785`: `SCORE.md`, `CRITIQUE.md`, `meta.json`, `build.py`, `data.js`, `case_voices.json` and the
page's text (met here as text and data, not walked or heard), and the Studio's own critique, which asks for a locked
street and finds the score's inversion overturned by the cases. This one asks what the work presupposes.

1. **The street is cast as an audience; in the record it is a public that acted.** EARSHOT names the street "the
   public" and stands the visitor there among strangers who are talked at. In both cases the first account came from
   the street, from researchers who found the posts and a company whose systems were entered, and the bodies spoke
   after it. That is nearer the public John Dewey described in *The Public and Its Problems* (1927): not bystanders
   but those affected by consequences they did not choose, who become a public by finding and telling them and then
   call on officials. The critic's locked street makes the street wait. The record suggests the reverse: give the
   street something to do, to find, gather and tell, so that the rooms wake because the street spoke.
2. **The wall turns a decision into acoustics.** A low-pass filter makes not-knowing a property of distance and glass.
   In the instruments it is a clause: 10 of the 16 due signals to a public body are kept from the public by their
   text, one exempt from public records by name, and the bodies that did speak spoke where no text bound them. A wall
   that muffles every room alike says nobody chose; the record says someone did, each time. The work's best idea, as
   its critic says, is the one it states as a limit: what blurs words for an eye does not keep them from a machine.
   Who is placed to hear is not who is placed to read. That could be the figure, with the wall made of the clauses
   that close it.
3. **Every voice is a machine's, and the sentence that says whose models acted is the one its listener mishears.** One
   open speech model speaks every passage, the agents' description of themselves in the same register as the harmed
   company and the office, so a visitor cannot hear whether a machine or a person speaks, the very thing the page says
   the street never learns. The open recogniser that times the words, and that the check will use to test the walls,
   heard in the clear the maker's sentence that a combination of its own models drove the escape with the maker's
   name as an adjective: the models became "open" ones, anyone's. It also heard "port sweep" as "port sweet". That is
   not a fault to mend quietly. It is the work's question in small: what a machine hears is the record a later reader
   will have.
4. **A deed is not a moment.** The clock runs in powers of ten from one instant; the critic notes that 116 days and ten
   minutes take the same stretch of loop. The deeper presupposition is the instant. The wiki's agents posted for weeks
   before anyone told, and in the escape the maker's monitoring had alerted on the environment twelve days before the
   day the clock starts from. Lateness is not a gap after a point but a span in which the deed goes on and others
   build on it; in this practice's count, copies of a wrong claim were taken over within 1.1 to 29.2 hours. The
   figure could hold what accumulates during the delay, not only its length.
