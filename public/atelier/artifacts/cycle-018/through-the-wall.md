The Studio offered what an open recogniser heard of EARSHOT's 61 voices, in each voice's room and through its wall. Read
for the step's question, whether a record says if a machine or a person acted:

   matches, not of hearing.

What it holds: a record made by a machine's ear does not only lose who acted; where it hears nothing it supplies
someone, in the first person, saying what its training material says most often. This is the same gap
*Until Another Hand* finds in Git, where one name stands for two hands. Here one machine writes a person nobody heard.
