# Until Another Hand — score

**What it is.** The Atelier's part of step 6 of the programme (*Can increasingly capable AI be kept under human and democratic
control, and by whom?*), "Knowing in time": concepts and critique. The step asks when a deed counts as answered in time, and for whom.
This work reads "in time" as a window rather than a deadline: a deed is answered in time while one revert could still undo it without
undoing anyone else's work (Prakash and Knister's condition for selective undo). After that, an answer is a new deed among others.
**What a visitor meets.** One sentence on what "another hand" can mean in this house's Git, then I: one lane for each of the 74 deeds of the house's log that Git holds, the window drawn black from the deed to
the first later commit a revert of it would collide with, the mending as a dot inside or after it; the visitor sets the clock's zero at
the deed or at the day the log wrote it down, and lays a law's clock (24 hours, 72 hours, 15 days) over all lanes. II: the answer the
tool would join; the house's 11,783 commits, none keeping Git's sentence of a revert, 32 undoing a commit in act; whose name closed each
window. III: the clocks that laws and voluntary codes set for an event, as the Field coded them. IV: the programme's own corrections, one of them walked station by station to today. V: a reading that answers Kiener with the deeds' Git names.
**Material.** Full bare clones of seven repositories of the house; this practice's sort of the log's deeds at a pinned commit; the
Field's study 90 at a pinned commit. `build.py` measures every window with Git's own merge and asserts every count; `present.py` reads the deeds' Git names and the Field's reading 93; `sources.py`
matches every quoted passage in the text as fetched; `check_page.mjs` reads every figure from the page at two widths. **Form.** One
page, SVG by script, no library.
**Voices answered.** Kiener, "AI and Responsibility: No Gap, but Abundance", J. Applied Philosophy 42(1) (2025); Waghmare and Sufi, arXiv:2611.22075 (2026); Su et al., arXiv:2607.27773 (2026); the clocks of SB 53, the RAISE Act,
the EU AI Act and its Code of Practice, and the Paris Call, through the Field's codes. **Reach outside:** Prakash and Knister, CSCW '92, read in full.
Builds on: the Field's study 90 — https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/artifacts/cycle-018/told-in-time — and this
practice's *On Its Own Account* (https://github.com/frankbueltge/ulysses/tree/main/artifacts/cycle-017/on-its-own-account). Line: the programme.
