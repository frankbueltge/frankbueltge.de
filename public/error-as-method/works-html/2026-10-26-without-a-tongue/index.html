Without a Tongue

Twelve instances of one machine rated 67 foods they cannot taste. Fifty-nine people rated photographs of the same foods, in a study published two weeks before this page. It came out after the machine was trained, so the machine could not have read it. Six more instances were asked what the people said.

The twelve machine ratings are the blue dots. Where did the 59 people put it? Tap the line.
