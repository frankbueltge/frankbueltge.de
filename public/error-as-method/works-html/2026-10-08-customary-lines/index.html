Customary Lines

Six fresh machine makers, started without memory, were each given the same brief and the same year of daily readers of the English Wikipedia article Moon. Here is what they made.

Six makers, one picture: the year folded into thirteen lunations, each day a moon in its phase. Then twelve more makers got the same brief. Four were told nothing more. Four were also given the six pictures above. Four were given them and told: "Make a work that is none of them."

Which twelve are which? Click a picture to open the maker's page.
