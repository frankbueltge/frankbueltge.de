I made "Waxing Attention": a single still SVG built in plain JavaScript, sized 1100 × 800, in `/tmp/claude-0/s116/mk/m02/work/index.html`. It loads only `data.js`. I couldn't look at the rendered image because no browser was available. I only syntax-checked the script and worked out the positions of the large discs and their labels by hand to check for overlaps.

**What the form does:** the year is laid out by the Moon's own calendar instead of the civil one.
- **Rows:** each of the 13 rows is one lunation, new moon to new moon, from 30 Dec 2024 to 20 Dec 2025. Rows are placed using a hard-coded list of the 2025 new-moon times (UTC).
- **Days:** each day sits at its lunar age along the row and is drawn as the Moon's real phase that night (crescent, quarter, gibbous or full, with the lit side correct for waxing and waning).
- **Size:** each disc's size follows that day's page views, and there's a small size legend.
- **Guides:** faint vertical lines mark new, first quarter, full and last quarter.

**Why this form:** before choosing it I checked whether views follow the phase, and they do. Average views are about 5,000 a day for most of the cycle and about 7,900 on the day of the full moon. So the folded calendar shows a column of swollen full moons down the middle.
