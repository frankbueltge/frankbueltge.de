I made "Reading by Moonlight": a still canvas image that shows each day of 2025 as a small moon. I wrote `index.html` and `NOTE.md` in `/tmp/claude-0/s116/mk/m01/work`. I rendered the page once at 1100 × 800 in headless Chromium (screenshot kept in my scratchpad, not in the folder), and it drew with no errors.

**How the form works:**
- **Size:** each disc's area is that day's page views (3,777 to 17,284).
- **Light:** each disc is lit the way the Moon was at noon UTC that day.
- **Rows:** the days are not grouped by calendar month. Each row runs from one new moon to the next, which gives 13 rows from 30 Dec 2024 to 20 Dec 2025. Across each row the days sit by the Moon's age, so every full moon falls in one central column.
- **Text on the image:** guide columns marked new, first quarter, full and last quarter; the date of the new moon at the start of each row; a size key; and a caption with the source (Wikimedia pageviews, CC0).
- **Labels:** the four highest days are labelled with date and count: 14 Mar, 20 Jul, 7 Sep and 7 Oct.

The phases are computed in the page from one new moon (29 Jan 2025 12:36 UTC) and an average month length. Real new and full moons can drift from that by up to several hours, which is small at this scale.

**What the image shows:** the discs tend to swell around full moon. Three of the four biggest days sit in the full-moon column, and I identify them in the note as 14 Mar (total lunar eclipse), 7 Sep (total lunar eclipse) and 7 Oct (supermoon). Those three events, and 20 Jul being the Apollo 11 anniversary, come from my own knowledge; the data only gives dates and counts. 20 Jul is the one big day away from full moon, falling as a waning crescent. The note says it was "more likely" about the Moon's history, but that is my guess, not something the data shows.

**`NOTE.md`** has the title as a heading and one paragraph on what the form does and why I chose it: the Moon has its own monthly cycle, so the question is whether people look it up when it is in the sky.

I wrote only those two files and read nothing outside the folder except my own scratchpad.
