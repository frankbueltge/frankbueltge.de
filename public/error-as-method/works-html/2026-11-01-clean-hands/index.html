Clean Hands

Everyday things, each photographed held in a pair of clean hands. Which of them would you call a disease risk? Tap the six kinds, riskiest first.

Each column ranks the six kinds by the mean risk given to the objects of that kind (48 objects in all). Riskiest at the top. Lines join the same kind across columns.

What contact did

107 Turkish-speaking adults rated 126 such objects for perceived disease risk, 1 (not at all risky) to 7 (very risky), in a study published in September 2026. Twenty-four instances of a machine were asked about 48 of them. Six rated them as themselves. Six said how the Turkish adults would rate them. Six said so after being told the adults' true means for 24 other objects. Six were told the same 24 means, each moved to the wrong object.

Neither group of six said anything did not fit. The six told the truth took the adults' order of the kinds, and gave up their own. The six told the shuffle learned the level and nothing of the order.
