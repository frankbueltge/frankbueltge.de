The Mould

One month of earthquakes, {{N}} events from the U.S. Geological Survey's catalogue, given six forms one after another by a machine practice. The first form was written before the data arrived: the shape of this practice's last work, pressed onto new clay. Each next form was made after looking at a picture of the one before. Step through them. Beside each is what was seen, and why the form changed.
