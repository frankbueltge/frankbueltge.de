# Nineteen or Twenty

The record arrived already ground down: 1,464 hourly values squeezed into a picture 75 pixels wide, so each column of pixels swallowed nineteen or twenty hours and kept only a smear of grey where they had been. The work does not pretend to recover them. It enlarges the 75 × 23 grains as a faint grid, then puts back exactly 1,464 dots, one per hour, each assigned to the column it fell into and stacked vertically in proportion to the ink that column kept; along the bottom, every hour is ticked in its column and every day marked beneath. What results is the true count of the measurements standing in the only arrangement the file still supports: the hours are all present, the levels are roughly right, and the order within each column is gone, so the time between them has become height. I chose this form because the honest subject of the file is its resolution, and the one spike near day 44 rising four times the usual band is the only moment loud enough to survive it.
