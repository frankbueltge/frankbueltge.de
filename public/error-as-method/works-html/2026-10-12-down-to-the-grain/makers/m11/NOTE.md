# Somewhere in the column

The file holds 1,464 hourly values but only 114 pixels, so no hour's value survives: each pixel column averages about 77 hours and each row a sixth of the range. The work draws every hour anyway, as its own hairline, but instead of a dot at a height it gives each hour a line spanning every band its pixel column says was inked, as dark as that pixel is dark. The result is a field of 1,464 lines that keeps the count of the record and admits the loss of its values: a broad, steady band through the lower two-thirds of the range for all sixty-one days, and one place, around days 42 to 48, where the record climbs to the top of the picture. I chose this because the only truthful thing to draw for an hour here is the range it could have held, and because drawing the hours, rather than enlarging the pixels, keeps the time the record was made of in view.
