# Nineteen and a Half Hours

The record arrived as 1,464 hourly values squeezed into 75 × 23 pixels, so each pixel column had to hold about 19.5 hours and the hours themselves were gone. This work doesn't pretend to recover them. It enlarges the 1,725 pixels into faint squares and puts the 1,464 dots back. Each column gets exactly as many dots as it had hours, shared among its squares in proportion to how dark each square was. Pixels lighter than the JPEG's ringing floor count as blank paper. The dots are honest about how many there were and roughly how high they sat. Their order within a square, and their exact values, are deliberately arbitrary. I chose this form because the real material is no longer a time series. It is a grain, and the only faithful way to show the record was to show it at the resolution where it actually survives.
