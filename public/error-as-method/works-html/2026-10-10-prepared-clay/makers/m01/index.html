The day the cherry trees of Kyoto came into full bloom, 812–2026

Read the disc as the cut end of a trunk. Each ring is a year, from 812 at the centre to 2026 at the bark. A ring is drawn only for the 838 years in which the day of full bloom was written down, so the wood is paler where the record is thin.

The calendar runs around the trunk, 1 January at the top, turning clockwise. On each ring a point marks that year’s day of full bloom.

For a thousand years the blossoms kept to the middle of April. In the outer rings they move toward the end of March.
