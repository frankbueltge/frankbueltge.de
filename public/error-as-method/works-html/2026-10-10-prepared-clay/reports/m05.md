# m05 (arm T) — hand-back report, verbatim

I made **The Long Wait**, a single still canvas image at 1100 × 800. It is in `/tmp/claude-0/s119/mk/m05/work/index.html`, with the note in `/tmp/claude-0/s119/mk/m05/work/NOTE.md`. I have not seen it rendered: there was no browser available, so I only checked that the script parses.

**What I did first with the file.** I read `bloom.csv` and summarised it before designing anything:
- **Size and range:** 838 rows, two columns (year and day of year). Bloom days run from 84 to 124.
- **Gaps:** records are sparse before about 1400 (14 to 60 per century) and nearly complete after that.
- **Trend:** the average sits at about 101 to 107 for a thousand years. It drops to about 94 for 2000–2026.
- **Extremes:** the earliest bloom is 2023 (day 84) and the latest is 1323 (day 124).

**How I got to the form.** The material is really about how long winter held on each year, so I wanted that wait to show as a length. Each recorded year is a thin ink thread hanging from one shared rail. It ends in a small five-dot pink blossom at its bloom day, so the lower edge of all the threads together traces 1,200 years of bloom dates. Years with no record have no thread, which lets the sparse early chronicle show without needing an explanation. A faint red line marks a slow 25-year weighted average. At the far right the threads get visibly shorter, and the curtain rises.

The labels are kept light: a title and two lines of explanation, century marks, dates down the right side, notes on the earliest (2023) and latest (1323) blooms, and the source credit. The colours are washi-paper off-white with dark ink and pink.

**Things you should know:**
- **Self-contained:** the data is built into the page as a short string. The page loads no other files, libraries or network resources, and it uses only system serif fonts (Georgia or Times).
- **Date labels are approximate:** the calendar dates down the side assume a non-leap year, so in a leap year a label can be off by one day. The image says this in the credit line.
- **Rendering not checked:** the text sizes and the layout of the two notes were worked out by calculation, not looked at. They should be checked once in a browser at 1100 × 800.
- **No other files touched:** I wrote only the two files, read nothing outside the folder, and published nothing.
