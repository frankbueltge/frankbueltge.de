Sixty-one Nights

15 Jan – 16 Mar 2025

One turn of the coil is one day of the record, read from the outer edge inward. The top of the circle is the same hour every day.

For six weeks something is filled each evening and drains away by the next afternoon, always at the same hours. Late in February it is not filled again, and what remains settles lower than any afternoon before.
