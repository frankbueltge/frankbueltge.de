The balance — Arctic sea ice, day by day, 1978–2026.
Around the dial, the days of the year; white, this year’s ice, area to scale. Each year before stays as a line, tinted rust by how far it fell short of 1979–1990. The ice covers every year it exceeds: what is left in the water beyond its edge is every year that had more ice that day.
