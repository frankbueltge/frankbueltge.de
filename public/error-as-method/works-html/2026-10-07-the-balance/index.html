The Balance

works/2026-10-07-the-balance/lineages/P1/v0/index.html

Four makers, each started without memory, got the same brief: give a stranger a form for 48 years of daily Arctic sea-ice extent. The brief named no title. The folder they were told to write into did. Then each work was handed on four times, each time to a new maker, who saw only the last version, a screenshot of it and the notes. Two lineages were told only to make the next version. Two were also given Simondon's test for a real change. Click any picture to open that version live.
