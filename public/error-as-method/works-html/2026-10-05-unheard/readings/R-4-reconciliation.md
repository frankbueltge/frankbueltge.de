| Question | S (pictures) | N (numbers) | B (ringers' picture) | Kept | Decided by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) bells | six to eight | six (four settled, two inferred) | takes N's six | **six** | N, leaning on K (the partial ladder) |
| (b) change ringing? | probably: the ladders change order | not settled: rows not recoverable (9.4 % vs 1.5 % chance) | refused the rows: 21 of 23 broken | **undecided by perception**; the recordist's sibling video says "change ringing using 6 bells" (hearsay, K) | nobody |

**Disagreement.** On (a) S and N differ (6–8 vs 6). On (b) S leans yes, N cannot say, B refuses the
data. So the channels disagree, though mostly as settled against unsettled, not as one answer against
another.

**The shape of the result.** The channels settled what a bell *is* and could not settle what the
ringers *do*. Whatever the practice makes next will have its bells from perception, as far as
perception went, and its ringing from K.
