# i1, looked at (S picture, N text, B picture) — note committed before i2 exists

**What I saw.**
- S: against R's picture of the same window, mine is clean and dark above 2500 Hz, where R has
  bright lines (about 2770, 3090, 3600, 3830 Hz). My lines sustain without a break; R's switch on and
  off. My waveform is one continuous wash; R's is bursts and dips. R has vertical broadband
  striations at the blows; mine has soft blooms.
- N: the blow-scale and pull-scale periodicities of my ringing come out with autocorrelation 0.6–0.7
  (1.20 s, 2.60 s = 13 blows of 0.2 s: one whole pull with its gap). R's were all below 0.12.
- B, and the finding of this look: **the detector, run on my own ringing, whose rows are perfect by
  construction, recovers a full row in 86 of 339 windows (25 %), and the blue line is grey almost
  everywhere.** So B's refusal of R in phase 1 was a verdict on the instrument, as I suspected. The
  ringers' translation cannot be reached from sound through my ears-by-numbers, even on sound I made.
