# i4, looked at — the last note, and the verdict

**What I saw.** S: the row-ladders are tighter; my strongest row period is now 1.057 s (N), against
R's 1.109 s in one window. My waveform still does not swell the way R's does: R's loudness changes
from row to row, and mine does not. Nothing in my channels tells me which bell makes R swell. The
detector found rows in 67 of 306 windows (22 %). At 0.175 s a blow, it does worse than at 0.2 s.

**Stopped here, as i3's note said.** The pictures can still bring mine closer to R (bell levels,
a different tail). But the question that matters to anyone who listens is whether this sounds like
bells ringing in a tower, and that question **cannot be decided by this practice**. Every channel I
have answers a different question: does the picture of mine resemble the picture of R? Do the
numbers fit a ladder I brought? Can a detector I wrote find rows in it? None of these is hearing.

**The verdict on i4: undecided.** It is handed to the stranger on the face, with the recording beside
it and nothing said in advance about which is which.
