Wendell's Rain Gauge 
Rainfall readings from my place 4 mi. north of Hooper, Nebraska
Every day since April 1971
*** Welcome!! 1999 is all typed in now. Thank You For Stopping By ***

Hello. My name is Wendell Haack. I carried rural mail out of the Hooper post office for 31 years and retired in 1996. Since April of 1971 I have read the rain gauge on the fence post by our garden every morning at 7 AM and wrote it down in a ledger.  That is going on 29 years and I have missed 41 days, mostly when we went to see our daughter in Colorado Springs and the neighbor forgot.

My grandson Travis put this page up for me in 1998 so the numbers would be some place besides the kitchen drawer.  I am still learning how to work it so if something is wrong let me know.

If you keep a rain gauge too I would like to hear what you got.  Especially if you are west of here, because that is where it comes from.

Readings are in inches.  T = trace (less than .01).  Snow is melted down on the stove and counted as water.

(The picture is the gauge, June 1998.  It is the plastic kind from Farm King.  I had a metal one before that until the hail got it.)

Things I wrote in the margin

May 8 1999 -- 2.35 in. between supper and midnight.  Hail the size of peas for about 10 min.  Lost all the tomato starts and had to buy more at the co-op.  Gauge ran over and I had to measure the overflow can.  Biggest day since July 1993.

Fall 1999 -- Dry.  November .21 is the driest November in my book since 1976.  Beans came out good anyway, Dale got 52 on the south quarter.

Jan. 1 2000 -- Y2K came and went.  The rain gauge does not have a computer in it.  0.00
