on Lake Winnebago, Fond du Lac, Wisconsin

Hello and welcome to my home page. My name is Gordy Pfaff and I carried the mail in Fond du Lac for 31 years until I retired in 1995. Now I have time to fish and my grandson Tyler says I have to have a web page so here it is. He showed me how to do it with the Netscape but I am doing all the typing myself so please excuse the mistakes.

That is my shanty in the picture. It is #14 and it is red because my wife Lorraine said if I was going to sit out there all day she wanted to be able to see me from the shore with the binoculars. I built it in 1984 out of plywood and it has a little wood stove and two holes and a bench. It has been on the lake every winter since except 1998 when the ice never got good. (What a lousy winter that was.)

Ice Report

I will try to put up what the ice is doing off Lakeside Park and Supple's Marsh. THIS IS NOT OFFICIAL. Always check with the bait shops and the clubs before you go out. Nobody should drive on the lake because Gordy said it was OK.
  • Jan 9 1999 - 14 to 16 inches off the park. Cars OK, trucks are going out on the marked roads. Pressure crack by the Calumet side is bad, stay on the plowed road.
  • Jan 2 1999 - 10 inches. Lots of shanties going out now.
  • Dec 26 1998 - Open water still in the middle. Stay off!!

Sturgeon Spearing

Spearing season opens the second Saturday in February. I have speared 3 sturgeon in 22 years of trying. The biggest was 61 inches and 74 pounds in 1991 and I got my name in the Reporter. If you never heard of this, you sit in a dark shanty over a big hole cut in the ice and look down and wait for a sturgeon to come by. Some years you see nothing for the whole season. My friend Arnie says it is like watching TV with the set turned off. He is right but I keep going.
