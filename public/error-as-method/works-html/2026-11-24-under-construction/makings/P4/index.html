THE JUNIATA & WESTERN
The J&W is a freelanced HO railroad set in the Allegheny mountains around 1952, the last year before the diesels took over. It is 11 ft by 19 ft and fills most of the basement except for the furnace and Bev's washer and dryer. (Bev says the trains are not allowed to go in the laundry room. I am working on her.)


Looking east towards "Gallitzin" tower. Picture is fuzzy, sorry, Kevin's camera.

Facts about the J&W:

  • Scale: HO (1:87)
  • Size: 11' x 19' around the walls
  • Mainline run: about 96 feet
  • Track: Atlas code 100 flex, hand laid in the yard
  • Control: MRC power packs, 2 cabs, block wiring (I wired it myself, of course - 34 years of signals!)
  • Locomotives: 9 steam, 2 diesels (don't tell anybody)
  • Started: November 1983
  • Finished: NEVER, ha ha
I have working block signals on the main line that I built from scratch using parts from Radio Shack. They are the position-light type just like the real PRR. Nobody else in our club has those.

MY RAILROAD CAREER
1963 - Hired by Pennsylvania R.R. as signal helper, Pittsburgh Division
1966 - Signal maintainer, Horseshoe Curve to Gallitzin
1968 - Penn Central (we don't talk about that)
1976 - Conrail
1997 - Retired. Got a watch and a cake.

I have probably climbed every signal mast between Altoona and Johnstown. In the blizzard of 1993 I was out for 31 hours straight. My father Stanley worked in the Juniata Shops for 40 years so you could say it runs in the family.
