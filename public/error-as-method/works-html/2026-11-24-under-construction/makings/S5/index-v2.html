Lorraine's Hummingbird Page

"The Hummers are Back!!"


My feeder out the kitchen window. Thats a male on the right, you can sort of see him.		 Hi and welcome to my page!! My name is Lorraine and I live in Pleasant Prairie Wisconsin, just outside of Kenosha. My son Mike set me up on here so this is my first web page ever, so be nice.

Every spring since 1991 I have wrote down the day the first Ruby-throated hummingbird comes to my feeder. I keep it in a spiral notebook on top of the fridge. My husband Ed says I am nuts but he is the one who calls me at work when he sees one first!!

So I thought I would put my dates on here and maybe other people would send me there dates too and we could see when they come up through the different states.

My Nectar Recipe

  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 4 cups water
  • Boil it a couple minutes and let it cool

DO NOT put red food coloring in it, they dont need it and its not good for them. The feeder is red already. Change it every 3 or 4 days when its hot out or it gets cloudy and moldy. I keep extra in the fridge in a old milk jug.
