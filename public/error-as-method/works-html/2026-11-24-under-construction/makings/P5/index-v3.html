Hello and welcome to my page! My name is Ruth Ann Mabry and I live in Lubbock, Texas . I am 61 years young and I retired in 1996 after 22 years as cafeteria manager at Dunbar Middle School. (If you went to Dunbar and remember the cinnamon rolls on Fridays, THAT WAS US. Please sign my guestbook!)

My grandson Tyler (age 14) set this page up for me on GeoCities and showed me how to do it so I am doing this all by myself now. So if something looks funny that is why. I am learning HTML a little at a time. Tyler says I am the only grandma at his school with a web page. I said that is because the other grandmas have more sense. Ha!

I have been collecting salt and pepper shakers since 1971 when my husband Dale bought me a pair of little pink pigs at a gas station in Amarillo on the way to see his mother. Now I have 347 pairs. Dale built me two curio cabinets out in the garage and I need a third one. Dale passed away in 1994 so I guess I will have to get my son-in-law Mike to do it. (Mike if you are reading this, HINT HINT!!)
