Welcome to Dale's Basement Railroad!!
The Horseshoe Curve in HO Scale ... more or less

Hello and welcome. My name is Dale Kowalczyk and I live in Altoona, Pennsylvania. I worked 31 years for the railroad, first the Pennsy and then Penn Central and then Conrail, mostly as a signal maintainer on the Middle Division. I retired in April 1997 and my wife Bernadette said I better find something to do or she was going to find something for me. So I started building the Horseshoe Curve in the basement.

My grandson Kyle (he is 14) set up this page for me at Christmas and showed me how to use the editor. I am still learning so please excuse the mess. I will be adding more as I go.

About the Real Curve

For those who don't know, the Horseshoe Curve is about 5 miles west of Altoona. The Pennsylvania Railroad built it in 1854 to get the trains up the Allegheny Mountain. It is 2375 feet long and the curve is 220 degrees. The grade is about 1.8 percent going west. Every train going over the mountain still goes around it. I have stood there more times than I can count, and worked the signals there for a good many years. There is a park there now with a funicular (incline) and you can go up and watch the trains. If you are ever in Altoona go see it, you will not be sorry.
