Hello and thank you for stopping by! My name is Gary Lindqvist and this is my very first Home Page on the World Wide Web. I am 52 years old and I have carried the mail on Rural Route 3 out of the Mora Post Office for 24 years this coming April. I took the "Internet For Beginners" class at the Mora Public Library in October (thank you Mrs. Ostlund!!) and this is my homework that never ended. Ha ha.

About Me

I was born in Cambridge MN and grew up on Hwy 65. I married my wife Jan in 1971 and we have one daughter Kristin who is a sophomore at UMD in Duluth studying to be a Physical Therapist. (Hi Kris!! Call your mother.) Jan works at the Kanabec County Courthouse in the Recorder's office. We are members of Trinity Lutheran. I like ice fishing, the Twins even when they are bad, which is lately, and fixing things that probably should be thrown out.

I bought our computer (a Gateway 2000 with a 233 MHz Pentium and 32 megs of ram) at the Best Buy in Coon Rapids last Christmas mostly so Kris could e-mail us from school. Now I am the one on it every night and Jan says I have a new girlfriend named Netscape.

Rural Route 3 By The Numbers

Route length: 118 miles (it was 96 until they combined us with part of Route 5 in 1993)
Boxes: 512
Gravel roads: about 60 miles of it
Vehicles worn out: 6 (current one is a '94 Jeep Cherokee, right hand drive conversion, 187,000 miles)
Times stuck in the ditch: I am not going to say. Jan knows.
Dog bites: 2 (both from the same dog, I won't name names, but he knows who he is)
Days missed for weather since 1975: 1 (Halloween Blizzard 1991, and that was the Postmaster's call not mine)

To my patrons on Route 3 who found this page from the little card I put in your box: Hello! Please remember to clear the snow away from your box after the plow goes by. I can't reach it from the Jeep if there is a snowbank in front of it and then you don't get your mail and you call the Post Office and Darlene gives me a hard time. Thank you!!

Duchess

Duchess is our black Lab. She is 11 and has a gray chin now but she still thinks she is a puppy when the UPS man comes. She rode the route with me for her first 6 years until the Postmaster said that was "not in the regulations." She knows the sound of the Jeep from a mile off.

I would put a picture of her here but we don't have a scanner. Jan's sister Bonnie has one so as soon as we are over there for Thanksgiving I will put some real pictures up. For now there is a picture of her I drew below.

The Ice House

I drew this in the Paint program. It took me most of a Sunday. I'm no artist but you get the idea!

I built the ice house in 1989 out of plywood and 2x4s in the garage. It is 6 by 8, has 4 holes, a Mr. Heater, a bench and a radio that gets WCCO and that's about it. We go out on Knife Lake as soon as there is 8 inches of good ice, usually around the middle of December.

Best day: 23 crappies and a 6 lb. walleye, Feb. 1996. I have witnesses.

In Memory of My Dad

Arvid Lindqvist 1919 - 1996. Dad carried Route 3 before me, from 1948 until he retired in 1975, and I took it over the next spring. He did it with a Model A for the first two winters and he never let me forget it. People on the route still stop me and tell me something he did for them, like the time he pulled the Hendricksons' boy out of the ditch in 1962 with the mail car. I miss you Dad.
