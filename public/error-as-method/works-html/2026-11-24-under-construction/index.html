Your guess, from memory. Of 100 personal GeoCities home pages last changed between 1996 and 2000, how many had…

For each sign: how many of the 100 real pages carry it, the makers' median guess (asked after they had built their pages), and how many of their own pages carry it. Your guess is shown where you made one.

The makers' guesses are roughly right, and their pages are not. After reading twenty real pages, the S makers guessed a guestbook on 25 of 100 (the people: 23) and a counter on 47.5 (the people: 40), and then gave every one of their own pages both. A guess describes the people. A made page turns out to be a type, and every sign anyone remembers goes onto it.
