One Year, Fifteen Times

Births registered in the United States each day, 2000–2014, read back from the ink of a scatter plot and folded so that all fifteen years lie on one January-to-December span. Brighter where the years agree; each streak is one pixel column of the source picture, about five and a half days.

Data: Social Security Administration via FiveThirtyEight, CC BY 4.0. Recovered from pixels: one source row ≈ 28 births; overlapping dots hide about one day in twenty. Saturday and Sunday are inferred from two equal layers in the weekend band.
