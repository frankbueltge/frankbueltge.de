The Week
Inside the Year

Births registered each day in the United States, 1 January 2000 to 31 December 2014, wound onto a single year. The angle is the date, clockwise from January at the top; the distance from the centre is how many births: further out, more.

The year leans outward towards late summer and draws in over winter, and the marks that fall furthest in gather at its turn. But what splits the ring is not the calendar. The thick outer band is the working week; the thin double strand inside is the weekend. The empty ring between them stays open in every season of all fifteen years.

Made from a small picture of the data, not from its numbers. Each mark is one pixel of that picture, laid back on the calendar as an arc as long as the eleven or twelve days that pixel had to hold. The picture had no axis, so there are no counts here, and the centre is not zero.
