# Born on a Weekday

Every one of the 5,479 days is a single dot, placed across the page by its date in the calendar year and up the page by the number of births registered that day, with all fifteen years laid on top of one another. I chose this overlay because the strongest thing in the file was not a trend over time but a split: births fall into two separate bands, Monday to Friday and Saturday and Sunday, with a gap between them that ordinary variation almost never crosses. That gap shows how much of birth is now scheduled. Once the two bands are visible, the dates that leave their band stand out on their own, so I marked only those: holidays (New Year, the Fourth of July, Thanksgiving, Christmas) that drop weekday births to weekend levels, the repeating dates such as 9.9.09 and 12.12.12 that rise above every ordinary day, and 6.6.06, which sank.
