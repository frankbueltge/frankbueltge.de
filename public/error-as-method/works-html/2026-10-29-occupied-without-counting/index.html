On each sentence's line from 1 to 7: how six instances rated it themselves (blue), where six said the people stood when they answered on the steps (green squares), and on the line from 0 to 100 (green rings). The orange bars, when shown, are the 16 to 35 people's own ratings.

Where would you put one?
