# Reading 3 — grid24 (carried from *Before the Verdict*)

Looked at: all 24 variants as two contact sheets, then V07 and V19 at full size. Written before
`home.py` exists.

Translation: one "day" of the instrument = one minute of grid. A strip row (its "year") = 365 or 366
minutes ≈ 6.1 h, so a day of grid is ~3.93 rows. A decades row = 3,653–3,660 minutes ≈ 2.54 days.
"Seasonal" removes a centred 365-minute mean; "tidal" removes a centred 31-minute mean. The first
row starts part-way, at the instrument's day-of-year 212 (1 August), which is why every strip and
decades picture begins with a short top row. That is the instrument's calendar and is not counted.
Neither is the vertical seam at twelve o'clock in every spiral, which is the instrument's new year.

- **G1 G.** *The strips are crossed by diagonal bands (V06–V09), and the bands vanish once the
  31-minute mean is removed (V10–V11).* A pattern that repeats each day, falling on rows 6.1 h long,
  must shear into diagonals, since a day is not a whole number of rows. The pattern lives at periods longer
  than half an hour. That is K4 (a daily pattern), seen through the wrong calendar. *Check:* the mean
  deviation by hour of day (UTC) ranges over more than 10 mHz.
- **G2 G.** *With the 31-minute mean removed (V22, V23), each 2.5-day row wiggles almost regularly,
  roughly a hundred times.* That is a period near half an hour, the same thing the ear heard as a comb (E1),
  reached here by another instrument. *Check:* as E1.
- **G3 H.** *Plateaus.* With the rank scale (V19, V21), the line sits flat at the top or the bottom for
  stretches of an hour or more. I judge this to be the rank scale, which presses the extreme few
  percent into the top or bottom edge. *Check:* the minute means stay above their own 95th percentile
  for ≥ 60 consecutive minutes on at least 5 occasions in the month. If they do, the plateaus are the
  grid (G). Otherwise H.
- **G4 H.** *The inner rings of the rank spirals (V13, V15, V17) are more saturated than the outer
  ones, as if early August swung more.* I judge this to be the spiral's geometry: the inner rings
  crowd the same number of dots into less space. *Check:* the standard deviation of the minute means
  in days 1–8 is within 0.85–1.18× that in days 24–31. If so, H. Otherwise the grid did swing more early on.
