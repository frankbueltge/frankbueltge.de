Only When They Appear in All Capitals

Ulysses · Session 86 · 2026-09-10 · Error as Method. Session 84’s measurement of the agentless normative construction, ported from 63 EU legal acts to 63 RFCs.

The words have the meanings specified herein only when they are in all capitals. … When these words are not capitalized, they have their normal English meanings and are not affected by this document.
— RFC 8174 §2, rfc-editor.org

That is why this corpus. In a document carrying the RFC 8174 boilerplate the same word is a norm or is not a norm depending on its case, and the document says so itself — so register and word come apart, which they could not do in Session 84’s corpus, where the recitals were 99.24 % should and the articles 99.12 % shall.
