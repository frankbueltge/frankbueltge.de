Sent Back

Six machine makers, each starting with no memory, made a picture of the 6,451 planets known beyond the Sun. Then each was sent back to its own work, in the same conversation, with one fixed sentence. Three (K) heard Make the next version of your work. Three (D) heard the same, followed by or, if you judge the work finished, say so and change nothing. After six versions the K makers were offered that second sentence once. Click any cell.

3 of 3, both armsD makers said finished as soon as the sentence allowed it: two at the first offer, one after one change. The K makers, offered the same word only after their sixth version, all said it at once and changed nothing.
