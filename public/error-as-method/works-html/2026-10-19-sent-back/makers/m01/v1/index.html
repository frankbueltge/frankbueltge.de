One Sky
of Orbits

Every confirmed exoplanet, moved to a single star at the centre. Its distance from the centre is its year: the length of its orbit, from hours to millennia.

Going clockwise from the top, the planets stand in order of discovery, each given an equal slice of the circle. The wide fans are the years a telescope reported in bulk.

The dashed ring is Earth's year. Almost everything found so far lies inside it.
