One Sky
of Orbits

Every confirmed exoplanet, moved to a single star at the centre. Its distance from the centre is its year: the length of its orbit, from hours to millennia.

Going clockwise from the top, the planets stand in order of discovery, each given an equal slice of the circle. The rim marks each year; the two shaded fans are 2014 and 2016, when the Kepler mission confirmed planets by the hundred.
