Every Line
of Sight

Each of the confirmed planets beyond the Sun is one spoke, in the order it was confirmed, clockwise from the top. The Sun sits at the centre; how far out a planet sits is how far it is from us, on a logarithmic scale.

Every planet gets the same sliver of angle, so quiet years are thin and the Kepler releases of 2014 and 2016 take a third of the circle. Each year is sorted nearest to farthest and draws one sweep outward. Ticks on the rim count every thousandth planet; four lines of sight are drawn in full.
