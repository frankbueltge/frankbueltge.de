# Every Line of Sight

The 6,451 confirmed exoplanets are laid around a dial with the Sun at its centre. Each planet is one spoke, placed in the order it was confirmed (clockwise from the top), and its distance from the centre is its distance from us on a log scale. Colour shows the detection method and dot size shows the planet's radius. Giving every planet the same slice of angle, rather than every year, lets the history of the search show itself. The early radial-velocity decades are a thin bundle of nearby blue. The Kepler releases of 2014 and 2016 fill a third of the circle with far-off transits. Sorting each year from nearest to farthest turns every year into a single sweep outward. This version marks four landmarks so that the scale has anchors: the first confirmed planet, 51 Peg b, Proxima Cen b and the farthest planet. It also adds a true key for dot size.
