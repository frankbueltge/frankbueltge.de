The Length of
Their Years

Every confirmed planet beyond the Sun, set on a dial of discovery. Going clockwise from the top: the year it was found, 1992 to 2026. Outward from the centre: how long its year lasts, from two hours to a million days.

The faint rings are the years of Mercury, Earth, Jupiter and Neptune. Very few of the found worlds sit where ours do; each way of looking reaches its own band of distance, and the dial is drawn by the instruments as much as by the sky.

The dust outside the rim is planets whose year is not known.
