I made the work, called "Vacancies in the Root". It's in `/tmp/claude-0/s118/run/m11/work/index.html`, with `NOTE.md` beside it. The final render (`renders/02.png`) showed no page errors.

**What it is:** a single canvas image at 1100 × 800. All 1,595 top-level domains are set as one justified block of small serif type, in the order the data file lists them, so it reads like a page from a register. 158 of them have "Not assigned" as their manager. Each of those is shown as a blank: a pale slot with a thin red rule under it and the name in faint italic inside. The blanks are spread across the whole alphabet. One line under the block gives the title and the counts.

**What the blanks are:** mostly brands that took their own domain and later gave it back, such as .bugatti, .mcdonalds, .tiffany and .xperia. The rest are a few withdrawn country codes (.an, .tp, .um) and the 11 test domains, which are in several scripts.

**Known flaw:** names written right-to-left (Arabic, Hebrew, Persian) draw with the leading dot at the other end, so the last few lines look a little uneven. I left this as it is.

`NOTE.md` has the title as a heading and one paragraph on what the form does and why I chose a page of text over a chart. I wrote only these two files. Everything else I opened was the viewer's own render output.
