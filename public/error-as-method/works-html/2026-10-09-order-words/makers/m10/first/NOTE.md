# Not assigned

All 1,595 top-level domains are set in alphabetical order as one justified block of type that fills the page, like a lease register or a wall of letterboxes. Country codes are in rust and every other name is in ink. Each of the 158 names whose manager is listed as "Not assigned" keeps its place in the order, but its text is replaced by a blank grey slot of the same width. Most of these are brand TLDs that companies applied for and later gave back, such as .abarth, .active and .adac, plus the test IDNs. I chose to show the whole root at once because the register only becomes legible as a whole. The vacancies matter most here, so they are drawn as gaps rather than as one more colour. That way the eye picks up where the root still reserves a name that no one holds.
