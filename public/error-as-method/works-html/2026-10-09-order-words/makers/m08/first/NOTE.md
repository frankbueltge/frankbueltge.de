# Tenancy

The whole root zone is set as one justified page of type, all 1,595 top-level domains in the alphabetical order of the IANA database, with nothing left out. Each name is inked by who holds it. Countries are in black. Names that belong to the seven largest portfolio registries are in red; those seven hold 434 of the domains between them. Everyone else is in grey. The 158 names that were delegated and then handed back, now "Not assigned", are struck through and left pale. I chose to show the names themselves, not points or bars, because the root is a list of words. Read as a page, it looks like a building register: the red shows how much of the alphabet a few landlords lease out, and the struck lines are the flats that brands such as .fiat, .intel and .mcdonalds moved out of.
