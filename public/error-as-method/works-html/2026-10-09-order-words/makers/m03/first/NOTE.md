# Held by No One

All 1,595 top-level domains are set as one justified block of text, in the order IANA lists them. Country codes are in bold and everything else is in a quieter grey. The 158 domains whose manager is "Not assigned" are boxed, and their names are faded inside the boxes. Most of these are brand domains that their companies gave back, like .bugatti, .mcdonalds, .tiffany and .volkswagen. The rest are retired country codes such as .an and .tp, plus the eleven test domains. I chose a wall of plain names because the root zone is, literally, a list of words. Read that way, the form makes you notice that the internet's top layer is now mostly brand vocabulary. The boxes leave a visible hole the shape of each abandoned name, scattered through the alphabet like vacant lots on a street.
