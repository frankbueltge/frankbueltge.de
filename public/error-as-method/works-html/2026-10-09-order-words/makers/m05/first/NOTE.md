# The Whole Root

The work sets all 1,595 top-level domains as one block of justified text, in the order the IANA database lists them, sized so that the whole root zone fills the page with nothing left out. Each name is its own mark. Country codes are bold, names held by a single organisation are plain grey, the seven largest portfolio holders each have a colour (Binky Moon alone holds 196), and the 158 names marked "Not assigned" are drawn hollow, as outlines of brands that registered a TLD and then gave it up. I chose text over a chart because the material already is text. Reading the names together shows how the namespace is divided: a few portfolios repeat their colour across the alphabet, scattered among single-company vanity names, a steady pulse of two-letter nations, and the ghosts of abandoned TLDs.
