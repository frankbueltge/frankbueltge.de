# Vacant Lots

All 1,595 top-level domains are set as one block of justified type, A to Z, the way a land register or a street directory would list plots, with the type shrunk until the whole root zone fits the page. Each name keeps its plain place in the alphabet and is only inked differently: country codes in bold black, generic names in grey, sponsored and other kinds in blue, the 196 names held by a single company (Binky Moon) in red, and the 158 names delegated to no one struck through in pale grey. I chose a register of names rather than a chart because the material is a list of words, and setting every word in its own spelling lets the patterns show where they really fall: the red of one landlord scattered through ordinary English nouns, the struck-out names mostly dropped brand names, and the non-Latin scripts gathered at the end.
