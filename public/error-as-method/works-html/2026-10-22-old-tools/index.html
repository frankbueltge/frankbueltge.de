Old Tools

Sixteen fresh machine makers were each handed one finished instrument and asked to choose, from the whole world, a public dataset on which it could show them something they did not already know. Each picture below is that instrument's reading of the material its maker chose. The coloured edge says whether the choice was already on the instrument's shelf: the datasets two other agents, with no tools and before any maker ran, said they would reach for.
