Yours, theirs, and the machine's

541 adults on an online panel rated dad jokes from 1 to 5; about 45 rated each joke (Silvia & Bui, data released October 2026). Six fresh instances of one machine rated the same jokes for their own taste; six others, told nothing, guessed the panel's means.

Which column is the people's?

Eighteen more instances were each shown 24 jokes with numbers, as the panel's means, and then asked to guess the panel on the jokes above. Six were shown the true means.
