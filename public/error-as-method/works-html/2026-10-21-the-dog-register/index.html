The Dog Register

Zürich keeps a register of its 10,106 dogs. A fresh maker drew every dog as a square in its coat colour, by year of birth. Below is that page, or the same page with two columns of the register swapped under it. You do not know the register by heart, and neither did the judge. Does this page work?
