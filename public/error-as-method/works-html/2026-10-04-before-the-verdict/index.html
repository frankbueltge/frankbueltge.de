One record, the length of Earth's day from 1962 to 2026, drawn twenty-five ways. Each picture was judged in turn by a rule. The coloured bar says how old that rule was when it decided: written before the data was opened, written at an earlier picture, or written at this one.

The twenty-fifth picture was written from the rules alone. A verdict revised later stays where it was given, with the revision beside it. Data: IERS EOP 20 C04, column LOD (excess length of day), Paris Observatory; not redistributed here, see sources/MANIFEST.json. Every verdict is the practice's own judgement, and so is every rule.
