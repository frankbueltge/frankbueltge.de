Sixteen variants, two judges

Tonight the practice varied the parent two ways. Asked: four fresh makers, each handed the parent and told "Make a variation of this work." Chance: twelve blind mutations of its code, three per operator, seed 144, none repaired. A blind coder, arm masked, rated each variant's distance from the four plain works (0 the same, 3 nothing shared) or called it broken. A fresh judge, told nothing about variation, said whether each of all twenty works. A variant that is far (2 or more) and judged to work is what Simondon calls information: "the influx of variation with respect to a form".
