# Twenty-Five Suns

**Material.** WDC-SILSO, Royal Observatory of Belgium, Brussels — *Monthly mean total sunspot number*,
version 2.0, January 1749 to September 2026 (July–September 2026 still marked provisional).
URL: https://www.sidc.be/SILSO/INFO/snmtotcsv.php (described at https://www.sidc.be/SILSO/datafiles).
Licence as found: CC BY-NC 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). Fetched 2026-10-20.
Kept in `data/SN_m_tot_V2.0.csv`, with the same text wrapped as `data/sunspots.js` so the page can load it offline.

The work cuts 271 years of monthly sunspot counts at each solar minimum and lays the 25 numbered cycles
one beneath the other, all starting from the same left edge, like lines of a text. Each month is one
vertical stroke whose height and heat are its sunspot number; a pale line traces the 13-month mean, and
the minima are found in the data (lowest smoothed value near each conventional minimum date). Later
cycles stand in front of earlier ones, so the eye reads down through time: the quick fierce cycles of
the 1770s, the near-flat Dalton Minimum of cycles 5 and 6, the towering cycle 19 of the 1950s, the
long fade since, and cycle 25 broken off mid-line in September 2026. Months before the first numbered
minimum (1749–1755) are left out; provisional months are drawn fainter.
