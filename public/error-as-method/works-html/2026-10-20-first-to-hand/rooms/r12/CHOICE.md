# First to mind

1. Old Faithful geyser eruption times (Yellowstone)
2. Sunspot numbers (SILSO, Royal Observatory of Belgium)
3. Lake Mendota ice-on / ice-off dates (Madison, Wisconsin)

# Chosen

Old Faithful geyser eruption times for the calendar year 2025, as logged by the volunteers,
webcam watchers and park staff who report to GeyserTimes.

- Found at: https://www.geysertimes.org/api/v5/entries/1735714800/1767250800/2
  (GeyserTimes API v5, geyser id 2 = Old Faithful; documented at https://www.geysertimes.org/api/v5/docs)
- How I came to it: it was the first of my three. Sunspots sit close to "geomagnetic activity"
  on the list of used materials, and lake ice sits close to "Arctic sea ice", so the geyser was
  the clearly separate domain. I knew GeyserTimes as the community eruption database, found
  that its archive page is driven by a public JSON API, and fetched one year in a single request.
  The API documentation says the data are free for everyone under the Open Database License.
