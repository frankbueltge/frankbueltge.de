# Twenty-Six Breaths

- **Publisher:** WDC-SILSO (World Data Center for the Sunspot Index and Long-term Solar Observations), Royal Observatory of Belgium, Brussels
- **Dataset:** Monthly mean total sunspot number, version 2.0 (January 1749 to September 2026, 3,333 months)
- **URL:** https://www.sidc.be/SILSO/DATA/SN_m_tot_V2.0.csv (listed at https://www.sidc.be/SILSO/datafiles)
- **Licence:** CC BY-NC 4.0, as stated on the SILSO data files page
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-20 (kept as `data/SN_m_tot_V2.0.csv`, with the same values in `data/sunspots.js` for the page)

The record is cut at each solar minimum, using SILSO's published dates for cycles 1 to 25, and each
cycle is laid down as its own row, so 277 years of counting become a stack of ridges, like breaths
seen side by side. Every month is one hairline, and its height is the mean number of spots counted
that month. Later rows cover the tails of earlier ones, the way a mountain range overlaps itself.
Read down the stack and the cycles' different characters show: the tall, jagged peaks of the 1780s
and 1950s, the near-flat rows of the Dalton Minimum around 1800, and how much more slowly some
cycles decay than others. The last row, cycle 25, is drawn in the only colour on the page. It stops
partway across, at September 2026, because the count is still going.
