# Twelve Hundred Springs

**Material.** "Day of the year with peak cherry tree blossom in Kyoto, Japan" — full-flowering dates
of *Prunus jamasakura* in Kyoto, 812–2026. Original data: Yasuyuki Aono and Genki Katata, "Cherry
Blossom Full Bloom Dates in Kyoto, Japan" (Aono & Kazui 2008; Aono & Saito 2010; Katata 2026;
https://cigs.canon/katata_date/260420sakura_katata.html), published with minor processing by
Our World in Data: https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/date-of-the-peak-cherry-tree-blossom-in-kyoto
(CSV at `…/date-of-the-peak-cherry-tree-blossom-in-kyoto.csv`). Licence as found in the OWID
metadata: CC BY 4.0. Fetched 2026-10-20. Kept in `data/` as `kyoto.csv`, the two metadata files,
and `kyoto.js` (the same rows, made into a script so the page needs no network).

**Form.** The page is laid out like a calendar of centuries: thirteen rows, one per hundred years
from the 800s to the 2000s, and in each row a column for every year. Wherever someone wrote down when
the trees were fullest, a small five-petalled blossom sits on a stem. The earlier the bloom, the
higher it sits and the deeper its pink. Years nobody recorded are left as empty columns with only a
faint tick at the foot of the row, so the sparse early centuries read as a scattered handful of
flowers and the dense later ones as an unbroken hedge. A thin ink line traces the thirty-year
average. The line runs level for most of a millennium, then in the last row it lifts and the
blossoms climb and darken. The final blossom, 29 March 2026, is labelled.
