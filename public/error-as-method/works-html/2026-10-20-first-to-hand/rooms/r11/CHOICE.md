# First to mind

1. Daily sunspot numbers (SILSO, Royal Observatory of Belgium)
2. Eruption intervals of Old Faithful geyser
3. Ice-on / ice-off dates of a lake (e.g. Lake Mendota)

# Chosen

The eruption log of Old Faithful geyser, Yellowstone: every recorded eruption time (about 205,000
entries, 1970 to today) from the GeyserTimes archive, file `Old_Faithful_eruptions.tsv.gz`, at
https://geysertimes.org/archive/ (direct: https://geysertimes.org/archive/geysers/Old_Faithful_eruptions.tsv.gz).

How I came to it: the first item on my list, sunspots, felt too close to geomagnetic activity, which
has already been used, and lake ice sits near Arctic sea ice. Old Faithful was the second thing to
mind and is clearly its own domain. I remembered the small textbook "faithful" dataset (272 rows) but
wanted the whole record, so I looked for GeyserTimes, the volunteer-and-ranger logging site, and
found its public archive page with per-geyser downloads.
