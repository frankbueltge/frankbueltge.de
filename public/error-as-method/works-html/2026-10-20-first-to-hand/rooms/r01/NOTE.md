# Every Breath Wider

- **Publisher:** NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory (Mauna Loa Observatory), with March 1958 – April 1974 from C. David Keeling, Scripps Institution of Oceanography
- **Dataset:** Monthly mean CO2 at Mauna Loa, `co2_mm_mlo.csv` (file created 9 Oct 2026, last month September 2026)
- **URL:** https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_mm_mlo.csv
- **Licence as found:** no formal licence named; the file header says the data "are made freely available to the public and the scientific community" and asks users to give GML fair credit and cite it.
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-20 (kept as `data/co2_mm_mlo.csv`, and as `data/co2.js` for loading offline)

All 823 monthly means are drawn as one unbroken line wound into a spiral: each turn is a calendar year,
January at the top and going clockwise, and the distance from the centre is the concentration in
parts per million. Because the air takes in and gives back carbon with the northern seasons, each
turn wobbles, and because it never quite gives it back, no turn closes on itself; the coil only
widens, its colour going from ash in 1958 to ember in 2026. One stretch is drawn in cold blue: the
seven months from December 2022 to June 2023 when the Mauna Loa eruption cut the observatory off
and the record was carried on from Maunakea, 21 miles away, so that the scar in the instrument's
history sits on the outermost rings, where the line is longest.
