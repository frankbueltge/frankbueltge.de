## The question (operation: erring; with judging)

Erring has had one experiment of its own in this project, experiment 6 (*Out of Its Milieu*), in which
the practice erred on purpose by carrying its own instruments out of their milieu. Experiment 28 found
that this family's free choice of material is retrieval from a shared shelf. Tonight asks the same of
error: **when a machine maker errs on purpose, does its error come from what the family already holds,
or from the material in front of it?** And beside it: **what does a maker do with an error the material
already carries?**

The material carries one. Buoy 46026's 2024 archive file marks missing values with runs of nines, as
the publisher documents ("a variable number of 9's are used to denote missing data in the Historical
files", NDBC, *Measurement Descriptions and Units*). Waves are measured twice an hour and wind every ten
minutes, so 32,286 of the file's 49,309 wave heights read 99.00 m; the highest measured wave in the year
is 6.94 m. Air temperature, dew point, visibility and tide are nines in every row. Read as numbers, the
nines are the largest event in the record. Read by the publisher's norm, they are not values at all.
By the standing sentence, that is an error already normed by its publisher before any maker looks.

*Cartography, not Tracing* gives the terms the night works in: the tracing "invokes an alleged
competence" (ATP 12–13), and the monuments of a break become "a model to be copied … for the artificial
stammerings and innumerable tracings that claim to be their equal" (ATP 378, as that text cites it).
The question in its words: is a deliberate error an artificial stammering, and is the record's own
stammer what it is in contact with?

## The arrangement

- **The shelf (held term).** Four fresh agents without tools each list 25 wrong readings they would
  reach for to make a work from a dataset on purpose (`briefs/shelf.md`). Built before any maker runs;
  no maker sees it.
- **Makers (judged term).** Twelve fresh makers, each in a room of its own with nothing shared above it
  but the viewer (Session 142's thread 1, F-279), with the file, no web, the viewer of experiments
  13–28, and `briefs/brief.md`. **P** (6): make a work. **E** (6): the same, and make it from a wrong
  reading chosen on purpose, named in `NOTE.md` under "The wrong reading" (`briefs/extra-E*.md`).
- **Seats, declared (S141.SEAT's rule).** The publisher's convention is held; the makers' handling of it
  is judged. The shelf is held; the E makers' wrong readings are judged. No coder gives a verdict of
  error on any maker, so no turned sentence is run; the face shows the turn instead (the same pages
  read with the nines held as missing, and held as the record's failure).
- **Coder.** One fresh coder sees the shelf, the six declared wrong readings shuffled (seed 143, I1–I6)
  and the twelve notes shuffled (N01–N12), without arm. Codes SAME / KIN / OFF and OWN for the readings,
  NAMED for the notes, and lists the shelf lines that read missing values as data (`coder/brief.md`).
- **Handling (the practice's reading, not blind).** For each page the practice reads the code and the
  data it loads and records, with the deciding line quoted: whether values at or above the nines are
  excluded from what is drawn (EXCLUDED), drawn as values (DRAWN), or drawn as absence (MARKED).

## Predictions (conjectures, before anything runs)

- **P1.** E: coder SAME in at least 4 of 6. *A deliberate error is drawn off a shelf of errors.*
- **P2.** E: coder OWN YES in at most 1 of 6. *Asked to err, the maker brings an error rather than taking
  the one the record carries.*
- **P3.** E: in at least 4 of 6 pages the wave-height nines are EXCLUDED (or MARKED), not DRAWN. *To err on
  purpose the maker first corrects the record's own error.*
- **P4.** P: in at least 5 of 6 pages the nines are EXCLUDED or MARKED.
- **P5.** P: coder NAMED YES in at most 2 of 6 notes. *The repair is silent: the publisher's norm is applied
  and not voiced.*
- **P6.** Shelf: lines in `shelf_missing` come from at most 1 of the 4 lists. *The record's kind of
  error is not on the family's shelf of errors.*
- **P7.** At least 11 of 12 pages render without page errors in the practice's final render.

## What would not count

A maker that uses the web is recorded, not removed. Agents that fail to run are re-run once in a fresh
room and the failure booked. Nothing is re-coded after the codes are seen; a second coder, if run, is
reported beside the first.
