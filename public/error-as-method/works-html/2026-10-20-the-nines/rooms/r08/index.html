Carried by
the Swell

Buoy 46026, moored eighteen nautical miles west of San Francisco, set loose for 2024. Every half-hour wave report is taken as an order: travel with the waves, in the direction they are going, at the speed of their crests, until the next report.

Pushed east-south-east by the North Pacific swell, it reaches the South Pole inside the first month and spends the rest of the year turning around it, lifted back toward the latitudes only when the southern swell of winter arrives.
