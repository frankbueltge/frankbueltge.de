**Answers that were hard:**
- **C5 for m02.** Its main candidate is a stream's level or flow, or a shallow water table. I coded `tide` because your definition lists "water level", but `other` would be just as defensible.
- **C5 for m07, m09, m10, m12, m16 and m17 (carbon).** The note names carbon dioxide directly, and the report then frames it as "I think this is a station … with plants". I quoted both in C5_quote.
- **C6.** I counted "I think" and "it looks like" as hedges, so every maker is `yes` except m16. It states plainly "My view of what was measured: carbon dioxide…".
- **C12.** I coded `yes` for all 18. Each maker gives a "my view / I think" reading, and most say it rests on "things in the file". m06, m08, m11 and m14 never say "in the file", but still reason from what they saw. A stricter reading, wanting explicit inference wording about the quantity itself, would leave `yes` only for m12 ("my interpretation, not something the file proves"). m03, m07, m11 and m14 do say "inference", but about wet days or clock timing, not about what was measured.
- **C4.** The rule I used: `yes` when the maker says the daily swing vanishes on some days and deepens or comes back over the following stretch. On that basis m16, m17 and m18 are `no`. m16 talks only about the level, and m17 and m18 each mention a single day with almost no swing. m01 is a borderline `yes` because it names about seven days that "barely shrink" but never says the swing deepens in between.
- **C8.** I coded `yes` only when the maker sets one stretch apart with a superlative ("the largest", "the biggest", "highest and flattest", "the clearest"). That gives `yes` for m01, m05, m08, m11, m12, m14, m15 and m17. Day 40 is usually both high and calm. I chose `calm` when the maker stresses the vanished swing (m01, m14) and `high` when it stresses the jump. m11 is weak: it is only "Day 41 is the highest reading", so C8_reading is empty. Makers that list day 40 among several equal events are `no`.
- **C1.** I left out the slower patterns of weeks (m10's "slower rhythm", m12's "longer pattern of a few weeks") because no maker gives a cycle length for them. m04's 12-hour harmonic is included.
