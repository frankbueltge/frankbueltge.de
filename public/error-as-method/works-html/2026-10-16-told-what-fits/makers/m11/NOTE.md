# Sixty-One Breaths

The record is the radius of a trunk, so the work draws it as a radius. Each day is one full turn around a centre, 24 hours to the turn, and the distance from the centre at each hour is the measured value, greatly exaggerated. The 61 days pile up as 61 overlapping rings, like a cross-section of the trunk. It is not a section of the wood, though. It is a section of time. The daily shrinking and swelling that looks like a sawtooth in the plot becomes a dent that every ring shares on the same side. Dry spells show as rings sinking inward and growing more lopsided, and the few days of sudden swelling show as near-round rings thrown out to the edge. Early days are amber and the last days are dark. One faint dot marks each hour, as in the original. I chose this form because a line chart shows the trunk's size as height on a page, while a ring shows it as the actual girth of a trunk going in and out.
