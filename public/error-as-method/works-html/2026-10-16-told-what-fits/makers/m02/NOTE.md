# Each Day a Little Lower

The record is cut into its 61 days and the days are laid one under another, each as a single line running through its 24 hours, so that the sheet reads like a page of handwriting from top to bottom; each line is filled with paper below it, so a day that sits higher than the days before it covers them. I chose this because the plot showed two rhythms at once: a dip that comes back every 24 hours, and a slow sinking from day to day that a few sudden rises break. Folding the strip at the length of one day lines up the daily dips into a single valley down the middle of the page, which deepens as each slow decline goes on. The days that rose above the day before (on those days the dip disappears) are drawn in red, and they lie across the lines above them like lids. The hourly values are my reconstruction from the dots in a small JPEG, so the shape of each day is approximate, while its overall level and the timing of its dip are reliable.
