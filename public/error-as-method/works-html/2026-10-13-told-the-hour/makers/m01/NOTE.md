# Thirty-Six and Three Fifths

The record arrived as a picture 40 pixels wide, so its 1,464 values had already been folded into 40 columns of about 36.6 each, and its heights into 12 grey levels. No single value can be got back out of that. The work gives each value its own hairline, placed at its true position in time, and lets the picture decide only what that hairline can honestly carry: the twelve greys of the column it fell into, or a mixture of two columns where it straddles a pixel edge. The result is the record at its full count and its actual resolution at once: 1,464 marks that move in steps of 36 or 37, with small red ticks at the 39 places where one column hands over to the next. I chose this form so that the loss is what you see, and so that no value is drawn more precisely than the picture allows.
