# Five Hills, Except

The 1,464 values are cut into lengths of 168 and stacked as ten ridges, one under the next, so that each ridge is one turn of a seven-day cycle and every position in the cycle lines up vertically down the page. Folded this way, the record's rhythm becomes a landscape of five raised days and two low ones, repeated and aligned row after row, until two rows near the bottom sag in the middle of the week where the hills should be. I chose the fold because that is where the material shows its structure: drawn as one long line, the repetition and the break look like noise around a slow trend. Lined up, they look like a habit and the one week it was set aside. The ridges keep no scale, axes or labels, because the source had none and only the shape is known.
