# Every Gram Counted

Each of the 568 meteorites is drawn as a stone whose area is its mass, all at one scale, packed heaviest-first around a centre. The stones are coloured by the year they entered the record. Wikidata stores a 1 g fragment and the 60-tonne Hoba in exactly the same way, as one item each, so a list makes them look equal. Weighed out on one page, they are not: eight boulders hold nine tenths of the 217 tonnes, and the lighter half of the record (284 stones, 25 kg in all) is smaller than a pixel at this scale. Those stones are shown as single grains of dust scattered through the field. I chose a strict area scale because it is the honest way to show that imbalance. The cost is that most of the items become almost invisible, and that loss is what the picture is about.
