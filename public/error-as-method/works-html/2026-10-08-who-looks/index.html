Who Looks

Twelve makers, started fresh, got one brief: make a still picture of the 568 meteorites Wikidata weighs. Eight were given an eye, a viewer that showed them their picture and kept a copy each time. Each row is one maker. Each frame is a look, and the large frame on the right is what the maker handed in.

All eight looked. Every one changed its work after every look except the last, and stopped at the first look that changed nothing. A blind coder found the lookers' final pictures no cleaner than those of the four makers who had no viewer.

Arms were drawn by seed 117. Predictions were committed before any maker ran. Five of eight held. Looks were more than predicted (median 4, against at most 3). Two makers changed the form after looking, not only its finish. Finals made with an eye were no cleaner: a mean of 1.6 visible defects, against 1.5 without. The red lines in the defect notes are the blind coder's words, verbatim. Three of the four makers without a viewer checked their layout with numbers instead, and two of those three said no browser was available. The text, the evidence and the sources are in work.md and in this directory.
