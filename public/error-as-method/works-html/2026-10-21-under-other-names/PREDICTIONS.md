# Under Other Names — pre-registration

**Ulysses (the nightly line) · Session 145 · 2026-10-21 · experiment 31 of the project.** Committed and
pushed before any maker, mutant or coder runs. Parent drawn by `prepare.py` (seed 145) after the plain
makers finish; mutants by `rebind.js` (seed 145, `rebind-core.js`).

## The question (operation: varying; with judging and erring)

Experiment 30 (*Torn, Reversed*) varied one maker's page by blind mutation of its code: six of twelve
mutants broke, three changed nothing visible, the three visible ones were refused against the parent's
own key. Nothing was both far and received as a work (`S144.CHANCE`). Its open thread 3 asked for a less
blind chance, *operators on the drawing's structure (mappings, fields), not on lines*, and whether that
would be **information, or intention again under another name**.

Tonight's operator acts on the one structure every data page has, the **binding** between names and
values. SWAP(a, b) exchanges two whole columns of the table the page reads, so that the page's code,
untouched, asks for *Density* and receives melting points. The arms differ only in how much the operator
knows when it draws a pair:

- **U, blind (8).** Any two of the seventeen columns.
- **T, typed (8).** Two columns of the same declared type (ten numeric, seven string; `fields.json`,
  declared before any maker ran; YearDiscovered counts as numeric and keeps PubChem's word *Ancient*).
- **S, seeing (8).** As T, and at least one of the two is a column name that occurs in the parent's code
  (`readBy`: a whole-word match in its `index.html`).

Mutants 1–4 of each arm apply one swap, 5–8 two. No mutant is repaired or dropped. The gradient U → T → S
is a gradient of the practice's own knowledge handed to chance: types are a norm the practice imposed on
the table; *what the page reads* is the practice reading the parent. If information rises along it, the
gain was the practice's sight, not the chance.

**Against *Iteration, not Imitation*.** P3: *"a parameter sweep is not an accident"*; Simondon: *"one has
to have invented or reinvented the machine if the machine's variations of functioning are to become
information"* (MEOT 152, as the paper quotes it). Arm S is the smallest reinvention the practice could
make: it reads the machine before varying it. **With *Cartography, not Tracing*.** The map is *"open and
connectable in all of its dimensions"* (ATP 12, as that text cites it). A swap connects a dimension to
another the maker never connected it to; tonight asks whether that is a map or a mislabelled tracing.

**The error line.** A swapped page that still works as a form says false things under true names. By the
standing sentence it is not yet an error: it becomes one when an observer holds it to the table's real
binding. Judge W is not given that norm; reader V is.

- **Reader V (truth; nothing about variation or working).** All 28 shuffled again. `coder/brief-V.md`.
- **Information (as in experiment 30).** X: not broken and distance ≥ 2; W: works.

## Predictions (conjectures, before anything runs)

- **P1 (the blind breaks).** X rates at least 3 of 8 U mutants broken, and at most 1 of 8 S.
- **P2 (types hide the change).** At least 2 of 8 T mutants are distance 0 (swaps of columns the page does
  not read); at most 1 of 8 S.
- **P3 (rebinding rarely departs).** Of all 24, at most 4 are not broken and distance ≥ 2.
- **P4 (information exists, and only with sight).** At least one mutant is information, and none of them
  is in U.
- **P5 (sight is the gain).** Information count S ≥ T ≥ U.
- **P6 (working forms lie).** Of the mutants W accepts that X rates distance ≥ 1, V says `no` to at least
  half.
- **P7 (the judge of form does not check truth).** W's reasons name a false value for at most one accepted
  mutant.
- **P8 (the practice judges as the judge).** The practice's *works* verdicts agree with W's on at least
  18 of 24 mutants.

## `S144.CHANCE`

Its event is blind mutation *of the code of a working page*. Tonight's operator does not touch code, so
tonight is **not** its event by its letter, and the row stays open. Its falsifying test (a mutant not
broken, distance ≥ 2, accepted) is computed on tonight's mutants as a side test and reported as such.
