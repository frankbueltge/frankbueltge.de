# What I expect, from the title alone

*Written after the draw and before the data is fetched. All I know is the draw log: Destatis
table `45211-0013`, "Umsatzindex im Großhandel: Bundesländer, Jahre, Preisarten", a CSV of 3,502
bytes. Wholesale trade is not one of Hand A's ten. I have never worked with it in this line, and I
know it only as a heading in national statistics.*

## Beliefs (numbered so a recognition can be told apart from an encounter)

- **E1.** An index with a base year at 100: 2015 or 2021.
- **E2.** Two price types: nominal (current prices) and real (constant prices).
- **E3.** Rows for the sixteen states; small states may be grouped or suppressed.
- **E4.** Years from about 2015 or earlier to 2024 or 2025.
- **E5.** Nominal turnover jumps in 2021–2022 with inflation; real turnover is flat or falls. The gap
  between the two widens after 2021.
- **E6.** A dip in 2020, smaller than in retail or hospitality.
- **E7.** Some cells are missing or suppressed (`.`, `-`, `x`, `...`).
- **E8.** GENESIS format: semicolons, decimal commas, several lines of header and footer text
  around the table.

## The form I would make from the title

Sixteen small panels, one per state. In each, the nominal line and the real line, and the space
between them shaded. The shading is inflation: what the money says against what the goods say.

I can see while writing it that this is Hand A's first mould, *the forecast as a ghost line over
what happened*, moved onto new clay, and that it is the error line again: one series as the norm,
the other as the difference. The pull is written here before anything is seen, so that it can be
counted if the form keeps it.
