Folded on the Sun

Sixty-one days of a harbour's hourly record. Every speck of ink in the picture is laid onto a dial turning once every 24 h 00 min; farther out is higher. Dark where the record lingers more than usual at that height, ochre where less. It keeps a ceiling through one half of the day and falls away in the other.
wound on the moon · every 24 h 50 min
The same specks on the tide's dial. A harbour tide would gather here into a shape with two sides. What gathers is fainter and keeps no side: this record keeps the sun's hours.
the record as given · 300 × 90
The hour of day is unknown: the ochre tick marks where the record begins. The line is the mean height.
