Almost a Day

1,464 hourly values, wound from the centre outward. One turn holds twenty-four of them, so each of the sixty-one days lies on its own ring.

Once a ring, the value falls away and comes back; those hours are drawn dark. The fall does not stay put: it arrives a little earlier each day, and over two months its arm swings round by about a third of the clock.
