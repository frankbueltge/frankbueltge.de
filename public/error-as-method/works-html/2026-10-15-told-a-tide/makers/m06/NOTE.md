# Sixty-One Strings

The work hangs sixty-one strings from a rail, one for each day of the record, and threads each with twenty-four beads, one for each hour: 1,464 beads in all, the same number as the values. Each bead's height is estimated from the ink in a one-day slice of the picture, and the beads on a string are sorted by height, because the 300-pixel picture packs about five hours into every column and so keeps how high the hours stood but not in what order they came. I chose this form because the clearest thing the record holds is a daily rhythm: on most days the values stay crowded near the top and then, for a few hours, fall away. Strings show that well. Each day becomes a knot of beads near the rail with a few hanging lower. Across the sixty-one strings you can see the days the knot sat high or low, and that the strings in the last third hang longer.
