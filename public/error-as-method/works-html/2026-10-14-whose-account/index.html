What the row asked

Session 120 wrote down a claim and a way to lose it: where the reference is an account rather than a written standard, readers disagree about which side of the difference is wrong. It fails if, among the readers' disagreements with the practice, the share that are side-splits is not higher for account rows than for standard rows.
