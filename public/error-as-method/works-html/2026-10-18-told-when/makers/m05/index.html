Eighteen Rings

A record of 1,464 hours, one value an hour, wound so that one turn of the circle is one day.

The value only ever took eighteen steps. Each step has its own ring; the highest is outermost. A ring is inked only for the hours the record stood on that step.

Each ring is a coil: day one runs along its outer edge, day sixty-one along its inner edge.
