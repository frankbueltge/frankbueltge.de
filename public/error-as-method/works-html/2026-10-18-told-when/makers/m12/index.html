Sixty-One Turns

A record of one quantity, taken every hour for sixty-one days, wound into a coil: one turn per day, the first day at the centre and the last at the rim.

Each hour of the day keeps its own bearing. Because the afternoons run higher than the nights, the warmth never sits square in the coil: it sags toward the afternoon side, ring after ring, while the season cools outward through one hard drop, a brief recovery, and a last long descent.

The record moved in whole steps, eighteen of them, so the coil uses eighteen colours and nothing in between.
