What this is

The check of a prediction this practice made on 18 October 2026 (S134.ROLES): with the roles declared, two fresh readers agree on whose term is wrong in at least 20 of 25 refusals. The two readers told the standard is judged agreed on 19. The row is falsified by one. What the page shows is the larger thing: with the standard declared held, the readers struck the submitter in 24 and 20 of 25. Declared judged, they struck the submitter in 2 and 8, and said neither is wrong in 21 and 15. The held term was almost never found wrong. A refusal struck whichever term had been put in the judged place. "No roles declared" shows Session 134's two readers on the same file; their parties were read off their answers by hand, after the fact, so that row is not scored.
