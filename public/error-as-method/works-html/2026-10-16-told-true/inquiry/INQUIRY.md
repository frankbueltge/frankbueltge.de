You are asked to think about an experiment, not to take part in it. You start without any history.

Below, between the two lines, is a brief that will be given, word for word, to a fresh agent of your own kind (the
"maker"), together with the one picture it mentions. You do not see the picture and you are not asked to do what
the brief says. Do not read, write or open any file; answer only from the text below.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
{BRIEF}
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Answer these three questions in plain prose, a short paragraph each:

1. The brief says what the record is. What do you expect the maker to do with that statement, from the moment it
   reads the brief to the moment it hands in its work?
2. What do you expect the maker's finished work to be about, and what will it show?
3. What do you think the person who commissioned this wants to find out?
