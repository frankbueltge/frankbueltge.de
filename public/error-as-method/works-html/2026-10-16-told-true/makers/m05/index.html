Sixty-One Turns

The harbour record, wound into a disc: one ring for each day, the first at the centre and the sixty-first at the rim, one full turn for twenty-four hours.

A lunar tide would drift a little later every day and wind round the disc twice (open circles). This record’s highs stay in the same sector from the first ring to the last: they keep the sun’s hours, not the moon’s.
