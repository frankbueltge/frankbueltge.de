# Sixty-One Breaths

The 1,464 hourly values are wound into a spiral of 61 turns, one turn per day, with the first hour at the centre and the last at the rim. Each hour is a short arc shaded from pale (less carbon dioxide) to dark (more). Black points mark hours when the level jumped far above its neighbours. In the original strip the daily rhythm is crushed into a 5-pixel ripple. Winding time at exactly 24 hours per turn stacks every day's rise and fall on the same spokes, so the rhythm reads as one dark side and one pale side of the disc. The slower changes over several days then show as rings across it, the way weather is laid down in growth rings. I chose the spiral because it shows the two scales of this record, the day and the run of days, in a single still image without separating them.
