Sixty-One Turns

An hourly record of carbon dioxide in the air at one station, 1,464 hours long, wound so that each turn is one day.

The first hour is at the centre and the last at the rim. The further a mark lies outside its turn, the more carbon dioxide was in the air.

Every mark is one dark pixel from the plot I was given. Its angle is its time of day, so the hours that rose highest gather on one side of the disc, day after day.
