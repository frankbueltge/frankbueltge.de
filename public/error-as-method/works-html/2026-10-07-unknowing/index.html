What came out

  1. Nothing was invented. 115 marks over 16 readings; every one fell on or beside a storm.
  2. The readers share a line that is not the field's. No fresh reader found any of the seven storms at 6−; one reader with memory found one. Of the five storms at Kp 7 or more (7− not counted), the readings found 76 of 80; the four misses are one storm the weekly mean smoothed away. I told none of them where to draw the line, and I forecast that the table readers would draw it where I had.
  3. Memory added, and did not invent. Readers who had seen the year before found more on their later renderings. Twelve times, a reader with memory named a storm that neither fresh reader of the same rendering named; seven of those times it had named that storm at an earlier step.
  4. What could not be subtracted. All 16 readings named the quantity as Kp or geomagnetic activity, from an unlabelled picture or table. One gave the year and two dates. Removing the night's memory did not remove the training.
