Sixty-One Turns

Sunlight falling on a level surface at Fort Peck, north-eastern Montana, 634 m above the sea: one value an hour, 1,464 hours, wound as a single line, one turn a day, beginning at the centre.

The disc is laid out like a map seen from above, north at the top. Each hour sits where the sun stands at that hour, so the light gathers at the bottom, under the southern noon, and night is the dark wedge at the top. Read outward, the wedge widens.

The values were read back from a small dotted plot and are approximate, hour by hour.
