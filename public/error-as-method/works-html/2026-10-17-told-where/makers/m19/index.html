Sixty-One Turns
One measured quantity, hourly, for sixty-one days, wound into a spiral: one turn for each day, the first day innermost. Brightness is the value. Where the record rests on its floor, the line goes dark.
Each day’s floor is turned to the bottom, so its high hours rise to the top. Small marks: first and last hour. Toward the outer turns the high hours dim and the dark wedge opens slightly.
