The twenty-four it was told

Before describing the people, twelve instances of the machine were shown 24 other pictures with the people's ratings. Six got the true numbers. Six got the same 24 numbers dealt to the wrong pictures: every number real, every pair false.

Four of the six who got the shuffled pairs said the numbers did not fit the pictures. None of them took the false order. Two of them gave up their own: they set almost every animal near the middle.

All 48, at once

Each dot is one picture. Across: where the people put it. Up: what one of the machine's voices said. A voice that knew the people perfectly would lie on the diagonal.
