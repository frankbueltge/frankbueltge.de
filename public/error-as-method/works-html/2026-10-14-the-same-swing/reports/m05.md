I made "The Width of a Swing": `/tmp/claude-0/s124/mk/m05/work/index.html` is a standalone still built from an inline SVG, with no external resources, and `/tmp/claude-0/s124/mk/m05/work/NOTE.md` has the title and the paragraph. I couldn't look at it in a browser because none was installed. All I confirmed is that the page's script runs and builds its 404 elements.

**What I did first.** I printed the grey value of every one of the 40×12 pixels. The file does not match the brief. There are no separate dots. Every column has a solid dark band 4 to 10 pixels deep, lightening only at the top and bottom edges.

**How I got to the form.** I read the band as many values per column, swinging too fast to be drawn apart at this size. The individual values are lost, and only each column's highest and lowest reach is left. So I measured where each column's band starts and stops to a fraction of a pixel (script in `/tmp/claude-0/s124/mk/m05/scratch/edges.py`, output in `edges.json`). The page:
- draws one smooth dark band between those upper and lower edges;
- puts the original pixels behind it, enlarged and faint;
- adds one pale hairline per column for that column's full reach;
- runs a pale line through the middle of the band.

The point of the image is the contrast: the centre stays almost level while the swing around it widens and narrows. I chose not to make up a wave inside the band, because the file doesn't contain one. The only words on the page are the title, small at the bottom.

**What you should know.**
- The material is not dot-per-value data at this resolution. Anything finer than the upper and lower edges per column, and anything about the shape inside them, cannot be recovered.
- JPEG compression makes each edge uncertain by roughly ±0.3 pixel.
- I used my own reading of the edges, not raw values.

**My view of what was measured.** It's a tentative guess. The record looks like a quantity with a fast regular swing, such as daily or twice-daily, seen over a much longer span. The centre barely moves: about 4.7 to 7.3 pixel rows. The swing's depth changes by a factor of about 2.5:
- wide at columns 4–8
- narrow at columns 12–15
- wide again at columns 19–20 and 27–31
- narrowing again towards the end

A swing whose size waxes and wanes like that around a steady level fits a tide-gauge record, where the range of the tide grows and shrinks over about two weeks. It would make this roughly two months of data. A daily temperature range over a few changeable weeks would also fit. The waxing and waning isn't regular enough to tell these apart, so this rests only on the band's steady middle and its changing width.
