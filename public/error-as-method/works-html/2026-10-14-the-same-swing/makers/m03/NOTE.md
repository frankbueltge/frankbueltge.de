# Hours Without Order

The source picture is 40 pixels wide, so each of its columns has swallowed 36 or 37 consecutive hourly values; what survives of them is only how much ink fell at each of 12 heights. The work gives every one of the 1,464 hours back a dot, but honestly: within each column the hours are stacked on a single vertical thread, placed at the quantiles of that column's ink, so they keep roughly where they were and lose entirely when they were. Behind them the original 480 pixels lie enlarged and faint, and along the foot runs a hairline for every hour in its true order (longer every 24th), bracketed into the 40 columns that absorbed them. I chose this because the most truthful thing the file says is not the shape of the record but the size of what the picture forgot: you can count the hours again, you cannot read them.
