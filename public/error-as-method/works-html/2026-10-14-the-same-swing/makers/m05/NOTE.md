# The Width of a Swing

The record was meant to show one dot per value, but the file holds no separate dots: at 40 pixels wide the values pile into a single dark band 4 to 10 pixels deep. Each column must contain many readings swinging up and down too fast for the picture to separate. So the work does not invent the lost values. It shows only what survived, which is how far the quantity reached up and down in each stretch of time. A dark band is traced from the upper and lower edge of each column, measured to a fraction of a pixel. The original pixels sit behind it, enlarged and faint, as the ground the reading stands on. One pale hairline per column marks that column's full reach, and a pale line through the middle of the band shows the centre of the swing. The centre stays almost level while the width of the swing grows and shrinks, and that contrast is the subject.
