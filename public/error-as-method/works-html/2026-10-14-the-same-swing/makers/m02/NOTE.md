# Sixty-one Swings

The record repeats every 24 values, and 1,464 is exactly 61 × 24, so the work cuts it into 61 days and hangs each day from one pivot like the sweep of a pendulum. Each day is a ring, the first closest to the pivot. The height of a value becomes an angle: the middle of the whole record hangs straight down, and higher values swing to the right. Each mark is one dark pixel of the source picture moved to its new place, with its darkness kept. I did not redraw or smooth anything. A pale arc behind each ring shows how far that day reached. I chose this form because the thing the record shows most strongly is not its level but how far it swings each day. Most days swing wide. Two spells of a week or more barely move, and on a line plot they get lost in the noise, but as a stack of arcs they show up as narrow waists in the fan.
