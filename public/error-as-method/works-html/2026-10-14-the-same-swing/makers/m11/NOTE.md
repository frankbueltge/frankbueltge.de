# Width of a Swing

The record arrived as a picture too small for itself. There were far more values than its 40 columns could hold, so the dots merged into one dark band, and JPEG compression blurred away whatever was left of the single values. What survives in each column is how far the values reached, up and down. The work keeps only that and does not invent the rest. Each of the 40 columns becomes a vertical stroke whose ink is the column's 12 original greys, stretched. Two red lines trace where the ink gives out. Below, the reach alone is drawn as 40 bars, and in the corner the whole 40 × 12 source is shown at a size where its pixels can be counted. I chose this form because the most honest thing the file says is not a sequence of values. It shows something that swung around a steady middle, wide, then narrow, then wide again, then narrowing.
