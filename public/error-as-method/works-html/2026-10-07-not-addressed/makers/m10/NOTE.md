# Fremont, Woven

The year is woven as a cloth: each of the 365 days is a warp thread running down the page and each of the 24 hours is a pick of weft across it, so every hour of 2025 becomes one stitch. Its depth of dye is how many riders crossed in that hour, and its colour is how they split between directions: madder for northbound, indigo for southbound, purple when the two were even. I chose a textile because the data is already a weave. The daily commute repeats as two dark bands, weekends thin into pale threads, and summer weaves denser. A cloth also keeps its flaws visible instead of smoothing them away. The one missing hour (2 am on 9 March, when the clocks sprang forward) is left as a dropped stitch. On 1 July the cloth changes colour overnight: the northbound share falls from about 65% to 30% while the daily totals stay the same. That is a seam in the record, not a change in the riding, and the weave shows it as one.
