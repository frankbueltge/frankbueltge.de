# Orchard

Each of the 365 days of 2025 is planted as a small tree, in twelve rows of months with one tree per day of the month. A tree has 24 branches fanned from the trunk like a clock face laid over a crown: midnight droops to the lower left, noon stands straight up, eleven at night droops to the lower right. Each branch is as long as the square root of that hour's crossings, the trunk thickens with the day's total, and every hour busier than about ninety riders bears a fruit, coloured ochre when most riders were heading north and plum when most were heading south. I chose a tree because the bridge's daily rhythm already has the look of growth. Workdays come up as forked, two-armed trees, with a morning limb and an evening limb that carry fruit in different colours as the commute goes one way and returns the other. Weekends grow round crowns that lean toward midday. Rainy winter days stay stunted, and the summer rows grow thick and laden. Without any axis to read, the year shows up as a season of growth and harvest. The one hour lost to the spring clock change is left as a sawn-off stub on the tree for March 9.
