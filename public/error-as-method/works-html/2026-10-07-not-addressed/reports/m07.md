I made "Twenty-Four Horizons", a still 1100 × 800 image built as inline SVG with plain JavaScript. It only loads `data.js`.

**The form:** each of the 24 hours of the day is one line drawn across the 365 days of 2025. The lines are stacked from midnight at the top to 11 pm at the bottom. Each one rises with that hour's total crossings, and each line covers the ones above it, like ridges in a landscape.
- **Down the page you read the day:** the night lines stay flat, and the 8 am and 5 pm lines rise high as the commute comes in and goes home.
- **Across the page you read the year:** the lines swell in summer and sink in winter, and every line has a fine comb where five working days are followed by two quiet ones.

**Annotations, in muted red:**
- The busiest hour of the year: 856 crossings, Tuesday 6 May, 5 pm.
- The quietest day: Christmas Day, with 375 crossings in total.
- The one missing hour: 9 March, 2 am, when the clocks went forward. It shows as a break in the 2 am line.

The page also has month and hour labels, a 500-per-hour scale bar, the year's total (1,029,510) and a source credit. It's on a cream paper background in serif type.
