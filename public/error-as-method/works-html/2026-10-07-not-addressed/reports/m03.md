I made *Orchard* as a single still image drawn on a canvas, plus its note. I never looked at it rendered: a screenshot would have meant writing a file you didn't ask for. I only ran the drawing script against a stand-in canvas, and it finished without errors.

**What it shows:** each of the 365 days of 2025 is a small tree. The trees stand in 12 rows, one per month, with one tree for each day of the month.
- **Branches:** each tree has 24, one per hour, spread out like a clock face over the crown. Midnight is at the lower left, noon points straight up and 11 pm is at the lower right.
- **Branch length** follows the square root of that hour's count.
- **Trunk thickness** follows the day's total.
- **Fruit:** any hour with more than 90 crossings carries one. It is ochre when most riders went north and plum when most went south.
- **The missing hour** is the 2 am lost to the clock change on March 9. It appears as a short cut-off stub on that day's tree.

As a result, workdays grow as forked trees with a morning limb and an evening limb, and the fruit on each limb is a different colour. Weekends grow round crowns that lean toward midday. Rainy winter days stay small and the summer rows are full and laden.

The page also has the title, a one-line caption, month names, a small dot under each Sunday, and a tiny key for the branch hours and the two fruit colours. It uses no libraries and no network. The random variation in tree placement uses a fixed seed, so the image comes out the same every time. It is laid out for 1100 × 800.
