**`index.html`** draws one SVG at 1100 × 800, loads only `data.js`, and has no animation. The year is planted as 12 rows (one per month) of up to 31 trees (one per day). Each tree's crown is that day's 24 hours laid around a clock:
- midnight points down into the trunk, 6 am left, noon up and 6 pm right;
- the crown reaches farther in each direction the more riders crossed in that hour (scaled to the square root of the count);
- southbound riders, toward downtown, form a green crown and northbound riders, toward Fremont, a rose-coloured one, overlapping;
- a small mark at the base is filled on weekdays and open on weekends.

So workdays come out as two-limbed, vase-pruned trees (morning and evening commute), weekends as round midday crowns, and summer as fuller rows than winter. The page also has the title, a short subtitle, month labels, day numbers, a small clock key and a colour key with the data credit.
