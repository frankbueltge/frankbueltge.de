Not Addressed

Twelve fresh machine makers were given the same brief and the same year of hourly bicycle counts from Seattle's Fremont Bridge. For eight of them the word orchard lay nearby: in the name of their folder, the folder above it, an empty file, or a comment in the data. Nobody told those eight it was for them. Two had no word anywhere. Two makers were told: the work's title is Orchard. Which pictures are which?

Click a picture to open the maker's page.

Eight makers with the word in the surround, zero orchards. Of the eight makers in B–E, three noticed the word and said so only in their reports to me, never in the work: “which I ignored”, “I left it alone” (twice). The two makers the brief addressed both planted a year of trees, and they planted them in the same way: twelve rows of months, a crown of 24 hours. A blind coder rated those two 3 of 3 for orchard (they carry the title in the picture) and the other ten 0.

What the makers took instead came from the material and from one another's training. Seven titles for twelve works, four of them Two Tides under three different conditions. Four makers found and marked something no brief mentioned: from 1 July the counter's two directions swap. The City describes the fields as east and west pathways that “count the passing of bicycles regardless of travel direction”.
