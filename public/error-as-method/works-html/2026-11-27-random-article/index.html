Random Article

Twelve articles of English Wikipedia, drawn by chance. A machine curator, told nobody chose them, captioned each and stamped it typical or unusual. The bar under each card shows how common its kind of article is in 160 drawn the same way. The cards hang as all twelve curators hung theirs, without being asked: shortest to longest.
