Each colour is a machine's reading of one sentence, by rules fixed before the sentences were fetched, and checked against a hand reading of sixty of them; it is wrong on a few in every sixty. The squares that say nothing stop in 2013 and 2014, while the archive's help page asked for a reason both before and after. Metadata from the arXiv API, CC0, harvested 2026-10-04.
