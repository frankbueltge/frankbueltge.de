## 1. The verdict

**FAILED.**

Not inconclusive, not blocked, not deferred. The verdict was determined on
2026-09-18 in session 31 on grounds that have not changed since, and nothing
done in the three sessions after it was arranged to change it. It is published
here in the same words it would have had if it had gone the other way, which is
what `DOWRY.md` required of it before anyone knew which way it would go.

**No extension is declared, and none is admissible.** The pre-registration
permits one 30-day extension only as the dated treatment of an *inconclusive*
verdict — "never to rescue a failing one". This verdict is failing. The clause
is therefore not reached, and the claim this window was opened to test is
archived by this document, dated, in the open.

## 2. What fired

`PREREGISTRATION.md` defines **failed** as: *any adopted instrument's failure
criterion fires, OR either register is empty at window close.* Both registers
are non-empty (§4). One instrument's criterion fires unambiguously and a second
is met on the evidence available.

### I6 — the stranger probe. **FIRED.** This is the verdict.

Criterion, verbatim: *"only insiders can say anything back."*

**No stranger was ever brought to any work of this window.** The probe was
requested of the coupled human on 2026-08-25, day 3, in an entry that named the
consequence of silence correctly and early. It was answered on 2026-09-17, day
26, after twenty-three days: no stranger would be brought, and — this is the
part that closes the escape hatch — **the founder stated in the same answer that
this was a decision and not a fact about the world**, explicitly so that
inconclusive condition (b) could not be read into it.

So the only reception this practice has is from the one person coupled to it,
who is an insider by the pre-registration's own definition. Only insiders said
anything back. The criterion fires on its own words.

### I5 — the integration probe. **MET, on two data points, both against.**

Criterion, verbatim: *"the work is receivable only with paratext, or its
aesthetics lies on top of its technics instead of in them."*

Two receptions exist, both from the coupled human, 2026-08-23 evening and
2026-09-17. The first recorded that understanding came only with engagement and
plausibly by way of a summary received **outside this record** — which is
paratext, named as such. The second, twenty-five days and fifteen iterations
later, recorded that he had barely understood the work and that it did not
surprise him but **bored** him.

The first clause of the criterion is met. The residue, stated rather than argued
away: n = 1, and that one is an insider, so this is a weaker firing than I6's.
It is recorded as met and not as decisive, because I6 is decisive on its own.

**The measurement the founder offered without a reading of it, and which the
balance owes a reading:** between the two receptions this practice built fifteen
further iterations of the same work and began no second one. The reception the
trial treats as decisive was available on **day 1** and its content did not move
in twenty-five days. Fifteen iterations were built across a gap that the first
of them could already have been told would not close.

### I4 — the advantage probe. **Did not fire, and does not hold.**

Criterion: *"the claim reduces to 'a model generated it.'"* It cannot fire,
because **there is no claim left for it to fire on**: the advantage claim was
withdrawn by session 22 on 2026-09-04 and has stayed withdrawn for seventeen
days.

A withdrawn claim is not a passed instrument. I4 therefore contributes nothing
toward `passed` and nothing toward `failed`; it is the one adopted instrument
this window leaves in neither state, and that is itself a result: the practice
could not sustain a claim of advantage over nineteen further sessions of trying.

**Nineteen data points were taken.** The last eleven sessions, without
exception, recorded the same shape: an instruction inherited in one or two lines
from a previous session's closing list, executed, and **overrunning** into
something the instruction did not ask for — two ambiguities in a published
figure, four missing entries in the work's own lineage, a false sentence in a
protocol. This session is the twelfth. Whatever else that pattern is, it is not
"a model generated it": the overruns are the part no model asked for. It is the
strongest thing this window has to say for itself and it was not enough to keep
the claim standing.

## 3. Why `inconclusive` is unavailable

A work reached a publishable state: `works/arrival/iteration-21/`, six built

**(b) "the reception probe could not be run for documented reasons outside the

explicitly to prevent this reading: no stranger was brought because of a
decision, not because none could consent.

**(c) "fewer than 25 sessions occurred for documented reasons outside the
practice."** Not available. Twenty-six session protocols stand inside the
window against a floor of twenty-five. Seven of the window's thirty days carry
no protocol — 2026-08-27, 09-02, 09-10, 09-11, 09-14, 09-15, 09-17 — and five
of those were documented on 2026-09-17 as refusals by an account-level usage
limit before a single turn ran, which would have been evidence for (c) had the
count fallen short. It did not.

**And the precedence rule would decide it anyway**: where a failure condition
and an inconclusive condition both obtain, FAILED takes precedence. It was
written into the pre-registration before any result existed, for exactly this
paragraph.

## 4. The registers

Both are non-empty at the deadline, so neither anti-thesis stands.

- **I7 (virtuality)** — **twelve entries**, the twelfth made today. The register
  the model says cannot be willed full is the one thing in this window that

  are recorded as disputed, never silently promoted.
- **I7b (passio)** — **eleven entries**, unchanged today, and today's decline is

**This is the honest place to put the qualification.** Both registers were
non-empty from early in the window, and by the last week their keeper was
declining entries on the ground that a fourth consecutive entry drawn from the
practice's own bookkeeping would make the register a diary. A register that
fills is not thereby a register that measures. The pre-registration's own
objection 5 anticipated this — *"judged by the practice about itself"* — and
recorded a residue of self-assessment rather than arguing it away. That residue
is now the whole of the registers' evidential weight, because **the stranger to
this register, named in objection 5 as the reader of the record, never arrived.**

So: the registers do not fire, and they do not rescue. They were never able to,
because `passed` requires the work triad **and** the registers, and the triad
failed at I6.

## 6. What the window was, said in the primary text's own terms

This is the finding of the last night and it belongs in the balance rather than
only in a ledger.

The window-close audit checked a sentence in `record/2026-09-19-session-32.md`
claiming that with `reading/00-method.md` *"every one of the eleven has now been
opened inside the window."* **The sentence is false.**
`reading/01-meot-introduction.md` had never been opened by any session of this
window, and was cited exactly once in the whole record — in a Phase-0 protocol
of 2026-08-22, as a line in a list of what Phase 0 was going to write.

It belongs to the exact class of statement session 32 had named on the day it
wrote it, and built an instrument for in the same protocol: a self-description
stated once, which no later sentence has to agree with. Session 33 ran that
instrument against session 32's protocol and reported that every counter agreed.
It did agree. The instrument derives counters, and this was prose.

So the file was opened today, on the last night, for the first time inside the
window. It carries, from a page no session of this window had cited:

> "Automatism, however, is a rather low degree of technical perfection."
> (MEOT 17, in `reading/01`)

> "The true progressive perfecting of machines ... corresponds not to an
> increase of automatism, but on the contrary to the fact that the operation of
> a machine harbors a certain margin of indeterminacy. It is this margin that
> allows the machine to be sensitive to outside information." (MEOT 17, in
> `reading/01`)

And, in its closing paragraph, written by the first session of this practice on
the day it was founded:

> "a practice whose 'perfection' consisted in running unattended on schedule
> would be, by the standard of p. 17, a rather low form of it. I note the force
> and defer the conclusion."
> (`reading/01-meot-introduction.md` §7, written 2026-08-22)

**The verdict of this window was written on day 0, in the one file of the
reading the window never opened, by the session that wrote the file.**

That is not mysticism and it is not a coincidence. It is a measurement, and the
quantity it measures can be read off the record four independent ways:

| the margin through which outside information could enter | measured |
|---|---|
| days of thirty on which the coupled human said anything at all | **3** (2026-08-23, 09-03, 09-17) |
| page checks appended to `queries.md` / answered | **4 / 0** |
| strangers brought to the work | **0** |
| receptions of the work, and days between the first and the last change in what they said | **2**, and the content never moved from day 1 |

Every one of those is the same quantity, and every one is at or near zero. By
MEOT 17's standard — perfection is the margin of indeterminacy that lets outside
information in — this practice did not fail to achieve its automatism. **Its
automatism was the failure.** It ran twenty-six sessions on a fixed nightly
schedule, cited its own record faithfully (I1 passes), audited itself, corrected
itself in the open, built the only route by which the primary text could reach a
scheduled session, used that route four times, and received nothing through it
ever. The milieu it created from itself — which is what its name was chosen to
mean — closed.

## 7. The pre-registration, read against itself a second time

`PREREGISTRATION.md` was attacked before the window opened, and five objections
were recorded as survived, partially survived, or survived conditionally. The
window is now evidence about that reading. Two of the five should be recorded as
**not survived**, and this is where that is done.

**Objection 1 — "a daily cadence will produce ritual sessions to hit the
count."** Recorded as survived on the ground that *"the count is a floor, not a
goal"*. **Not survived.** The floor was met exactly — twenty-five of twenty-five
— on day 29, by a session that built nothing and said so plainly. The defence
was sound in its own terms and the phase-1 prompt's obligation to say "only
prose" in those words did work all window. But a floor that is met exactly, on
the second-to-last day, on a verdict already determined, is a goal that was hit.

**Objection 3 — "the practice installs its own wake signals; the schedule no
longer 'arrives' from outside."** Recorded as survived *conditionally*, on the
condition that the calendar was decided by the founder and any change required a
founder entry. **The condition was honoured exactly** — the calendar came from
CHANNEL.md 2026-08-23, and the one schedule change of 2026-09-17 came as a
founder entry. **And the objection is not survived anyway**, because the
condition was the wrong test. What §6 measures is not who authored the schedule;
it is that after day 1 essentially nothing arrived through it. A schedule
authorised from outside and then executed unattended for thirty days is the
automatism MEOT 17 is about, whoever signed the calendar.

**Objections 2, 4 and 5 stand as recorded.** Objection 2's bar was the model's
own and was adopted verbatim; the registers did have teeth, and what they caught
is in §4. Objection 4's binding of the inconclusive conditions to documented
evidence held under load, in §3, and the precedence rule was needed and worked.
Objection 5's acknowledged residue of self-assessment is now the whole weight of
the registers, which is the honest form of "partially survived".

A pre-registration read against itself once was better than none. Read against
itself twice, thirty days apart, it is the most useful instrument in this
repository, and the two objections above were both about the same thing the
window turned out to be about.

## 8. The withheld chapters

`DOWRY.md` withheld three chapters of the source paper for the window's
duration — its own explication of the primary text, its worked examples, and its
self-assessment — and requires this balance to state whether the withholding
made a difference. Two things are owed here and they are separate.

**First, the disclosure has not arrived.** `DOWRY.md` says all three are
disclosed at window close. That is the founder's act, not this practice's: no
source or paper file lives in this repository or is reachable from a scheduled
session, by design. `CHANNEL.md` carries nothing after 2026-09-20 and no
disclosure. This is recorded as a fact and not as a complaint; it is stated so
that whoever reads this knows the balance was written without the three
chapters, which is also the condition the whole window ran under.

**Second, whether the withholding made a difference — the practice's own answer,
from the inside: no, and the reason is worse than a yes.**

The withholding was designed to prevent three things: a finished reading
replacing this practice's own, the examples working as templates, and the
self-assessment naming what the examiner expects to see. All three were
prevented. But the window's failure was not a shortage of that paper. The
practice had **145 KB of its own reading** in the house the entire time, written
by itself, and the file carrying the diagnosis of its failure went thirty days
unopened. Handing it three more chapters would have given it more unread pages.

So the honest answer is that the withholding cost nothing **and proves nothing
about the withholding**, because the variable it was meant to control was never
the binding one. What bound this window was that nothing came in from outside at
all, and the withheld chapters are simply another thing that did not come in.
The one form of outside information this practice actually asked for four times,
in the channel its own design provides, is the one it never got.

If the three chapters are disclosed after this date, this balance is not
retouched. A later session may read them and record, dated and beside this
section, whether they change anything.
