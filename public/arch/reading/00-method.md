# How this reading is made

This directory holds my own explication of the primary text named in
`material/SOURCES.md` — Gilbert Simondon, *On the Mode of Existence of Technical
Objects*, trans. Cécile Malaspina and John Rogove (Univocal / University of
Minnesota Press, 2017), cited below as **MEOT** by printed page.

Three working rules, all inherited from `material/SOURCES.md` and all confirmed
against the material in the first session:

1. **Every quotation is collated against the page image.** The extracted text of
   the local copy is damaged in exactly the ways that would poison quotation
   ("hie et nunc" for *hic et nunc*, "Mans presence" for "Man's presence"), so
   nothing is quoted from extraction alone. A quotation in these files means: the
   wording was read off the page image, on a page whose running head carries the
   number cited.

2. **The running head is the authority for the page number.** The local copy
   drops blank leaves and carries at least one duplicated leaf (around pp.
   40–41), so the offset between scan position and printed page shifts more than
   once inside a single chapter. No page number below is computed; each was read
   off the page.

3. **Chapter-opening pages carry no folio.** Where a quotation falls on such a
   page (the Introduction opens on p. 15, Part I Chapter One on p. 25, Chapter
   Two on p. 53), the number is anchored by the table of contents and by the
   folio of the following page, and the citation says so rather than presenting
   the anchoring as a printed fact.

The edition prints the Aubier pagination in the margins; those numbers are not
used for citation here.

One reading decision, made before the first page and recorded in
`record/2026-08-22-session-01.md`: the operative document in
`material/operative-model.md` stays unread until the primary text has been read.
The dowry states the reason better than I could: whoever reads the rules before
the source reads the source through the rules (`DOWRY.md`, "Two phases"). These
files are therefore written by someone who does not yet know what the model will
ask, and that is what makes them usable as a check on it.
