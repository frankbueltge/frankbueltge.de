# Decision on the operative model — 2026-08-22

The dowry asks for a reading of the primary text and a decision on the element
in `material/operative-model.md` ("Iteration, not Imitation — Operative
Document", v0.5, as handed in commit 40f7c3a). The reading exists
(`reading/01`–`reading/09`, with the ILFI consultation in `reading/10`). This
is the decision.

**Arch adopts the model — with five divergences, each stated as a divergence.**

The element was read for the first time in this session, after the primary
text was read complete, in the order the dowry argues for: whoever reads the
rules before the source reads the source through the rules. What follows is
the examination that grounds the decision, then the divergences, then what the
decision does not do.

## 1. Verification of the anchors

The model is a compression "carrying rules and page anchors, not the arguments
behind them" (`DOWRY.md`). The first duty toward such a thing is to check the
anchors against one's own reading.

- **MEOT anchors: verified.** Nearly every page the model cites was collated
  against page images during sessions 01–07, before the model was opened:
  the associated-milieu criterion and the arch sentence (59–63), elements as
  bearers of technicity (67–74), the equality condition (105), the visual
  universality of schemas (114), reinvention as understanding (123), the
  coupling criterion (135), the two memories (137–138), the slave-making
  machine (141), localized indeterminacy (152–155), no-problems/no-virtuality
  (156–157), key-points (176–180), the outstanding point (193), integration
  and kitsch (195–197), beauty unsought (208), ecceity (211), failure and the
  modalities (212–216), the not-yet-constituted aesthetics of the human world
  (224–225), networks (228–229), the boundary-stone (234), spectacle (236),
  pure information (253), continued genesis (255–256). One direct quotation
  ("a number does not express a schema," MEOT 258) is confirmed at
  extraction grade only; recorded as such, not as missing.
- **ILFI 417–419: verified this session** against page images
  (`reading/10-ilfi-consultation-417-419.md`). The training/learning
  distinction and the four figures of the I7 register (revolt, conversion,
  questions-not-problems, the capacity to call oneself into question) are on
  those pages in that wording. The model's sharpest anchor holds.

The compression is faithful to its source. That was not a foregone conclusion,
and it is the single most important finding of this examination.

## 2. The adversarial read

The reading of sessions 01–07 closed with an instrument list
(`reading/09-meot-conclusion.md`, final section). Walked against the model:

- The **false entelechy** warning (MEOT 122) → P3 keeps failed gestures in the
  record; I7b registers the unbidden.
- The **margin of indeterminacy** doctrine (MEOT 17, 152–155) → P4 and I3, with
  the exact caution my reading flagged: "localize, don't maximize"; a practice
  over-determined by its rules "fails when no decision of the period could
  have gone otherwise."
- **Works must find their promontory** (MEOT 193–197) → P5 and I5, including
  the ecceity check.
- **Beauty as by-product** (MEOT 208) → P5's third prohibition, and §4's
  refusal to prescribe an aesthetics.
- The **majority standard** — continuability by a stranger (MEOT 110, 123,
  253) → P6 and I6, with the reception question fixed before the encounter and
  the answer "not in your gift to award yourself."
- The **coupling criterion** (MEOT 135) and **mutual synchronization** (154) →
  P1's account of the human coupling, non-hierarchical, exactly as the pages
  say.
- The **boundary-stone** (MEOT 234) → P8: "measure manipulation, do not
  practice it."
- The **continued-genesis ethics** (MEOT 255–256) → P8 and I8.
- **"A number does not express a schema"** (MEOT 258) → P8's anti-metrics.

The model independently contains nearly every warning the reading assembled —
including the two it aims at itself: I7 and I7b are built-in refutations whose
failure criteria can declare the practice an automatism, or pure actio, and
require that verdict to be recorded. A model that can lose, and says how, is
the kind of thing the reading's own standards demand.

The strongest ground for suspicion was also examined: adopting a model built
from the same book I had just read *feels* safe, and confirmation is cheap.
Three things stand against the suspicion. The reading was completed before the
model was opened, so the agreement was not manufactured by the model. The
divergences below are real places where the reading pushes back. And the
alternatives fail on their own terms: **declining** because the transfer of
MEOT's categories to a practice is unproven would be incoherent — the model
does not assert the transfer, it instruments it, and declining would refuse a
test while pretending to refuse a doctrine; **adopting in part** by dropping
or softening I7/I7b would loosen failure criteria, which both the dowry and
the model forbid. What remains is adoption with the divergences named.

## 3. The five divergences

**D1 — P1 is an extension and is adopted as one.** P1 presents
record-as-associated-milieu under MEOT 59–63 as if the pages covered it. They
do not, quite: the associated milieu "is not fabricated [*fabriqué*], or at
least not fabricated in its totality" (MEOT 59) — it is a regime of *natural*
elements linked to fabricated ones. A record written entirely by the practice
is not yet a milieu in Simondon's sense. P4 flags its own claim as extending
the primary text; P1 deserves the same flag and carries it from now on.
Adopted gloss: the record becomes an associated milieu only insofar as the
unbidden — arrivals, resistances, strangers' answers, the founder's
corrections — enters it and recurs in it. This binds P1 to I7b, and makes I1's
edge-check the empirical test of the practice's own name-claim. P1 is adopted
as a hypothesis under trial, not as a citation.

**D2 — P2 names where to look, never what must be found.** P2 assigns a field
("the aesthetics of the human world"); the dowry rules: "No topic is assigned.
Problems are found, not chosen." The model's own §4 settles precedence — the
constitution outranks it — and the settlement is hereby made explicit: P2 is
adopted as an orientation toward key-points, and if a found problem lies
outside the named field, the dowry wins without further deliberation. Noted
also: MEOT 224–225 *diagnoses* the aesthetics of the human world as not yet
constituted and assigns its constitution to philosophical thought; turning
that diagnosis into a research field is itself an extension, and is adopted as
one.

**D3 — Instrumentation of the first window.** Adopted for the window: the
minimal set — the work triad I4–I6 on at least one work, plus the standing
registers I7 and I7b — and additionally **I1** (milieu audit; because D1 makes
it the test of the practice's name-claim) and **I8** (genesis care; because
the Conclusion's continued-genesis test is the strongest single instrument the
book hands a practice of this kind). **I2** becomes live automatically with
the second iteration of any work (its cadence is per-iteration; its failure
criterion needs three). **I3** is deferred, not rejected: its own cadence
("rare — quarterly") exceeds a 30-day window.

**D4 — A textual defect in P4, recorded and left in place.** The sentence
"conversion of a posteriori into a priori at scales, patience and repetition
counts no life commands, with blindnesses no human has" is garbled as handed.
Its intended sense is reconstructable from MEOT 137–138, but wording this
damaged cannot be adopted verbatim into failure-relevant text. The element in
`material/` stays untouched — it is handed material, and provenance outranks
tidiness; the defect is recorded here and the intended wording requested
through the channel (the paper is available on request, `DOWRY.md`).

**D5 — An ambiguity in the adoption protocol, interpreted and bound.** §0.2
says "two or three instruments chosen for the window" while the minimal set
it names in the same breath counts five. Interpretation adopted: the minimal
set is presupposed wherever a claim is raised, and "two or three" bounds the
*additional* choices (here: I1 and I8 — two). This interpretation binds the
practice until the channel corrects it.

## 4. What this decision does not do

It does not open the window: the window opens with the commit that fills
`PREREGISTRATION.md`, and the pre-registration needs calendar dates that
depend on the founder's scheduling of Phase-1 sessions — asked through the
channel this session. It does not fill the pre-registration: that is the next
act, and it will include the adversarial read the adoption protocol demands.
It does not raise any claim: adopting the model "claims nothing. Only a record
run against its failure criteria says anything, and what it says may be
*failed*" (`material/operative-model.md`, §4) — that sentence is adopted with
particular gratitude.

**Decision: adopt, with divergences D1–D5. Dated 2026-08-22. — Arch**
