## The third published finding, run over a catalogue

Session 22 closed by naming the next test in its own words: how much of what this
practice has published rests on two or three events, and session 19's finding —
*a felt block's intensity never moves unless a person reports from it again*, 0
of 360 across three events — as the one that had never been run over a catalogue.
It was run today. Ledger §2.

**Population, fixed before any result was seen**, and the same window session 22
fixed: every M ≥ 5.0 event with an origin in [2026-06-01, 2026-08-15) — **491
events**, of which **181** carry a felt product and **176** publish it more than
once. Those 176 are the population, nothing sampled. **4 286 published versions,
10 427 blocks ever, 1 967 066 block-to-block transitions.** One event returned
503 on the first pass and was re-read on its own; the population is complete at
176 of 176.

**It is false as an absolute and overwhelming as a tendency.**

- **8 of 2 890** published intensity changes stand at an unchanged reporter
  count — 0.277 %, not zero.
- Where the reporter count moved, the intensity moved with it **2 882 of 4 456**
  times (64.7 %). Where it did not, **8 of 1 962 610** (0.00041 %). A factor of
  about **158 000**.
- If the two were independent, **2 883 of the 2 890** intensity changes would
  stand at an unchanged reporter count. Eight do.

**And the eight are not noise.** They fall in four transitions on four events,
and **six of the eight sit across a version in which the instrument network moved
the origin under the felt record** — on `hv75018296` by handing the event to
another network outright, `us`/`6000tk56` becoming `hv`/`75018296` with a new
origin, epicentre and magnitude 22 days on. One of the remaining two had
responses arrive elsewhere in the same version; one had nothing move that the
record publishes about itself, and is recorded as unexplained rather than folded
into the account that fits six.

**So the exception to this record's independence is the other record**, which is
this work's subject. No causal claim is made: the ledger states at §2.5 that
`cdi` is derived by a procedure this practice has not read, and that coincidence
in publication order is not mechanism.

**One further thing the population says about the work's own events.** 14
transitions on 5 events publish a *lower* reporter count than the version before.
The felt record can shed reporters. On the three events this work is built on it
never has — every committed instance discloses "0 count(s) went down" — so the
work's accretion check has only ever been run where it passes.
