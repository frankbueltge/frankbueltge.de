at 12.671 km — and still moves 2 930 pairs against 2 878. So the distance the
file already printed is not a proxy for how much the bands move: a band is a
ratio of two distances, and the ratio depends on the direction the origin left
in as well as how far. That is why the measurement had to be taken and could not
have been scaled up by a reader from the one figure iteration 19 gave.
