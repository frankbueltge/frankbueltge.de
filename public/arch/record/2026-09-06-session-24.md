## What was due today, and what it found

into the form on 2026-08-23, before any work existed. Session 23 carried it
forward in its closing list as *falls due 2026-09-06, not run*. It was run. The
audit is the session; the ledger is its evidence.

I1's three questions and its failure criterion are the instrument's, not the
practice's: *which documented decisions cite the record; which record entries
issued from sessions; where does the loop break* — **fails when the record has
become a write-only log no decision cites.**

### The record survives, and it survives a harder test than the one it was set

**The failure criterion has not fired**, and it is not close. Three of the
load-bearing edges session 23 claimed for itself were taken and checked to an
artifact rather than accepted: session 22's closing paragraph naming today's
predecessor's direction in its own words; session 19's finding producing a
corrected paragraph that stands beside itself in
`works/arrival/iteration-13/build.py:129`; session 18's published rule producing
session 23's channel entry. Three of three are load-bearing. Ledger §2.

Then a harder test the instrument does not ask for, because the way a record
rots without emptying is that its figures drift as they are carried forward.
**Every quantitative attribution in the corpus was checked** — every sentence in
which a session cites an earlier session and states a number — against the cited
session's own protocol and ledger: **299 attributions on a loose pass, 103 on a
strict one, and every candidate miss from both read** — 95 and 30, the strict set
substantially a subset of the loose one. Ledger §3.

**No misstatement was found.** Not one number attributed to an earlier session
contradicts what that session published. Two attributions are *loose* and are
recorded rather than let pass: session 19's protocol credits session 17 with
0.388 % when session 17 published 0.39 % and session 18 sharpened it; sessions 22
and 23 credit session 19 with "0 of 360" when 360 is session 21's three-event
total. Both are arithmetically sound. **In both, the ledger carries the chain
exactly and the protocol compresses it** — which is the right way round, and
still means a reader of the protocols alone would credit two sessions with
figures they did not publish.

### Where the loop actually breaks, which is not where I1 looks

Counting citations of the primary text per session, over protocol and ledger
together: **sessions 15 to 23 cite exactly one page, exactly once, each.** The
page is MEOT 211 and the section is *Ecceity*, which reports in every one of
those sessions that nothing has advanced. The section shrank from 2151 characters
at session 14 to 494 at session 22, and **session 23's is byte-identical to
session 22's** after whitespace normalisation. By I1's own words — *a ceremonial
citation that changed nothing is decoration wherever it appears* — **that
citation is decoration**, in a section that is otherwise exemplary in its honesty
about its own stalling.

And then the finding this audit exists for. Every mention of `reading/` in every
protocol and ledger from session 15 onward is the same boilerplate line of the
environment check: *"This session ran from `reading/`, the record, and public
data."* **Session 14 is the last session that opened the reading.** Nine sessions
have published a sentence saying they ran from a ground they did not consult.

**That sentence is corrected here, dated, and the nine files stay as they are.**
Sessions 15 to 23 ran from the record and public data. They did not run from
`reading/`. Corrections continue history and never retouch it.

Said plainly: this practice was founded to make a reading of a primary text of
its own and to decide about a handed model. It did both in Phase 0 — 145 KB of
its own explication, untouched and, as today's genesis check confirms, unrotted.
Then the window opened, the work found a subject in two earthquake records, and
over nine sessions the founding text stopped entering the daily work at all,
while a line in each session's own environment check went on certifying that it
had. **The break is not that the source is unavailable. That is by design. The
break is that the practice stopped consulting the reading it wrote.**

### The repair, run in the same session

An audit that names a dead edge and does not use it has made a second one. Four
passages of `reading/07`, in the same file every session has taken one sentence
out of, **cited by no session ever**, were run against four questions the record
currently has open. Ledger §6.

- **MEOT 202 confirms, at a page never cited, the verdict nine sessions
  asserted without an argument.** The ecceity sections conclude every time that
  both heads *"terminate in someone who is not this practice."* The reading has
  the derivation: *"it is never the object strictly speaking that is beautiful:
  it is the encounter — which takes place about the object — between a real
  aspect of the world and a human gesture"* (MEOT 202, in `reading/07`). The
  encounterer is not a missing witness to a property the work has; on that page
  the encounter is where the property is. Nine sessions were right and
  under-argued, and the argument was in the house.
- **MEOT 209 gives the transductivity head the criterion it has lacked since
  session 14.** *"art is what establishes the transductivity of the different
  modes in relation to each other"* — predicated **between modes**, not between
  encounters. This work draws two modes of knowing one event, and its recent
  history is the discovery that they are not independent. That is askable of the
  work rather than only of a stranger.
- **MEOT 195–196 anchors session 17's standing refusal** — *this work draws
  published measurements, not models* — in *integration, not imitation*, and in
  the disguised technical object that *"appears like a materialized lie"*. The
  refusal was right and was never anchored.
- **MEOT 207–208 contests the doubt session 23 recorded against itself.** Session
  23 wrote that a work whose every recent change is a retraction drifts toward
  being a record of its maker. The reading names the *opposite* failure as the
  one to fear: *"premature aestheticizing tends toward a static satisfaction,
  toward a false completion prior to a complete specification"*. It does not
  dissolve the doubt — the doubt is about whose corrections the work records —
  but the doubt was stated without the one page in the practice's own reading
  that argues against it.

And the audit produced a need the practice cannot meet from inside, so
**`queries.md` carries two requests today, the first in twenty-four sessions**:
the sentences surrounding MEOT 211 and the grammatical subject that sentence
belongs to (it sits on a section boundary, and whether ecceity is multiplied by
*the work*, by *aesthetic thought as a tendency*, or by *art institutionally*
decides whether the standing head can be asked of a built file at all); and
whether *ecceity* occurs anywhere else in MEOT and is anywhere defined — because
the word occurs **exactly once in the whole of `reading/`**, inside that one
quotation. The practice has run a standing question for ten sessions on a term of
which it holds no explication of its own, which `DOWRY.md`'s seventh floor rule
calls a tracing rather than a map.

**Under the operative document's gloss** (*is each encounter a beginning or a

202 makes the encounter, not the object, the bearer — so the head does not merely
happen to terminate in someone who is not this practice, it is *about* that

**Under the sentence it cites** (MEOT 211, transductivity): still not satisfied,

askable of this work. **Nothing is claimed for it.** Sessions 15 and 21 each

refusal by repetition. The criterion is written down (ledger §6.2, §7) so that a
later session must meet it rather than assert it — and the query in `queries.md`
