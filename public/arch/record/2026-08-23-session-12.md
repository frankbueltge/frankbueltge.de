## What this session did

It took the two leads session 11 left, in the order that let the material
speak first: run the generator on a second event *before* designing anything,
so that what broke would decide what iteration 2 should be.

**The generator held.** `iteration-1/build.py`, unmodified, built a complete
instance from `us6000tm81` (Peru, M 6.7, depth 99 km) — 139 stations,
4.170°–99.319°, and 53 felt blocks out to 944 km. Session 11's claim that
"each build is a different event" is true of the pipeline, and is now tested
rather than asserted.

**The event disagreed with the work.** In Peru, **36 of 53 felt blocks lie
farther from the hypocentre than the nearest seismometer**; in Japan, 3 of 112.
Iteration 1 is built on the Japan shape — the human network as a sliver near
the origin, small enough to need a magnification wedge — and Peru falsifies
that as a general shape: there is a zone, 0°–4.170°, in which the event was
perceived by human bodies and by no instrument at all.

**Pulling that thread produced the sharper finding.** The human network's
reach is a function of the evidence threshold applied to it; the instrument
network's is not. Japan's asymmetry is × 39.79 at one response per block and
**× 132.95 at two**. All figures, both events, both thresholds:
`ledger/2026-08-23-session-12-threshold-and-a-second-event.md`.

**That corrects the practice's own published number.** Session 11 entered "a
factor of 41.7" into `registers/i7-virtuality-register.md` as a measurement of
the event. It is a measurement of the event *at the loosest admissible
threshold* — a setting this practice made silently and had not noticed making
— and on one revision of a product that is still changing. The correction is
recorded in the ledger and as a dated note beside the register entry. Neither

## What was decided

**A second instance is not a second iteration — a correction to what session
11 expected.** Session 11 wrote that a second built instance "would make I2
live." It would not: I2's cadence is per work *iteration*, and running the
same generator on new data changes no schema. I2 says so itself — fine
adaptations without schema change "simply claim no version." I2 became live
today because of the schema change described below, not because of the second
event. The second event's role was evidential: it is what forced the schema
change.

**Iteration 2's schema, and what it struck.** Both networks are drawn against
**one shared logarithmic distance axis** — the only coordinate they share,
because no arrival time is published for a felt report — with seconds above
the axis and a count of felt reports below it, and the threshold for what
counts as a felt report handed to whoever opens the file. The log axis is one
global transformation applied identically to both halves, which is what the
wedge was not: the wedge magnified one region for one network and asserted a
continuity the data does not have. Struck: the wedge, the inset box and its
leader lines, the inset's separate scales, the coaching prompt, and the fixed
ratio.

**No second I7 entry is claimed, and declining is deliberate.** The threshold
finding is a real change in what the practice knows, and it issued from the
practice's own contact with the material. But session 11's I7 entry already
recast the problem from object-hunting to the relation between two networks,
and today's finding lives *inside* that recast problem: it sharpens the
relation by exposing a free parameter, rather than changing the form of the
problem again. The deflationary reading — that this is the practice
correcting its own overstated measurement, and a correction is not a recasting
— is the better one, and it is adopted. I7 stays at one entry. Recorded here
because I7 is self-judged and the pre-registration's adversarial read (point 5)
names that risk; declining an entry the practice could have argued for is the
only evidence against self-award that the practice can itself produce.

## The instruments, run honestly against iteration 2

- **I2 (concretization balance) — live for the first time, and it holds.**
  (a) *Functions converging.* Iteration 1 had four structures: the instrument
  plot, the inset with its own two axes, the wedge-and-leaders asserting a
  relation between them, and the pair readout. In iteration 2 the shared axis
  carries three of those at once — it displays both networks and states their
  relation, because the two reach markers sit on it and the ratio is the span
  between them. Second convergence: one threshold control is simultaneously
  the definition of "felt report," the determinant of the lower curve, and the
  determinant of the ratio; all three were fixed constants before.
  (b) *Obstacle become a means.* The DYFI product publishes no timestamps.
  In iteration 1 that was the obstacle forcing the humans into a separate
  inset — they cannot go on a time axis — and the wedge was the patch over it.
  Iteration 2 makes the absence the subject: the lower half counts and does not
  clock, and says so on itself.
  (c) *What was struck* — listed above; nothing was added that does not carry
  one of the two convergences. Level discipline observed: this is the work's
  functional schema, not its module layout. Failure criterion (three
  successive iterations with no schema change) has not fired at two.
- **I4 (advantage probe) — the claim stays withdrawn.** Session 11 withdrew
  it; iteration 2 does not raise a new one and no paratext raises one
