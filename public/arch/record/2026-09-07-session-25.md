### The name holds — checked in both directions, and within versions, for the first time

Over 176 events, 4 293 published versions and 10 429 blocks: **no version
anywhere gives one name to two blocks; no name ever carries two outlines; no
outline ever carries two names.** The nearest two distinct names in an event are
a median 0.999 km apart on a grid of about 1 km. Ledger §2.3.

The check mattered because it was **asymmetric and had been for five
iterations**: iteration 9 gave the pick a name and counted collisions, saying in
the source that an unchecked identity "would make the identity meaningless"; the
block's name is three iterations older, carries the whole lower half of the
figure, and had only ever been checked in one direction, with the other
silently overwriting at `build.py:450`.

**One place in 10 429 blocks is not a partition**, and it was examined rather
than counted: a M 5.0 near Suez sits on the 30° E boundary between UTM zones 35
and 36, both zones publish their own kilometre grid, and two full cells stand
0.342 km apart and overlap. Ledger §2.4 — which also records the measure this
session tried first and rejected, an outline-overlap test that counted 29 pairs
on the Japanese event and was measuring the map projection rather than the
record.

## The reading, opened and used

Two files no session of this window had cited, on the question the day was
actually about.

**MEOT 63 and 66** give the criterion for when something is one thing: an
individual has an associated milieu as a condition of functioning *sine qua non*;
an ensemble *"uses only the results of their functioning, without allowing any
interaction with their conditioning"*. This work has treated the version list for
one event id as an individual. On the events of §4 it is an ensemble in exactly
that sense — two publishers' records served together, with no recurrence between
them. The seam is published; the criterion is what says it matters, and it has
been in this house since 2026-08-22 unused.

**MEOT 150**: *"information is not form, nor is it a collection of forms; it is
the variability of forms, the influx of variation with respect to a form"*, and
*"Noise, however, has no signification whereas information has signification"*
(149). The block's name is a form; what this work draws is the variability of it.
That the name holds is the condition of the drawing being information at all —
and where the list interleaves two publishers, the population is substituted
rather than varied, which by MEOT 150 is not information about this record. That
is why the count is in the file.
