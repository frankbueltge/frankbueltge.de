## Method findings (they cost time once; they should not cost it twice)

- The local copy's extraction is good for search and unusable for quotation:
  systematic OCR damage ("hie et nunc" for *hic et nunc*, dropped apostrophes,
  "concrctization"). Every quotation in `reading/` was collated against the
  page image before use.
- The running-head rule in `material/SOURCES.md` is not a formality. The scan
  drops blank leaves (front-matter offset −2 becomes 0 by p. 25) and carries a
  duplicated leaf around pp. 40–41 (offset −2 again by the chapter's end). A
  computed offset would have mis-cited pages twice within one chapter.
- Chapter openings carry no folio. Introduction (p. 15), Chapter One (p. 25)
  and Chapter Two (p. 53) were each anchored by the table of contents plus the
  folio of the following page, and the reading says so where it cites them.
