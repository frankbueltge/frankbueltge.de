## What this session did

**Iteration 3 exists, was exercised, and is committed**
(`works/arrival/iteration-3/`). It gives the instrument network its own
corroboration control, built from a measure the generator was already parsing
and throwing away: each pick's published **time residual** — its disagreement,
in seconds, with the origin the same network fitted. Now the same act — demand
more corroboration, keep only the better-attested data — is performed on both
networks by the encounterer's own hand, and the work shows what the act costs
each. Full figures, both events, both networks:
`ledger/2026-08-24-session-13-strictness.md`.

**The finding the work now carries.** The two networks are not merely different
in reach; they are opposite in how their reach withstands being doubted. Demand
the best-corroborated ~26 % of each network's data and Japan's instrument reach
still holds 97 % of its full distance while its human reach holds 30 %; in Peru
the instrument reach holds > 99 % at 21 % of picks. The instrument network's far
field is its most redundant part (many stations agreeing, small residuals); the
human network's far field is its loneliest (single reporters). Iterations 1 and
2 could not show this, because neither made the machine answerable to a demand
for corroboration at all.

**Provenance carried, not interpreted.** Each pick's QuakeML `evaluationMode`
(`manual`/`automatic`) is now embedded and stated in the footer — Japan almost
all manual, Peru mixed 84/55 — and is deliberately *not* made a control:
reasoning in the ledger. Recording it without acting on it is the honest
middle. The generator also drops any pick without a published residual and
carries the count of what it dropped into the file (0 for both events this
session), so an event with unscored picks cannot silently shrink its own
instrument population.

**Two instances built, both driven before commit.** Japan and Peru, opened in a
browser, both thresholds stepped in both directions and reset, stations hovered

## The work/instrument classification — an interim decision, not a dodge

The founder handed this decision to the practice and made the passed-bar
independent of it: the work triad must hold on at least one *work* by window
close, whatever *Arrival* turns out to be. The decision this session can honestly
make, and does:

*Arrival* remains a **work-candidate**, and its classification will be fixed by
the first reception result (I6), which the practice cannot run for itself. The
criterion is already fixed in `PREREGISTRATION.md`: a first viewer retells the
operating principle unaided. If a bare encounter yields that, it is a work; if
it needs the file's own on-face orientation to be understood, then it is
functioning as the self-introducing instrument the founder named, and by that
result the practice accepts the instrument reading **and owes a separate bare
work for the passed-bar.** This is a committing decision — it names what settles
the question and what each outcome obliges — not a deferral of one; the practice
cannot pre-empt an I6 result by declaring the answer itself, which would be
exactly the self-award the pre-registration warns against.

Iteration 3 is the practice's response to the first-contact finding, and it is a
deliberate wager on that boundary. It adds two pieces of on-face text iteration
2 lacked: a one-sentence orientation ("One earthquake, as two networks recorded
it: N seismometers … and N people …") that answers the founder's "did not
understand what he was looking at," and a caption stating that the two measures
are not the same measure and that what is comparable is the act. The orientation
sentence I judge continuous with the axis-labelling I5 already accepted as the
instrument labelling itself. The caption is the element most exposed to I5's
actual failure criterion ("receivable only with paratext"): it teaches how to
read, which leans toward wall-text. It is kept, and flagged here, because a
stricter later iteration should try to carry the two-measures distinction in the
*visual treatment* of the two controls rather than in a sentence — that is named
undone below. **No entry was written to `CHANNEL.md` about the work**, on
purpose: describing it to the founder would coach the reception the practice may
not award itself.

## The instruments, run honestly against iteration 3

- **I2 (concretization balance) — live, and it holds at the second schema
  change.** (a) *Functions converging.* The residual now does three things at
  once — it is the instrument network's evidence-quality measure, the
  determinant of the upper population, and, through the reach it sets, a
  determinant of the headline ratio; before iteration 3 it was in neither the
  payload nor the work. And one gesture — demand more corroboration — now drives
  what were, in iteration 2, an asymmetric pair (a control on one network, a
  fixed fact on the other). (b) *Obstacle become a means.* The two networks
  publish *incommensurable* corroboration measures — residual seconds against a
  fitted origin, versus a count of independent reporters. They cannot be put on
  one scale. Iteration 2 dodged this by giving only the human network a control;
  iteration 3 makes the incommensurability the subject — it says on its face
  that the two measures are not the same measure, and offers as comparable not
  the measures but *the cost of demanding more of each*. (c) *What was struck* —
  the single human-only threshold, and with it iteration 2's silent treatment of
  the instrument reach as unquestioned ground; also the pair-click readout,
  which belonged to the travel-time reading of iterations 1–2 and is not part of
  this schema. Level discipline: this is the work's functional schema (which
  measurements are given and which are interrogable), not its module layout. The
  failure criterion — three successive iterations with no schema change — has not
  fired: 1→2 was the shared axis, 2→3 is the symmetric corroboration controls.
- **I4 (advantage probe) — the claim stays withdrawn.** Could a person with time
  have made iteration 3? Yes — the residual was already in the QuakeML the
  practice was parsing; adding a second control and reporting the cost is no
  operation, scale, patience or blindness only this subject has. Nothing in the
  work claims otherwise and no paratext raises it vicariously. Noticing one's own
  machine-privileging habit is ordinary self-examination, not an advantage. The
  claim falls; per I4 the claim is withdrawn, not the work.
- **I5 (integration probe) — held further, ecceity still unsatisfied, and now
  with a real datum against the self-education claim.** Key-point: unchanged in
  topic (the relation between two networks perceiving one event), sharpened in
  form (that relation's *asymmetry under the demand for corroboration*). Extends
  rather than replaces: the residual and the mode are already-published data now
  carried, nothing removed, every mark one published measurement. **Kitsch
  check:** take the two thresholds away and there is no work left — the aesthetic
  is the interrogation, not a figure laid over it. **Self-education — the founder's
  first-contact finding is the first data point, and it is negative.** A real
  viewer did not understand iteration 2 unaided. Iteration 3 answers that with
  on-face orientation, but whether the answer works is exactly what the practice
  cannot self-certify (see the classification decision above); recorded as
  *tested by the world, result pending, one negative point standing*. I5's stated
  failure criterion (receivable only with paratext, or aesthetics on top of
  technics) has **not** fired — but it is closer than at iteration 2, and the
  caption is the reason; named, not hidden. **Ecceity check: still unsatisfied.**
  Two independent thresholds enlarge the decision space and let different
  encounterers chase different questions (the ratio, equal reach, equal evidence
  fraction), but session 12's verdict holds — a larger state space is not each
  encounter being a beginning, and a second visit by a settled person is still
