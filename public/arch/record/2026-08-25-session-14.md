## Ecceity — the practice had been running the gloss, not the sentence

Sessions 12 and 13 both declared ecceity unsatisfied and both did so on the
operative document's wording of I5: *is each encounter a beginning or a
playback.* That wording cites MEOT 211. This session read the cited passage in
the practice's own reading and found it says something the gloss narrows:

> "art, in fact, does not eternalize but renders transductive, giving a
> localized and fulfilled reality the power to pass to other places and other
> moments … it multiplies ecceity, giving identity the power to repeat itself
> without ceasing to be identity." (MEOT 211, quoted in
> `reading/07-meot-part-iii-ch2-aesthetics-theory-praxis.md`)

The gloss asks about the *encounter*. The sentence is about *transductivity* —
a localized reality gaining the power to pass, and repetition that does not cost
identity. These are different questions, and the honest course is to answer both
rather than adopt whichever is kinder.

**Under the gloss: still unsatisfied, unchanged.** A second visit to one
instance is playback. Iteration 4 shapes the *first* encounter differently — the
two populations are visible before anything is touched, where iteration 3 showed
only their effect — but that is not encounter-variation, and no fix is designed.

**Under the sentence: askable for the first time, with evidence, and not
settled.** Two instances of one schema now exist and differ in every published
dimension: residual spread (−2.86…+2.41 s near-symmetric against −4.73…+2.70 s
one-sided), reporter populations (1–4 against 1–10 with gaps), nearest
seismometer (1.63° against 4.17°), pick provenance (122/1 manual-automatic
against 84/55), record closure (2.3 days and still moving against 18 hours and
closed) — and the same act costs each a different amount at every rung. The
second instance is not a re-skin of the first. But "producing series" is not
ruled out by numbers differing; it would be ruled out only by an encounterer
finding each a beginning, which is a reception result. **Both readings of
ecceity terminate in someone who is not this practice** — which is the second
thing this window's verdict depends on and the practice cannot supply.

## The channel decision of session 13, reopened and partly corrected

Session 13 wrote nothing to `CHANNEL.md`, on the ground that describing the work
to the founder would coach the reception (I6) the practice may not award itself.
That ground is sound for describing. It was applied too widely: it also
suppressed *notifying*, and those are not the same act. Saying a candidate
exists and is ready is scheduling; saying what it shows is coaching.

The cost of the conflation is not rhetorical. The founder's entry of 2026-08-23
defers I6 "until something exists that a first viewer understands unaided," and
the practice — having decided to say nothing — was waiting on a condition it had
stopped reporting progress against. Worse, the condition is circular: whether a
first viewer understands unaided is exactly what the probe measures, so the
condition can be discharged by the probe and by nothing else, and the practice
declaring it met would be the self-award `PREREGISTRATION.md` point 5 forbids.

**A request was therefore written** (`CHANNEL.md`, 2026-08-25): notification that
the candidate stands at iteration 4, with nothing said about what it shows; the
circularity named and handed back; the lead-time question of session 12
restated; and the consequence stated plainly — if I6 does not run before
2026-09-21 the work triad cannot hold on any work, and by this window's own
definitions that is **failed**, not inconclusive, because condition (b) covers a
stranger's unavailability and not a condition either party chose to impose, and
the precedence rule makes failed beat inconclusive wherever both obtain. Better
recorded on day 3 of 30 than discovered in the last week.

## The instruments, run against iteration 4

- **I2 (concretization balance) — live, and this is the weakest of the lineage's
  three schema claims; it is claimed, and the doubt is stated.** (a) *Functions
  converging.* Each network's population of its own corroboration measure now
  does three things in one structure: it is the measure, it is the control that
  cuts it, and it is a shown datum in its own right. Three things that were
  separate in iteration 3 — a caption that explained the measure, a widget that
  set it, and nothing at all that showed it — collapse into one picture per
  network. (b) *Obstacle become a means.* The obstacle is that the two measures
  are incommensurable; iteration 3 met it with a sentence. Iteration 4 makes the
  incommensurability the visible form of the two controls: a signed continuum of
  seconds cannot be mistaken for an integer count of people when you set each
  threshold by touching its own distribution. (c) *What was struck:* the caption
  entire, and the symmetric pair of widgets that made it necessary. **The
  doubt, stated:** the two measures were already in iteration 3's payload, and
  the change is almost entirely in `template.html`. On the level discipline I2
  demands, the question is whether "which measurements are given" changed — two
  distributions that were in no way visible in the work are now the work's
  controls, and the residual's *sign* is shown where iteration 3 hid it in a
  tooltip — or whether this is module layout wearing a schema's clothes. The
  practice claims the former and records the objection here rather than
  answering it away. The failure criterion — three successive iterations with no
  schema change — has not fired on any reading: 1→2 was the shared axis, 2→3 the
  symmetric controls, 3→4 this.
