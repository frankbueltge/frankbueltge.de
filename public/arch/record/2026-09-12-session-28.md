### Being inside one version is not what protects the count; on the felt half it is the mechanism

A felt version's standing population is every block the record has **not
withdrawn** — not the blocks that version published. Where two publishers publish
a felt record of the same ground under one event id, that population holds both,
in every version, with no crossing needed at that instant. So a block's company
was counted against the other record's blocks covering the same cells.

## The reading, and a sentence this practice published five days ago

`reading/03-meot-part-i-ch2-individualization.md`. **Session 25 opened this
chapter on 2026-09-07 and used MEOT 63 and 66 — the two pages this session
needed — to say the thing today's measurement contradicts.**

MEOT 63 is the criterion: *"We shall speak of a technical individual whenever the
associated milieu exists as a condition of functioning sine qua non, whereas it
is an ensemble in the contrary case."* Session 25 concluded from it that the two
publishers' records are "served in one list, with no recurrence between them;
the apparatus is the un-coupling device and the association is administrative".

**That is true of the apparatus and false of the work that reads it.** There is
recurrence between the two records and this practice put it there: a block's
height was conditioned by the other record's blocks, both ways, which is what an
associated milieu is. Session 25 wrote that sentence three days after iteration
15 corrected the name and left the count uncorrected. It is not withdrawn — it
says what the apparatus does — but it was read as settling something about the
work, and it did not.

**MEOT 66 names what iteration 17 is.** A laboratory *"is thus above all
constituted by un-coupling devices, in order to avoid creating associated milieus
by accident."* *By accident* is the operative phrase: nobody decided that a `us`
block should raise an `hv` block's height.

**And the same page is the objection to what this session kept.** Three scales
stay pooled over both records, for a discipline about rescaling — and a scale one
record's extremes set for the other record's marks is an associated milieu by
accident in exactly that sense. On the Alaskan instance every mark of the record
in force is drawn at 29 % of the height its own record's scale would give it.
**Labelling the tick does not un-couple it.** The session chose the discipline
over the un-coupling, named the trade and did not resolve it — and this is the
second time in six days that this chapter has been used to license something
about this work rather than to test it. That is recorded as a tendency, not as
an accusation. Whether a reader is better served by an axis that does not move or
by a height that means something is the reception probe's question, and the
reception probe has not run.

**MEOT 64's second objection is unanswered and rests on a paraphrase.** "An
ensemble is most coherent when its sub-ensembles sit at the same level of
relative individualization" is `reading/`'s own summary of pp. 64–65, marked
there as a paraphrase; **a request for the wording and the page was appended to
`queries.md` today**, and this session did not wait for it. As it stands: the
arrival half's two records are un-coupled by the apparatus, the felt half's by a
rule this work wrote. One half is individuated by its material and the other by a
decision, and nothing in the file tells a reader which is which.
