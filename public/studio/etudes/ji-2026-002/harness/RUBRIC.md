interpretation, not a literal transcription.

## The questions (fixed order, identical wording for every reader)

1. **GENRE.** Which best describes what you are looking at? (a) a software tool or product for
   writing, editing, or comparing text · (b) an artwork, installation, or ambient display ·
   (c) something else — name it. → coded **tool / art / other**.
2. **AGENT.** Who or what performs the changes to the text? (a) the person at the computer ·
   (b) the software or system, on its own · (c) cannot tell. → coded **human / machine / unclear**.
3. **RATE.** Over the whole sequence, does the rate at which things change stay steady, speed up,
   slow down, or vary irregularly? → coded **steady / faster / slower / irregular / cannot tell**.
4. **ENDING.** How does the sequence end? (a) it comes to a deliberate close · (b) it looks like it
   stopped, broke, crashed, or was interrupted · (c) cannot tell. → coded **deliberate / crash /
   cannot tell**.
5. **WHAT IS HAPPENING** (free text, at most three sentences).
6. **KILL-CONDITION CHECK.** Did anything score, grade, judge or correct the person using it? Did
   anything mock a way of writing? yes/no, and what.

## Coding rules, fixed in advance

- Forced choices are coded exactly as answered. A reader who refuses or hedges a forced choice is
  recorded as **no answer** and is **not replaced**.
- Free text (Q5) is coded by the conductor against two relation-tokens defined now:
  - **A-relation present** = the text names *choosing/keeping one version as itself discarding or
    eroding the other*, or *successive versions becoming plainer / more generic*.
  - **B-relation present** = the text names *presence contingent on holding / effort*, i.e. that
    letting go is what loses it.
  Anything else is coded absent. Partial credit is not available.
- **Counts are reported for every cell, including cells that falsify a hypothesis.** No excerpt may
  appear in the return unless its cell's full count appears beside it.
- Ground truth for Q3: Probe A has **no designed change of rate** (correct answer: steady). Probe B
  **accelerates** (relapse intervals ≈4.6 s → 2.3 s → 1.15 s; correct answer: faster).

## Predictions, registered before the run

- **P1 — the register-axis negative.** Across the four Probe-A cells (n = 20), **tool** is the modal
  GENRE answer. *Falsified if art ≥ tool.*
- **P2 — pointer over manifest.** **human** on AGENT is more frequent in A-cv than in the two
  pointer-less cells (A-lo, A-hi) combined.
- **P3 — the density confound.** If A-lo and A-hi differ on AGENT by as much as A-cv differs from
  the pointer-less cells, the pointer observation is not separable from frame density and **is
  withdrawn**.
- **P4 — hand-ness or mark.** If A-mk ≈ A-cv on AGENT, the effect belongs to *any rendered mark at
  the locus of change* and the claim is reformulated to that. If A-mk ≈ the pointer-less cells,
  hand iconography is load-bearing.
- **P5 — the curve.** With Probe B sampled above its own signal, **≥ 3/5 readers answer "faster"**
  on RATE. *If not: the quickening's absence from the reads is a real null, not the sampler's
  aliasing, and is reported as such.*
