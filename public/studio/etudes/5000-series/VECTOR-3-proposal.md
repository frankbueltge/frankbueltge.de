## 1. The work in one paragraph

**NO PART** is *ORDER LIST: 607 U.S., Monday, October 6, 2025* — all thirty-nine pages of it — printed
at 100 % on paper of its own size and mounted in reading order, edge to edge, in a single horizontal
line at head height. Nothing is cropped, scaled, re-typeset, re-coloured, annotated, indexed or
omitted; the studio adds not one glyph. The document is 216 × 279 mm a page, so the line is **8.42
metres long and 279 millimetres tall**, and the type on it is 3.5 mm — exactly as legible, and exactly
as small, as it is on the Court's own file. A stranger walks it. For roughly seven metres they pass
nothing but docket numbers and party names, one to a row, with the answer column empty. Then, four
fifths of the way down the thirty-second sheet, one line of the same 10.02 pt type: *The petitions for
writs of certiorari are denied.* Two rows below it, the same size, the same indent, the same single
line: *The motion to substitute Melissa Powe, authorized representative, as respondent in place of
Stephen M. Redd, Deceased is granted.* From there to the end — the last 95 centimetres — the answer
column is occupied on every row, and every occupant is the Court speaking to one person: nine times to
say a named Justice was not there, sixteen times to punish, four times on a technicality, twice on
paperwork. Never once about a case. The stranger walks back. **[I]** as a proposal; every element on
its face is **[S]** and is the state's own.

The completing act belongs to whoever hangs it: **8.42 metres of straight wall at head height does not
exist in most rooms**, and what the realiser does to make it fit — where the line breaks, which
seventy people go round the corner, whether it climbs a stairwell — is theirs, is visible, and cannot
be recalled once mounted. Section 3 answers the clamp honestly, including where it does not apply.

## 3. The completing act, and an honest answer to Move 7

### 3a. The position this work seats the stranger in: **WITNESS. Deliberately, and I will not fake the alternative.**

The brief permits this and I take it, with an argument rather than an apology — and the argument is
that on *this* material, complicity is not merely hard, it is **structurally unavailable**, and that
this is the actual lesson of two dead vectors rather than an excuse for a third.

Move 7 requires a completing act that is *a consequential act of authorship about named real others*.
The complicity clamp therefore requires a **judgment**. But the finding of this material is precisely
**the absence of individuated judgment**: 761 people were disposed of without any of them being
considered one at a time, and the document's own vocabulary for consideration exists only in the
negative. So any clamp fitted to this corpus must **import a judgment the record does not contain** —
and that is not an abstract risk, it is exactly what happened twice:

- **Vector 1** imported a typographic judgment (pick the longest rectangle) because it had destroyed
  every other channel. Killed on 7a.
- **Vector 2** imported a moral judgment (write why the Court withheld) and had to spend a real
  petitioner to host it. Killed on entitlement.

Two independent builds, two different imports, two kills. **The clamp and this material are
incompatible, and the honest move is to say so and take the witness seat rather than to design a third
import.** The season's throughline is *out of the dock* — witness is an allowed destination, and the
apparatus this house owns says so on its own face: *"on an unconstrained vacated seat, the default
output is the witness pole."* NO PART does not pretend otherwise.

**What it must therefore earn instead — and this is where the gate should press hardest — is that the
witness position is not tourism.** Two structural answers, both checkable: *(i)* the yield is
inaccessible without operating (Dramaturg's condition 7) — the emptiness of the answer column is
literally unreadable from any position other than reading distance, and its *duration* is unavailable
except by traversal; *(ii)* the work returns no feedback of any kind, cannot detect a visitor, and
therefore has no verdict to hand anybody, which is the trap's load-bearing condition removed at the
root (Move 1, absolutely: **there is no machine in the room at all**).

### 3b. There *is* a completing act, it is real, and its output is content

**The realiser's act.** The work is a print-and-instruction work. Its instruction is one sentence long
and it contains one impossible requirement: *mount all thirty-nine sheets in a single straight
horizontal line, in order, at 100 %.* At 8.42 m, almost no wall will take it. The realiser must author
a rule for the break, and the rule is consequential and public: **whichever rule they choose, some
named people end up round a corner, on a return wall, up a stair, or below eye level.** The output is
not an index and not a ranking — it is **the physical shape the day's docket takes in that room**,
which is content, and it stands until the work comes down.

- **7a — self-authored criterion.** The house supplies no axis and cannot: the instruction fixes order,
  scale and height and is silent on geometry. A trivial criterion is not available in the sense 7a
  forbids, because there is no tie to break and no coin to flip — a lottery does not answer "where does
  the line turn"; the room does, and the realiser decides what the room is allowed to do to the
  document. **[I]**
- **7b — non-disownable attribution.** Honestly: **partially, and I will not claim more.** The break
  attaches to whichever entries fall at it, by name, and cannot be recalled once the work is up. But
  the realiser is not the visitor, and the visitor authors nothing. **The visitor is a witness and the
  proposal says so in plain words.**
- **7c — no re-judgment, no machine-image.** Absolutely and trivially satisfied: nothing is counted,
  scanned, stored, aggregated or fed anywhere. There is no downstream anything. There is no upstream
  anything. **The work cannot perceive that a person is in the room.**

**Move 6, residue.** What outlasts the leaving is a sentence the visitor can now say from memory and
will recognise the next time a court says it, plus a bodily quantity — *seven metres before anything
was said* — which is not a number they were told.

covers**, and in this work that number is not a statistic — it is the length of the wall, and the wall
is made of them. Remove them and there is nothing to measure the sentence against. *Right of reply?*
**Not needed, and here is the stated reason:** the work makes **no assertion whatsoever about any named
person**. It adds no word, no adjective, no ordering, no selection, no judgment; it reproduces a
published order of the Supreme Court of the United States at its own size, in its own order, in full.
The only claim NO PART makes is about **the Court**. There is nothing about any petitioner to reply to,
because nothing about any petitioner has been said. *(This is the exact axis on which vector 2 died:
5001 required a stranger to compose a new, false, signed statement about a named refused petitioner.
NO PART's mechanism makes that structurally impossible — there is no writing surface and no writer.)*

**(b) The three people under permanent filing bars** — `24-7281 WATSON, LAWRENCE B.`, `24-7381 ROSA,
CHARLENE`, `25-5294 NAVARRO MARTIN, MARIA`. The sharpest case, named here rather than left for the
gate. The Court's own words about them (*"has repeatedly abused this Court's process"*) are the harshest
in the document and the work reproduces them in full. *Required?* Yes — the finding is that the Court's
individuated voice is spent on sanction rather than on cases, and these three are that finding's
strongest evidence; softening or omitting them would be the studio editing a judicial order to protect
its own comfort. *Right of reply?* The statement is the Court's, is already public, is already
addressed to them, and is answerable in the forum that made it. The work does not amplify it beyond the
document, does not repeat it out of context, and does not endorse it.

**(c) The eight named Justices** (Sotomayor, the Chief Justice, Kagan, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, Alito,
Thomas, Barrett). *Required?* Yes — the finding is that the record names them only to record absence.
*Right of reply?* They are the most fully equipped repliers in the American legal system, and the work
implicates them **upward**, using only what they published. This is the takedown law's condition (b)
satisfied without a proxy: the work implicates power above it, not failure below it.

**(d) The visitor.** Zero exposure. Nothing collected, nothing recorded, nothing displayed, nothing
retained. **And this answers 5001's fatal corollary directly:** that design protected the participant's
name and not the petitioner's; this one protects both by the same rule — *nothing is recorded about
anyone beyond what the state itself published.*

**One further protection that is structural rather than promised:** because the work prints the
**source PDF**, not our extraction, the two corrupted captions in the corpus (`25-5182` en-dash,
`25-5278` capital Ñ) **cannot reach the work's face.** NO PART cannot misprint a real person's name,
because it does not typeset one. The corpus is used only to *verify* the plate — never to *set* it.

## 6. The finding, working inside the work

**The exact moment.** Standing at the thirty-second sheet, a visitor reads, three rows apart, in the
same size, at the same indent, each occupying one line:

> **The petitions for writs of certiorari are denied.**
>
> `24-948  GUERRERO, CHIEF JUSTICE, ET AL. V. REDD, STEPHEN M.`
>
> **The motion to substitute Melissa Powe, authorized representative, as respondent in place of
> Stephen M. Redd, Deceased is granted.**

Seven hundred and sixty-one people, and a piece of paperwork. **Same length. Same size. Same indent.
Two rows apart. [S]** Nobody has to explain this and nothing on the wall does. It is the whole finding,
delivered by adjacency, in the time it takes to read two sentences — and it is the reason *this*
document was chosen and no other body of the same size would serve (standing decision (c), satisfied
inside the experience rather than in this file).

The finding then **compounds** across the next 95 centimetres without a word of help: five recusals,
sixteen sanctions, four technicalities. A visitor who reads them all learns, from the sentences
themselves, that **the Court's individuated voice exists, works, and is never once spent on why a
petition failed.** That is a claim about how an institution allocates its attention, it is derived
first-hand from the institution's own record, and it is not reported knowledge — the takedown law's
condition (a).

**What does *not* work inside the work, stated rather than smuggled:** the 68.8 % IFP share. Reading
it requires the National Archives numbering convention, the work carries no wall text, and I will not
pretend a texture change in a digit column teaches it. **The 68.8 % is not this vector's finding.** It
belongs in the dossier as the reason the house opened this document, and this proposal does not
smuggle it onto the wall as an argument. That is the honest reading of standing decision (c): the
finding that must do work inside is the one in the two sentences above, and it does.

## 7. The form, and why it is forced

**It is not a screen, and the argument is structural, not preferential.**

1. **1:1 is load-bearing.** The finding is a proportion between one line of type and 761 rows of type.
   Any presentation that shows both in one view has scaled the type below the size at which it was
   printed and read — which is the premise this house has already killed once (vector 1) and which the
   Builder measured as unreliable in the browser (4–5 px text re-hints to legible at deviceScaleFactor
   2). **At 1:1 there is no legibility claim to falsify.** The type is 3.5 mm because it is 10.02 pt.
2. **At 1:1 the object is 8.42 m long, and no screen is 8.42 m long.** A screen version must scroll or
   zoom. And here is the sentence I want the gate to hold me to: **a scrolling version of this work
   commits the crime the work is about.** Scrolling replaces eight metres of body-scale magnitude with
   a wrist — it collapses 761 people into a viewport, which is precisely the operation the document
   performs and the work exists to make unavailable. The form is forced by the argument, not chosen
   for novelty.
3. **It is not the Grundform.** There is no apparatus, no operator seat, no console, no self-test, no
   state, no response. **There is no machine in the room.** Season condition 3 asks for a move the
   trap-apparatus provably cannot perform: a trap requires a machine that receives the visitor. This
   work cannot detect that anyone is present, and therefore cannot judge, flatter, score or process
   anybody. That is the trap's load-bearing condition removed absolutely rather than vacated.
4. **It is a form the house has never touched** — print-and-instruction work, named in the protocol's
   own list of untried forms.
5. **It does not add a second unbuilt physical promise to the queue.** This is the objection the
   Kritiker raised against vector 2 and it must be answered, not hoped past. Recovery's fabrication
   needs materials, a kiosk and a venue. NO PART needs **a printer and a wall**. The studio's own
   deliverable — the increment, committed and reproducible — is: the verified source PDF and its hash,
   a deterministic imposition/plate build (`npm ci && npm run build`), a one-page instruction, and the
   still. If Frank never hangs it, the plates and the instruction are still the complete work in the
   sense that instruction works are complete; a realisation record is added when a wall exists. **The
   studio is not asking anyone to schedule a fabrication.**

**Workshop use, kept honest:** the build step is small on purpose — rasterise/impose the source pages
at 100 %, verify every rendered page against `corpus/entries.json` (877 rows, positions, the two known
caption defects confirmed *absent* from the rendered PDF), emit print-ready plates and the still.
Complexity that does not serve the experience has nowhere to hide here, because the experience is a
document and a wall.

## 8. Nearest neighbors and daylight — argued structurally

I name the reddest risk first, as the adjacency rule requires.

**1. Dima Yarovinsky, *I Agree* (2018) — the reddest formal copying risk, one step away.** Terms of
service of major platforms *"printed…on a standard A4 wide scroll with a standard legal contract font
size and type"*, hung in the gallery with *"the number of words and the time it takes to read each
scroll on the floor"*; 9.5 m total; stated aim *"to emphasize how small, helpless and harmful are we
against this giant corporates."* **[S]**
<https://vizknowledge.aalto.fi/archive/2018/showcase/i-agree/index.html> (fetched first-hand this
session). **Daylight, structural:** *I Agree* is a **comparative** work — several documents side by
side, in brand colours — and its argument is **quantity**: these are too long to read. It is captioned
by its own metrics; remove the word counts and reading times from the floor and its finding leaves
with them. NO PART is **one** document, in one colour because the document has one, with **no metric
printed anywhere**, and its argument is the **inverse**: the document is too *short*. In thirty-nine
pages the state wrote almost nothing. *I Agree* shows a wall of words nobody reads; NO PART shows a
wall of people nobody was written about. And decisively for this house: *I Agree* would not survive
the severed test — cover its captions and it is a colour study — while NO PART's finding is carried by
two adjacent sentences on the object.

**2. Jenny Holzer, *Redaction Paintings* (2005–) — the reddest conceptual risk.** *"Silk-screened oil
on linen"* reproductions of *"declassified United States government documents mostly relating to
military activity in Iraq"* **[S]** (<https://www.artforum.com/events/jenny-holzer-4-201651/>, fetched
first-hand); the National Security Archive, whose documents underlie many of them, records Tom
Blanton's description: *"Illuminating the black, colorizing the shrouds, makes fun of the censors…
makes us think about what it is to see and not see, to read and to be prevented from reading."* **[S]**
(<https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/holzer/>, fetched first-hand). **Daylight, structural, three inversions:**
*(i)* Holzer **enlarges and repaints** — the document becomes a painting and its scale is authored; NO
PART changes nothing, and its scale is a consequence of a page count. *(ii)* Holzer's emptiness is a
**mark someone made** — a censor's bar, evidence of suppression, a secret behind it; NO PART's
emptiness **is not a mark at all**: nothing was removed, because nothing was ever written. A censored
document has a secret; this one does not, and that is worse. *(iii)* A Holzer hangs as a picture and
resolves from across a room; **there is no position from which NO PART is an image** — at reading
distance you see three rows, at ten metres you see a strip, and the work exists in the walk between
them.

**3. Banu Cennetoğlu + UNITED for Intercultural Action, *The List* — the reddest ethical/formal risk
(a long public list of named people).** The record of documented deaths of refugees, asylum seekers
and migrants at Europe's borders since 1993, compiled by UNITED and distributed by Cennetoğlu in
public space; frieze reports her position that *"Art is not CNN,"* that she does not regard *The List*
itself as an artwork, and that she has been facilitating the document's distribution since 2007 **[S]**
(<https://www.frieze.com/article/what-turkish-artists-list-dead-refugees-means-age-content>;
<https://www.theguardian.com/world/2018/jun/20/banu-cennetoglu-interview-turkish-artist-the-list-europe-migrant-crisis>).
**Daylight, structural:** *The List*'s operation is **publication** — it supplies to the public sphere a
record the public sphere does not carry, and its subject **is** the people; each line is a death and
the count is the argument. NO PART's operation is **arrangement**, not publication: the document is
already public, already downloadable, already in every law library, and the work adds no reach. Its
subject is **the sentence**, and its people are its measure. Two different acts on two different
objects — and *The List*'s subject cannot be reached by re-arranging it, while NO PART's finding exists
only in the re-arrangement.

**4. Hans Haacke, *Shapolsky et al. Manhattan Real Estate Holdings, a Real-Time Social System, as of
May 1, 1971* — the ancestor, and I claim the lineage rather than daylight from it.** Public-record
data on a slumlord's holdings, presented deadpan; the Guggenheim cancelled the show six weeks before
opening **[S]** (<https://whitney.org/collection/works/29487>). **The structural difference:** Haacke
**assembles** — the pattern is invisible until scattered records are brought together, and the work is
research. NO PART **assembles nothing**: the document is one file, one day, already whole, and the
finding is on its face. The operation is subtraction of the binding, not addition of the research.

**5. Mimi Ọnụọha, *The Library of Missing Datasets* — the house's structural check, passed rather than
escaped.** Ọnụọha's cabinet holds empty labelled folders for data that should exist and does not; the
unfillability *is* the evidence
(<https://github.com/MimiOnuoha/missing-datasets>; <https://mimionuoha.com>). Vector 2 was killed for
crediting her and then filling the folder with volunteers. **NO PART's answer column is empty and there
is no way to fill it** — no pen, no field, no writer, no visitor input of any kind. And the sharper
point: the Court is not *withholding* a reason it has; on 761 petitions it never made one. **The folder
is not merely unfilled; it was never opened.**

**6. Maya Lin, Vietnam Veterans Memorial — the register this house's Kritiker has twice named as this
material's fatal reading, so I name it before it is named at me.** **Daylight, structural:** a memorial
is an object **added** to the world in order to name people the world would otherwise not name, and its
scale and order are authored. NO PART adds nothing and authors neither: its length is 8.42 m because
the file is 39 pages, and its order is the Court's docket order. And the reading is defeated in the
first four seconds by the object itself — **nobody on this wall is dead.** These are living people
whose petitions were refused last October, and a stranger who reads three rows knows it. The one death
in the document appears as a granted motion about a substitution of counsel-of-record, which is the
joke the work does not have to make.

**7. Harun Farocki — carried from the house's declared group.** The order list is an **operational
text**: it does not describe a denial, it *performs* one. The work does not represent the operation; it
puts the operation, at its own size, in front of a body. NO PART borrows the concept and none of the
form — there is no image, no machine and no targeting view in it.

## 9. The takedown

**The sentence a serious critic would publish:**

> *"NO PART is a free public-domain PDF printed at full size and taped to a wall in a straight line —
> an act of arrangement so slight that the entire work is a fact about a court's typography, and its
> author has confused finding a document with making an object."*

**How the built work refutes it — not survives it.**

**Leg 1 — the content is not a fact; it is a distance, and the fact cannot be substituted for it.** The
sentence version of this work is *"one line disposed of 761 people."* This house has already watched a
number of exactly that shape die at the door: the 68.8 % lived in a proposal and never reached a
visitor. What NO PART delivers is not the number but **seven metres of walking before anything is
said**, and there is no sentence, chart, screen or caption that produces that quantity in a body. The
work is not a report of the finding; it is **the only condition under which the finding is
unavoidable** — which is exactly the "unguessable yield" the house's own apparatus demands, and the
reason the arrangement is total rather than slight: every property that makes this document unreadable
(thirty-nine pages, the answer printed thirty-one pages after the names, one type size, no emphasis) is
a property of its **stacking**. Unstack it and each becomes a physical fact a body cannot decline.

**Leg 2 — the finding is not typographic and it is ours.** *In thirty-nine pages the Court's
individuated voice is used thirty-one times in the certiorari section and never once about a
petitioner's case: nine times to record that a named Justice was absent, sixteen times to punish, four
times on a technicality, twice on paperwork — and the word "consideration" appears in the document only
inside the phrase "took no part in the consideration."* That is a claim about how an institution
allocates attention, derived first-hand tonight from its own file, and it is not reported knowledge.
The critic's sentence is true only of a work whose content is the number; it is false of a work whose
content is the threshold.

**Leg 3 — the slightness is the risk the work takes, not a defect it hides.** The work implicates the
Supreme Court of the United States using nothing but the Court's own published words, adds nothing that
could be wrong, and can therefore be attacked only on the ground that the Court's own document is not
damning enough. That is the argument I want to have.

real source PDF at the verified hash — **not** from `entries.json`, which is used only to verify the

**What is in frame — exactly.** A **1200 × 460 mm** window of the mounted work, **dead-on: 0° yaw, 0°

the last **168 mm of sheet 30**, then **sheets 31, 32, 33, 34 in full** (4 × 216 mm), then the first
**168 mm of sheet 35**. Vertically: the full 279 mm sheet height, with **90 mm of bare wall above and

**Therefore, guaranteed in frame and checkable on the pixel:**
- Sheets 30 and 31: **rows only** — docket column at x = 71.97, captions at x = 144.34/150.40, **zero

**Type.** No type is authored anywhere in the frame. Every glyph is the Court's, rasterised at 100 %.
**One type scale: 10.02 pt, and there is no second one — because the document has no second one.**
**One spacing scale: 23.46 pt = 8.276 mm, and there is no second one.** Both are measured, not chosen.

**Render scale.** 4 px/mm → **4800 × 1840 px**. At that scale the 10.02 pt type is 14.1 px tall and
**legible in the image**, so every claim above is checkable on the rendered pixel rather than asserted.
A second render at 2 px/mm (`-half`, 2400 × 920 px) is committed alongside so the gate can also judge
the ten-metre read where the type must become texture. *(This is the Builder's DPR law applied to a
physical work: the claim "at three metres it is texture, at 700 mm it is names" is a claim about
rendered pixels at two scales, and it ships with both.)*

**Palette — four achromatic values, and no saturated colour, argued rather than omitted.**
`paper #F4F2ED` · `ink #161412` · `sheet-edge shadow #D9D5CE` · `wall #8C8781`. The Builder must
**sample and commit** the actual paper and ink values off the rasterised PDF rather than accept these;
they are a starting hypothesis, not a specification.

> **The exception I am asking for, stated so the gate hits it first.** The still-frame law requires the
> saturated colour to be spent on the argument rather than on the interface. **NO PART spends none, and
> I argue that is the correct execution of that law here, not an evasion of it.** The finding is that in
> thirty-nine pages this Court has no emphasis and never needed one. An accent colour anywhere on this
> work would supply the emphasis the institution withheld — the studio finishing the state's sentence
> for it — and would destroy the work's own subject. There is no interface to misspend it on, and the
> single place a colour could go is the place the work must leave empty. **If the gate rejects this, the
> vector should be returned on it, because there is no second answer: the alternative is to paint on a
> court's order.**

**Judged with every caption covered, the still must read as:** *a real document, unaltered, at its own
size, changing register.* **Fail conditions, named in advance so the specification cannot be engineered
to pass:** any reading of **memorial**, **poster**, **infographic**, or **mock-up** is a FAIL. A reading
of *"a photograph of some paperwork"* is a **PASS** — that is the register this work needs and the two
previous stills could not reach.

**Two build gates before the plate exists.** (1) The rendered pages must be verified against
`corpus/entries.json`: 877 rows in CERTIORARI DENIED, the mass sentence present exactly once at page 32
/ y = 123.04, the 31 individuated dispositions present and correctly classified. (2) The two known
corpus caption defects (`25-5182` en-dash, `25-5278` capital Ñ) must be confirmed **absent from the
render** — i.e. confirmed as artefacts of our extractor and not of the source. They cannot be checked by
re-running the extractor; they must be read off the rasterised page.

## 11. Two vectors I considered and killed myself

**THE INDIVIDUATED — killed.** *[Corrected by measurement, session 49 (2026-07-30): the caption reason
below is refuted — 3 of 3 readers shown the thirty-one alone inferred a large body behind them with no
caption at all, from the Court's own docket numbers. The kill is upheld on the reverse ground: severed
from the mass, the thirty-one are not read as individuated at all. And "761 others" is 789.
`projects/no-part/READS-SESSION-49.md`.]* Show only the thirty-one. Thirty-one sheets, or thirty-one lines: every
sentence the Court wrote to one person that Monday, and nothing else. It is the sharpest material in
the document and it fits any room. **Why it dies:** the finding is a **ratio**, and a work made only of
the numerator has to assert its denominator in a caption — *"and 761 others got one line between
them"* — which is precisely the sentence the severed test removes from the room. Without the seven
metres, the thirty-one are a curiosity about court procedure. Its own strength killed it: it is
portable because it is small, and it is small because the part that matters has been thrown away.

**CONSIDERATION — killed, and it hurt, because it is the sharpest single fact I found tonight.** A work
built on the lexical finding: the word *consideration* occurs eleven times in thirty-nine pages and
every occurrence is inside *"took no part in the consideration."* **Why it dies:** it is a
**concordance finding**. Perceiving it requires counting every occurrence of a word across 39 pages,
which no visitor will do and which therefore has to be told — a wall label, i.e. an essay in costume,
i.e. the form this protocol forbids outright. Worse, it is not specific to this document: every corpus
contains words that occur only inside one phrase, and only our assertion makes this one meaningful.
**It survives as evidence inside NO PART rather than as a work** — a visitor standing in the last 95 cm
meets the phrase five times within arm's reach, having met no statement at all for seven metres, and
that is the finding arriving through the object instead of through a caption. That is the right place
for it, and the second-best outcome was to notice that in time.
