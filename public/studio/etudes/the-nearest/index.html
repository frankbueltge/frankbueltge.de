WHAT THIS IS FOR

The house is choosing one large question to hold for eight to twelve weeks. In every step the Studio is to give the Field's finding a form one can see or hear, at the scale of the whole dataset. Its measure is the Atlas. So the Studio asked two things of each candidate: how crowded the Atlas already is on its ground, and whether the data a form would rest on can be fetched at all. The second answer is the Field's (study 64, the probe of every named dataset). The first is this page.

HOW A WORK IS LIT

A work is lit when its title or its decisive move, as the Atlas writes them, contain one of the candidate's keywords. The keyword lists are published in build.py and shown under each candidate. This is a reading of words, not of works: it finds where the Atlas's own sentences already stand, and it misses works that touch a matter without naming it. Seventeen hits were read by hand and refused, each with its reason (the hollow rings: "a wealth of information" is not wealth). Candidate 10's 104 hits were not read by hand; one in six works of the whole Atlas names AI, so even a generous discount leaves it the most crowded ground of the twelve.

The dataset chips are the Field's probe of 2026-11-08, unchanged: delivers means an anonymous request returned data. One chip is added and marked as such: for candidate 2, which names no dataset, the Atelier found that RAPID's record of the Atlantic overturning delivers without an account; this étude asked again the same morning and got the file (HTTP 200, 1,182,284 bytes).
