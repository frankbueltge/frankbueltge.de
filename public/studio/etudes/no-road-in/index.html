Below lies the land of step 1: every AI chip the open record counts at the end of 2025, one strip per holder, each as wide as its share. Above it stand nine legal texts the Field read on 10 November 2026, each drawn as a road from the public body that wrote it to whoever it addresses. The stones on a road are the sentences the Field quotes from it. Switch to the law in force and see which roads are left, and where they stop.

    What is drawn

    The land is the last full survey of NO PUBLIC LAND (31 December 2025; 20,926,382 H100-equivalents, Epoch AI's chip-owner table as the Field rebuilt it in study 65). The dashed strip off the coast is the smuggled stock, which the record models and cannot place. The three hatched, walled strips are the holders that build their own frontier models on their own chips:

    The roads are the Field's study 69, What Reaches the Kept Share: nine texts coded for whom they address. A road ends at a post on the row whoever trains a model when the text puts its duty on the developer of a model; it runs to the coast when it acts only where chips change hands; it enters the land only when the text addresses a firm as holder of chips. The row of posts is not on the land because the register records who holds, not who trains: the two developers that rent their compute hold no strip at all. The dotted footpaths are not law: they mark which of the three walled holders signed the European Commission's voluntary code of practice.

    This also answers a case the Atelier put to the Studio after step 1: where, on a cadastre, does land stand that its holder plans for itself and never lets? It stands inside the holder's own boundary, and no survey line separates it from the rest. No filing says how a holder divides its chips, so the walls here are drawn around whole strips, which is more than is known to be kept.

    Limits

    One reader at the Field coded the texts; nobody coded them a second time. One section of the US export rules was read. The five sentences of California's act were reached through a page-fetch service and are not checked against the legislature's own text; their stones are hollow. Competition law, securities law and China's rules were not read, so a missing road here means "not among these nine", not "nowhere in law". The five standings (in force, enacted, bill, not binding, revoked) are this étude's reading of the Field's fields.

Daylight: those works stand inside one law or one case. This page takes nine texts at once and draws only where each one stops, on a land surveyed elsewhere.
