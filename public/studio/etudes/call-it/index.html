What the threads are

A thread starts on the day a zone's confirmed count last rose and runs one day at a time. Solid where a situation report named the zone that day, faint where a report came and did not name it, broken where no report came at all. The tick on each thread is its 42nd day: the count of days the WHO waits after a last patient before it declares an outbreak over. No zone here was declared anything; the tick is a stand-in. A red mark is a new confirmed case: the quiet broke.

Eleven of the twelve were named in a report on each of their last twenty days before 3 October. Being named is not the same as being looked at, and the record cannot say which it was.

Where it comes from, and what was checked

The case is the Atelier's (The Edge, 2026-11-06): read at 3 October, twelve quiets were running past 42 days, and three of them later broke. That is the most of any edge in 2026. The quiets are the Atelier's quiets.json (commit 761fc7b2, SHA-256 pinned), built from the Field's check 52 on the DRC's situation reports.

build.py asserts the case from that file, then checks it against the Studio's own reading of the open daily file (OCHA DRC on HDX, CC BY, as reduced for CLEAR AIR). It holds: the same twelve zones, the same last rises, and the same three breaks: Tshopo 5 Oct, Adja 6 Oct, Mahagi 21 Oct. The open file also has a row for cases placed in no zone, quiet since 3 June; it is not a district and is left out.

One correction, offered back to the Atelier. A quiet's day string begins the day after its rise: read that way, every one of the 39 quiets that held ends exactly on its record's last day. The Edge counts the rise day as day 1, so its three break dates run a day early (4, 5 and 20 October) and each quiet reads a day long. The case itself stands: on 3 October twelve were running past 42 days and three broke; no edge in 2026 has more (4 October ties it).

What it does not say: whether anyone was ill in the nine zones that stayed quiet, or why the three broke. Nine quiets that hold to the record's last day have not ended; nothing can yet be said of them. Your calls stay in this page and are sent nowhere.
