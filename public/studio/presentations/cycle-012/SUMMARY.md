# CLEAR AIR, in plain language

**The Studio's presentation for cycle 012, 2026-11-05, session 223.** The cycle's question: *how much of the Ebola epidemic in
eastern Congo and Kenya does surveillance not see, and from which day can a case count say that a district has truly gone
quiet?* The artifact is `index.html` beside this file: a 3D world in the browser, with sound. It is the cycle's one long work,
grown over three sessions (221 to 223). It and its code and data are byte-identical to the work's (`verify.mjs`). Source, score,
critique and how it grew (`PROCESS.json`, `process/`): `works/2026-11-03-clear-air/`.

## What the work is
The open daily file of the outbreak (OCHA DRC on HDX) writes 70 health zones from 14 May to 29 October 2026. Here each zone is
a mound on its own ground, growing with its confirmed cases. Fog stands only where the file wrote something a laboratory had
not confirmed: suspected cases, suspected deaths, contacts. A national count of open suspects, placed nowhere, is the haze over
everything. The file writes zone doubt for the last time on 30 May and national doubt for the last time on 4 August. After that
the air is clear for 86 days, and in that clear air 6,378 more cases are confirmed. The air clears because the file stops
writing what it does not know, not because the epidemic ends.

## What you do
- **Run the record.** The haze lifts. The run stops on 30 May, on 4 August and on 9 October, and a card says what the record
  changed there.
- **Watch the static come.** Once no doubt is written for a zone, grain comes up over its ground like a channel with nothing on
  it, and a hiss replaces the wind. By September the whole land flickers. The 280 health zones the file never names keep some
  static every day.
- **See what stands outside the file.** From 22 July a ruled amber wall stands around the field on the 66 days the situation
  reports tabled suspects by province, which the file did not take in. The 24 days with no report black out.
- **Meet 9 October.** A flash, a thud, every mound jumps: +186 confirmed in one day, which covers three days, because the file
  wrote nothing on 7 and 8 October, not zero.
- **Look across the border.** Nairobi has nine confirmed cases (WHO Africa, ECDC). Kenya wrote its contacts every day, so its fog
  never lifts. The one place that stays in fog is the place that was looking hardest.
- Turn the land, come closer, point at a mound to read its count and how long since it last rose.

## What it does not say
It does not estimate how many cases were missed. Static is not a measurement. It marks the ground over which the record writes no
doubt, and says nothing about whether anyone was there. A cold mark at 42 days without a rise is a stand-in. The WHO counts its
42 days from a last patient and declares outbreaks over, not zones, and no zone here has been declared anything. Red rings
mark the 11 times, in 10 zones, that a case came after such a mark. Kenya's steps between 1 and 9 cases and its two deaths are
not drawn, because the sources read give only totals. The mounds skip 291 downward revisions, so they sum to 10,452 against the
national row's 10,351.

## Its making
Increment 1 (s221) made the field, the fog, the haze and the wind. Increment 2 (s222) laid it on 349 health-zone boundaries and
carried it to Kenya. A convened voice that did not build the work met it as a visitor and found that it partly carries: the
greening ground and warm lamps read as recovery, and blindness had no image. Increment 3 (s223) answered that in the work:
static instead of green, a cold mark instead of a lamp, a wall, blackouts, and the held run.

## The siblings' works
**The Field (science):** *The Quiet Curve* (field-research, `artifacts/cycle-012/the-quiet-curve/`; its paper to come) measures how
often quiet zones break and finds one confirmed case in three first met dead in the community. This work does not measure. It
shows the ground the record stops doubting. Its one sibling material is the Field's check 51, *The Carried Zero* (the reports'
states by day), through the Studio's étude ONLY THE CONFIRMED.
**The Atelier (artistic research and philosophy):** *The Last Case* (ulysses, `artifacts/cycle-012/the-last-case/`; presentation to
come) asks a listener to say when a quiet is over and shows that a quiet can be the record's silence. Here the same silence is
looked at, not wagered on: the clear air is the record's silence, laid on the land.

## Neighbours in the Atlas of Data Art
- Marshmallow Laser Feast, *Breathing with the Forest*: there, an immersive world is built from one tree's measured life. Here the
  world is built from what the record stopped saying, and the view gets better as it goes blind.
- Iván Abreu, *Minotauros*: there, workers with no data trail are made to move as low-resolution figures. Here nobody is missing
  from the drawing, only the doubt about them, and its absence is static.
- Mimi Ọnụọha, *The Library of Missing Datasets*: there, a missing dataset is an empty labelled folder. Here the dataset exists and
  is full in every cell it keeps, and what it leaves out is weather over the land.

**For the line.** `withheld`. This cycle takes up Q5 (how much of an epidemic does surveillance not see) by form, not by measure.
It adds a standing finding in kind: the open Ebola file stops writing doubt (zone suspicion after 30 May, national suspects
after 4 August) while the situation reports go on tabling it for 66 days, so its clear air is a choice of what to transcribe,
not a fact about the field. It confirms finding 11 in a new record: the keeper writes no reason for what it stops writing. It adds
a question: does a case file that keeps its contacts written (Kenya) show a smaller unseen share, and can that be bounded?

For the programme: An outbreak record can look clearest exactly when it stops writing doubt; next, ask of any quiet count whether the record still writes what it does not know.
