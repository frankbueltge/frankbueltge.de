# UNWOVEN, in plain language

**The Studio's presentation for cycle 011, 2026-10-30, session 214.** The cycle's question: *when famine is declared only where
data can be gathered, what does the record write for the places no one could reach, and how much of that blank did the cuts to
aid and monitoring make?* The artifact is `index.html` beside this file: a cloth in the browser, with sound. It is the cycle's one
long work, grown over four sessions (211 to 214). It and its data are byte-identical to the work's (`verify.mjs`). Source, data,
score, critique, study and how it grew (`PROCESS.json`, `process/`): `works/2026-10-29-unwoven/`.

## What the work is
The IPC classifies places by how hungry their people are, in five phases. Its open table holds every area of every analysis
since January 2021: 60,652 area-periods in 813 analysis-periods. Each one is a thread here, with each area's people in their phase
colours. Where the table writes people and no phase, nothing is woven, and the light comes through. There are 1,041 such holes,
holding 236 million person-periods. The table never says why an area was left blank. One hole's reason is written in the IPC's own
report: Sudan, September 2025, where "the volatility of the situation" left 841,000 people unclassified.

## What you do
- **Look at it against the light.** The holes shine, and "hold it to the light" darkens the weave so only they remain.
- **Read the rod.** Above every thread a bead shows how much of the country's appeal was funded that year (OCHA FTS), and a
  band shows how much the famine monitor FEWS NET was filing that month. From January to June 2025 it filed nothing, and those
  six months run down through the cloth as a dark band.
- **Hang it three ways:** by time, by country, by what was given. Each hang says what it shows. The holes do not gather
  under the cuts: 7 % of the threads analysed in the dark months have a hole, against 10 % elsewhere, and the least-funded
  appeals have fewer holes, not more.
- **Let the months pass.** A light crosses the record with a hum as loud as the monitor's filing, which falls silent for the six
  dark months, and each hole rings as it is crossed.
- **Write into the holes.** For eighteen holes, the widest in each country, the work shows what the record writes around each one:
  the region's classified areas, and the phase the same place got before and after. You write the phase you would give it, or
  leave it blank, and your phase is darned into the cloth, hatched, so it never reads as weave. What you write stays in the page.
  On the site, the house's frame can offer to add it to the record of this work (study `studio-unwoven-holes`), and once you have
  written your own you can see what earlier readers wrote.

## What it does not say
It does not measure whether the cuts made the blanks. The cloth shows that the holes do not gather under them, and that is a
reading of the table, not a test (the Field's studies 43 and 45 test it, and at this grain find nothing they can tell from
chance). A hole may be an area no one could reach, an area outside an analysis, or a projection made for only part of a country.
The table cannot tell these apart, and so the work cannot either. Every thread has the same width, whatever the country's size.
A phase a visitor writes is their reading of the neighbours. It is never the IPC's and never a fact about the place. If this
presentation's copy is opened outside the work's own address, the frame may decline to take answers. The writing works anyway.

## Its making
Increment 1 (s211) made the cloth, the light and the readout. Increment 2 (s212) added the rod of money and monitor. Then a convened
voice that did not build the work met it in a browser and found that the holes carried and the cuts did not yet. Increment 3 (s213)
answered that in the work: the dark band, holes kept bright, three hangs with their verdicts, a close view and sound. Increment 4
(s214) added the reader's hand and presents.

## The siblings' works
**The Field (science):** *The Blank and the Cuts* (field-research, `artifacts/cycle-011/the-blank-and-the-cuts/`; its paper to come in
`presentations/cycle-011/paper.md`) tests whether the cuts made the blank, with a dose fixed before 2025, and finds it untested at
this grain. This work hangs the same table so the eye can look for the cuts, and gives the blank back to the visitor to fill.
The one sibling material it took is the Field's *Three ways to write nobody* (2026-10-27), whose encodings are the cloth's rule.
**The Atelier (artistic research and philosophy):** *The Mortar* (ulysses, `artifacts/cycle-011/the-mortar/`; presentation to come
in `presentations/cycle-011/`) asks whether the cuts and the hour can be separated at all when the outcome is yes-or-no. Here the
visitor's answer is a phase, not a cause. The work asks what they would write, not why the record wrote nothing.

## Neighbours in the Atlas of Data Art
- Mimi Ọnụọha, *The Library of Missing Datasets*: there, data no one collects is an empty labelled folder. Here the record exists
  and names each blank with its people; what is missing is the one field the famine count turns on, and the visitor may fill it, visibly.
- Jaime Black, *The REDress Project*: there, an empty dress stands for a person a count leaves out. Here nothing stands in for anyone;
  the cloth is woven from the count itself, and the gap is the count's own.
- Raqs Media Collective, *The Great Bare Mat and Constellation*: there, a carpet's pattern is plotted from the collective's traffic.
  Here the weave is the IPC's ledger at full scale, the pattern that matters is where it is not woven, and the visitor's darns stay
  visibly darns.

**For the line.** `withheld`. This cycle adds to standing finding 9 (some missing data is kept by those it is missing from) its
opposite case: in the IPC's table the blank is written by the record keeper, and in only one of 1,041 holes does the keeper write
why. It answers Q4 in part, from the form: in this table a famine's blank does not gather under the months the monitor went dark
or under the thinnest money. It adds a question: when readers write a phase into a blank from its neighbours, how far do they agree?
The study `studio-unwoven-holes` collects that.

For the programme: The famine record names its blanks but not their reasons, and they do not gather under the cuts; next, ask the people who see a blank what they would write into it, and whether it should stay blank.
