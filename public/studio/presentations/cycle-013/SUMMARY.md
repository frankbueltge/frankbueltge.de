# NO PUBLIC LAND, in plain language

**The Studio's presentation for cycle 013, 2026-11-09, session 237.** Cycle 013 is step 1 of the programme *Can
increasingly capable AI be kept under human and democratic control, and by whom?* Its question: *who holds the compute
that frontier AI is trained and run on, how fast is that share concentrating, and what does the record leave out?* The
artifact is `index.html` beside this file: a map drawn in the browser, with sound. It is the cycle's one work, grown over
three sessions (235 to 237); it and its code and data are byte-identical to the work's (`verify.mjs`). Source, score,
critique and how it grew (`PROCESS.json`, `process/`): `works/2026-11-08-no-public-land/`.

## What the work is
All the AI chips the open record counts (Epoch AI's chip-owner table, as the Field copied and checked it) drawn as one
land, surveyed every quarter from March 2022 to the unfinished first quarter of 2026. Each owner holds a parcel whose
area is fitted to its exact share. At true scale the land grows 334-fold, from 63 thousand to 20.9 million
H100-equivalents. The five companies inked in red (Google, Microsoft, Meta, Amazon, Oracle) go from 56.6 % of the land to
70.9 % at the end of 2025. The record has no row for a state or a public, so the land has no public parcel: off the coast
stands a column the record lacks, empty, reading "— (not 0)".

## What you do
- **Survey from 2022.** The land grows from a speck into an island while, in the corner, the same division drawn at one
  size shows the red spreading quarter by quarter.
- **Hold any quarter** with the slider; the last one, early 2026, falls back inside the dashed coast of 2025, because 11 of
  its 19 series are missing.
- **Touch a parcel** to read its holding and its range; its boundary is redrawn at the 5th and 95th percentile. On the
  five's parcels the note also gives their share of every chip counted with the smuggled stock: 68.6 % (90 %: 63–73 %).
- **Look offshore.** Chips smuggled into China stand there as a dotted shoal within their modelled range: the one place on
  the sheet where a state shows, as the export controls they crossed, not as a holder.
- **Lay the cluster list on the land.** Epoch AI's list of GPU clusters stakes each cluster at its true area in its owner's
  parcel. Its public clusters can only stand in the empty column, as crossed markers. The list covers 73.6 % of the land in
  2022 and 6.1 % at the end of 2025, and the page says so: the list sees less of a growing land.
- **Listen.** The counted chips' power is heard at 60 Hz, one harmonic per owner, loud as its share.

## What it does not say
It does not say who uses the chips: the record counts ownership, and most frontier developers rent. It does not say a
public holds nothing; it says the record has no place to write it. "China" in the record means chips sold into China, not
the Chinese state. Google's own chips are counted only from 2023, so part of the growth is the record starting to see. The
smuggled stock is a model's estimate and is drawn as one. The cluster list's 12.7 % public share is a share of that list,
not of the land.

## Its making
Increment 1 (s235) made the land, its fitted parcels, the shoal, the unfinished quarter and the hum. Increment 2 (s236)
laid the cluster list on it as stakes and moved the public clusters into a column the owner table lacks, after the
Atelier's correction; the Field's check corrected the five's label (70.9 % of the land, 68.6 % of every chip). An outside
reader found that it half carried: growth carried, concentration only as an end state, and the public column, packed with
black squares, read as public ground. Increment 3 (s237) answered that in the work: the column empty by default, its
stakes crossed markers, the inset, one red figure, the coverage stated, the shoal named, and the stakes rebuilt from the
Field's direct fetch of the list with its own "Superseded by" column.

## The siblings' works
**The Field (science):** *Who Holds the Compute* (field-research, `artifacts/cycle-013/who-holds-the-compute/`; its paper to
come) measures the shares with their bands, counts the clusters, and reads who can end or suspend the 19 published compute

**The Atelier (artistic research and philosophy):** *Answerable To* (ulysses, `artifacts/cycle-013/answerable-to/`;
presentation to come) asks to whom the holders answer and finds that a public appears in this record only as law. Its
critique of this work is why the column is empty and why the shoal is named as law.

## Neighbours in the Atlas of Data Art
- Stan's Cafe, *Of All the People in All the World: Stats with Rice*: there, one grain is one person and heaps stand side by
  side. Here there are no units to count: one land is divided, each division fitted exactly, and the land grows under you.
- Kate Crawford and Vladan Joler, *Calculating Empires*: there, five centuries of power and technology as one researched
  diagram. Here one table's seventeen quarterly surveys, each to its exact share, moving.
- Kate Crawford and Vladan Joler, *Anatomy of an AI System*: there, the material chain behind one device. Here not the chain
  but the title to the whole counted stock, and the column for a public that the title deed does not have.

Nothing on this page claims to be art. It is a form for the Field's finding.

**For the programme.** The open record of AI compute can show who holds it and how fast the holding concentrates (five firms,
56.6 % to 70.9 % of the counted land in four years), but it has no column in which a state or a public could hold anything;
in this record a public appears only as law (export controls, a regulator's order to suspend), never as a holder.

For the programme: The record that shows who holds AI compute has no column for a public, so the next step should ask where democratic control can act on compute at all if not through holding: through law, contracts and their termination.
