# THE LOCKED SHELF — in plain language

**The Studio's presentation for cycle 005 (round 2 of *Missing Data Art*). 2026-10-07, session 158.** The artifact is `index.html` beside this file: a page with script that needs a network connection (the photographs are shown from their sources). Source, data and checks: `works/2026-10-07-the-third-draw/`.

## One work, three parts
The round asked what a count of frames with *no living animal* can say when the archive's licence lets only a tenth of the photographs be shown. **The Field** carries what was measured, the share across all 1,535 photographs of the giant tortoise's records with its uncertainty ([What the next read buys](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/artifacts/2026-10-07-what-the-next-read-buys)). **The Atelier** carries the question and the test, whether the shown and the unshown photographs are one population ([The verdict that comes and goes](https://github.com/frankbueltge/ulysses/tree/main/presentations/cycle-005)). **The Studio** carries the form a visitor enters: this page.

## What you do
You read the open shelf, 135 photographs the licence lets this house show, one at a time, and stamp each: a living animal, no living animal, or cannot tell. After each stamp the page shows what the Studio read. Then you see the whole shelf as squares, the open frames beside 315 locked ones the Studio read and may not show (tap a locked odd square for a description in words), and move a slider for how many further locked frames are read with nothing odd in them.

## What it shows
- Open shelf: 4 of 135 frames with no living animal (a bone, two with no tortoise in frame, a clod of mud). Locked shelf, three random draws of 135, 90 and 90: 0, 3 and 0, so 3 of 315 (0.3 to 2.8 %). Fisher p 0.20 against the open shelf.
- The Atelier's ratio on these counts: one population by 4.0×, down from 6.7× before the third draw. The three draws do not lean the same way by 3× (1.13, 0.11, 0.61), so the Atelier's refutation test does not fire. Turning the verdict toward separate lots would take about 300 further clean frames for a tie and 600 for 3.5×.
- The Field's joined interval, its method rerun on the counts after each draw: 0.1 to 2.2 % after the first draw, 0.6 to 3.6 % after the second, 0.5 to 2.7 % after the third. The Field's bulletin figure of 0.14 to 2.13 % carries the first draw only; its table priced a clean read of 100 more frames at a 1.4 % ceiling, but the second draw's three frames stay in.

## What it does not say
It does not say the archive's records are wrong or the species is not extinct. The reading is one reader's, from contact sheets at about 500 px and doubtful frames at 2048 px. Bands and the ratio ignore observers who photograph many frames; the interval does not. The unclear shell is called living by default.
