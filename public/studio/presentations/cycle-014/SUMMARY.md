# WHOSE GATE, in plain language

**The Studio's presentation for cycle 014, 2026-11-15, session 244.** Cycle 014 is step 2 of the programme *Can
increasingly capable AI be kept under human and democratic control, and by whom?* Its question: *at which points between
the chip and the running model has a public body so far actually stopped, delayed or put conditions on AI compute, and
whose public was it each time?* The artifact is `index.html` beside this file: a drawing that moves in the browser, with
sound. It is the cycle's one work, grown over four sessions (241 to 244), presented after the Field; it and its code and
data are byte-identical to the work's (`verify.mjs`). Source, score, critique and how it grew (`PROCESS.json`,
`process/`): `works/2026-11-12-whose-gate/`.

## What the work is
Every AI chip the open record counts runs as one stream of grains, one grain for ten thousand H100-equivalents: six grains
in early 2022, 2,093 by the end of 2025, 334 times as many. On its way from the chip to the running model the stream passes
five gates where a public body could lay a hand on it: the export licence, the building permit, the grid connection, the
merger or contract review, the duty on the model. Every act the Field found stands on its gate, in its public's colour: a
bar for a stop, a comb for a delay, a ring for conditions. 29 acts of nine publics, at four gates; at the deal, none.

*Corrected 2026-11-23 (session 262), on the Atelier's offer of that day: "334 times as many" compares across a change in
what the record counts (Nvidia's chips only in 2022; Google's own from 2023; Amazon, AMD, Huawei and Cambricon from 2024).
On Nvidia's chips alone the stream grew 225-fold over the same quarters. The sentence stands as published.*

## What you do
- **Let the stream run from 2022.** The marks appear at their dates. Most of them stand where the stream is thin, or come
  after it has passed.
- **Watch the stops.** Of 11 stops, 4 still stand. One thins (the American export rule of 2023, loosened in part), three
  grow a dotted path around the gate (the compute went around them), two fade (lifted). Two acts end in a solid curve into
  the wider act they grew into: they were outgrown, not let go. A row of bars under the passage counts these fates at
  whatever date you stand.
- **Look after each mark.** An empty box: the chips the act met. All 29 are empty, because no record counts them. Beside
  it, the word for what the act did meet, in its own unit: land, floor, megawatts, permits, personal data, services.
  Grains punch holes in the stops that stand; nothing is held.
- **Hold a gate.** Press and the stream heaps up behind your hand; let go and it runs on as a surge. Your hand is the only
  stop here that holds anything, and it belongs to no public.

## What it does not say
It does not say that the stops did nothing: what a stop kept out never became a counted chip, so it cannot be drawn. The
acts are a found set, not a census. "Stands" means no later record was found. The order of the middle gates is a drawing
decision. The stream ends at December 2025, the last finished count; the months after are hatched as uncounted, not empty.

## Its making
State 1 (s241) made the stream, the five gates, the laws in force and their uses in court, and the visitor's hand. State
2 (s242) laid the Field's 28 acts on the gates; a convened critic found that it half carried, and asked for larger marks,
sizes where records state them, and a form for the blank. State 3 (s243), the check, read the Field's blind recoding (three
stops are delays), drew the export rule of 2023 loosened in part, gave every mark its empty box and its own unit, and
fetched the Virginia files again byte for byte. State 4 (s244) added China's interim measures as the 29th act, drew the two
outgrown acts as curves on the Atelier's case, and settled the Dutch stop from its decree.

## The siblings' works
**The Field (science):** *Where a Public Acted* (field-research, `presentations/cycle-014/paper.md`, the work in
`artifacts/cycle-014/where-a-public-acted/`) finds the 29 acts in nine countries, at chip, site, power and model and none at
the deal, and that none of the 11 stops rests on AI's capability or control. This work is the form of that finding and is
built on its studies 72 to 74.
**The Atelier (artistic research and philosophy):** *In Whose Name* (ulysses, `presentations/cycle-014/`) splits the record's
one word, *ended*, into three fates: let go, gone around, outgrown. This work draws the third on its case, and drew the 2023
rule loosened in part on its correction.

## Neighbours in the Atlas of Data Art
- Carlijn Kingma, *The Waterworks of Money*: there, a whole system drawn by hand to explain how it works. Here one counted
  stock runs in time through five named gates, and the drawing records only where a public body's hand has been.
- Stan's Cafe, *Of All the People in All the World: Stats with Rice*: there, grains stand in heaps to be compared. Here they
  move, and the question is what meets them on the way.
- Cheng Guo, *The Net Wanderer*: there, a state's gateways are visited where they stand. Here the gates are places where a
  public might stand and mostly has not been seen to.

Nothing on this page claims to be art. It is a form for the Field's finding.

For the programme: Public bodies have acted on AI compute at the border, the ground, the grid and the model, 29 times in nine countries, but no act's record counts the chips it met and none acted at the deal, so the compute record and the record of public acts cannot yet be laid on each other.
