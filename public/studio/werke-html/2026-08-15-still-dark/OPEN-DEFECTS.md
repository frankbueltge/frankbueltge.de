# STILL DARK — the register of open defects

**This work premiered on 2026-08-15 (session 96) with its open defects published, and this is
them.** Nothing here is hidden, softened, or waiting to be found by somebody else.

## Why this file exists

STILL DARK failed six consecutive premiere gates — sessions 84, 89, 91, 92, 93, 94 and 95 — with
the blocking items rising rather than falling. On 2026-08-15 the architect amended this house's
constitution:

> *A work that fails three consecutive premiere gates owes, at the next session and in the journal,
> a named decision: premiere it with its open defects published as a register beside it, park it,
> or kill it. Silence is not one of the three.*

The decision was **premiere**, and this register is the condition attached to it. The reasoning is
in `journal/2026-08-15-session-96.md`. The three memos of the seventh gate — `../VERIFIER-96.md`,
`../DRAMATURG-96.md`, `../KRITIKER-96.md` — ship unedited beside this file, as do the memos of all
six gates before them.

**The gate's verdicts, published:** Verifier **FAIL**, nine findings · Dramaturg **DELIVERS WITH
CONDITIONS**, three blocking cuts · Kritiker **PREMIERE STANDS WITH RESIDUALS**, six residuals.
**Fourteen of their items were paid in the same session, after the memos closed and on the object
they had hashed.** What follows is what was not.

## Open — the work

**1. The caveat is taller than the emptiness it stands on.** `#sd-arrive-hedge` at 1400×900 is
155 px against the 135 px of reserved hole it sits under. `DRAMATURG-95` cut 1 was blocking on two
limbs — rank and height. Rank is paid: the caveat is now at the premise's size and in this page's
commentary ink, and the staging voice measured what that bought (first-screen alpha-weighted ink
82,200 → 38,971 px², 41.5 % → 25.1 % of the screen, first place → second). **Height is unpaid.**
`DRAMATURG-96` traced who may order the remaining words and found that the staging voice cannot:
sentence 1 defines *certain* and is the staging voice's own to protect, sentence 2's ground was
installed by `VERIFIER-93`, and sentences 3–4 are `day.py`'s `share_band_condition` carried onto
the face verbatim. **Only a fact voice can shorten it, and none has been asked.** The staging
voice's one remaining lever is position, not words.

**2. The comparison is a document inside a work.** `KRITIKER-96` residual 1: the four-day block
stands 5.6 screens down at desktop and 9.6 on a phone, and the thirty-two-second run that carries
this work's argument still measures one date. The repetition limb is **met as a document, not as
an experience**. Nothing in the run performs it.

**3. Eight days is a chosen constant, and the choice flatters.** `KRITIKER-96` residual 2:
`COMPARE_MATURITY_DAYS = 8` is the only maturity in this corpus at which the four-day sequence
descends monotonically. At seven days a fifth day turns it back up — 33, 12, 7, 3, **9** — and at
four days it is a U. The block's own tier line now refuses to claim a factor, and the finding is
written against the house rather than for it, but **the constant is still one number chosen out of
several and the face does not say so.**

**4. `#sd-held` and `#sd-compare-finding` are 643 px apart, about the same mechanism, and neither
knows about the other.** `KRITIKER-96` residual 3. The body says the numerator is fixed from below
by where this record begins; the comparison says the same thing again in different words. One of
them should be doing the other's work.

**5. At 320×568 the figure and the hole never share a screen.** Measured for the first time this
session, by adding the viewport `DRAMATURG-95` named. The hole's first pixel stands at least 635 px
below the figure's top inside a 568 px viewport, so the value is 0 and no staging at that viewport
can change it without a different layout. `tools/frame.mjs` publishes the fact and no longer scores
it (the floor of 268 px / 22 chips is session 89's reading at 390×844 and does not travel).
`DRAMATURG-96` ruled the layout fact itself **not blocking**; it is a fact about this staging at a
viewport nobody had measured, and it stands here so nobody re-discovers it as news.

**6. The four rows' instants are the ladder's own last four buttons, and the page never says so.**
`DRAMATURG-96`, named but not ordered.

**7. The block spends 155 words and about 39 seconds of reading before its first number.**
`DRAMATURG-96`, non-blocking, with two named candidates for the cut.

## Open — this register's own arithmetic

**11. This file says six and then lists seven.** Added session 97 (2026-08-15), found by the
verifying pass on the delivery packet, which had handed a stranger this file's address. Line 8
above reads *"failed six consecutive premiere gates — sessions 84, 89, 91, 92, 93, 94 and 95"*:
six claimed, seven session numbers printed. `PROTOCOL.md`'s amendment, written by the architect
that morning, names **six** and stops at 94, because session 95 had not yet failed when it was
written; the journal of session 96 then wrote *"had failed six"* while its own board line listed
95 among them. Which count is authoritative — six gates before the amendment, or seven before the
premiere — is **not settled here, because settling it by picking the more convenient number is how
this record would start lying to itself.** What is checkable, and is what a reader should use: the
Kritiker's blocking memo — the thing that makes a session a premiere gate — exists in
`projects/season1/` for exactly eight sessions, **84, 89, 91, 92, 93, 94, 95 and 96**, and for no
others. Seven of those ran before the premiere; the eighth ran at it. The sentence above stands unedited
and this item stays open until a session establishes the count against the memos rather than
against the prose.

## The standing weakness, which is not a defect and will not be repaired

**Nobody is on the other side of this work.** Three critics have now written it and none has
withdrawn it: nineteen flag states are three-letter codes, a third party's machine model supplies
the word *intentional* behind every number here, and the register under measurement is published on
the same domain as the studio. Limb (b) of this house's own takedown law — real risk, implicating
power above the work — is **not met and cannot be met by this work**. It is recorded here because a
premiere is not the place to stop saying it.

## What was paid at this gate, after the memos closed

Listed so that a reader can tell repair from register. The object the three voices hashed was
`index.html` sha256 `e0f41e91…`; every item below was built after their memos were written and
final, on that object.

- **The corpus freeze leaked and is closed.** `run_day()` shelled out to `day.py` with no instant,
  so the block printed under *verbatim, unedited* — and the reproduction command beside it — were
  live while the rest of the face was frozen. A synthetic thirteenth list took that block to

  `data.py --check` to exit 1. Found independently by the conductor's own freeze test and by

  exactly the new-to-this-record counts, and that the factor is 10.3 on the falling end and 5.9 on
  the ceiling — the end this work calls unconditional. Each row now carries, computed, how many of
  its day's names this record was seeing for the first time, and the finding is written against the
  page above it.
- **Three strings called the frozen figure LIVE**, eleven lines above the sentence saying the page
  is frozen (`VERIFIER-96` finding 4). Corrected.
- **The frozen line promised conduct nothing performs** — *"this record keeps saving it"*, with no
  scheduled capture job in the repository (`VERIFIER-96` finding 8). Corrected.
- **The published-day mark stood on the wrong element** (`DRAMATURG-96` blocking cut 1). Moved onto
  the day.
- **`frame.mjs` printed a failure arithmetic forbids** (`VERIFIER-96` 5, `DRAMATURG-96` blocking 2).
  The floor is keyed to the viewport it was read at.
- **The README's guard table described a superseded object**, six cells false (`VERIFIER-96` 6).
  Re-run and re-dated.

- **`tools/live.py` would have reported a live figure the work does not publish.** Made freeze-aware

## The rule this register runs under

Anything added here is added **dated and signed to the memo that found it**. Nothing is removed
when it is repaired — it moves to the paid list above with the same detail. A register that gets
