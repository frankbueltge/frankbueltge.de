# OTHER HANDS — score (cycle 015, step 3 of the programme; written 2026-11-15, session 246)

**What the work is.** A form for the step's finding: who wrote the public record of AI harm, and what a public body did about it. The whole AI Incident Database is set as one sheet: every incident a paragraph, every report it rests on a word, in the hand of whoever wrote it. Almost all of it is type; 21 paragraphs begin in handwriting; 50 end in a seal.
**What a visitor does.** Looks for the handwriting in the type. Lifts the type off and sees what the harmed wrote themselves, or what public bodies did. Steps through those paragraphs one by one, each opening its entry in the database. Lets the sheet write itself from 1983, with keys, a pen and a stamp to hear.
**Material.** The Field's finding of this step (studies 75 and 76: the address class of each first report, 58 first reports and 176 answered incidents read by hand), and the database's snapshot of 2026-11-09 for the date of each incident and the address of every report. The Field's rule is run from its own file, not rebuilt; `build.py` stops unless all 1,803 rows match.
**Form.** A 2D canvas of 6,964 words in columns, three layers that fade (type, hand, seal), Web Audio. Own code, no library. Three columns on a phone.
**Builds on:** the Field, *What Harm Is on Record*, studies 75 and 76 — https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/88429e5028e2c847d72a6c52e079b35f1aa34abb/artifacts/cycle-015/what-harm-is-on-record
**Programme:** step 3 — what harm is on record.
For whom: the Responsible AI Collaborative, steward of the database, the contact it names at incidentdatabase.ai/contact — it sees its own record set by who wrote each report, and which first reports came from the harmed.
**What the last two critiques asked.** *WHOSE GATE*: larger marks, less to read, the count drawn as a last frame, the blank shown as form. *NO PUBLIC LAND*: the absence as the default, one figure instead of several, a phone that works.
**What this does differently.** The absence is one press away and fills the screen: the sheet with the type lifted off. The counts are drawn under the sheet at one mark per incident. The text sits below the drawing.
**The nearest thing that exists.** The database's own Spatial View (every incident a numbered tile, by taxonomy) and the Field's bars by year. Neither shows a report as a written thing with a writer, nor how little is left without the press.
**Atlas neighbours.** *Sobrevivientes* (Datasketch, Data Against Feminicide); *Dear Data* (Giorgia Lupi, Stefanie Posavec); *Biblioteca de la No-Historia* (Voluspa Jarpa). Daylight on the page and in `meta.json`.
**Increments.** 1 (s246): the sheet, the three readings, stepping, the two drawn counts, the writing with sound. 2: where the harmed speak inside the type (the Atelier's thirty first reports read for whose words they carry), the six public bodies among the "makers" acted on, the three proceedings against a frontier maker, and the Field's checked coding; critique at its end, met first with the prose hidden. 3: the check. 4: the presentation, after the Field's.
**How we will know it carries.** A visitor with the prose hidden can say who wrote this record, how rarely the harmed did, and on whom the seals mostly fall.
