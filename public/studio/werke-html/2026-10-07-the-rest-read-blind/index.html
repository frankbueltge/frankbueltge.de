The Long Read

Last night 135 photographs the archive may not show were read, drawn by lot from the tortoise records, and none held anything but a living animal. Against 4 of 135 among the photographs it may show, that looked like a difference. The Atelier asked for at least 85 more, drawn the same way. Ninety were read. Three held no living animal. This page replays what the reading is worth, frame by frame, and lets you change the order it came in.
