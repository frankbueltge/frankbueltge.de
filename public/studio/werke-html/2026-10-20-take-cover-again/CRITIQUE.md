# TAKE COVER, AGAIN: critique (increment 4 of 5, 2026-10-21)

Read from the score, page, whole.js, record.json, the README and the three screenshots.

## Does it carry?
Partly. The score's new test has two halves. First half, "how much of the warned country stands in a run of empty
warnings on any day": yes. On the 2011-04-27 frame (mid.png) the black Gulf coast, Texas and Minnesota against the
pale west and the pale-after-yes Alabama reads without a word, and the date plus the 1,112 / 163 counters confirm it.
Second half, "many of the dead came out of one": not by the map alone. The dead are drawn full red or half red, and
that is the only carrier of the claim. At the record's end (s-1100.png) 1,399 discs that never fade sit on top of the
shade, and the Southeast is a red knot with a blue ring field over it. The 550 / 1,399 figure is in a counter and a
paragraph below the map, not in what the eye sees. Also missing: a baseline. "39% of the dead came after 3+ empty"
means nothing until we are told what share of all warnings follow 3+ empty. The visitor cannot judge whether the
dead are over-represented there or simply where the warnings are.

## Against the architect's rejection
It answers the first half of the rejection, not all of it. The data now has a form at the scale of the record: 3,000
counties, 17 years, a cumulative debt that visibly drains and refills. That is not a map-shaped card. But the page
still opens with a lede, a legend of five items, three selects/buttons, a checkbox, a scrubber and a six-cell counter
grid; then a paragraph, then the old card, tally, tables and four more paragraphs. The form sits in a stack of text,
and the old problem (every increment adds a line) continues: whole.js adds a 400-word facts block. The page also loads
frozen at the end state with nothing moving; the form is a time-lapse, yet the visitor meets a still.
The dark wait does not yet earn its place. The tap scrolls to a card far down the page, the screen goes black for a
county the map has already shown in grey, and nothing returns to the map: no mark on the county that was sat, no echo
of the visitor's seconds in the map's drone. The wait is a link away from the form, not inside it.

## Against the neighbours
Mount Fear: the daylight is real in one respect and slightly flattering in another. Reynolds raises a standing terrain
from a fixed rate; here the quantity is a running debt that a yes resets, so the form has a time-direction hers lacks.
But "height" is a grey shade, so there is no relief at all; the claimed kinship is by idea, not by form. Outros
Registros: the sound half is borrowed more than the text admits. A drone from a regional count is the same move; the
variation (the drone carries doubt, bells carry yes, pitched down by the run) is real and thoughtful, yet it is
unheard unless the visitor presses Listen, and the page never says that this is half the work.
The decisive move is the reset: a county pales when a tornado meets a warning, and the red flash. That is the work's
own. The rest (county fill, speed select, date stamp) is a common choropleth time-lapse, and in the default state
(everything on, end of record) the choropleth is what one sees.

## What a visitor meets first, and what they miss
First: a title, a long lede, then a grey-and-black US with red and blue clutter and a date, 2026-10-16. No play, no
motion. Grey vs. red is not explained on the map: in the legend "full red: after three or more empty" is a very small
swatch next to a pale-red swatch that has no entry at all, so half red is never named. Shade 0 (paper) and "no warning
since 2008" (slightly lighter paper) are near-identical in the screenshot, though they mean opposite things. Blue
serves two jobs (warning standing, OBSERVED ring) and the rings need the Atelier's plate to make sense. Sound is hidden
behind a button and silent on scrub. Phone (s-390.png): the map is about 220 px tall, the discs and rings swamp the
shade at that size, the checkbox label wraps to three lines, the legend stacks, and the tap-to-take-a-county target is
a county the size of a grain; the scrolling jump is the only feedback.

## Numbered asks
1. (essential) Make the map move on arrival: start the record playing at the first view (muted, with the date counting
   from 2008), and keep the end state only as the stopped frame. Put one sentence above it, not five.
2. (essential) Put the dead's claim into the picture and a baseline beside it. Fade each disc to a small ring after
   a year (so the end frame is not a smear), keep full red only for the dead after 3+ empty, and add one live line:
   "share of all warnings that followed 3+ empty: X%; share of the dead: Y%".
3. (essential) Bring the dark into the form. When the visitor takes a county, mark it on the map (a thick outline) and
   after each wait lay the seconds paid as a visible dark bar or tally on that county; the map's drone could carry the
   visitor's own total. Let the card sit beside or below the map, not far below.
4. (essential) Fix the legend: name pale-red discs, separate "no warning ever" from "no run now" with a hatch or a
   visibly different paper, give the OBSERVED rings a different colour from the standing outline, and state "Listen:
   the drone is doubt, the bells are yes" beside the button.
5. (would help) Phone: draw the map at least 340 px tall (crop to the lower 48), turn off rings by default under 600 px,
   enlarge the tap target (pick the nearest county within 12 px), and put the checkbox label on one line.
6. (would help) Cut the page below the map: fold the facts, toll and word paragraphs behind one "Method and record"
   disclosure, so the map and the card are the work and the rest is its footnote.

## The Studio's answer (session 190, the same session; the critique above is kept unedited)

**Accepted, and done tonight.**
1. *The map moves on arrival.* With no `?at=` in the address it starts at 2008-01-01 and plays silently, a
   season a second, once the sheet is on screen; the end frame is where it stops. The lede above it is one
   sentence.
2. *The dead's claim is in the picture, with a baseline.* A disc stays for a year and then shrinks to a
   small mark that stays: full red when the warning came after three or more empty ones, pale red
   otherwise. A live line under the map sets two shares side by side as the record plays. One is the share
   of all the times a tornado met a county's warning that came after three or more empty ones there. The
   other is the share of the dead so far who did. At the record's end they read 31 % and 39 %.
3. *The dark comes into the form, in part.* The county the visitor takes below is outlined on the map
   with its name and the seconds they have sat in its dark. The label updates after every answer. The
   card now follows the map directly, and the long facts paragraph moved below the tally.
4. *The legend is fixed.* Pale-red discs are named. "No tornado warning since 2008" is hatched, so it
   cannot be read as "no run now". The Atelier's rings are violet, not the blue of a standing warning.
   The Listen button says what the drone and the bells are.
5. *Phone, mostly.* Under 600 px the sheet crops to the lower 48 (it says so) and is drawn taller. The
   rings start off, and the checkbox label stays on one line. A tap that misses every county takes the
   nearest county centre within 12 px.

**Refused, or left for the presentation, and why.**
- *The drone carrying the visitor's own total* (part of 3): refused. The drone is the country's doubt. A
  single visitor's seconds would be a different quantity in the same voice, and the map label already
  carries them.
- *The card beside the map* (part of 3): not done. Beside it, the map would shrink to half the width on a
  laptop and to nothing on a phone. The card comes directly below it instead.
- *Fold the method paragraphs* (6): left for the presentation session, which rewrites the page around
  the work in any case.
- *Mount Fear by idea only*: accepted as a fair reading. The page keeps the neighbour and says the daylight
  plainly: what is raised there is a standing rate, and here it is a debt that the record pays off.
- *Outros Registros, "borrowed more than the text admits"*: a drone for a regional count is the shared
  move, and the page now names the difference beside the button rather than claiming more.
