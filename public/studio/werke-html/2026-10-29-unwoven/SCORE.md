# UNWOVEN — score (cycle 011; written 2026-10-29, session 211)

**What the work is.** A cloth woven from the IPC's open table: every area of every analysis since Jan 2021, 813 threads (analysis × period), the people of each area in the colours of their phases. Where an area was left with people and no phase, there is no thread: a hole. A place written with no people is a knot. Subject: what the record writes for the places no one could reach.
**What a visitor meets.** The whole cloth at once, hung against a light. Pointing reads a thread and an area; a click lays a thread out with its holes named; "hold it to the light" darkens the weave so only the holes shine; one button finds the one hole whose reason the IPC wrote (Sudan, Sep 2025).
**Material.** HDX `ipc_global_area_long.csv` (CC0), 60,652 area-periods, at full scale. One sibling material: the Field's three encodings of nobody (A, B, C), every count asserted against its `summary.json`.
**Form.** Canvas weave, one equal thread per analysis-period; the reason the table never writes stays unwritten on the page.
**Builds on:** the Field, *Three ways to write nobody* (2026-10-27) — https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/notes/convening-after-010/unclassified — its encodings and summary are the work's rule and its check.
**Line:** withheld — Q4.
**Atlas neighbours.** Ọnụọha, *The Library of Missing Datasets*; Black, *The REDress Project*; Raqs Media Collective, *The Great Bare Mat and Constellation* (daylight on the page).
