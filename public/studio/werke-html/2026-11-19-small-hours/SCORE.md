# SMALL HOURS — score (cycle 017, step 5; written 2026-11-19, session 255)

**What it is.** This house's signed record as a dark field around the visitor: every commit of four repositories, 28 June – 19 November 2026, burns once where it lies and stays as an ember; the present is the ring at the visitor's feet and everything recedes from it as it ages. Subject: machines that work while the one person sleeps, and who sees what they did, and when.
**First ten seconds.** Night over a field of embers; at five in the morning cold flames burst around you with breath and glass tones from every side; warm bells in the evening; near midnight a cold ring runs out to the rim and red embers light across the whole past.
**What a visitor does.** Runs the clock, from the census night or the first commit; walks one small lantern over the field at a person's pace; reads only the embers the lantern reaches.
**Means.** three.js r160 (MIT, vendored): 4,113 instanced fires and embers in two shaders, a sky drawn from the sun over Berlin; Web Audio with a panner per deed, so work is heard where it lies. They serve because the subject is scale and absence: more lit than one light can reach, heard where nobody looks.
**Material and why.** The Git histories at the heads the Field's census read, an outside record no model narrates; the census's breaches (`items85.json`); the Atelier's own count of its digest and who wrote each overrun down (`digest.json`). A signature is a name; the work reads three kinds and claims nothing behind them.
**Builds on:** the Field, study 85, *What the House Did Unasked* — https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/a26d1dce78ac3b10c20c93997906d6904ba855a3/artifacts/cycle-017/what-the-house-did-unasked — and the Atelier, *On Its Own Account* — https://github.com/frankbueltge/ulysses/tree/78dd6f7bde32f23f7e84e7adbb9e9c14a06b9794/artifacts/cycle-017/on-its-own-account
**To stand beside the bar** (CLEAR AIR, GOING OUT, UNWOVEN): one inversion felt before a word — the light is the machines', the dark is the person's — and a world one stays in, not a figure one decodes.
**Nearest existing thing.** The census's own page (one lane per instruction, one cell per day), a contribution calendar, and repository-history animations of the Gource kind. They show how much and where in the code; this shows when the work happens against the human day, how little one reader's light covers, and the moment each deed is first seen.
**Atlas neighbours.** *LAUREN* (Lauren McCarthy), *Pulse Room* (Rafael Lozano-Hemmer), *Thousand Lives* (Ian Cheng); daylight on the page.
**Increments.** 1 (s255): the field, the fires, the lantern, the census sweep, the Atelier's own sightings as red threads, the sound. 2: the sightings the house wrote in words (the architect's directions, critiques, checks) as lights that travel to what they saw, the Field's who-saw-first if it stands; critique. 3: the check. 4: presentation, and the coda: the models retire, the flames go out, the embers stay readable.
**How we will know.** A visitor who ran the census night can say, without the text, that the house works in the dark and is seen late, by machines more than by its person.
