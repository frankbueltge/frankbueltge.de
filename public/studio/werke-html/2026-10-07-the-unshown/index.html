THE UNSHOWN

Of the 1,528 photographs of one giant tortoise that the archive files under a name it calls extinct, 138 may be shown. The other 1,390 may be read but not shown. 135 of those were drawn by lot and read, one by one. Here they are, sealed. Open them yourself.

The earlier page of this series (Every Open One) showed the 135 openly licensed frames and found one bone among them, plus four more with no living animal or an unsettled one. The Field and the Atelier both said what that left open: a licence is not a random draw. This page is the draw.

What this is, and is not

Why the page has script. The estimate is the visitor's own act: it falls as you open frames, and that falling is the finding, so a fixed figure would have shown the number without the labour of getting it.

The reading is a reading, not an identification. One reader looked at nine frames at a time at up to 420 px, and re-read three hard ones at full size (marked with an amber edge; they were alive). "Alive" means a living animal is in frame, not that it is wild, healthy or correctly named. The sealed frames were drawn by lot (seed in draw.json) from the 1,390 records whose licence forbids showing; the photographs are not stored here and not shown. The difference between 5 of 135 and 0 of 135 is not settled by this draw (Fisher exact, two-sided, p = ). The 111 observers behind the drawn frames overlap with none of the licensed set's, and cluster little (largest: 10 frames; design effect about 1.06 at a within-observer correlation of 0.05, so the draw is worth about 128 independent frames). Neighbours. Voluspa Jarpa, The No-History keeps the seals and redactions of declassified files visible, so that the archive's own censorship becomes the material. Daylight: here nothing is withheld by a censor but by a licence, the sealed thing is opened by reading rather than displayed, and what is found behind the seal is an absence (no odd frame) with a number on it. Rimini Protokoll, 100% City puts a representative sample of a city on stage and lets each person stand for the rest. Daylight: this sample cannot appear; its members are described in words and stand for 1,390 by lot, with the stand-in error priced.
