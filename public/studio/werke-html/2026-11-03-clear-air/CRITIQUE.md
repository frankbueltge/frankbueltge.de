# CLEAR AIR — critique (end of session 222, 2026-11-04; a convened voice that did not build the work, meeting it as a visitor)
Verdict: partly carries. The clearing is felt, but nothing in the picture says "blind", and the greening ground tells the opposite story.
1. The first half is strong. On 26 May the screen is a flat grey wash and the mounds are almost invisible. By 29 Oct the air is clean and the mounds stand tall. A visitor feels that it got clearer and the towers grew.
2. The second half works against the work. In clear air the ground goes moss green, lamps light and "zones lit: 11" rises. Green and lamps read as recovery, the opposite of the intent. The work shows the quiet, not what the quiet rests on.
3. Blindness has no image. I could not find the amber band at 1100 px. The 280 unwritten zones read as map background, not as places nobody wrote about. In clear frames the eye goes to the north-east cluster and to Nairobi, never to the empty ground between the mounds.
4. The key sentence ("you cannot see whether anyone looked between them") is only in the text. In the scene, clarity is a reward, and nothing makes the visitor doubt it.
5. Kenya is the one working argument: fog stays over the place that looked hardest. But it comes only in the last week, and the cloud looks like decoration without its caption.
6. The black days are the most honest gesture, but they pass unexplained in a long scrub and read as a rendering fault. The 9 Oct jump of 186 has no visual event of its own.
7. Sound: wind that fades in clear air is right, but only if sound is on. The band's rumble is too faint to carry anything. A muted visitor gets nothing.
8. Dead weight: three lines of 13 px HUD numbers, the tiny country labels. The HUD sentence does the work the picture should do. At 390 px the scene is mostly black, the mounds are pixel-sized, and the canvas is cut at the bottom.
Changes that would matter most, in the work:
- Let doubt remain after the fog goes. Give ground with no written doubt a grain, hatching or static, so it reads as unlooked-at. Take the green away, or let it turn dull grey once the haze lifts.
- Give the band and the black days weight: a wide, visible wall of suspicion on the horizon from 22 July, and a real blackout on no-report days.
- Stage the key moment. Hold or slow the run at 4 August, and let 9 Oct land as one visible burst.
