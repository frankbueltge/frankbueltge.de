# CLEAR AIR — score (cycle 012; written 2026-11-03, session 221)

**What the work is.** The Ebola outbreak in eastern Congo as one field of 70 health zones over 169 days (14 May – 29 Oct 2026), a 3D world. Mounds grow with confirmed cases; fog stands only where the open file wrote something uncertain (suspected cases, deaths, contacts); the national count of open suspects is the air itself. The air clears because the record stops writing doubt, not because the epidemic ends. Subject: what surveillance does not see, and what a quiet zero rests on.
**What a visitor meets.** A field in thick haze on 26 May. "Run the record" carries it to perfect visibility by 4 August while the mounds keep rising; wind sounds the fog. Ground greens with each zone's quiet; a lamp is lit at 42 quiet days; a red ring marks a case after a lamp. Black days have no report; an amber band on the horizon is the reports' province suspicion, outside the file.
**Material.** OCHA DRC on HDX (CC BY), all 70 zones and the national row, checksum asserted in `build.py`. One sibling material: the Field's check 51 (report states by day), via the Studio's étude ONLY THE CONFIRMED.
**Form.** three.js world (MIT, vendored); orbit, hover, a day slider; Web Audio wind.
**Builds on:** ONLY THE CONFIRMED (the Studio, sessions 218–220) — https://github.com/frankbueltge/studio/tree/main/etudes/only-the-confirmed — and the Field, *The Carried Zero* (check 51) — https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/artifacts/2026-11-02-the-carried-zero.
**Line:** withheld — Q5 (how much of an epidemic does surveillance not see).
**Atlas neighbours.** *Breathing with the Forest* (Marshmallow Laser Feast); *Minotauros* (Iván Abreu); *The Library of Missing Datasets* (Mimi Ọnụọha). Daylight on the page.
**Increments.** 1 (s221): the field, the fog, the haze, the band, the lamps, the wind. 2: the field laid on the land by province and carried across the border to Kenya, written with what WHO outbreak news gives; critique at its end. 3: answer the critique in the work and present (4 only if needed).
**How we will know it carries.** A visitor who ran the record can say, without the text, that the clear air in August is the record going blind, not the epidemic going away.
