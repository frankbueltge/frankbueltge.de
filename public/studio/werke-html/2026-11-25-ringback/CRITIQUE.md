# CRITIQUE — RINGBACK, state 2 (2026-11-26)

**1. Does it hold?** The start sentence and "Place the calls" are right: the visitor does something and is then made to wait. But the first ten seconds are nearly black: two small lights at the near corner and a faint ripple that rises in the opposite, far corner (see 4). The click at 0:15 is the one event, and with sound off it is a colour change on one dot.
A patient visitor stays for the first crossed voice at about 0:54, and that is the work's real invention: an answer to someone else, overheard on one's own unanswered call. No Answer had the unreachable ringing; it did not have that. I would show someone that minute.
I doubt most visitors stay the ten minutes. After the third passage the stage stops changing in kind: one voice at a time, one caption, the same brown ground. Listening Post holds by mass and rhythm; here it is 47 quotations in a queue. The tab title that keeps counting is a good reason to come back.
Beside the bar it is thin to the eye. CLEAR AIR has a land with bodies on it; state 2 in a still is state 1 plus a shelf of grey dots. It stands beside its neighbours for the ear, not yet for the eye.

**2. What the form makes felt before any text.** That I called and nobody picked up; that the only pickup was the ordinary emergency line; that people are talking, and not to me. That is "who is obliged to answer": felt, and strong.
Not felt: "by when" and "who answers for it". The moment the public knew is a brightening of a few seconds that I could barely find in the frame at act 8; before and after look the same. Ten acts were owed and 37 were not, and they sound and look alike; only the caption's last clause says so.
The rim says the opposite of the finding. The bodies no text names, who served the subpoenas and filed the suit, are eleven identical faint dots in an even row, the weakest things on stage, while the named receivers who did nothing glow warm. A subpoena weighs what a press release weighs.

**3. Means.** The tones, the crossed line and the uncompressed time carry the question; the lights beyond the rim only mark attendance, and the wave field is at present decoration, because it does not move where the lights are.

**4. What it lacks.**
First, a correction for the check. The ground is drawn mirrored front to back against the lights: ring and voice sources are written at `1 - z` (ringback.js lines 242, 248, 254) and the field is displayed at `z` (line 151). At 0:08 only the two near-corner lines ring and the ripples stand at the far-left corner; 9-1-1's ground "falls still" across the field from its light; a rim voice shakes the near edge. Fix this before anything else: the score's one felt fact depends on it.
Second, give the uncalled their force and the public its hour. Let a hard act from the rim (subpoena, suit, cease-and-desist) cross the whole field as a front from the far edge that visibly shakes the named, ringing lights, and let a statement stay a small disturbance. Let the light that comes when the public knows not go out again, so that every public body's voice arrives in a lit world and the maker's early ones in the dark.
Smaller. End in the form (voices stop, the rim stays lit, the rings go on alone) rather than on a paragraph of totals. On a phone, when the lines start together, the labels pile into an unreadable knot over the caption (n2-25); and the 9-1-1 light sits at the top, far from the thumb, so the "near corner" is lost.
