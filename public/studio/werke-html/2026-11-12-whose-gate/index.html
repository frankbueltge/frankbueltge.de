WHOSE GATE

The Studio · a form for step 2 of the programme: where a public body has in fact stopped, delayed or put conditions on AI compute, and whose public it was

Every AI chip the open record counts, from 2022 to the end of 2025, runs here as a stream. One grain is ten thousand chips, counted in H100-equivalents. On its way from the chip to the running model the stream passes five gates where a public body could lay a hand on it. A stake is planted wherever one did. Press and hold a gate to close it with your own hand.

hold a gate:

What you are looking at

The stream is the Field's rebuilding of Epoch AI's table of AI chip owners: 16 finished quarters, from 63 thousand H100-equivalents in March 2022 to 20.9 million in December 2025, a growth of 334 times. Six grains in the first quarter, 2,093 by the last. After December 2025 the record has no finished count, so the passage runs empty; the chips did not stop coming.

The five gates are the points the step's question names: an export licence, a building permit, a grid connection, a merger or contract review, a duty on the model. A coloured mark on a gate is a law in force there, in the colour of the public whose law it is. A stake is one use of such a law that the Field found on the authority's own pages.

The dotted channel that leaves before the first gate and passes around it is the record's separate model of chips smuggled into China: 66 grains at the end of 2025, somewhere between 29 and 159. It exists as a category only because export law forbids it.

What stands so far

Six stakes at the border, in five announcements of the United States Department of Justice between August 2025 and October 2026: thirteen people and one small company charged over chips sent to China. Each was planted after its chips had left. One is filled: a man and his company pleaded guilty. One open ring at the last gate: California's Attorney General is investigating OpenAI, under no statute his release names. The European Union has had the power to fine model providers since August 2026 and has used it on no one its page names. No penalty imposed was found under any of the three laws.

On this sheet no public body's hand has yet stopped, delayed or put a condition on a single grain. That is a statement about what has been read, not about the world: licences refused, permits withheld, connections delayed and mergers reviewed are exactly what the Field is reading in this step, and the three unread gates wait for it.

Your hand

Holding a gate shows what a stop would hold: the stream heaps up behind your bar, and runs on as a surge when you let go. Your hand is nobody's public. It is there so the stakes can be read against it.

Sources and method

Built from two files of the Field, read at pinned commits and checked by hash at build time: study 65, Who Holds the Compute (Epoch AI's chip-owner table, CC BY 4.0, epoch.ai/data/ai-chip-owners) and study 70 in What Reaches the Kept Share (twelve files fetched from their publishers; each stake below links the release it rests on). build.py asserts every count on this page against those files. People who were charged are not named here; a charge is an accusation, not a finding.

Limits

The passage is a diagram of the question, not of any one chip's journey: a chip that never crosses a controlled border never meets the first gate's law, and the order of the middle gates is a drawing decision. Which gate a law stands at is this page's reading of the Field's record. The grains are rounded: one grain is ten thousand H100-equivalents, and the first quarter's six include everything counted before 2022. The uses are one day's reading of the authorities' pages by the Field; no court docket was read. The date California's act took effect is second-hand in the Field's record.

Neighbours

Here one counted stock runs in time through five named gates, and the drawing only records where a public body's hand has been.
