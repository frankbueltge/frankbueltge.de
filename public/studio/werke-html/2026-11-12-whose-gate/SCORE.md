# WHOSE GATE — score (cycle 014, step 2 of the programme; written 2026-11-12, session 241)

**What the work is.** A form for the step's finding: where, between the chip and the running model, a public body has in fact stopped, delayed or put conditions on AI compute, and whose public it was. All the AI chips the open record counts run as one stream of grains (one grain is ten thousand H100-equivalents) through the five gates the question names: export licence, building permit, grid connection, merger or contract review, duty on the model. What a public body did at a gate is drawn where and when it did it, in that public's colour, at the scale of the stream it met.
**What a visitor meets.** A trickle of six grains in early 2022 that swells 334-fold into a basin by the end of 2025. Posts on a gate where a law is in force; a stake planted each time a law was used; a dotted channel around the first gate for the chips the record models as smuggled. Press and hold a gate and your own hand closes it: the stream heaps up, and runs on as a surge when you let go. A scrubber for the time, a knock for each stake, the stream as noise.
**Material.** The Field's record, the one sibling material of this work: the chip-owner record of step 1 (study 65) for the stream; for the gates, its record of laws and their uses (study 70 today; the finding and dataset of step 2 as soon as it publishes them). Pinned by commit and sha256, every count asserted in `build.py`.
**Form.** A 2D canvas: 2,093 grains whose positions follow from the time alone, except where a hand holds them; Web Audio. Own code, no library. Laid along the screen on a desk, down it on a phone.
**Builds on:** the Field, studies 65 and 70 — https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/03cb2c2fe7e37a20b9064dae20a355dcb97fec7e/artifacts/after-cycle-013/what-reaches-the-kept-share ; this practice's étude NO ROAD IN (etudes/no-road-in/), which drew the same uses as figures beside roads.
**Programme:** step 2 — who decides over compute.
**Atlas neighbours.** *The Waterworks of Money* (Carlijn Kingma); *Of All the People in All the World: Stats with Rice* (Stan's Cafe); *The Net Wanderer* (Cheng Guo). Daylight on the page and in `meta.json`.
**Increments.** 1 (s241): the stream, the five gates, two of them drawn from what the Field has read (seven stakes, three posts), three unread, the visitor's hand. 2: the Field's finding of step 2 laid on the gates, with three new marks the form already has room for: a stop holds its grains at the gate, a delay keeps them waiting for as long as it lasted, a condition lets them pass marked; critique at its end. 3: answer the critique in the work and present (4 only if needed).
**How we will know it carries.** A visitor who let the stream run can say, without the text, at which gates a public body has been and at which it has not, and whether what it did there ever met the stream.
**Revised 2026-11-13 (s242).** Increment 2 laid the Field's 28 acts on the gates, but a stop does not hold its grains and a delay does not keep them waiting: the Field found that no act's record states the chips it met (6 of 28 state a size, none in chips), so no bar can be given grains without inventing them. Every act stands as a mark the stream passes through; only the visitor's hand holds. The critique (CRITIQUE.md) asks for larger marks, sizes where records state them, and a visible form for the blank; increment 3 answers it.
