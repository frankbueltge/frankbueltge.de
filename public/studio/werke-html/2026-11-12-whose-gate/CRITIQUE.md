# Critique — WHOSE GATE, end of session 2 (2026-11-13)

The stream is good. Grains thicken from a trickle (2022) to a packed basin (2025), and that growth reads without words. The vertical layout on a phone holds up too. Everything else asks the visitor to read.

What a visitor sees at 1100px: five thin vertical posts, each carrying a stack of small coloured squares and circles about 10px wide. At full count that is 28 marks beside a 130px tall field of grains. The marks are tiny against the stream. The legend says "stop, delay, condition", but a stop is a small bar the grains do not visibly feel.

Does it carry? Partly. "Where": yes. Border and ground are crowded, power is thin, the deal gate is bare, and the model gate sits beside the busiest grain flow. That reads as a map of attention, and the empty merger gate is a real finding. "Whose": the colour dots work, but ten hues need a legend, and the US red on the border is the only one that stays in the eye. "What lasted": weakly. Lifted acts are faded and hollow, but at 10px a pale square against a cream ground is nearly invisible. "4 stand, 6 lifted, 3 gone around, 1 replaced" is only in a sentence under the slider. The drawing shows 3 gone around as one dotted loop and a cluster of 66 smuggled grains. That is the strongest idea in the piece, but it hangs in the lower left, away from the gates it bypassed.

Nothing met the stream: this is the central claim and the drawing does not make it. The grains pass the bars at the same density whether a stop is standing or lifted, because "its record states no size, so none is held". That is honest, but it is a missing mark presented as a statement ("none is held" is text, not form). Nothing in the drawing says "these acts have no number". The planned stop that holds its grains, a delay that makes them wait, and a condition that marks them were not built, and increment 2's own score promised them. Dragging your hand on a gate is the only place the stream is actually stopped, and the work never connects that to the 28 acts.

After December 2025 the field runs empty and says so in italics across the middle. That is correct, but the visitor sees a work that died, not a record that ended.

Sound: I did not judge it by ear. It is a knock per stake, which is a pulse, not the dataset.

The three changes that matter most:
1. Make an act's size visible. Give each mark a height or width that is a real quantity where the record has one (the $4.5 billion charge, the $60 million sold, the MW of a grid refusal), and a distinct hollow "unsized" form where it has none, so that the work shows how much of the record is blank. Nothing now shows the 28-versus-0 asymmetry.
2. Let the standing stops hold something. A standing bar should visibly catch and divert grains, and a lifted one should let a ghost of the pile fall away. Today the bars sit among grains like labels. Without this, "what lasted" is only fading.
3. Scale up and de-label. Marks at least 3x larger, stops running the full height of the passage, and one count (4 stand, 6 lifted, 3 around, 1 replaced) drawn as the last frame instead of written. Pull the dotted bypass up to the border gate where it began, and drop the long list from the page's first screen.
