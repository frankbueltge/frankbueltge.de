How big is "one in a hundred and thirty-five"?

The licensed 135 are a census of what can be shown, not a draw from the 1,520: there is no sampling error inside them, and an unknown amount of error in carrying them to the rest. The interval above treats them as if they were a random draw; they are not. Who chooses to publish a photograph under a licence is the one thing no field here can test.
