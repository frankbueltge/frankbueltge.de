# EARSHOT — score (cycle 018, step 6 of the programme; written 2026-11-22, session 260)

**What it is.** A building at night, seen from the street. Every signal that eleven public texts on advanced AI set up (51: a report, a record, a notice, a list) is its own passage, spoken in the room of the one it is addressed to: a public body, the firms downstream, the maker's own record, or the street, which is the public. Subject: knowing in time, and who is placed to know.
**First ten seconds.** A lit office building behind frosted glass; at the release its rooms wake one after another with muffled voices and light whose lines cannot be read, while in the street, where you stand among strangers, clear voices say the published texts and their words appear as they are said.
**What a visitor does.** Waits for the deed and listens to which rooms wake, and when, on a clock of powers of ten from a minute to two years; clicks any room to stand where its reader stands, and then that room is clear and the street is behind the wall.
**Means.** Neural speech from an open model run offline (one voice per text), word times from an open recogniser, Web Audio with every voice on two paths (inside, or through two low-pass walls) and placed in space, a canvas building in perspective whose rooms are drawn behind frosted glass. The question is about hearing in time, so its form is a place of hearing; a reader's position decides what reaches them, so position is the one thing the visitor can change. Weighed: a radio waterfall of the 51 (a display, not a place) and a 3D corridor (the kind of scene of SMALL HOURS). New to this practice: voices, words tied to speech, a wall as a filter, and a machine listener as the check of what a wall lets through.
**To stand beside the bar** (CLEAR AIR, GOING OUT, UNWOVEN). One inversion felt before a word: the clear voices at your ear say nothing of the deed; everything about the deed is said behind glass.
**Nearest existing thing.** The Field's own page of the same 51 (one row per signal on a clock, four squares each), the instruments' own texts, regulators' incident-reporting pages. They show who must report what by when; this lets you stand outside and hear that it happens without you.
**Material and why.** The Field's study 90, every passage quoted, every code, at its commit: the step's evidence, offered to this practice. The voices say only the passages, never anything a model wrote.
