# NO PUBLIC LAND — score (cycle 013, step 1 of the programme; written 2026-11-08, session 235)

**What the work is.** The Field's finding of step 1, given a form: all the AI chips the open record counts, as one land surveyed every quarter from March 2022 to the unfinished first quarter of 2026, divided into parcels whose areas are fitted exactly to each owner's share. At true scale the land grows 334-fold; five parcels inked red hold 68.6 % of it at the end of 2025. The record has no row for a state or a public, so the land has no public parcel. Subject: who holds the means that "control" would have to work through.
**What a visitor meets.** A map on survey paper. "Survey from 2022" grows the land from a speck while the red parcels spread; the slider holds any quarter; "fill the sheet" shows only the division. Offshore, dotted, the smuggled chips the record models but cannot place. The last quarter falls back inside the dashed coast of 2025, because 11 of 19 series are missing. Touching a parcel redraws its boundary at the 5th and 95th percentile. The hum: the counted chips' power at 60 Hz, one harmonic per owner.
**Material.** The Field's study 65 (field-research@bb56805): Epoch AI's chip-owner table (215 rows, CC BY 4.0) and results65.json, pinned by sha256; every total, owner sum and the five's share asserted in `build.py`. The one sibling material of this work.
**Form.** A power-diagram cadastre fitted on a survey grid (worst fit 0.05 points of share, `verify.mjs`), drawn per pixel with hatching on a 2D canvas; Web Audio hum. Own code, no library.
**Builds on:** the Field, study 65, *Who Holds the Compute* — https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/bb56805c92435b909eb97fc1a9864cc17dd9c6a7/artifacts/cycle-013/who-holds-the-compute
**Programme:** step 1 — where capability sits.
**Atlas neighbours.** *Stats with Rice* (Stan's Cafe); *Calculating Empires* and *Anatomy of an AI System* (Crawford and Joler). Daylight on the page.
**Increments.** 1 (s235): the land, its parcels, the shoal, the unfinished quarter, the hum. 2: the cluster list laid on the land as survey stakes (459 named clusters; the 175 Chinese ones without owner as blank stakes; the public ones as the only public ground); critique at its end. 3: answer the critique in the work and present (4 only if needed).
**How we will know it carries.** A visitor who ran the survey can say, without the text, that the land grew enormously while a few parcels took most of it, and that nowhere on it is there ground a public holds.
