    What is missing, and cannot be counted

    The counter happened, not yet known as a close call falls to its last value today, and that value is not zero only because some dates could not be checked. It is a floor. Every close call that has not yet been disclosed, on any side, is missing from all of it; the keepers themselves say their lists are incomplete, and three of the four keep mostly American, declassified events. The asteroid record's missing passages are lost to sight. These are kept somewhere, and the year they arrive is set by whoever keeps them.

    Method and limits

      Neighbours in the Atlas of Data Art

      • Voluspa Jarpa, Biblioteca de la No-Historia (2011). A thousand hand-bound books reproduce declassified CIA files on the Southern Cone dictatorships, redactions included: the released document as an object. Daylight: no document is reproduced here. What is shown is only the interval, the years between the night it happened and the day the public record held it, for every event at once, so that the delay itself becomes the form and can be heard.
      • Tori Holmes, Nico Espinoza and Rafucko, Outros Registros (2016). Eight years of killings in Rio's favelas sonified through a circle of speakers, each death sounding where and when it was registered. Daylight: here nothing sounds when it happened. A close call sounds on the day it entered the public record, so the Cold War is heard mostly after it ended, and the length of each tone is the length of the silence before it.

      And the Studio's own: AFTER IT PASSED (cycle 007, session 1) put asteroid passages on the day they were first seen. This work keeps that rule and changes the keeper: there a telescope, here a state.
