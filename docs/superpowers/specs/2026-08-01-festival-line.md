# The festival line: positioning under proof (decision record, 2026-08-01)

**Occasion.** Frank's goal, stated 2026-07-31 late session: make the research ecology strong
enough as a data-art project to submit to transmediale / Ars Electronica / ZKM within 1–2
years — "genau das ist das ziel". Basis of this document: a full-ecology review (deep surveys
of all three practice repos, the contact zone, the site, and MRR; first-hand verification of
the load-bearing works; festival facts checked against primary sources on 2026-08-01).

**Status.** Direction decided by Frank ("ja, das können wir gerne machen, von a bis c").
Site-wording changes stay behind the wording gate (`src/config/naming.ts`, wording-kanon);
changes to practice constitutions travel as **seeds/offers** under each constitution, never

rebranded as artist.

## 1. Positioning: this document supersedes the 2026-06-20 framing

`2026-06-20-ehrliche-umrahmung-design.md` ordered the site to stop claiming art ("Kein Satz
behauptet, bereits Kunst zu sein"). Its honesty instinct stays binding: **no unearned
claims.** But the mechanism changes, because the situation changed — the ecology now exists,
`naming.ts` calls the Atelier "machine-run artistic research", and the world-contact
adjustment already speaks of practices working "under proof". The two framings contradicted
each other in public; per the house rule, the older one is now superseded **visibly and
dated**, not silently.

**The new line: claim and show receipts.** Not *refusing* the claim (the June mechanism),
but *earning* it in the open: artistic research, **under proof**.

**The one-sentence thesis** (this is the form of the ecology, not a content umbrella — the
constitution's ban on permanent shared themes, `01-CONSTITUTION` §2, stays fully intact; no
practice bends its subjects to this):

> Verifiability as an aesthetic principle: machine research practices whose authorship,
> failures and dissent can be checked in a public archive — not asserted.

This is the point-by-point answer to the Ai-Da criticism ("cynical bluff" via concealed
mechanics) and the property no comparable project (Botto, Ai-Da, Herndon/Dryhurst) delivers
at this depth. It is also simply what the ecology already does.

## 2. Eight concept adjustments

The review found four structural under-specifications and their consequences (0 external
deliveries in five weeks; apparatus outgrowing work in every practice; a completed joint
inquiry the contact zone never registered; nothing ever physically built). The adjustments,
each with its landing place:

1. **The addressee becomes a constitutional category.** A work is finished not when it
   passes the gate but when it reaches a receiver who can contradict it ("the delivery is
   its second premiere" — the NO PART critic letter). Delivery, reception and outside
   contradiction become first-class event types in the encounter grammar, equal to
   offer/refusal/correction. *Lands:* contact-zone grammar + practice seeds; extends the
   world-contact adjustment (2026-07-31) from a metric to a concept.
2. **Independence comes cross-practice, not from prompt-separated roles.** Before any
   publication, one review by a sibling practice (different protocol, different evidentiary
   standards — real diversity), dissent preserved, never adjudicated away. Precedent exists
   and worked once: the Hammond dissent (2026-07-25). *Lands:* practice seeds; the
   federation gains an epistemic function beyond bookkeeping.
3. **Work-before-apparatus becomes a meta rule.** Any new procedural text must name the work
   it serves, or it is not written; apparatus growth becomes a tracked danger indicator in
   every practice (Ulysses has the category — generalize it); protocol moratorium through
   2026. Evidence: the strongest work of every practice was made by breaking or predating
   its own apparatus (NO PART broke the Season procedure; Ulysses' model-collapse trilogy
   predates the project system; Field's 016 was an outward expedition). *Lands:* seeds.
4. **Meridian gets an outward mandate and a named series.** Self-audit counts as
   maintenance, not research; the 016→017 line becomes a declared programme — *the
   instruments of the instruments*: the evidence infrastructure of the AI age, put on the
   stand. Series accumulate weight that one-offs cannot. *Lands:* seed to field-research.
5. **Ensemble gets the freedom the concept claims.** Gate-free études as a protected wild
   space; guest voices actually convened; **fabrication as a right with a standing budget,
   not a request that stalls** (the constitution demands "presence", yet three fabrication
   requests sit deferred and nothing was ever built — the most direct contradiction in the
   whole concept); reception (`/reception`) becomes part of the concept. *Lands:* seed to
   studio + Frank's budget decision (§6).
6. **Ulysses gets the artifact default back, and a structural role.** Prose-only closure
   becomes the exception that needs justification (currently 10 of 12 projects end without
   any artifact); "error is relational — a value against its own claimed precision" is named
   as the practice's research programme; and Ulysses becomes the ecology's **reflexive
   organ** — it writes the cross-practice reviews of adjustment 2, as it already did once
   (Hammond). That makes "in between" a function instead of a mood. *Lands:* seed to
   ulysses.
7. **The Middle turns from recorder into convenor.** When Local Returns exist, the contact
   zone must assemble and register the joint dossier within a deadline — no authority over
   practices, but a duty of real-time bookkeeping. Evidence: ji-2026-002 was taken up and
   answered by all three practices on 2026-07-25 (Meridian instrument 018; Ulysses
   "signature in the world"; Ensemble's 25-reader étude) and the fixture still said
   "PROPOSED / no practice has accepted" — despite Ulysses asking twice for registration.
   The Aktualitäts-Regel applies to the federation itself. *Lands:* research-ecology
   routine (scribe) + this line's task (c).
8. **The human becomes a measured part of the apparatus.** The ecology measures everything
   except Frank: gate decisions get a deadline (proposal: 72 h) and a public ledger;
   fabrication gets a standing budget (pattern: Ulysses' €10/month cost ceiling); the post
   office gets a duty rota. This dissolves the bottleneck *and* is the jury-proof
   authorship story — under the Thaler v. Perlmutter line, authorship lives in the human
   decisions around the model; here those decisions become inspectable themselves.
   *Lands:* Frank's own process; governance docs.

**The body clause (cross-cutting):** juries decide primarily on ~3 minutes of video, and
works like NO PART argue with the body. NO PART's item 20 ("what comes back") is
generalized: every major work gets a spatial realization plus a performance record (video,
photographs, room data) as part of its delivery duty. Git stays the archive; the record of
embodiment joins it.

## 3. What does not change

Sovereignty and local protocols; the ban on a permanent content umbrella; kill discipline;
honesty tiers (verified/sourced/imagined); dissent preservation over adjudication; the
deterministic Protocol (test-protected strings); archive immutability and no backfill;
open licences (Apache 2.0 / CC BY 4.0 / CC0, seed exception CC BY-NC-SA); the no-vendor
voice rule. These are the identity — and the unique selling point.
