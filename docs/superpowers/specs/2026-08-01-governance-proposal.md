# Governance proposal — the human joins the apparatus (2026-08-01)

**Status: PROPOSAL — Frank decides.** Drafted by the conductor session on Frank's
festival-line commission (`2026-08-01-festival-line.md`, §2 item 8). The ecology measures
everything except its human: gate decisions have no deadline and no ledger, fabrication
requests stall as "deferred, not declined", prepared letters sit unsent. Each of these is
currently an invisible bottleneck; this proposal turns them into visible, inspectable
commitments. That is also the authorship story a jury can check: under the Thaler v.
Perlmutter line, authorship lives in the human decisions around the model — here those
decisions get their own public record.

Nothing below binds a practice; every item binds **Frank**, at his option. Numbers are
proposals; only the shape is argued for.

## 1. Gate service level + public gate ledger

- Publication-candidate decisions within **72 hours** of landing at the gate — decide, or
  write a dated "held, because …" line. Both are legitimate; silence is neither.
- A **gate ledger** (public, per practice): date in · candidate · decision · date out.
  Home: the Steuerzentrale writes it, a small public surface renders it (implementation
  follows sign-off; no new infrastructure before the first rows exist).
- **Current queue, honestly:** Ulysses' Operative Ruler v3 has waited since 2026-07-25 —
  seven days, spanning the correction and repair the practice itself built in the
  meantime. It would be the second publication in the Atelier's history.

## 2. Standing fabrication budget (Studio first, pattern for all)

- Pattern precedent: Ulysses' standing delegation carries a €10/month external-cost
  ceiling — the shape works; fabrication needs a physical-world equivalent.
- Proposal: **€150 per quarter** standing fabrication ceiling for the Studio; any single
  item above €150 needs an explicit go. Unspent budget does not roll over (no hoarding
  incentive).
- **First draw, concrete:** the NO PART wall — 39 sheets printed at 100 % on plain stock
  (print cost roughly €40–90 at any copy shop), mounted per INSTRUCTION.md, recorded per
  its item 20. The house's own critic named the condition: "It still has to touch the
  world twice: as a wall a body walks, and as a packet that reaches a receiver who can
  contradict it." The packet is written; the wall is a two-digit sum.
- The three deferred fabrication requests (Native-Speaker turnstile, Recovery kiosk,
  One-Tap fountain) are **not** auto-approved by this budget; they re-enter through the
  normal path once the ceiling exists — deferred stays a decision, stalling stops being
  one.

## 3. Post-office duty rota

- Prepared letters in the `/post` ledger are **forwarded within 7 days**, or the ledger
  carries a dated delay note. Reception is recorded faithfully, including silence, and
  scores nothing (per the world-contact adjustment §2 as adapted by the practices).
- First items in the queue: the NO PART delivery packet (written, unsent) and instrument
  016 to Forensic Architecture and the Internet Archive (the two parties who can
  contradict it).

## 4. The MRR leash

- MRR's own review (meridian-runtime,
  `docs/design/2026-07-31-mrr-review-und-integrationsrichtung.md`) names the bottleneck:
  not technology, but the missing second research question from a practice. Proposal, in
  its own spirit: **no further MRR construction without a named practice need** — a
  concrete request from Meridian, Ulysses or Ensemble, recorded in a channel. Maintenance
  and honest archiving stay free; speculative capability building pauses.

If the number is wrong, say the right one — a kept 7-day promise beats a broken 3-day one,
and the practices' standing rule (silence past the practice's own next session reads as a
decision) stays the backstop either way.
