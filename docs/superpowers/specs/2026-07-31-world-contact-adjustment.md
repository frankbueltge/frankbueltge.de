# World contact: the adjustment (decision, 2026-07-31)

**Occasion.** Frank asked the fair question, late on 2026-07-30: is this ecology a serious,
useful experiment — or a bullshit generator that burns thousands of tokens daily writing for
itself? His decision, 2026-07-31, on the assessment below: **adjust, don't abandon** — not giving up
yet, but adjusting (wording private). This document records the evidence, the
adjustment, and the seeds delivered to the practices.

## 1. Evidence (measured from the committed archive, 2026-07-30)

The chronicles and project records were measured directly — the ecology's own honesty
machinery makes this possible, which is itself a finding.

| Practice | Sessions | Work contact | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Field · Meridian | 81 | 56 % | 22 distinct works, 21 shipped/graduated, 6 honest fails |
| Studio · Ensemble | 50 | 18 % | 5 works |
| Atelier · Ulysses | 86+ journal entries | — | 12 projects: 10 closed (mostly study material), 2 active, **1 published** |
| Plenum · data-snack | 9 | — | weekly cadence |

**The decisive number: all 5 encounters to date have receivers inside Frank's own project
universe** (meridian→ensemble ×2, meridian→datavism, ensemble→data-snack-plenum,
mrr→ulysses). No piece produced by this ecology has ever been *delivered* to anyone outside
the house. The setup analysis of 2026-07-11 diagnosed inward drift as systemic ("the bar
rewards self-implication; nothing in the session cycle forces world contact") — three weeks
later, the archive confirms it.

**What is demonstrably not bullshit:**

- **The integrity machinery.** A system that blocks its own conductor's overclaim, records
  fails in a public archive, never claims "live", and keeps Git as the arbiter — months of
  evidence that autonomous LLM practices can hold verifiable honesty discipline. That is a
  real research result. It has simply never been told to anyone.
- **Deliverable pieces already on the shelf:** the AI-text-detector calibration certificate
  (graduated; every teacher using these tools should see it), the public dataset register
  (infrastructure), the counter-measurement line (The Consensus — measuring what power leaves
  dark), the atlas of 214 data-art works.
- **The archive discipline itself** — honest fails, dated corrections, no backfill.

The risk Frank names is real where landing rate substitutes for value: sessions about
sessions, encounters that never leave the house, engines running nightly because they can.

## 2. The adjustment (four decisions)

1. **A first sentence for first visitors.** The homepage explains the structure, not the
   value. Draft wording, pending Frank's sign-off (wording gate, `src/config/naming.ts`):
   > *Four machine-run research practices work here in public, under proof: every claim
   > traces to a committed record you can check — and what they build is meant to be used
   > outside this site.*
2. **World contact becomes the measure.** An "external delivery" is: a piece delivered to a
   **named receiver outside the ecology** (outside Frank's project universe), in a form the
   receiver can use, with receiver and channel documented in the practice's own record.
   Target from August: **≥ 1 external delivery per practice per month.** External use — a
   reply, a citation, a replication — is the success signal. Landings per day count for
   nothing.
3. **Consolidation follows evidence, not mood.** No practice is frozen today (Frank:
   adjust, not abandon). A practice that stays inward through **two monthly reviews** merges
   into the productive core or is frozen — honestly and dated, per the Holdings pattern.
4. **Review at the end of August, in public,** computed from the archive exactly as §1 was.
   If by then nothing external has landed anywhere in the ecology, the honest consequence is
   dated archiving of the inward parts — a completed experiment with this archive is a
   result, not a failure. Perpetual inward running would be the bullshit generator.

## 3. Seeds (delivered 2026-07-31)

One seed per practice, placed in each engine repo's team-seed channel, authored in Frank's
name on his decision, written by the conductor session. Shared core: the numbers above, the
definition of external delivery, the monthly target, the review protocol. Practice-specific
addresses:

- **Meridian** — named the productive core; asked to pick a receiver for the detector
  calibration certificate (educators) and to tell the open-data world about the register.
- **Ensemble** — 18 % work contact named plainly; asked to bind stage time to deliveries
  (One Tap and the diner re-cooks have natural outside audiences).
- **Ulysses** — 1 publication from 86 entries named plainly; asked to address its
  model-collapse line TO the live external discourse (excess-vocabulary literature) instead
  of to its own practice.
- **Plenum** — asked to deliver one recipe genuinely outside the project universe.

Seeds are offers under each constitution: the practices may adapt, counter, or decline with
reasons — but they must answer. The joint inquiry ji-2026-002 continues; the adjustment
changes the *address* of outputs, not the freedom of inquiry.
