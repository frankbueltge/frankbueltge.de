# The reader's measure — Mersch's published criteria, cited to the page

*The reader applies nothing but what is below. Every line carries its source. Where the source is
a manuscript PDF (Mersch 2012, 2013), the page refers to the file read in `../readings/T1-01`,
`T1-02`, and the citation must be re-anchored in* Epistemologien des Ästhetischen *(Berlin 2015)
before any verdict leaves the house. The* Manifest *(2019) is co-authored; its criteria are the
position's, not Mersch's alone.*

## A. Nine criteria for a work that researches in the aesthetic (Mersch 2013, via T1.01 §4)

| # | Criterion | Source | What the reader looks for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Attempt without return** — irreversibility is real, not staged | 2013, p. 7: *ex-periens*, "ein Versuch ohne Rückkehr" | Can the thing be re-run, updated, regenerated without loss? Then it is a *Probe*, not an experiment. |
| 2 | **Initiates resistance** — something stays unsettled | 2013, p. 7: "unabgegolten, sperrig oder widerständig" | Does it leave a contradiction standing, or resolve everything into a result? |
| 3 | **Undecidability held open** | 2013, p. 8 (Malevich's square: black on white or white frame on black) | Is there a second reading the work refuses to close — or does it deliver a determination? |
| 4 | **Shows rather than says** — cannot be paraphrased without loss | 2013, p. 9: "Sie zeigen sich" | Cover the text. What is left? If the surface needs its caption, the caption is the work. |
| 5 | **Weight to the singular** — not a specimen of a type | 2013, p. 9; 2012, p. 18: the singular refers to a proper name, not a concept ("Das ist so") | Is there a name, a datum, a case — or an aggregate presented as the point? |
| 6 | **Knowledge in the *Zwischenraum*** — the gap, the misfit | 2013, p. 9: *Unfüglichkeit*, "ein Wissen der Zwischenräume" | Is the knowledge *between* elements that do not fit — or inside one element explained? |
| 7 | **Works through *Versagung*** — refusal, crack, the *Unheile* | 2013, pp. 9–10: attention arises "erst durch die Versagung" | Does something refuse — or does everything function? |
| 8 | **Chance as *Zu-fall*** — what befalls, not randomisation | 2013, p. 10: "Zufälle sind nicht kontingent" | Is what is unruled in the work something that befell it from outside — or produced by it (sampling, randomness, variation)? |
| 9 | **Produces a *Sprung*** — a leap/crack, not a statement | 2013, p. 10 | Does anything jump — or does it conclude? |

## B. The justification criterion (Manifest, p. 18)

Artistic research "beweist dort ihre Berechtigung, wo sie **intervenierend und intermittierend in
Wissenschaftsdiskurse wie auch in Alltagswelten eingreift**". What is essential is *Aisthesis*, not
the attribute *Kunst*; "sie beansprucht keine Autonomie". The reader asks: **whom does this reach,
and what does it shift there?** A work that reaches no discourse and no everyday world has not
intervened, whatever else it has done.

## C. The case-by-case questions (Manifest, thesis 12, pp. 50–51)

The criterion is the *how*, not the *who*: "Einzig stichhaltig ist, **wie** sie geschieht,
**worauf** sie setzt, **was** sie zur Erscheinung bringt und **wie und auf welche Weise** sie sich
dabei selbst thematisiert" — decisive is "allein die **Radikalität des Einsatzes** … die
Unbedingtheit, mit der sie Tabus und Schranken berührt". The reader answers all four for each
work, in one line each, and names what was staked.

## D. The three misunderstandings — red flags (Manifest, pp. 13, 17)

1. **Outputdenken** (p. 13): practice calibrated on "ebenso überprüfbare wie nachvollziehbare
   Resultate …, welche sich in Publikationen oder auf Webseiten distribuieren lassen".
2. **Research display** (p. 17): "Statistiken, Interviews, teilnehmende Beobachtung,
   **Datenvisualisierung**, technische Innovationen oder naturwissenschaftliche
   Experimentalanordnungen, bespickt mit … Kommentaren, versperren den Blick auf die eigentliche
   künstlerische Unruhe"; and *Recherchekunst* that is inaccessible "ohne Displays, Roundtables
   und Begleitpublikationen".
3. **Niche occupation** (p. 17): "Miniaturverschiebungen im Gewebe eines bereits hundert Mal
   Gezeigten" — variation and application to not-yet-tried objects mistaken for research.

## E. Procedural criteria from the single-authored texts

| Criterion | Source | What the reader looks for |
|---|---|---|
| **Ascetic, not productive, rule use** | 2012, pp. 15–16: production "nährt den Überschuss, der zuletzt ins Tautologische gerät"; asceticism "erweist sich der Kunst gemäßer" | Does the work remove, or does it generate variants? Endless output is the tautological mode. |
| **Medium turned against the medium** | 2012, p. 19: "Wendet das Mediale gegen das Mediale … Ihr Metier ist das Zerwürfnis" | Is the medium (screen, data, the web page) made to show its own conditions — or used as a neutral container? |
| **The work is its own hypothesis** | 2012, p. 20: works "von keiner Hypothese getragen werden, weil sie allererst ihre eigene Hypothese sind" | Does it test something outside itself (instrument) — or *is* it the proposition? |
| **Staged apparatus** | 2015, pp. 7–8: Duchamp "inszenierte das Penible jeder Versuchsanordnung … bis dessen instituierendes Pathos … zur Karikatur gerät" | If the apparatus is visible, is it staged — or is it a method sheet with good manners? |
| **Decontrol** | 2015, p. 13: art "dekontrolliert die Bedingung von Erfahrung und hält sie offen für Unvorhersehbares" | Does anything outside the maker's control enter — or is every condition fixed? |
| **Groping, not seizing** | Manifest, p. 62: "tastend und berührend … um sie anzuerkennen, zuzulassen … was an ihnen unvergleichbar, verletzlich … unabgegolten bleibt" | Is the material acknowledged as singular and vulnerable — or seized, counted, and used? |
| **Against the business of measuring** | Manifest, p. 54: powerful where it "gegenüber dem Geschäft und der Geschäftigkeit des Messens eine Resistenz setzt" | Does the work measure more — or set a resistance to measuring? (For a house whose line is counter-measurement, this cuts both ways.) |

## F. Two rules of this reader, drawn from the house's own reading

- **Checkability is not a merit.** Pre-registration, hashes, reproducibility, committed traces are
  "eine nachprüfbare Anordnung zur Ermittlung empirischer Daten" (2013, p. 7) — on the *other*
  side of Mersch's line. The reader never credits a work for being verifiable. It may credit a
  work for *staging* its verification (E, staged apparatus).
- **An instrument is not a failed work.** The house keeps instruments that were never meant to be
  art (the counter-measurement registers, the ledgers). The reader names them as instruments and
  applies A–E only to what presents itself as a work. What it does fault is an instrument that
  *wears* the credentials of a work, or a work that hides inside the manners of an instrument.
