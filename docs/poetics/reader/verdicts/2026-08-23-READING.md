## What the reading found, read across all eighteen

1. **The shelf dresses instruments as works.** Fourteen of seventeen live pages are instruments by the
   reader's measure — and by their own: almost every one *says so in its footer*. The recurring "one
   move" is the same sentence fourteen times: say *instrument* in the first line, not the last, and
   take the work's costume off the heading (the poem for a title, the courtroom, the ghost story, the
   contract, the confession). This is the house's own rule — instruments are not works (v3, Move 1) —
   applied to a page that was built to present them as experiments.
2. **Where the house claims a work, the claim lives in the sentences.** The four SAYS verdicts
   (Policy, Protocol, Society, W1) fail the same test — cover the text and a dashboard, a chart, a
   simulation with a preamble, six rules with a changelog remain. In each case the reader could name
   the one thing on the page that *would* show (a tower rising under no hand; the added tick on the
   second rule; the four dials and the empty fourth) and found it told before it was allowed to appear.
3. **Outputdenken is the house style.** Provenance paragraphs, hashes, "method and sources disclosed",
   nearest-neighbour shelves and daylight measures appear on the page as credentials. The reader was
   told never to credit them and read them, every time, as the first misunderstanding (*Manifest*,
   p. 13) — the house's honesty worn as the work's merit.
4. **One refusal each, buried.** Several instruments contain a genuine *Versagung* — the forty shut
   doors in Bycatch, the event Invoked Past will not name, the three struck-through headlines in
   Editorial Deadline, the dial asked to read below its own scale in Headroom — and in every case it is
   under screens of explanation. The reader's moves are mostly subtractions.
5. **W1 read by the reader as the founder read it.** SAYS: the rules show a standard re-ruled, nothing
   more; war, death, twenty-five, the absent reason are all on the page. The founder's verdict of the
   afternoon and the reader's of the evening agree, independently. That is one data point for the
   measure discriminating the way a person does — one, not a result.

## What this reading cannot yet say

Whether the criteria **discriminate** — the test the reader exists to run (README, second purpose) —
needs works known to be good and works known to be bad, and the house has supplied neither: every
page here is the house's own, and the reader found no SHOWS among them. Two readings are open: the
house has no work that shows (consistent with the founder's own verdict on the ecology, 2026-08-08),
or the measure as operationalised cannot award SHOWS to anything. Only the founder's six — three he
holds to be good, three bad, named by him — can separate the two. Until then this is a reading of
the house, not a test of Mersch.

Second caveat: one model, one evening, one prompt. The CLI run with the founder's renewed login is
the replication; where its verdicts differ from these, the difference is the first measurement of the
measure's stability.
