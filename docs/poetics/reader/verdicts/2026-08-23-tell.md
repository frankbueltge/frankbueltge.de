# Delve into the intricate realm — read 2026-08-23

## Seen, before reading
A dark page under a personal-site header. A headline in machine-speak — "Delve into the intricate realm" — then a subtitle explaining it is about words the machine overuses and how they flooded science after ChatGPT. A giant "14×" with a caption about "delve" in scientific abstracts. A single rising line, flat from 2018, a dashed vertical marked "ChatGPT", a steep climb to 2024, a dip at 2025. Below, a monospace table of eight words with before/after rates and multipliers: showcasing, delve, commendable, intricate, realm, pivotal, meticulous, garner. Then a section called "Nearest neighbors" listing three published studies that did the same thing, conceding "no new territory", a source line, a "Method sheet →" link, and a footer declaring "An instrument — method and sources disclosed, source code open." A stranger takes it for a well-made explainer chart about AI vocabulary in papers — a data-journalism card, not an artwork. What it is about is clear from the surface in one second; what it does beyond reporting is not.

## Verdict: INSTRUMENT
A counter of eight words that says so honestly in its last line, wears a poem for a headline, and spends its middle third telling the visitor that three other people counted first — the only sentence on the page that shows anything is the title, and it is the one the page rushes to explain.

## Thesis 12, answered (Manifest, p. 51)
- **How it happens:** a nightly count of eight words across PubMed abstracts, divided by a 2018–2022 baseline, rendered as one line and one table.
- **What it stakes:** nothing beyond a query quota — an already peer-reviewed metric, re-run smaller, with the admission printed on the page.
- **What it brings to appearance:** that science adopted a dialect it did not choose, and — unworked — that the dialect is retreating.
- **How it thematises itself:** a headline written in the dialect, a footer confessing "an instrument", and a self-audit of neighbours posted as content; the shelf's sharpest claim ("an AI tool measuring AI's footprint") appears nowhere on the page.
- **Radicality of the stake:** low; the one taboo within reach — the instrument's own author writes in the measured dialect — is left untouched.

## Misunderstandings (Manifest, pp. 13–17)
- **Outputdenken** (p. 13): "method and sources disclosed, source code open" and "Method sheet →" offered as the closing credential — the house's honesty presented as the work's merit.
- **Research display** (p. 17): one data visualisation, one table, three citations with commentary — the display is the whole page.
- **Niche occupation** (p. 17): the page names its own niche — the same metric on the same corpus, at smaller scale — and stays in it.

## Cover the text
Remove every sentence and a title in machine-speak survives as a found object, a line that climbs after an unlabeled dashed mark and falls at the end, and a monospace column of eight words beside rising numbers. The word list still shows — a vocabulary nobody chose, laid out like a ledger — and the fall at the right edge still shows. Why the line climbs does not; the dashed line becomes a mark without a cause. Something shows without its captions; it is the list and the dip, not the 14×.
