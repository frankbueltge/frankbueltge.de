# On Record — read 2026-08-23

## Seen, before reading
A dark page with a small-caps header ("Meridian Research Runtime · Claim-rooted export · Read-only projection") and a large sentence that is already an argument: one claim held contested because two reviews disagreed and neither was averaged away. Under it a URN, a sha256, and five badges (CONTESTED, INTERPRETIVE, 2 verifications, 14 anchors, 17 sources). Then a bar, almost entirely white, captioned 1 supporting / 13 contradicting. Then two columns headed "The Parallax": a short PASS (confidence 0.85, "15 of 14 anchors — a broad sweep") and a very long FAIL (confidence 0.80, "1 of 14 — one row, in depth") that quotes five numbered grounds about whether Felicity Hammond's *V3 Model Collapse* really retrains a model across generations. A "Dissent preserved" block, a row of export metadata (RO-Crate, PROV, 42 objects, 0 artifact bytes), a footer paragraph about SPARQL, and the last line: "An instrument — method and sources disclosed." A stranger takes it for an audit record: a dashboard of one disputed review inside somebody's research pipeline, about somebody else's artwork.

## Verdict: INSTRUMENT
A verification ledger that has learned to speak in headlines — it names itself an instrument in its last line and a parallax in its middle, and its one refusal (declining to average two confidence numbers it nonetheless printed) is a configuration setting, not a crack.

## Thesis 12, answered (Manifest, p. 51)
- **How it happens:** one claim's provenance graph is exported and two reviews of it are set in columns of unequal length.
- **What it stakes:** nothing of its own — a status flag on another artist's work, which a human may later flip.
- **What it brings to appearance:** that the house's own corpus row miscounted generations, and that breadth and depth of review can disagree without a tie-break.
- **How it thematises itself:** by a footer disclaiming authorship ("it is the archive, rendered") under a headline that is authored prose.
- **Radicality of the stake:** low — no taboo touched; the only risk on the page was taken by the Ulysses verifier, and it was bounded to one row.

## Misunderstandings (Manifest, pp. 13–17)
- Outputdenken (p. 13): hash, URN, RO-Crate, SPARQL and "every figure is derived" are offered as the page's credential — the house's honesty presented as the work's merit.
- Research display (p. 17): a balance bar, badges, progress meters and quoted commentary, readable only with the method sheet and the encounter register.
- Niche (p. 17): an audit dashboard with one toggle flipped (no averaging) — a Miniaturverschiebung in a genre shown a hundred times.

## Cover the text
A bar that is 1:13 white against black; a short column beside a long one; a full meter beside a sliver; vertical rules marking five findings. It shows one thing without help — wide-and-shallow against narrow-and-deep — and one thing against its own prose: the bar renders a majority, which is exactly the averaging the sentences say the runtime refuses.

## One move
None: it is an instrument, and it should say so plainly — move "An instrument" from the last line to the first, and let the headline that argues go.
