# All Along the Watchtower — read 2026-08-23

## Seen, before reading
A dark page with a song title for a headline, a subtitle about "SGP4 in the browser", a label "STUDY — RUNNING SINCE JUNE 12, 2026", two paragraphs of method, and then a small form: a "Locate me" button, two empty fields for latitude and longitude, a "Compute" button. Nothing has been computed; the first screen is an empty form with a privacy promise repeated twice. Below the fold comes a section comparing the page to two competitor apps, then a section titled "The Densification of the Gaze" with a bar chart rising from 2005 to 2025 in two greys, a long caption, a collapsed table, and two bold-led paragraphs explaining what the chart is not. The last line before the footer says "An instrument — method and sources disclosed, source code open." A stranger would take it for a privacy-minded satellite-tracking tool that has grown a chart about its own data, wearing a Dylan title it never mentions again.

## Verdict: INSTRUMENT
A satellite-pass calculator that names itself an instrument in its last line and a song in its first, and spends the distance between the two proving it is better than two apps — a method sheet that borrowed a work's hat.

## Thesis 12, answered (Manifest, p. 51)
- **How it happens:** a visitor enters coordinates; propagation runs locally; a list appears; a chart derived at build time sits below.
- **What it stakes:** nothing of the maker's; the privacy promise is the only exposure, and it is an engineering exposure.
- **What it brings to appearance:** a count of eyes overhead and a cohort curve in which the state gaze goes dark on the chart exactly as it grows in reality — the one appearance the page has.
- **How it thematises itself:** in prose, at length, and with a competitor table — it thematises its own merit, not its own condition.
- **Radicality of the stake:** none touched; no taboo, no barrier, a CC-BY catalogue and a free plan.

## Misunderstandings (Manifest, pp. 13–17)
- **Outputdenken** (p. 13): "committed daily", "derived at build time — no figure here is typed", "source code open", "56 committed observations" offered as credentials.
- **Research display** (p. 17): a data visualisation with a 90-word caption, a fold-out table, and a "nearest neighbors" audit in front of whatever unrest there was.
- **Niche occupation** (p. 17): SatFleetLive plus an EO filter — the page's own words, a combination of two already-shown things presented as the claim.

## Cover the text
A song title, an empty form with two fields and two buttons, and a bar chart of two greys rising from 2005 to 2025. The chart still shows a growth — of something, owned by someone — and the dashed lines still show a doubt; the form shows nothing until pressed. Growth survives; gaze, orbit, commerce and the missing military all leave with the sentences.

## One move
None: it is an instrument, and it already says so plainly in its last line — so let it say so in its first, and return the Watchtower to the song.
