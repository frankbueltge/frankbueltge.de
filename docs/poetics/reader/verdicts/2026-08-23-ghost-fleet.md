# Ghost Fleet — read 2026-08-23

## Seen, before reading
A dark page with a large title, "Ghost Fleet", a one-line tagline about ships switching off their transponder, and today's date. Below it a boxed "Case of the day": not a ship but a nine-digit code with no flag, "a fishing-gear tag", which went silent for 22 days between two coordinates that lie an ocean apart, with a link out to Global Fishing Watch. Then a very large "100", a count of transponder shutdowns inside some national zone "lately", and a sum of "transponder-days of darkness". Then a list: ten vessel names, a flag or a dash, a number of days, an EEZ, an arrow. Further down: three named competitors, a paragraph on what they do better, a disclaimer that "intentional" is someone else's machine-learning estimate, a link to a method sheet, and a footer line that says outright: "An instrument." A stranger takes it for a daily dashboard of a maritime-surveillance dataset that somebody else produced, re-listed with a literary title.

## Verdict: INSTRUMENT
A re-sort of Global Fishing Watch's table that says so itself in the footer — and then dresses the top of the page in a ghost story, a "darkness" sum, and a headline ghost that turns out to be a buoy.

## Thesis 12, answered (Manifest, p. 51)
- **How it happens:** a nightly join of a public dataset with EEZ polygons, re-listed with a title.
- **What it stakes:** nothing of its own — the "intentional" judgment, the data, the risk of accusation are all handed back to GFW by the disclaimer.
- **What it brings to appearance:** a roster of ten names and a round "100" — the appearance of a count, not of a sea.
- **How it thematises itself:** honestly, in the last line: "An instrument"; dishonestly, in the first: "Ghost Fleet", "darkness".
- **Radicality of the stake:** none; no taboo, no border touched that the cited neighbours have not already crossed with crews and months of reporting.

## Misunderstandings (Manifest, pp. 13–17)
- **Outputdenken** (p. 13): "method and sources disclosed, source code open" is offered in the footer as the page's standing — the house's honesty, presented where a work's merit would go.
- **Research display** (p. 17): a data visualisation "bespickt mit Kommentaren" — the neighbours paragraph and disclaimer outweigh the display they comment on.
- **Niche occupation** (p. 17): a miniature shift (EEZ instead of GFW's hotspots) in a fabric GFW, Skylight and Outlaw Ocean have shown a hundred times; the page names them itself.

## Cover the text
Left: a title, a nine-digit code, a big "100", a sum, ten names with day-counts and zone names, eleven arrows. It still shows a ledger — and a ledger is what it is; nothing of the sea, the switch, or the dark survives the cut, and the one vignette that tried to show (vanished here, resurfaced there) was a sentence.

## One move
None: it is an instrument, and it should say so plainly — at the top, in place of the ghost story, and with the counter's caption agreeing with the counter.
