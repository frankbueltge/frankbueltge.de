# Consensus — read 2026-08-23

## Seen, before reading
A dark page, a one-word title, a dated subtitle that already carries scare quotes around „independent". Below it a quoted tabloid sentence about Meghan and Harry, framed as "today's most independently reported sentence", then the count: word-for-word across 162 outlets. Then a wall of small grey chips — andoveradvertiser, basingstokegazette, bournemouthecho, bracknellnews, bucksfreepress — which to a stranger reads as one regional publisher's stable of local weeklies, though the caption calls it "scattered placement across several markets". Then a very large 34.9%, a 38th percentile, a table of yearly medians, a correction note, a provenance paragraph, a list of three academic neighbours, and a footer line that says what this is: "An instrument — method and sources disclosed, source code open." I would take it for a churnalism dashboard with a joke in its headline.

## Verdict: INSTRUMENT
A measuring apparatus that admits it in its last line, having spent the lines above it dressed in a work's irony and a work's title — and whose one funny mechanism is that its classifier looks at 162 Newsquest-shaped mastheads and calls them scattered.

## Thesis 12, answered (Manifest, p. 51)
- **How it happens:** a nightly batch picks the widest sentence, counts its domains, classifies the why, publishes a number.
- **What it stakes:** nothing of its own; the stake is the tabloid's, the risk is GDELT's uptime.
- **What it brings to appearance:** one sentence in 162 mouths — and then hides it under a percentage.
- **How it thematises itself:** in a footer ("An instrument"), a correction box, and a "Nearest Neighbors" list that argues its own novelty.
- **Radicality of the stake:** none; no taboo is touched, a known technique is run daily.

## Misunderstandings (Manifest, pp. 13–17)
- **Outputdenken** (p. 13): "method and sources disclosed, source code open", the committed job trace, the provenance paragraph — offered as credentials, they are the house's honesty, not the work's.
- **Research display** (p. 17): a yearly table and a headline number, bespickt with a correction note and a provenance paragraph.
- **Niche occupation** (p. 17): the page names its neighbours itself and locates its novelty in one added column (WHY) — a Miniaturverschiebung, but an instrument is allowed that.

## Cover the text
What remains: a large number, a percentile, a table of percentages, and a grid of 162 small grey domain chips. The number and the table are a dashboard and show nothing. The chip wall still shows — one thing repeated 162 times — but only if the one sentence above it survives the cover, and it is a sentence.

## One move
None: it is an instrument, and it says so — in the last line. Say it in the first, and take the work's costume off the heading: the irony in „independent" and "most independently reported" is a caption doing what the chip wall already does.
