# Iceberg Theory — read 2026-08-23

## Seen, before reading
A dark page under a personal-site nav. A very large "64 %" and one sentence: each language version omits 64% of what the others state. Two lines of small monospace say sovereignty disputes are taken from Wikipedia's own categories, one topic a day, deterministic by date. Then a block headed "Cyprus · 75 % omitted · edit:autoconfirmed · measured 2026-07-29": a matrix of German sentences ("Ist ein Inselstaat", "Liegt westlich von Syrien") against twelve language codes, ticks and dots. The full page is eighteen thousand pixels of this — nineteen such matrices, each truncated with "… and N more claims" — three of them prefaced by twelve scripts for one name (Taiwan / Republik China / 中華民国), and at the bottom a box labelled "THE REGISTER, CHECKABLE" with counts of git commits, a list of three academic neighbours, and a footer naming the extraction model. A stranger takes it for a data dashboard on Wikipedia bias, and is right. The title "Iceberg Theory" exists only in the browser tab; the claims are in German on an English page and nothing explains why.

## Verdict: INSTRUMENT
A counting apparatus for encyclopaedic omission that, having confessed its genre is "solved", pins a Hemingway title to its chest and offers its git log as the difference — the one thing on the page that shows is the list of twelve names, and it is a sidebar.

## Thesis 12, answered (Manifest, p. 51)
- **How it happens:** a daily job reads one disputed place in twelve Wikipedias through a language model and commits a tick-or-dot grid.
- **What it stakes:** nothing of the maker's; the judgement of what a version "states" is delegated to an extractor the page names in its footer.
- **What it brings to appearance:** uneven density of dots — and, three times, twelve scripts for one contested name.
- **How it thematises itself:** as a register — checkable, never retconned, neighbours cited — it thematises its bookkeeping, not its seeing.
- **Radicality of the stake:** low; disputed islands are the genre's stock object, as the page's own neighbour note admits.

## Misunderstandings (Manifest, pp. 13–17)
- Outputdenken (p. 13): "The process is the differentiator: every measurement is a git commit" — the credential offered is distributability on a webpage.
- Research display (p. 17): a Datenvisualisierung with commentary is the entire surface; the Unruhe, if any, is behind it.
- Niche occupation (p. 17): "the omission measurement itself is a solved genre" — by its own account a variation on INFOGAP with version control added.

## Cover the text
Remove every sentence: a big 64, twelve column codes, nineteen fields of ticks and dots of uneven density, and three lists of one name in twelve scripts. The dot fields show that something is unevenly present but not what, where, or to whom; they are the caption's footnote. Only the name lists still show — the same island refusing to be called one thing — and they are the part the page treats as an aside.

## One move
Take "Iceberg Theory" and "THE REGISTER, CHECKABLE" off it and call it a register; then give the twelve names for one island their own page with no grid beneath them.
