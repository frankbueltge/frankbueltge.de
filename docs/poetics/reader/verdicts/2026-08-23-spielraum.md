# Headroom — read 2026-08-23

## Seen, before reading
A dark page under a personal-site header. A title, "Headroom", then a lede that already delivers a conclusion ("It is not reachable"), then a paragraph defining an acronym. Below that, a KPI tile in the largest type on the page: **0.80**. Then four company cards — Google, Meta, Microsoft, Amazon — each with a thin horizontal scale from 0.80 to 1.20, a hatched field on the left, a vertical line at 1.00, one filled dot to the right of the line and, for three of the four, small open circles walking leftwards into the hatching, year by year. Amazon's scale is empty. After that, six screens of what a stranger would take for an appendix: quoted sentences from corporate reports, a table of figures with VERIFIED/SOURCED labels, a table of formulas, caveats, a "nearest neighbors" list, a "Method sheet" link. I would take it for a data-journalism fact-check about data-centre efficiency — a well-mannered dossier with one good diagram at the top and a filing cabinet beneath.

## Verdict: INSTRUMENT
A measuring apparatus with one genuine figure — a dial asked to read below the end of its own scale — that has dressed itself as an experiment, announced its result in its first sentence, and then buried the dial under seven screens of referee cards.

## Thesis 12, answered (Manifest, p. 51)
- **How it happens:** A formula applied to four annual reports, redrawn as a dial with a forbidden zone, then documented.
- **What it stakes:** Nothing of its own — the subjects' numbers are at stake, the apparatus is not.
- **What it brings to appearance:** One figure: a required efficiency that lies below the physical floor, year after year, moving further away.
- **How it thematises itself:** As honesty — tiers, caveats "on the face", a method sheet; it thematises its own checkability, not its own conditions.
- **Radicality of the stake:** Low; no taboo is touched, no barrier crossed — the page itself notes Google "discloses the figures openly".

## Misunderstandings (Manifest, pp. 13–17)
- **Outputdenken (p. 13):** VERIFIED/SOURCED tiers, formulas printed on the face, "last checked", a method sheet — checkability offered as the credential.
- **Research display (p. 17):** Quotes, two tables, caveats, a neighbours list, bespickt mit Kommentaren — the filing cabinet blocks the view of the one figure.
- **Niche occupation (p. 17):** Partly: a PUE leaderboard is a hundred-times-shown object; the page's own "nearest neighbors" section performs the novelty check that should have been a work's silence.

## Cover the text
Four thin scales, a hatched field on the left of each, a vertical line, one filled dot on the right, open circles stepping leftwards into the hatching; one empty scale; one very large "0.80"; columns of numbers. It still shows one thing: a required mark drifting deeper into a no-go field while the actual mark stays put, and one company with no mark at all. It does not show what the line is, why the field is forbidden, or that the empty scale is a refusal rather than a gap — the hatching carries the whole work, and the hatching is a convention.

## One move
Cut it to the four dials and the empty fourth — no lede, no KPI tile, no tables on the face; move every sentence to the method sheet behind one link — or leave it as it is and say instrument plainly, off the experiments shelf.
