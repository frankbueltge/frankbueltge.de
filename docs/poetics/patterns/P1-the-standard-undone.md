# P1 — The Standard Undone

*Status: drafted 2026-08-22 from T1.02. Cheap test not yet run.*

## Operational core

1. **Name a standard the practice itself relies on** — a unit, a threshold, a cadence, a
   canonical file, a measure whose authority the practice's own findings rest on.
2. **Expose an instance of it to a real process the practice does not control**, and record
   what comes back exactly, without correction.
3. **Fix each outcome as an artifact** — dated, hashed, kept, in the manner of a preparator
   mounting a specimen.
4. **Promote the deviations to standards.** Publish them *as* the measure, curvature intact,
   beside the original they were calibrated against.
5. **Keep the series small, finite and unrepeated.** Duchamp dropped three threads and never
   dropped a fourth to improve the result.
6. **Stage the apparatus, not only the result** — added 2026-08-23 from Mersch 2015, pp. 7–8.
   The calibration, the exact settings, the meticulous recording and archiving and publication
   are not the work's backstage; they are its material. They must be overdone until "dessen
   instituierendes Pathos … zur Karikatur gerät". A standard exposed with a discreet apparatus
   is a demonstration; a standard exposed with its whole instituting pathos on show is the
   work. The operation is auto-referential: an experiment about experimenting, which "weist auf
   uns selbst zurück" rather than at a fact outside us.

## Provenance

Marcel Duchamp, *Trois Stoppages Étalon* (1914): three one-metre threads, calibrated against
the Paris standard metre, dropped three times from a height of one metre; each fall a
different figure; each fixed on canvas and built into a measuring stick whose curve mocks
measurement. Mersch, *Kritik der Kunstphilosophie* (2012), pp. 14–15: "Die Indetermination
des Resultats diskreditiert das metrische System als Willkür", and the practice conducts
itself as "ein Stück Wissenschaftskritik, die sich dezidiert ästhetischer Verfahren bedient,
nicht nur, um deren Methodologie auf den Prüfstand zu stellen, sondern um sie zu unterlaufen".
Lineage: Jarry's 'pataphysics, the science of the exception (ibid., p. 15). And Mersch, *Was
heißt, im Ästhetischen forschen?* (2015), pp. 7–8, where the same work is read as a
**meta-experiment**: Duchamp "inszenierte das Penible jeder Versuchsanordnung, die Genauigkeit
des Settings … wie gleichermaßen die säuberliche Aufzeichnung, Archivierung und Veröffentlichung
der Wurfresultate" — an inventory of this house's own apparatus, read as the artistic move.
Note also 2015, p. 3, n. 10: whether the falls were genuinely chance or helped along is
"unerheblich" — the work does not rest on the purity of its randomness.

## Machine translation

**What a machine can do, and better.** Hold the original standard with full provenance, so
the deviation is measurable rather than asserted. Perform the exposure verifiably and record
it immutably. Refuse to average across the series — the hardest part for any statistical
habit, and trivial for a rule that forbids it. Keep the artifacts for decades.

**What a machine cannot supply: the lawlessness itself.** Per T1.01 §6, nothing befalls a
system that supplied the randomness. `Math.random()`, sampling, temperature, model roulette —
all *actio* in costume, and all void here. The exposing process must lie outside the
practice's control. Available candidates in this house: network latency and timeouts; a
third-party API's refusal or rate limit; an institution's response time; the actual arrival
order and gaps of nightly data; a physical sensor's noise; a registrar's or log's own clock.

**The judgement that stays human.** Which standard is worth undoing — i.e. which measure the
house's own authority actually rests on. Choosing a harmless one produces a demonstration
rather than a work.

## Cheap test

Pick one standard this house genuinely leans on. Expose it three times to a process the house
does not control. Show a stranger only the original standard and the three artifacts.

**Go criterion:** the stranger can say what was measured, and that something went wrong with
the measuring, **without a wall text**. (Criterion 4 of T1.01: showing, not saying.)

## Failure test

- **Simulable deviation.** If the practice could have computed, predicted or parameterised
  the outcome, it was not *Zu-fall* but variance, and the piece is a demonstration of
  tolerance ranges.
- **Correction.** If outcomes are smoothed, selected among, retried or "cleaned", the
  standard has been re-imposed and the procedure is void. The failed instance stays in the
  series (v3 Move 2.6: flawless execution proves nothing).
- **Harmless target.** If undoing the standard costs the practice no authority, nothing was
  wagered.
- **Discreet apparatus.** If the calibration and the record-keeping stay backstage, step 6 was
  skipped and the piece is a demonstration of variance with good provenance.
- **Instrument creep.** If the result is then used as a measurement of the world, the work has
  turned back into an instrument and inherits the instrument's obligations instead of the
  work bar.
