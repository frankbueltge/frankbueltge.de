# T1.01 — Mersch, *Nichtpropositionalität und ästhetisches Denken* (2013)

Read 2026-08-22. Paraphrase with page numbers; the source is copyrighted and stays where it
is (`PHD/Artistic Research/Mersch/`), per the handling rule of 2026-08-22. Quotations are
short and cited.

**Why this text first.** The house's record holds only Mersch's 2015 objection. This program
needs the *positive* criterion, and it has to come from his own aesthetics rather than from
his critique — otherwise "a work that survives Mersch" is a target with no target board.
This text supplies it.

## 1. The argument in outline

- **The propositional enclosure** (pp. 1–3). A proposition is "A ist B", ruled by identity,
  non-contradiction and *tertium non datur*. If knowledge is grounded in propositions, then
  the kind of knowledge propositions produce is itself propositional, "das eo ipso jede
  Nichtpropositionalität … aus[schließt]" (p. 2) — a closed circle. Mersch adds a
  genealogical barb: *Urteil* derives not from *Ur-Teilung* but from `irteilan`, the
  handing down of a court decision (p. 3). Judgement is juridical before it is
  epistemological, and law is prior to science, "bis heute unterhalten beide eine tiefe,
  aber unterirdische Verwandtschaft" (p. 3).
- **What the aesthetic was allowed to be** (p. 1). Until Kant and partly beyond, the
  aesthetic waits in the "Vorhölle der Intuition", to be purged by the concept. Its
  judgements are matters of *Sentiment*, not of truth.
- **Mersch's move** (p. 6, "Testung"). Not to claim the aesthetic is another kind of
  judgement, but that it is **another thinking** — "nicht nur als ein 'Anderes' des Denkens,
  sondern als ein 'anderes' Denken, das seine eigenen Formen und Praktiken verfolgt".

## 2. The load-bearing passage for this program (p. 7)

Mersch distinguishes the artistic experiment from the scientific one, and the distinction is
the whole target:

> the experimental in art rests "nicht in erster Linie auf einer methodisch angelegten
> Probe, einer nachprüfbaren Anordnung zur Ermittlung empirischer Daten", but — in the
> literal sense of *ex-periens* — is a "wagemutige Unternehmung, gleichsam ein Versuch ohne
> Rückkehr". It is a practice that wrestles with perception-events; something in them stays
> "unabgegolten, sperrig oder widerständig", and the initiation of such resistances is the
> result of an **unsystematic** testing (p. 7).

Three consequences, each usable:

1. **An attempt without return.** Irreversibility is constitutive, not decorative. A trial
   that can be re-run is a *Probe*, not an *ex-periens*.
2. **Resistance is the product**, not the obstacle. The work initiates something that will
   not be settled.
3. **Unsystematic.** Method is on the other side of the line.

And what the aesthetic experiment manifests is a ***positio*, not a pro-position** (p. 7):
a taking of position that raises its own claim to knowledge without asserting truth or
falsity — "sie zeugt Exempel für Exempel von einer Singularität".

## 3. How such knowledge is made — the operational vocabulary

- **De-Synthesen** (pp. 7–8). Objects and events act as "arguments" that perform no
  synthesis: they do not formulate identity or generality, they point, they show —
  "ekstatische Begründung". Every synthesis does violence to disparity. Examples: Turrell's
  installations, whose appearing plunges perception into indifference; Malevich's *Quadrat*
  (1915), a picture that leaves undecided whether it shows a black square on white ground or
  a white frame on black (p. 8; Mersch notes the later title *Schwarzes Quadrat auf weißem
  Grund* was added afterwards and "verkürzt" the possibilities of sense).
  → **Undecidability held open** is a form, not a failure to decide.
- **Singuläre Paradigmata** (pp. 8–9). Not works but provisional constellations that stage
  or *los-lassen* events, and thereby yield epistemic effects. Something shows itself "ohne
  intentional gewollt zu sein" — with all the connotations "des Neutrums und der *passio*"
  (p. 8). Adorno, cited approvingly: the prototype of the artwork is the **firework** —
  "apparition kath' exochen … aufblitzende und vergehende Schrift, die doch nicht ihrer
  Bedeutung nach sich lesen lässt" (p. 8, Adorno *Ästhetische Theorie* 125f.).
  → Ephemerality and unreadability are not deficits; they are the prototype.
- **Differenzpraktiken** (p. 9). The aesthetic *epistēmē* "verfügt das Konträre": vexations,
  chiasms, paradoxes. It produces "ein Wissen der Zwischenräume, ihrer Lücken, Klüfte oder
  Abstände". What carries is the *Stellung* of the elements — their resonance or their
  **Unfüglichkeit**, their misfit, which releases *energeia*. Like catachresis: the figure
  says nothing but gives an unsayable expression through its literal "Un-Fuge". Such figures
  cannot be paraphrased or translated: "**Sie zeigen sich**" (p. 9).
- **Versagung and Sprung** (pp. 9–10). The aesthetic points at something through
  "Brüchigkeit oder Unerfüllbarkeit"; "Risse oder Brüche dokumentieren das 'Unheile'". It is
  productive above all where it *refuses* every identity-practice or levelling
  reconciliation, indicating what does not add up, what stays incommensurable. Attention
  arises "erst durch die Versagung" (p. 10). What is given to be recognised comes not as a
  sentence but as a **Sprung** — the German holds both *leap* and *crack*, and Mersch means
  both (p. 10, with Heidegger: "Der Satz macht einen 'Satz' im Sinne eines Sprungs").
- **Zufall as Zu-fall** (p. 10). With Beuys' hare that keeps changing direction: "Das
  'Woanders' ist eine Strategie des 'Zu-Falls'. **Zufälle sind nicht kontingent**; sie
  bezeichnen vielmehr das, was wörtlich 'zu-fällt'" — what befalls, and thereby makes
  singular paradigms available as possibilities of knowledge. One route to it is "die
  Forcierung von Paradoxa".

## 4. The criterion, derived — a target board

A work "researches in the aesthetic", on this text's terms, if it:

1. is an **attempt without return** — its irreversibility is real, not staged (p. 7);
2. **initiates resistance** and leaves something unsettled (p. 7);
3. holds an **undecidability** open instead of delivering a determination (p. 8);
4. **shows rather than says** — it cannot be paraphrased without loss, so a wall text cannot
   stand in for it (p. 9);
5. returns **weight to the singular** rather than treating it as a specimen of a type (p. 9);
6. makes its knowledge in the **Zwischenraum** — the gap, the misfit, the *Unfüglichkeit*
   between elements (p. 9);
7. works through **Versagung** — refusal, crack, the "Unheile" — rather than through
   resolution (pp. 9–10);
8. treats chance as **Zu-fall**, what befalls, not as randomisation (p. 10);
9. produces a **Sprung**, not a statement (p. 10).

## 5. What this does to the house — the uncomfortable finding

Mersch's line runs directly through the house's own honesty machinery. Pre-registration,
reproducibility, the committed trace, "derivation matches the committed export", the sealed
§2 search — every one of these is a "methodisch angelegte Probe, eine nachprüfbare Anordnung
zur Ermittlung empirischer Daten" (p. 7), which is the definition he places on the *other*
side of the line from aesthetic experiment. The objection is not rhetorical; it is
structural. A machine practice that pre-registers everything is, by this criterion, doing
the opposite of researching in the aesthetic.

**The way through is not to weaken the apparatus but to change its role: apparatus as
material, not as method.** Where the verification machinery is what gets refused, broken or
made undecidable *inside* the work, the work is not assimilating to technoscience — it is
using technoscience's own equipment as its resistant material. Haacke is the precedent to
test in T6: he used deeds, photographs and typed records, and the resistance came from the
institution that cancelled him.

This also draws the line between the two layers precisely: the instruments (Consensus, the
registers, the traces) **should** stay methodical — they were never supposed to be art, and
v3's Move 1 already says so. The error would be to let an instrument's checkability count as
a work's aesthetic credential.

## 6. Consequences for the program's standing theses

- **H1 is armed by Mersch's own etymology** and gets sharper. His "Zufälle sind nicht
  kontingent … was wörtlich 'zu-fällt'" (p. 10) is exactly the distinction the thesis needs:
  randomisation produces contingency, and contingency is *not* Zu-fall. Cage's chance
  operations are procedures for producing contingency (and for removing the ego, which a
  machine does not have); Bacon's accident is something that befalls the painter and is then
  answered. Revised H1: **a machine's random number generator cannot produce Zu-fall,
  because nothing befalls a system that supplied the randomness itself.** What can befall a
  machine practice is the world's refusal — latency, a 404, an unanswered request, a
  resistant material, an institution that says no. That is where its passio is available,
  and it is not simulable.
- **H2 (clinamen) gains a reason beyond charm.** A rule flawlessly executed produces no
  *Versagung*, and without Versagung, per p. 10, no attention and no recognition arises. The
  deliberate deviation is therefore not decoration on the rule; it is the condition of the
  work's saying anything at all.
- **H4 (punctum) is directly supported** by §4.5: the singular's own weight, not as an
  example of a type. Barthes should be read next to this in T5, not against it.
- **New thesis, H5.** Undecidability held open (Malevich's ground, p. 8) is a machine-native
  form: a practice that holds two readings of the same committed evidence, refuses to
  resolve them, and can prove it did not tamper with either, has an advantage no painter
  had. To be tested — the risk is that "we could not decide" reads as evasion rather than
  form, and the difference must be perceivable in the encounter (criterion 4).
