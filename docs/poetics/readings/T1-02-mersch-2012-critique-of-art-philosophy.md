# T1.02 — Mersch, *Kritik der Kunstphilosophie. Kleine Epistemologie künstlerischer Praxis* (2012)

Read 2026-08-22, pp. 1–20 of 22. Paraphrase with page numbers; the source stays in
`PHD/Artistic Research/Mersch/`. Quotations short and cited.

This is the more useful of the two texts read so far. T1.01 gave the criterion; this one
gives **procedures** — and it names, precisely, the move this program had guessed at.

## 1. The line of argument, briefly

Mersch works through the tradition that granted art an epistemic force only to take it back
in the same breath: Baumgarten (aesthetic cognition as *scientia sensitiva*, perception of
*singularia*, but disciplined by memory and reason, pp. 2–3); Kant (imagination as mediator,
but "Anschauungen ohne Begriffe sind blind", so art's knowledge stays "chronisch prekär",
p. 3); Hegel (art as *sinnliches Scheinen der Idee*, ranked below religion and philosophy
because form must overcome material, pp. 4–6); Heidegger (breaks with the primacy of form
via the *Streit* of Erde and Welt, but ends by subordinating everything to *Dichtung* and
language, so "die Materialität … zuletzt doch der Poetik geopfert", pp. 9–12).

Against all of them he sets Ad Reinhardt's *Art-as-Art* (1962) as the refusal of philosophy's
mandate — "Art is art-as-art and everything else is everything else" (p. 1) — and Derrida:
philosophy encloses art in a circle, and "der Philosoph der Kunst … begnügt sich damit, von
Kunst zu plaudern" (p. 13).

His own claim: art's knowledge is neither propositional nor non-propositional but **a third**
— "eine Erkenntnispraxis sui generis" (p. 13), because the pair propositional /
non-propositional has already conceded the measure to the propositional side.

## 2. The centre of the text for this program: Duchamp's *Trois Stoppages Étalon* (1914)

Mersch treats it (pp. 14–15) as the exemplary case, and Duchamp called it his most important
work.

**The procedure.** Three threads, each exactly one metre, calibrated against the Paris
standard metre, dropped three times from a height of exactly one metre onto a horizontal
plane. Free fall, air resistance and boundary conditions give a different figure each time.
Duchamp fixes each fallen thread on canvas *like a preparator* and builds from them
"Maß-Stäbe" — measuring sticks whose curved edges mock any serious act of measurement.

**What it does.** "Die Indetermination des Resultats diskreditiert das metrische System als
Willkür" (p. 14). The repetition produced "eine Serie von Einzelfällen, die jedes Mal das
singuläre Maß eines zufällig gekräuselten Raumes wiedergab" (p. 14). Duchamp: "Es gibt
keinen realen Grund, Kausalität zu verwenden. Warum sie nicht ironisch verwenden, indem man
eine Welt erfindet, in der die Dinge anders herauskommen als in der gewöhnlichen" (p. 14);
and "selbst die Schwerkraft ist eine Form von Zufall oder Konvention" (p. 15). The threefold
fall points at once to iterability and to rite — "um die Exoterik der Wissenschaften auf
ihren esoterischen Ursprung zu verweisen" (p. 14).

**Mersch's framing, which is the finding this program needed** (p. 15):

> artistic practice here conducts itself as "ein Stück Wissenschaftskritik, die sich
> dezidiert ästhetischer Verfahren bedient, nicht nur, um deren Methodologie auf den
> Prüfstand zu stellen, sondern um sie zu unterlaufen, zu unterminieren und mit ihrem
> Anderen, ihrer Kontingenz und Grundlosigkeit zu konfrontieren".

The guess of T1.01 §5 — *apparatus as material, not as method* — is exactly this, and it now
has a name and a canonical case. A house whose remit is counter-measurement has, in Duchamp,
the precedent for turning its own measure into the material that comes undone. The Duchamp /
Mersch lineage of measuring measurement is already claimed in the v3 draft (§6) as the
instruments' pedigree; what was missed is that it is a **procedure for works**, not a
pedigree for instruments.

## 3. 'Pataphysics — the science of the exception

Duchamp draws on Jarry (p. 15): 'pataphysics as, in distinction from metaphysics, "die
Wissenschaft vom Partikularen statt des Allgemeinen, der Ausnahme statt der Regel, des
Nebensächlichen statt des Gewöhnlichen" — *epi meta ta physika*, what is *added* to
metaphysics; a second physics of the lawless. And from the Collège de 'Pataphysique came
**Oulipo** (Queneau, Perec, Calvino, Eco, p. 15), whose formal constraints — Perec's novel
without the letter *e* — sought to found art as structural play obeying only syntax.

This confirms the program's H2 lineage and connects it to Duchamp rather than leaving it as
a literary curiosity.

## 4. Positive and negative rule use — the verdict on generative machines

Mersch distinguishes two uses of rules (pp. 15–16, referring to his own *Positive und
negative Regeln*): rules as an "un-endliche Produktion des Zufalls", or rules as the
"Askese einer Entleerung". His judgement is unambiguous:

> "Letztere erweist sich der Kunst gemäßer: Die Produktion nährt den Überschuss, der zuletzt
> ins Tautologische gerät, während die Askese das Nichts privilegiert, das allererst zu einem
> Anderem, noch Unbekannten zu öffnen vermag." (pp. 15–16)

**This is a verdict on machine art at the level of its default mode.** Endless generation of
variants — the thing a machine does best, and the entire economy of generative image
production — is the *positive* use, and it ends in the tautological. The mode "der Kunst
gemäßer" is subtraction: the lipogram, the emptying, the constraint that removes.

A machine practice that wants to make art should therefore work **ascetically**, against its
own facility. That is counter-intuitive, actionable, and testable.

## 5. Inframince and the *petites perceptions* — where a machine has a real advantage

Duchamp's *inframince* and Leibniz's *petites perceptions* (p. 16): the micro-phenomena that
vanish in the angle of attention — the sound clothing makes rubbing against itself, the smell
in old furniture, the particular colouring of a floor walked on daily, the unseen shadow in a
niche; "winzigste Farbnuancen oder kleinste noch hörbare Abstände zwischen zwei Tönen, die
Nichtanwesenheit der Stille". The arts have worked on such thresholds, "etwa durch Ausstellung
von nur über große Zeitläufe verfolgbare Materialerosionen" (p. 16).

Here the machine-advantage bar can be met **as aesthetic material** rather than as scale: a
machine holds intervals below human resolution and durations beyond human patience. Erosion
over years, differences below the threshold of noticing — these are not "big data", they are
the inframince, and they are natively machine-accessible.

## 6. Amethodicity — what replaces pre-registration

The decisive passage on method (p. 20). Scientific experiments are determined by
identity-procedures, above all repeatability, "die die Phänomene erst zu Zeichen machen",
whereas artistic ones

> "amethodisch verfahren und von keiner Hypothese getragen werden, **weil sie allererst ihre
> eigene Hypothese sind**. Auch operieren sie nicht im Iterativen, vielmehr erschöpft sich
> ihr Pathos gerade im Einzigartigen und Nichtwiederholbaren." (p. 20)

The work *is* its hypothesis. It does not test one. This settles the tension named in T1.01
§5: pre-registration cannot be an *artistic* credential, because a pre-registered work has
subordinated itself to an identity-procedure. It remains entirely legitimate for the
instruments.

## 7. Heidegger's six stations, and why "Maschinenwissenschaft" is the sharpest form of the problem

Mersch follows Heidegger's history of *experimentum* (p. 20): *empeiría* originally means "auf
etwas stoßen und zwar solches, was einem zustößt", something occurring "ohne unser Zutun";
then experience as *Wider-fahrnis*; then "Zugehen auf etwas", surveying, scouting; then
**Er-proben** — actively sought and lured out, with instruments (lens, microscope,
telescope); then collecting, ordering, classifying as subjugation under a conceptual scheme;
finally methodical empiricism with its "Vorgriff aufs Regelhafte", forcing the experienceable
under causality. Characteristic of the whole sequence is "ein Übergang von der passio zur
actio, deren vorläufig letzte Stufe die Naturwissenschaft als Mathematik und
'**Maschinenwissenschaft**' darstelle" (p. 20).

A machine practice therefore begins at the far end of a drift away from passio — Mersch's
own genealogy places it there by name. The consequence for this house: a passio register kept
as bookkeeping does not touch the problem. The drift has to be **reversed inside the work**,
and per T1.01 the only *Zu-fall* available to a machine is the world's refusal.

## 8. Two further usable passages

- **"Wendet das Mediale gegen das Mediale"** (p. 19). Art "organisiert eine Wahrnehmung in
  der Wahrnehmung … sie erschüttert die Bezüge". Its *Kon-stellationen* aim at producing
  something "Nichtaufgehendes, Nichtschließbares": "Ihr Metier ist das Zerwürfnis, im
  Wortsinne: die *katastrophé*". This is the sharper reason behind the screen line's demand
  that a work be made "out of properties only a screen has" — not novelty, but turning the
  medium against itself.
- **Eigenname against Begriff** (p. 18). From the singular only the singular can be said; it
  refers to a proper name, not to a concept (with Lévinas and Adorno). Adorno on art's most
  insistent intention: "Das ist so" — deictic, admitting no contradiction. Direct support for
  H4: in a data work the *punctum* is a name, never an aggregate.

## 9. Procedures extracted for the catalogue

- **P1 — The standard undone** (Duchamp's *Stoppages*): submit your own measure to a lawless
  real process, fix the deviation, let it stand as the new standard. → `patterns/P1`.
- **P2 — Ascetic rule use** (negative rules, Oulipo's lipogram): make by removing, against
  the machine's own facility for production. → `patterns/P2`.
- **P3 — Inframince exposition**: work at thresholds below human resolution or beyond human
  patience. Draft after T2.
- **P4 — Katastrophé / medium against medium**: constellate so that the relation does not
  close. Draft after T5, where the effect-patterns are read.
