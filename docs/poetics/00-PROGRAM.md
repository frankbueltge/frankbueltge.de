# Poetics — a research program

**Founded 2026-08-22** on the founder's direction (wording private): a new research program,
in the lab, deliberately outside the practices and outside the existing experiments —
connecting to what exists is allowed, changing it is not. Working title; the wording gate
applies.

## 1. The question

How does something new come about, and by what patterns does it take effect?

Four parts, in the order they will be worked:

1. Which **procedures** have artists used to produce what they could not have planned
   (Bacon's manipulated accident as the paradigm case)?
2. Which **patterns of effect** reliably make an aesthetic experience possible — the way
   dramaturgy, film and music have transferable structures that work?
3. What makes a **data work** singular and consequential rather than illustrative?
4. Which of these can a **machine** adopt — autonomously, or hybrid with a human on the
   loop — and which are constitutively human?

## 2. Why the program exists: the located gap

The house holds three layers of art-theoretical equipment. Two of them exist:

| Layer | Present | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Evaluative** | yes — the work bar (ecology v3 draft, Move 2), the ten principles distilled from 2000 years of event-objects, the published critique of data art, the saturated middle and the kitsch floor of the data-in-form dossier | tells you whether a work is **bad** |
| **Epistemic** | yes — Ulysses' Toolbox v0.3: twelve-plus strategies, each with a use-case and a failure test (Adversarial Premise Test, Technical-object Reversal, Trace-chain Exposure, Agential-cut Comparison, Singular Measure, Problem-field Mutation …), derived from Barad, Simondon, Derrida | tells you how to make a **research move** |
| **Poetic** | **no** | would tell you how a **form comes into being** and by what means it **takes effect** |

Roughly ten thousand lines of research foundation exist, and not one of them is a generative
procedure. The absence is not an oversight: the toolbox was built out of epistemologists,
not out of workshop accounts. Epistemology says what a move *means*; poetics says how a move
is *made*.

## 3. What this program is not

- **Not a practice.** No constitution, no persona, no nightly cadence, no engine, no
  automation. *(2026-08-23: a practice that took the T1 ground as its own was **founded and
  archived the same day** on the founder's decision — `practice/CONSTITUTION.md`, post-mortem at
  its head. Its first candidate, W1, failed the founder's reading; its ground was found to be
  the machine's deficit rather than Mersch's centre; the re-grounded version was declined as one
  more practice showing its own limits to no one. This programme stays exactly what this line
  says. Its §5 criterion is open again: W1 no longer counts as the named work candidate.)*
- **It does not touch `arch`.** That test-bed's own rule is that its hermeneutic reading
  (U1) must precede its model decision. Doing that reading here would contaminate the very
  experiment arch was founded to run. This program reads *other* material for *other* ends,
  and hands arch nothing but source locations if asked.
- **It does not touch the ecology practices or the existing experiments.** It may cite them.
- **It is not a sixth dossier.** The house's documented failure at exactly this point is
  Bishop's *research display substituting for a proposition*. See §4 and §5.

## 4. Deliverable format — the anti-dossier rule

The output is a **pattern language** in the format Ulysses' toolbox already uses, one
file per procedure in `patterns/`. Every entry carries all five fields:

1. **Operational core** — what is actually done, in steps a machine could follow.
2. **Provenance** — who, when, and the source with page number.
3. **Machine translation** — what a machine can do with it, what it cannot, and why.
4. **Cheap test** — runnable inside one session, with a stated go/no-go.
5. **Failure test** — what would show the procedure was misapplied rather than the material
   being uncooperative (the toolbox's own discipline).

Reading notes live in `readings/` as **paraphrase with cited page numbers**. No source text
is committed: the sources are copyrighted, reading is not distribution, and the rule of
2026-08-22 stands — bibliography and citation in the repo, the texts stay where they are.

## 5. Failure criterion, pre-registered

Fixed in advance so the program can lose:

- **By 2026-09-19**, at least **three** procedures have passed their cheap test, and
  **one work candidate** is named that could not have been proposed before this research.
- If either is missing, the program has failed, says so in the record, and is archived
  dated. A well-read failure is still a failure.

Reading without a named work at the end is the exact pattern this program exists to break.

## 7. Standing theses — each able to lose

- **H1.** Cage and Bacon use chance in opposite directions: Cage removes the ego, Bacon
  exploits the accident. A machine has no ego to remove, so Cage's use is empty for it;
  Bacon's is available, and it is *operational passio* — the actio/passio loop Mersch
  demands, as a technique rather than an aspiration.
- **H2.** The *clinamen* — a documented, motivated violation of one's own rule — is the
  technique that answers "a machine executing a rule flawlessly proves nothing".
- **H3.** What made data art consequential was never the mapping but the **addressee**: it
  endangered someone. Haacke's cancelled exhibition and Lombardi's networks are the
  evidence; the house's own principles (debtor, wound, stake) are the same finding arrived
  at from the other side.
- **H4.** In a data work the *punctum* can only be the singular named datum. An aggregate
  can inform; it cannot wound.
