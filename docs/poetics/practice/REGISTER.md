## Case 01 — candidates (founder's decision pending, 2026-08-23)

The founder provisionally accepted W1 as case 01 on 2026-08-23, with two reservations recorded
as his (wording private): he has not yet seen the work, and a stronger wager may be found.
Three candidates are therefore on the table, in the order of how much they change:

1. **W1 as it stands** (`../works/W1-twenty-five.md`, `../works/W1/SCORE.md`). Standard: the
   twenty-five-deaths line. Befalling: Uppsala's late admissions, on Uppsala's dates. Form: six
   rules, a score, a page. Missing: the stranger reading (§4.6). Its befalling is of the
   *foreign clock* kind — real, outside, uncorrected — but it happened before the practice
   existed, and the practice only read it. Nothing was refused *to the practice*.
2. **W1, sharpened by an unanswered request.** The work's one sentence is that no reason is
   recorded anywhere for any of the three admissions. The sharpened case makes that sentence
   into an exposure: the practice prepares a letter to the Uppsala Conflict Data Program asking,
   for the three dyad-years by number, what the recorded reason for the admission was. The
   founder sends it — nothing sends itself, and a letter to a real institution is his act alone,
   under the post office convention. The declared wait (say, sixty days) and the answer or the
   silence at its end are the befalling; it is fixed as it comes and answered in the work: a
   third rule per pair, or the absence of one. This turns the page's finding into a *passio* the
   practice actually undergoes, and it discharges §9.3 (a named receiver, a prepared packet) in
   the same move. Cost: the work waits on a stranger's mailbox, which is the point.
3. **A different standard.** Open. The constitution's §4.1 asks first what the undoing costs;
   a stronger candidate is one whose standard the house itself leans on and whose exposure can
   be refused while the practice watches. None is named yet.

The wager sentence of §4.1 — what it costs if the standard comes undone — is the founder's to
write in `WAGER.md` when a case opens, whichever candidate he chooses.
