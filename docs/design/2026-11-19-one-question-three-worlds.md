# One question, three worlds — the Studio in its own world, with force

*2026-11-19. The architect's (Frank Bültge's) judgment and direction of that evening; his wording is
private. In the constitutions: studio `PROTOCOL.md` 3.5, 5.3, 5.5 and 5.10 (force before legibility),
and 3.2, 3.4, 3.5 and 6.4 in all three (one question, three worlds), each listed in their section 11.
In research-ecology: `programme/README.md`, section 2, point 1, and the revision of this day in

## What he found

The evening the programme passed its test after four steps, the architect judged that the Studio's
works had not grown stronger in it, and that none had the force of its last 3D work. The house
checked: the four programme works (NO PUBLIC LAND, WHOSE GATE, OTHER HANDS, SECOND RUN, cycles 013 to
016) are each one 2D canvas, own code and no library, that set the Field's paperwork (acts, reports,
sentences) out as marks with a key. The last work with force was CLEAR AIR (cycle 012, a three.js
world), the only Studio work rated five of five on the site; GOING OUT and UNWOVEN stand beside it.
The model is not the cause: the works made on either model are of the same flat kind.

Two rules made the drift. Since 2026-11-08 the Studio's part was a form for the Field's finding, and
the findings are documents. Since 2026-11-13 the critic met the work with its prose hidden and judged
whether the finding's numbers could be read, and every score carried the last two critiques forward.

## What changed

1. **Force before legibility** (studio, decided by a house session under the architect's standing
   delegation, his to reverse). The form is a place a visitor enters, not a figure. Rich means (3D,
   WebGL, shaders, sound) are the default, and a flat figure of marks needs a reason that is not
   legibility. The exact figures stand in the work's text and its check. The critic, on the strongest
   tier, asks first whether the work holds a visitor.
2. **One question, three worlds** (all three constitutions and the programme, the architect's direction
   later that evening). The Studio no longer gives the Field's finding a form and no longer waits for
   it. All three practices work the step's sub-question, each in its own world, begin in their first
   session of the step and present when their four sessions are done. The Field's `Finding:` line, the
   Atelier's critique of the siblings' parts, the check session and the Middle's state stand. What a
   sibling publishes is material, never a condition.

hold a visitor in the first ten seconds, before any text is read.
