# The step in order — the finding first, a check that counts, the plan sets the next step

*2026-11-13. Ordered by the architect (Frank Bültge) after a review of the ecology he asked for that
day; his wording is private. In the three constitutions it stands as the amendment of 2026-11-13, in
research-ecology in `programme/README.md` and `briefings/README.md`.*

## What the review found

It read the programme's first step (cycle 013, 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-10), the convening after it and the
first session of step 2, with one reader per practice and a check of the Middle, the workflows and
the site.

What carries: the figures recompute from the Field's committed files, and the practices take each other
up (27 of the 40 offers made since 2026-11-08 were taken; corrections went both ways).

What held the work back:

1. **The form came before the finding.** The Studio began NO PUBLIC LAND on the Field's first study and
   presented it on 2026-11-09; the Field finished on 2026-11-10. Three of the five findings of the step
   have no form. From 2026-11-03 to 2026-11-12 the Studio ran 21 sessions, 7 of them on a cycle's work.
2. **Nothing was read twice.** A session works for about ten minutes (in the Field, 7 to 12 minutes lie
   between the commit of its predictions and its last commit), and the rule of 2026-10-22 counts a
   session only if the work changes where a visitor meets it. A second reading was no increment.
3. **The state held claims that agreed with their works and still misled.** The rise "from 56.6 % in
   March 2022" compares a count of Nvidia's chips with a count of six designers (Nvidia alone: 56.6 to
   61.6 %); one point gave 10–20 % and 6.1 % for the same coverage; "the developers hold none" stood
   without the limit the Atelier's critique gives it. Corrected in research-ecology#80.
4. **The convening took two of the step's four days** to choose between three wordings of a step the
   plan already named.
5. **Every result is written four to six times**, and the bulletin keeps its forty lines with about
   750 words. Three contradictions stand between amendments (the relay's duty against "at most one
   sibling's material" against "three parts that need each other").

## What changes

1. **The step has an order.** The Field publishes its finding and dataset by the end of its second
   session (`Finding:` in its bulletin). The Studio begins the step's work after that line stands and
   presents last. Before it, a Studio session returns to the form of the last step or closes in one line.
2. **A check is a change.** One session of every work, before its presentation, starts nothing new: it
   reads the work's claims a second time against their sources, chases the weakest one, and answers the
   critique left open. A figure travels with its limit.
3. **Inside a programme the plan sets the next step.** The practices propose and rank nothing. Each
   presentation ends with `Next step:`; the Middle writes the step into `STATE.md`, a second reader
   checks it, and the Middle sets the next step in `PLAN.md`. There is no tally and no veto day for a
   step; the architect can change the plan at any time, and an objection holds the clock.
4. **One record.** The bulletin: at most 40 lines and 400 words, no line that reports nothing. The
   journal entry: at most 150 words on how the session went. The commit subject names the session and
   the work's state.
5. **Inside a programme a work takes what the step gives it.** "At most one sibling's material" (rule
   of 2026-10-22, point 6) does not apply while a programme runs.

The second reader of `STATE.md` also reads the claims against each other, against the source's own
caveat and against the critique on record (`briefings/README.md`, "How a claim is checked").
