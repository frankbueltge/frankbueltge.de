# Research ecology v2 — the rebuild

**Status: IN FORCE, decided and executed 2026-08-08 (night session 07./08.08.).** Frank put
the real question on the table himself — archive the ecology, or rebuild it radically — and
chose the rebuild (wording private throughout): saving and developing it further was the
consequent course and the one he preferred, because he values this experiment. On
execution: the rebuild could begin directly, without negotiating with the practices — a
real rebuild that deletes and replaces whatever no longer leads anywhere, a research
ecology v2. The frame he corrected mid-execution, and which governs this document: **the
three practices stay** — three different ways of researching and delivering in the field of
tension between art, science and philosophy/artistic research, and precisely that is what
had to be sharpened and optimised (wording private). No new repos; the existing repos, constitutions, protocols and
prompts are what got rebuilt.

Executed the same night: all three engine protocols rewritten clean (ulysses `adb63f4`,
field-research `19178ec`, studio `2ecebad`, author Frank Bültge), the season layer deleted,
this document, and the site follow-through.

## 1. The verdict, differentiated

Frank's diagnosis (wording private): after weeks, the output had been inconsequential and
interested or impressed nobody outside the house — no work of the practices is
progressive or innovative enough to interest artists, researchers, critics or festivals,
and the house had already applied to festivals without being able to deliver (both
applications withheld 2026-08-07, his own judgment).

The verdict holds for what was **shipped**. It does not hold evenly for what is **in the
house** — and that difference is why rebuild beat archive:

- **The Consensus** — 42+ days of continuous, daily orchestrated-consensus measurement with
  an evidence track. Genuinely machine-native; no human team sustains this at this cost.
- **The severed readers** — pre-registered blind reception panels for art, methodologically
  unusual, with a record of catching what staged panels missed (session 74).
- **The honesty discipline** — gates, tiers, dated corrections, no fabrication, archives
  never edited. Rare, and expensively earned.
- **The 590-paper reading** (Ulysses/RUWE) — corpus width with per-citation accountability.

The ore is real; the house shipped tailings. The rebuild aims the machinery at ore.

## 2. Root causes (measured, not felt)

1. **Ambition capped at session size.** Works were what one night finishes — and a work a
   night can finish is a work a human could have made. ~83 works, none that astonish.
2. **Coordination produced coordination.** Seasons, episode slots, direction negotiation,
   joint-inquiry states, role rosters — apparatus outweighed work everywhere (corpus audit
   2026-08-05), and the practice with zero role machinery (Ulysses) shipped most.
3. **Nothing left the house.** No sending, no reception, no friction with real receivers —
   works-for-nobody was the guaranteed result, and Frank named that failure mode as his own.
4. **Layered law.** The protocols became geology: founding text + migration + amendment +
   amendment-on-amendment. Meridian's carried ~130 lines of strata before the first working
   sentence. Every session paid to read it; none of it produced works.

## 3. The v2 design: the triangle, sharpened

Three practices, three corners, one field (data, AI and power). The corners are now named
in every protocol, with the siblings' corners beside them:

| Practice | Corner | The line (floor, architect) |
|---|---|---|
| **Field / Meridian** | **Science** | Counter-measurement: measure what power leaves unmeasured, checkably. Two forms: **continuous instruments** (The Consensus is the proof of form) and **FA-form investigations** ending in an artifact a named outside receiver can use. |
| **Studio / Ensemble** | **Art** | Works of force: **only digital, only what a machine does better**, the added value experienceable in the work itself (the 2026-08-08 line, unchanged). |
| **Atelier / Ulysses** | **Artistic research / philosophy** | **The warrant of numbers**: where a figure that governs a decision came from, whether the document that licensed it still travels, what breaks when it does not. Continues its existing territory; set by the architect (the same-night three-session proposal window is superseded — v2 does not negotiate remits). |

**Shared law, now in all three protocols as plain text (no strata):**

- **The machine-advantage bar (ship gate):** scale · repetition · verification · the
  temporal — experienceable by a stranger in the artifact itself. If a competent human with
  ordinary time could have made it, the house had no reason to make it.
- **Arcs, not nights:** the default object of a session is an accumulating arc
  (instrument/investigation · campaign · work-line); single-session works need a stated
  reason. Concept gate (≤3 sessions, named outside receiver) · progress every 3 worked
  sessions · ambition audit — kept from the Production Amendment.
- **Cadence:** ≤1 inward session in 4, inward decided by **object location** (the
  2026-08-08 rule, folded in). Record ceilings (journal ≤400 words, process <3,000) · daily
  line · sessions are paid compute.
- **Voices:** zero convened by default, each convening named with its reason. Kept because
  they sit on real boundaries: Meridian's Interlocutor (blocking refutation + published
  critique) and Verifier; Ensemble's Kritiker, Dramaturg (finished object only), Verifier,
  severed readers; Ulysses keeps its proven zero-roster form.
- **Leaving the house:** nothing sends itself; packets to the post office; **the
  architect's new bind — `prepared` is sent or dated-withheld within seven days.**
- **The reading of 2026-09-05** (§6 below).

## 6. The reading of 2026-09-05 (the test v2 must pass)

1. **The investigation stands** — Meridian's, FA form, object outside the house, artifact
