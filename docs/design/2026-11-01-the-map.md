# The map — decision record (2026-11-01, IN FORCE)

*Frank's decision of 2026-11-01, wording private. Amends `2026-10-25-the-briefing.md` and
`2026-11-01-the-checked-briefing.md`; everything else of them stands.*

## Why

The briefing is what the ecology knows of the world, and it feeds Third Reading, Ducking, the watchlist
and the practices' next question. Frank asked for it to be a real mirror of what is going wrong. Counted
the same evening, it was not:

- Poverty, inequality, the concentration of money and power, democracy and rights were no matter in
  either edition. AI was three of ten in the second. Sudan, Gaza and Myanmar were absent from the second.
- It asks what is new, and a state of affairs has no date. Nothing announces that 824 million people live
  in extreme poverty.
- Six fields stood in the rules, and nothing checked them. The rule against repeating a cycle's subject
  took hunger out of the mirror altogether.
- Common Ground, the attention side, read the American edition of Google News alone. By signal count the
  ledger was 35 % encyclopaedia pages, 29 % developer platforms and 3 % news. In thirty days and 25,803
  signals, *poverty* matched twice, and every *Sudan* in the ledger was a football fixture.

Many rankings of humanity's problems exist, and none of them can be the base, because each has a
standpoint: the WEF asks leaders, 80,000 Hours has its school, Ipsos measures worry. So the base is not a
ranking but a map.

## The decision

1. **Eleven standing fields:** war, hunger, poverty, inequality, concentration of money and power,
   climate, health, displacement, democracy and rights, information and manipulation, AI. Canon:
   `src/lib/briefing/map.ts`; their sources: research-ecology `briefings/MAP.md`.
2. **Every briefing opens with the map:** one line per field, with a dated figure, its original source, a
   direction and the field's attention. A field without news still has its line.
3. **Matters come from the map:** each has a field, at most two per field, and a field that stayed empty in
   both earlier briefings has to have one now. A state can be a matter. The check enforces this from cycle
   012 on (`briefing/2`), and the two earlier editions count, so 012 owes poverty, inequality, power and
   democracy a matter each.
4. **The rule against repeating moves.** A proposal may not repeat the subject of the two cycles before it.
   The briefing may: a mirror does not leave out what was just looked at.
5. **An outside check on "Urgent but quiet":** the second session reads it against the newest lists of
   neglected crises (CARE, NRC) and names what it leaves out.
6. **Common Ground changes with it:** Google News is read in ten English-language editions, and the
   watchlist follows the fields.

## Common Ground

- **Sources.** Ten editions (US, GB, AU, IN, PK, PH, NG, KE, ET, ZA), twenty stories each, a story two
  editions carry counted once: from about 3 % of the signals to about 15 %. English on purpose, because the
  one count matches English terms. Google offers no English edition for the Arab world, and AE, SA and LB
  fall back to the American one; the method sheet says so.
- **Watchlist.** Nineteen editorial terms, one to four per field, with their Wikipedia articles, tracked
  from the next run, never backfilled. They give each field an arc on news search, forums and pageviews.
- **A repair on the way.** The ceiling on promotions counted the whole list, and the briefing's seeds had
  filled it: the machine had promoted nothing since 2026-09-09. It now counts only the machine's own terms.

## What moves with it

- **A step in the record.** From the day file of 2026-11-02 the ledger has more news in it. Committed days
  are not rewritten. Shares before and after are not the same measure, and the three method sheets date it.
- **The words.** Loud, quiet and absent keep their fixed rule, so more matters will read quiet. A word after
  that day does not compare with one before it. The first map says so.
- **Third Reading** takes its matters and counts as before. A round whose thirty days cross the date mixes both.
- **Ducking** keeps its channels for now. Fixed faders, one per field, are the next step and a separate
  decision: on the first count the fields move far more over the record than ten matters do.
- **The practices** read the map as part of the briefing. Their constitutions do not change: `Docks onto:`
  still names a matter.

## What it does not do

It does not make the world's attention measurable. Common Ground stays a set of public charts. And it does
not rank the fields: which matter is most urgent stays a judgement, written down and checked.
