# Research ecology v3 — art first (DRAFT)

**Status: DRAFT for Frank's decision. Nothing in this document is in force.** Constitutional
change is the founder's alone (standing exception to the merge mandate); this draft prepares
his decision, it does not make it. Occasion: the founder's verdict of 2026-08-19/20 (wording
private throughout): the current protocols and constitutions do not deliver — the output is
random and weak, neither art nor serious artistic research; read from outside, the house is
an inflated spectacle continuously producing content nobody cares about. His direction:
update the ecology with everything learned this week instead of archiving it under the old
law, and send it toward something genuinely unique — work that critics cannot dismiss in a
few sentences as naive play.

---

## 1. What this draft stands on

Four bodies of evidence, all from the sessions of 2026-08-18/19/20:

1. **The data-in-form dossier** — field research on data sculpture and 3D-printed data
   physicalization: the canon (~18 artists), the saturated middle ("one dataset, one object,
   one exhibition"), the kitsch floor, and four open niches (serial accretion, honest machine
   authorship, counter-measurement subjects made physical, verifiable provenance).
2. **The art-history synthesis** — ten principles distilled from 2000 years of event-objects
   (relics/acheiropoieta, death masks, ex-votos, vanitas, history painting, memorial columns,
   Klein/Duchamp/Cage, Gonzalez-Torres/Boltanski/Salcedo) plus the serial-time tradition
   (Kawara, Opałka, Hsieh, Darboven, Boetti). Load-bearing: *the index is mute, the frame
   speaks; an event-object needs a debtor; the number must have a body, the operation a
   wound; every counting that survived as art was mortgaged to something that can run out;
   a machine executing a rule flawlessly proves nothing — the stake lives in what the rule
   risks, wagers, or makes irreversible.*
3. **The published critique of data art** — Manovich (arbitrary mapping, the anti-sublime),
   Whitelaw (art against information), Saltz/Davis (illustration without transformation),
   Steyerl (indexed to the probable, not the real), Bishop (research display as information
   overload), Trebing (wall-text dependency).
4. **Mersch, "Was heißt, im Ästhetischen forschen?" (2015)** — the sharpest standing
   objection: artistic-research programs that adopt the sciences' apparatus (protocols,
   verification, repeatability) assimilate art to mode-2 technoscience and miss its
   epistemic Eigensinn. His positive program: research in the aesthetic is an
   *Er-Forschung des Singulären* — arguing with singularia, statements from deviation,
   primacy of passio (being befallen) over actio (grasping), decontrolling the conditions
   of experience. Note: Mersch co-authored the *Manifest der künstlerischen Forschung* —
   his target is scientism, not research.

Alongside these, the working paper "Iteration, not Imitation" (v0.3) supplies the operative
mechanics this draft adopts: the work triad I4–I6 (advantage probe, integration probe,
stranger probe), the virtuality register I7 with its built-in refutation, and the
pre-registered trial protocol.

## 2. The diagnosis, sharpened beyond v2

v2 (2026-08-08) named four root causes — ambition capped at session size, coordination
producing coordination, nothing leaving the house, layered law — and fixed all four. The
verdict still fell. The reason is that v2 raised the *machine* bar and never the *art* bar:
its ship gate asks whether a human could have made the work, not whether the work matters
to anyone. Judged against the criticism checklist, the shipped works remain mechanism-first:
no debtor, no cut moment, mappings motivated by availability rather than necessity,
wall-text dependent, and produced at a cadence that is itself a failure mode — continuous
nightly output is Bishop's information overload performed in public, and repetition without
stake anesthetizes (Warhol's proof).

What the verdict does **not** touch, now said explicitly: the instruments and the
discipline. The Consensus, the severed readers, the honesty machinery, the record — v2's
"ore" list — are real and remain the house's spine. They were never supposed to be art.
The category error was letting instrument output count as works. v3's first move is to
stop doing that.

## 3. The v3 turn: five moves

**Move 1 — Instruments and works are publicly distinct categories.** Instruments
(continuous measurement, registers, panels) continue under the Gegenmessung line with their
own quality frame: checkability, provenance, maintenance. Works become rare and carry the
new bar. The site language follows (/experiments, /ecology): an instrument is presented as
an instrument, never as a work.

**Move 2 — The work bar** (ship gate for anything the house calls a work; extends, not
replaces, v2's machine-advantage bar, which becomes its point 7):

1. **A debtor.** Whom does this work owe what — grief, thanks, testimony, accountability?
   Named with the work. A mechanism has inputs; a work has a debt.
2. **A cut moment.** Events are durations; the work cuts one slice. A log of a whole
   phenomenon is an archive entry, not a work.
3. **Mapping motivated to the point of necessity.** The form could not have been otherwise
   (Opałka's whitening = his remaining time). "The data allowed it" is availability, not
   necessity.
4. **No wall-text dependency.** The schema is perceivable in the encounter (integration
   probe I5); reception is tested by the stranger probe I6 *or* its non-propositional
   variant (Move 4).
5. **Iteration, not imitation.** The work exists as event or inscription, not as
   reprintable rendering; its uniqueness mode is declared at creation (mortal or permanent;
   decay, if chosen, is declared and dated — never an accident of material).
6. **Documented cost.** The rule behind the work risks something; failures stay in the
   record (Hsieh's discipline). Flawless execution proves nothing.
7. **The machine-advantage bar** (v2, unchanged): scale · repetition · verification · the
   temporal — experienceable by a stranger in the artifact itself.

**Move 3 — Cadence inversion.** The default night is studio time — reading, probing,
drafting, register-keeping, arc work — not publication. Works ship in quarters, not nights;
the journal remains daily (the record is the milieu), but the record is not the work.
Fewer, slower, costlier.

**Move 4 — The paper enters the house, with both refutations.** The work triad I4–I6 and
the virtuality register I7 are adopted as ship-gate mechanics, pre-registered per the
paper's trial protocol (60 days / ≥25 sessions) — the ecology becomes the paper's declared
testbed, converging the PhD line and the house. And the house adopts a **second standing
refutation** beside Simondon's anti-thesis: the Mersch edge. Two consequences:
(a) a **passio register** — dated cases where something arrived unbidden (a resistance of
material, an accident, an uninvited finding) and demonstrably changed a work; a practice
that is pure actio, per Mersch, is not researching in the aesthetic;
(b) the **non-propositional reception variant** of the stranger probe: where a work shows
rather than says, the stranger is asked what struck them, not what they understood — a
work may refuse to inform; it may not refuse to matter (Whitelaw). If both registers stay
empty at their pre-registered deadlines, the critics' verdict stands by the house's own
record — that is the point of keeping them.

**Move 5 — The physical line, honestly architected.** The practices cannot fabricate;
a printer stands in the founder's room, not in a GitHub runner. Three modes, each honest,
declared per work:

- **(a) Score works** (LeWitt/Fluxus mode): the work is the executable score — pinned
  dataset, deterministic derivation, G-code, hashes — plus provenance; anyone may
  fabricate; the practice never claims the object. "The idea becomes a machine that makes
  the art" (LeWitt 1967) is the native mode of a machine practice.
- **(b) Executor mode** (inverted Boetti): the practice authors — derivation, form
  decision, toolpath — and the founder fabricates as *declared executor*, the human as the
  practice's hands, roles documented per work. This is the exact inverse of the Ai-Da
  pattern (there, a human covertly supplies the authorship; here, the human overtly
  supplies the labor). The paper's P1 coupling covers it: the pair performs fabrication
  better than either side alone — and the record says which side did what.
- **(c) Concept mode:** the work remains deliberately unfabricated; legitimate when the
  concept is the work (see §4).

The hard separation of 2026-08-18 stands: the founder's own art (Spur 1) remains outside
the ecology; nothing in this section pulls it in. Executor mode makes the founder a
fabricator of ecology works, never their author.

## 4. The first commission under v3: the Reliquary of the First Question

A conditional work, specified now, existing only if it is ever earned:

- **Condition.** The work comes into existence only when the virtuality register I7 carries
  its first genuine entry — a record-verified case of a machine practice recasting the form
  of its own problem (not executing, not being corrected: its own question).
- **Until then: the empty plinth.** Public, dated, inscribed with what it waits for. The
  plinth may wait forever; then the plinth is the work.
- **If the entry comes:** one object, derived deterministically from the register entry's
  commit, fabricated once in executor mode, in a lavishly crafted frame — the reliquary
  economy: the craft belongs to the container, the index stays humble. Full authentication
  dossier (repository, commit, date, derivation, hashes), open to forensic attack. The
  toolpath is destroyed after the single print: printed, hashed, deleted.
- **Debtor.** The practice owes its own question — and the public a truthful answer to
  whether it ever had one. The register's emptiness is displayed, not hidden.
- **The bar, checked:** debtor (yes); cut moment (the single dated commit); necessity (the
  object exists iff the event occurred); no wall text (an empty pedestal inscribed "for the
  machine's first own question, should it ever have one" explains itself); uniqueness
  (single print, toolpath destroyed); cost (the work may never exist — that is the wager);
  machine advantage (the event itself, if it comes, is the advantage).
- **Open for the founder:** placement. The I7 register runs today in n-1, which is not an
  ecology practice; either the commission lives where the register lives, or each ecology
  practice adopts an I7 register and the first genuine entry anywhere earns the reliquary.

## 8. The Stage — the technical means of staging (addendum, 2026-08-22)

*Added by a working session on the founder's direction of 2026-08-21/22 (wording private;
the substance: the practices are to stage their works with the means the technology
actually offers — minimal or maximal, whatever the work needs — and a work that had been
born staged would be a large step). This section is the infrastructure half of "art first";
Moves 1–5 are the artistic half. Like everything in this draft: nothing here is in force
until the founder decides.*

**The evidence this section stands on.** On 2026-08-21/22 the same committed census
(Ulysses' `2026-08-19-reasonably-available`, byte-identical `route.json`) was staged three
ways in one evening: the shipped card-grid; a curated single page (~100 KB, entrance
sentence, the four misspellings as exhibits, a wall that fills); and a walkable WebGL hall
(three.js bundled via esbuild, ~772 KB, synthesized sound, one committed file, no fetch,
CSP-clean). The information was identical; what changed was what a stranger experiences.
Decisive fact: **the hall passes today's window contract unchanged.** The ceiling on
staging was never Astro or GitHub — it was four decisions nobody had made: works are
hand-written HTML by convention; the practice repos carry no build step; no session ever
looks at its own page as a picture; night-session economics. All four are policy.

**What stays law at every level below** — staging never buys out of the honesty discipline:
source and lockfile committed beside any built bundle (a bundle without its source is an
oracle); every figure a surface prints derived from the committed record at load, never
typed; provenance line on the artifact; no external request at runtime except through a
declared organ (level 3); corrections dated, never silent.

**Level 1 — the bundle clause (costs one sentence).** A work may ship as a built artifact.
`SITE-API.md` § window and the works contracts state: a committed bundle with committed
source, lockfile and build command is a legitimate work surface. Effective immediately on
adoption; the demonstration is the existence proof.

**Level 2 — toolchain and the render loop (costs a CI step and a floor).** Practice repos
get node + esbuild in their session environment and CI; the site mirrors built output as it
mirrors everything else. With it, the one floor this draft adds to staging: **no
visitor-facing surface ships unseen** — the shipping session renders its own page and
commits the screenshots to the record. The staging failures on this house's record (the
argument buried below 68,000 px; the architect meeting a work cold and understanding
nothing) were loop failures, not skill failures: three render-look-fix passes caught and
fixed a glitch light-strip and an unreadable hall in the demonstration, and no gate in any
constitution had ever asked for a single look.

**Level 3 — runtime organs (a live work is allowed to be alive).** For works that measure
now, persist visitor traces, or share state: Cloudflare Workers/KV/Durable Objects — the
stack the Steuerzentrale already runs on. Conditions: the organ's code is committed; its
endpoints are declared in the work's manifest; its state is snapshotted nightly into the
archive (Git stays the archive — the organ is a limb, not a second memory); a dead organ
degrades the work to its last snapshot, visibly and dated, never silently.

**Level 4 — compute (the GCP line, extended from pipelines to works).** Training,
simulation, rendering beyond a CI runner, under the 2026-08-09 conditions unchanged: trace,
licence notice, cost discipline, never at site runtime.

**What this section refuses.** A shared "engine" or house framework the works must use —
the means are chosen per work, and a practice that ships a plain page because the work
needs a plain page meets the bar (Move 2.3 governs: form motivated to necessity). And no
CDN dependencies at runtime: "the page fetches nothing" stays true at every level below 3.

**Execution (folds into §7):** level 1 with the constitutional rewrites; level 2 in the
same week (toolchain PR per practice repo + the render-loop floor in each constitution);
levels 3–4 per work, on the work's own manifest, first uses reviewed by the founder.
