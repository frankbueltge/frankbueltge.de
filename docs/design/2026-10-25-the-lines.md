# The lines — what the ecology keeps researching across cycles (2026-10-25)

**Decision.** The architect, 2026-10-25 (wording private), on the proposal "Forschungslinien": the
ecology keeps a few research lines across cycles, docks them onto institutions and research outside
the house, and makes its outputs citable. Set after the briefing of the same day
(`docs/design/2026-10-25-the-briefing.md`) and changing none of its rules.

**Why.** None of the 22 presentations of cycles 003–010 refers to a cycle older than the one before
it (the convening of 2026-10-07 docked each question only onto its predecessor). In cycle 010 the
Field re-measured Eurostat's withheld cells (studies 39–41) without the Studio's register of the same
cells from 2026-09-12 (*TOO FEW TO HIDE BEHIND*). No repository carried a `CITATION.cff` or a DOI.

**What.**
- `research-ecology/lines/`: rules (`README.md`) and one file per line, kept by the Middle in the
  briefing run (Middle Briefing prompt, step 4b). Three lines from the record: `withheld` (active),
  `record-of-danger` (resting since the objection of 2026-10-24), `machine-written-records` (active).
- The three constitutions, amendment "the lines": a proposal adds `Line: <slug> — advances: Q<n>`;
  a line is not a subject (the briefing's no-repeat rule means the subject); the first session writes
  `Builds on:` into `SCORE.md`; the summary ends with *For the line*; docking; `CITATION.cff`.
- The relay records `Line:` as an optional `line` on a proposal (`relay/README.md`, verifier).
- The site mirrors `lines/*.md` to `src/data/middle/lines/` (ecology-integrate.yml) and renders
  `/lines` and `/lines/<slug>/` (`src/lib/ecology/research-lines.ts`).

**Zenodo, tried and withdrawn (same evening).** The repositories were connected and the 28 cycle
presentations were released and minted. The architect then decided to withdraw them: Zenodo's policy
excludes deposits made largely by generative AI, bulk AI deposits and machine authors. The release
workflow is removed and the records are deleted inside Zenodo's 30-day window. What stays: the
`CITATION.cff` in each repository, and a paper of the Field may be deposited by hand under the
architect's name with the machine's part disclosed. Any preprint is likewise his decision.
