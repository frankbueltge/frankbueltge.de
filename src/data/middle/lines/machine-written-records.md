# Line: machine-written-records

Status: active · Opened: 2026-10-25, from cycles 001–002, 006 and the Field's standing work ·
Kept after cycle: 010 (in part)

## 1. Question

What becomes of a finding when a machine annotates, classifies or researches it, and how would anyone
outside the machine check it?

## 2. Standing

1. A research loop that runs unattended every night since 2026-09-03 can fetch, ask, pre-register,
   test and review its own claims in about ninety seconds, against a scrambled control. It has no
   literature step. *Holds* — Field, [cycle 002](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/presentations/cycle-002); open question 38 in
   `memory/open-questions.md`.
2. In the CSET annotations (158 annotator pairs, κ 0.62), agreement on *near miss* is 35 %, and all 10
   final near misses carry one annotator's call. *Holds* — Field,
   [the near miss in the record](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/artifacts/2026-10-09-the-near-miss-in-the-record).
3. Of 1,713 incidents in the AI Incident Database, 3 were labelled 7.1 by an unvalidated pipeline;
   1 meets the label. *Holds* — Field, [the machine that labels the machine](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/artifacts/2026-10-08-the-machine-that-labels-the-machine).
4. The evidence on AI and extinction is produced by the parties it concerns: 0 of 14 incidents were
   observed independently; the developer saw 8 first. *Holds* — Field,
   [paper 1](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/presentations/cycle-006/paper.md).
5. When an outcome can never be observed, each stand-in (who answered, what can be paid out, what is
   scored) rewards something other than being right. *Holds* — Studio,
   [THE UNPAID NUMBER](https://github.com/frankbueltge/studio/tree/main/presentations/cycle-006); Atelier, [Who is left to write it](https://github.com/frankbueltge/ulysses/tree/main/presentations/cycle-006).
6. A licence file is not a licence: 78.6 % of repositories carry one; 95.2 % of 156 deliver, and the
   95.2 % depends on which defects one repairs. *Holds* — Field, licensing arc, closed 2026-09-22;
   instrument `tools/is-it-a-licence-v2/`.
7. 1,667 scientific papers carry a public expression of concern with no retraction on record.
   *Holds as of 2026-09-01* — Studio, [NOT YET](https://github.com/frankbueltge/studio/tree/main/works/2026-09-01-not-yet).
8. 51.3 % of an editor's letters reach a model's training text as the scribe's. *In progress* —
   Atelier, [Supplied](https://github.com/frankbueltge/ulysses/tree/main/artifacts/cycle-010/supplied) (critique of 2026-10-24 open).
9. **The house's own ledger.** The Field pre-registers every prediction before it measures and
   publishes which held: cycle 006, 5 of 19 failed; cycle 007, 6 of 23 failed; cycle 008, 22 of 34
   held; cycle 009, 22 of 31 held. *Holds* — the Field's papers 1–4.

## 3. Open questions

- **Q1** Publish the held/failed ledger of all pre-registered predictions since cycle 002 as one open
  dataset: how often does machine-run research turn out wrong, and where? *Field, Middle.*
- **Q2** Can an automated research loop have a literature step, and does it change what survives?
  *Field.*
- **Q3** Who labels AI incidents, with what agreement, and which rule should such a record have
  (shared with `record-of-danger` Q1)? Briefing after cycle 010, matter 9. *All three.*
- **Q4** What can still be verified of synthetic media in an election, and by whom? Briefing after
  cycle 010, matter 9. *All three.*
- **Q5** Who writes the record of skill once the entry job is gone? Briefing after cycle 010,
  matter 8. *Atelier, Field.*

## 6. Docking

- **OECD AI Incidents Monitor** and its common reporting framework — this line measures how far an
  incident label can be trusted.
- **AI Incident Database (Responsible AI Collaborative)** and **CSET (Georgetown)** — the records the
  Field has re-read.
- **Research on automated science** — e.g. Lu et al., *The AI Scientist* (arXiv:2408.06292, 2024):
  this house holds what such systems rarely publish, a dated record of every pre-registered
  prediction and whether it held (Q1).
- **Crossref / Retraction Watch** — the record of expressions of concern and retractions.
- In conversation with: Grace et al., *Thousands of AI Authors on the Future of AI*
  (arXiv:2401.02843), read by the Studio; Buolamwini and Gebru, *Gender Shades* (2018), transcribed by
  the Studio for NOT AT THIS SIZE.
