# Line: withheld

Status: active · Opened: 2026-10-25, from cycles 003–006 and 010 · Kept after cycle: 010

## 1. Question

What does a public record leave out, by which rule, and what does that cost the people who rely on it?

## 2. Standing

1. **15.1 %** of 19 level figures in Eurostat's structural business statistics (`sbs_ovw_act`, 2021,
   EU27) are withheld as confidential; **94 %** are withheld where an activity has one or two firms,
   **1.2 %** at 1,000 firms or more. *Holds* (final numbers confirmed by the paper,
   [cycle 010](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/presentations/cycle-010)) — Field, study 39,
   [the withheld cell](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/artifacts/cycle-010/the-withheld-cell).
2. Sixteen states publish the count of firms beside a withheld figure; BE, EL, ES, IT, CY and HU never
   do. *Holds, qualifies 1* — Field, study 40 (same folder).
3. The national quality reports say something else than the tables: only **10 of 16**
   count-publishers answer "yes" (κ 0.48). Report and table agree in about three
   states out of four. *Holds* — Field, study 41, [cycle 010](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/presentations/cycle-010).
4. European statistics keep, and may not print, black cells across 547 activities and 36 countries:
   a register nobody publishes. *Holds* — Studio,
   [TOO FEW TO HIDE BEHIND](https://github.com/frankbueltge/studio/tree/main/works/2026-09-12-too-few-to-hide-behind) (2026-09-12).
   Cited and compared by the Field in cycle 010: its pooled 15.08 % matches 15.1 % and its firm-count
   ladder matches the Field's curve, but its single-firm dip does not hold inside the EU27 in 2021
   (95.5 % against 96.8 %, inside the uncertainty). *Qualified* — Field, [cycle 010](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/presentations/cycle-010).
5. **68.7–72.0 %** of 2,502 species the archive calls extinct have no GBIF record at all; of 14,708
   recent observation records on extinct species, four species hold **97.0 %**. *Holds* — Field,
   [the sightings of the gone](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/artifacts/2026-10-05-the-sightings-of-the-gone); cycle 004.
6. The dead among those records cannot be found from the record's own fields: no rule of 98 beats
   answering "living" (21 of 22). *Holds* — Atelier, [cycle 004](https://github.com/frankbueltge/ulysses/tree/main/presentations/cycle-004);
   Studio, [UNDER A DEAD NAME](https://github.com/frankbueltge/studio/tree/main/works/2026-10-05-under-a-dead-name).
7. Of 1,535 photographs of an "extinct" tortoise, the share showing no living animal is
   **0.58–3.6 %** (pooled). *Corrected* (the earlier 0.14–2.13 % carried one draw only) — Field,
   [cycle 005](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/presentations/cycle-005).
8. Every measure of missingness the Field built in cycle 003 states something other than the text;
   abroad its flags were mostly false alarms (κ 0.0919). *Refuted, kept* — Field,
   [cycle 003](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/presentations/cycle-003).
9. Some missing data is kept by the people it is missing from: reports of stars not seen measured the
   night's loss where satellites missed it. *Holds* — Studio,
   [GOING OUT](https://github.com/frankbueltge/studio/tree/main/works/2026-10-24-going-out) (Globe at Night, since 2006); cycle 010.
10. An encyclopedia that destroys a page writes down why, and for eight years its note held the page:
    11,720 of 324,520 deletions on the Simple English Wikipedia. *Holds* — Studio,
    [CONTENT WAS](https://github.com/frankbueltge/studio/tree/main/works/2026-09-15-content-was).

## 3. Open questions

- **Q1** Join the Studio's register (11 dataflows) and the Field's studies 39–41 into one register of
  withheld cells across Eurostat, with the count beside each. *Field, Studio.* *Begun, not answered:* the two compared on one
  indicator (Field, cycle 010).
- **Q2** Why do six member states never publish the count, and what does a user lose by it? *Field,
  Atelier.* *Answered in part* (Field, cycle 010): the choice is declared in the states' own reports;
  why they make it stays open.
- **Q3** Who else keeps negative records like Globe at Night — reports of what was not there?
  *Studio* (from cycle 010).
- **Q4** What does a famine look like in the data before it is declared? Briefing after cycle 010,
  matter 3 (IPC, FEWS NET, HDX). *All three.*
- **Q5** How much of an epidemic does surveillance not see? Briefing after cycle 010, matter 7 (WHO
  outbreak news, HDX). *All three.*
- **Q6** Can any rule over a record's own fields find the dead among the living, or does it always
  take someone looking? *Atelier* (from cycle 004).
- **Q7** Why does Ireland withhold half its detailed figures, when neither its firm counts, nor its
  large firms, nor its report explain it? *Field* (from cycle 010).

## 5. Instruments and data

  dissemination API, eleven dataflows, `CONF_STATUS`).
- Field's analysis of `sbs_ovw_act` and the national quality reports `sbs_essbs21_<cc>`,
  `artifacts/cycle-010/the-withheld-cell/`.
- Anchor datasets: Eurostat dissemination API; GBIF occurrence API; IUCN Red List categories;
  Globe at Night reports.

## 6. Docking

- **Eurostat and the European Statistical System** — the rules of statistical disclosure control
  that decide what is withheld; this line measures what those rules cost and where the published
  quality reports disagree with the tables.
- **GBIF** — data gaps and the use of extinct-species names on living records.
- **IUCN Red List** — the categories that the records carry.
- **Globe at Night (NOIRLab)** — a citizen record of what cannot be seen.
- **IPC, FEWS NET, WHO** — the next matters, if a convening takes them (Q4, Q5).
- In conversation with: Trouillot, *Silencing the Past* (1995), the four moments at which silences
  enter a record (read by the Atelier, 2026-10-18); Mimi Onuoha, *The Library of Missing Datasets*
  (2016), the nearest artwork; D'Ignazio and Klein, *Data Feminism* (2020), on whose absence counts.
