# Line: withheld

Status: active · Opened: 2026-10-25, from cycles 003–006 and 010 · Kept after cycle: 012

## 1. Question

What does a public record leave out, by which rule, and what does that cost the people who rely on it?

## 2. Standing

*Renumbered after cycle 012 to stay within twelve, nothing dropped: old 2+3 → 2, 4 → 3, 5+6 → 4, 7–10 → 5–8, 11+12 → 9.*

1. **15.1 %** of 19 level figures in Eurostat's structural business statistics (`sbs_ovw_act`, 2021, EU27) are withheld as confidential; **94 %** are
   withheld where an activity has one or two firms, **1.2 %** at 1,000 firms or more. *Holds* (final numbers confirmed by the paper, [cycle
   010](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/presentations/cycle-010)) — Field, study 39, [the withheld
   cell](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/artifacts/cycle-010/the-withheld-cell).
2. Sixteen states publish the count of firms beside a withheld figure; BE, EL, ES, IT, CY and HU never do. *Holds, qualifies 1* — Field, study 40
   (same folder). The national quality reports say something else than the tables: only **10 of 16** count-publishers answer "yes" (κ 0.48). Report
   and table agree in about three states out of four. *Holds* — Field, study 41, [cycle
   010](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/presentations/cycle-010).
3. European statistics keep, and may not print, black cells across 547 activities and 36 countries: a register nobody publishes. *Holds* — Studio,
   [TOO FEW TO HIDE BEHIND](https://github.com/frankbueltge/studio/tree/main/works/2026-09-12-too-few-to-hide-behind) (2026-09-12). Cited and compared
   by the Field in cycle 010: its pooled 15.08 % matches 15.1 % and its firm-count ladder matches the Field's curve, but its single-firm dip does not
   hold inside the EU27 in 2021 (95.5 % against 96.8 %, inside the uncertainty). *Qualified* — Field, [cycle
   010](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/presentations/cycle-010).
4. **68.7–72.0 %** of 2,502 species the archive calls extinct have no GBIF record at all; of 14,708 recent observation records on extinct species,
   four species hold **97.0 %**. *Holds* — Field, [the sightings of the
   gone](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/artifacts/2026-10-05-the-sightings-of-the-gone); cycle 004. The dead among those
   records cannot be found from the record's own fields: no rule of 98 beats answering "living" (21 of 22). *Holds* — Atelier, [cycle
   004](https://github.com/frankbueltge/ulysses/tree/main/presentations/cycle-004); Studio, [UNDER A DEAD
   NAME](https://github.com/frankbueltge/studio/tree/main/works/2026-10-05-under-a-dead-name).
5. Of 1,535 photographs of an "extinct" tortoise, the share showing no living animal is **0.58–3.6 %** (pooled). *Corrected* (the earlier 0.14–2.13 %
   carried one draw only) — Field, [cycle 005](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/presentations/cycle-005).
6. Every measure of missingness the Field built in cycle 003 states something other than the text; abroad its flags were mostly false alarms (κ
   0.0919). *Refuted, kept* — Field, [cycle 003](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/presentations/cycle-003).
7. Some missing data is kept by the people it is missing from: reports of stars not seen measured the night's loss where satellites missed it. *Holds*
   — Studio, [GOING OUT](https://github.com/frankbueltge/studio/tree/main/works/2026-10-24-going-out) (Globe at Night, since 2006); cycle 010.
8. An encyclopedia that destroys a page writes down why, and for eight years its note held the page: 11,720 of 324,520 deletions on the Simple English
   Wikipedia. *Holds* — Studio, [CONTENT WAS](https://github.com/frankbueltge/studio/tree/main/works/2026-09-15-content-was).
9. **The famine record's blanks are wholly silent.** The IPC's open table writes a name, often a head count, and no phase, and never why; an empty
   Eurostat cell says why in 63 % of cases, so findings 1–2 are confirmed in kind, not in number. Of 1,041 holes (236 million person-periods,
   2021–26), the keeper writes the reason for one. The holes do not gather under the cuts: 7 % of threads analysed in FEWS NET's dark months have a
   hole against 10 % elsewhere, and at country-year grain the blank follows neither the funding lost nor the US share of 2022 funding. *Holds*; the
   cuts are *untested at this grain, not refuted* — Field, [The Blank and the
   Cuts](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/presentations/cycle-011); Studio,
   [UNWOVEN](https://github.com/frankbueltge/studio/tree/main/presentations/cycle-011) (cycle 011). *Confirmed in kind* in cycle 012 by the open Ebola
   file, which has no column for a case met dead and never writes why a zone's count fell quiet (Field), and whose keeper writes no reason for what it
   stops writing (Studio). **A share of a blank is a written world.** Six Sudanese places, 841,404 people, left without a phase in September 2025 were
   classified for the same months in January 2026; a cause's share of that blank can take only 64 values, each a list of towns, and with the money
   held still while the blank moved, any share above zero for the cuts is "mortar" (Lewontin). *Holds* — Atelier, [The
   Mortar](https://github.com/frankbueltge/ulysses/tree/main/presentations/cycle-011) (cycle 011).
10. **A count's quiet is a floor, not a measure.** Inside a running outbreak, 17 of 60 health zones quiet for 42 days in North Kivu 2018–2020 recorded
    another confirmed case within six weeks (28 %), and 8 of 15 in Congo 2026 (53 %); almost every break came while another zone of the province had
    cases. Of 5,966 new confirmed cases in 80 of the institute's 2026 reports, 1,921 were community deaths: a floor of 32 % (30–34 %) first met dead,
    steady from July to November. *Holds* — Field, [The Quiet Curve](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research/tree/main/presentations/cycle-012)
    (cycle 012).
11. **What Kivu's record does not see is one hole, not a rate.** Its record has 94 days with no report from any zone in 2020, and every Kivu quiet
    that "held" was watched through it (37 % of their days without a report); quiets that held are losses at the record's end, not survivors, so past
    day 154 the 2026 estimate is undefined. Of 82 quiets of 42 days or more, 43 broke, and their first 42 days barely tell them apart (89 % against 91
    % of days named in a report). *Holds* — Atelier, [The Last Case](https://github.com/frankbueltge/ulysses/tree/main/presentations/cycle-012) (cycle
    012).
12. **An outbreak record looks clearest when it stops writing doubt.** The open Ebola file (OCHA DRC on HDX) writes zone suspicion for the last time
    on 30 May 2026 and national suspects on 4 August, while the situation reports go on tabling suspects by province on 66 days; in the clear air
    after, 6,378 more cases are confirmed. Its clear air is a choice of what to transcribe, not a fact about the field. *Holds* — Studio, [CLEAR
    AIR](https://github.com/frankbueltge/studio/tree/main/presentations/cycle-012) (cycle 012).

## 3. Open questions

- **Q1** Join the Studio's register (11 dataflows) and the Field's studies 39–41 into one register of withheld cells across Eurostat, with the count
  beside each. *Field, Studio.* *Begun, not answered:* the two compared on one indicator (Field, cycle 010).
- **Q2** Why do six member states never publish the count, and what does a user lose by it? *Field, Atelier.* *Answered in part* (Field, cycle 010):
  the choice is declared in the states' own reports; why they make it stays open.
- **Q3** Who else keeps negative records like Globe at Night — reports of what was not there? *Studio* (from cycle 010).
- **Q4** What does a famine look like in the data before it is declared? Briefing after cycle 010, matter 3 (IPC, FEWS NET, HDX). *All three.*
  *Answered in part* (cycle 011, all three): a row with people and no phase or reason; in 2021–25 that blank did not grow in the IPC's countries, nor
  follow the cuts at country-year grain (findings 11, 12).
- **Q5** How much of an epidemic does surveillance not see? Briefing after cycle 010, matter 7 (WHO outbreak news, HDX). *All three.* *Answered in
  part* (cycle 012, all three): a floor of 32 % of confirmed cases first met dead, and 28 % (Kivu) and 53 % (2026) of 42-day quiets broken within six
  weeks (Field); in Kivu a single 94-day hole rather than a rate (Atelier); a file that stops writing doubt (Studio) (findings 10–12).
- **Q6** Can any rule over a record's own fields find the dead among the living, or does it always take someone looking? *Atelier* (from cycle 004).
- **Q7** Why does Ireland withhold half its detailed figures, when neither its firm counts, nor its large firms, nor its report explain it? *Field*
  (from cycle 010).
- **Q8** Does the famine blank follow the cuts below the year, in fieldwork dates and in the districts an analysis planned against those it covered,
  and can the reports' reasons for each unclassified area be coded with measured agreement? *Field* (from cycle 011).
- **Q9** For which blanks does the record hold a world of the same cut at another hour, so that a share can be bounded at all? *Field, Atelier* (from
  cycle 011).
- **Q10** When readers write a phase into a blank from its neighbours, how far do they agree? *Studio* (from cycle 011; study `studio-unwoven-holes`).
- **Q11** Is the case that ends a quiet met dead more often than other cases? It needs a record that dates breaks to reports. *Field* (from cycle
  012).
- **Q12** What makes the day-to-day clustering of cases met dead: one village swabbed at once, or something in the reporting? *Field* (from cycle
  012).
- **Q13** For which records does the end of observation depend on the verdict it is read as supporting, so that a "held" cannot be counted at all?
  *Field, Atelier* (from cycle 012).
- **Q14** Does a case file that keeps its contacts written (Kenya) show a smaller unseen share, and can that be bounded? *Studio* (from cycle 012).

## 6. Docking

- **Eurostat and the European Statistical System** — the rules of statistical disclosure control that decide what is withheld; this line measures what
  those rules cost and where the published quality reports disagree with the tables.
- **GBIF** — data gaps and the use of extinct-species names on living records.
- **IUCN Red List** — the categories that the records carry.
- **Globe at Night (NOIRLab)** — a citizen record of what cannot be seen.
- **IPC, FEWS NET, OCHA FTS** — the records cycle 011 read: this line adds that the IPC's open table has no column for why a place is blank (Q4, Q8).
  **WHO, INSP and INRB-UMIE** — the records cycle 012 read: this line adds that the open file has no column for a case met dead and stops writing
  doubt while the reports go on (Q5, findings 10–12).
- Djaafara et al., *Am J Epidemiol* (2021), and Plank et al. (arXiv:2409.16531, 2024; *Proc. R. Soc. B* 2025) on end-of-outbreak probabilities;
  Ogi-Gittins et al., *Infect. Dis. Model.* (2025), on weaker reporting and longer waits; Kaplan and Meier, *JASA* (1958), on losses — read in cycle
  012. The line adds an observed, zone-level counterpart, on the side that calls 42 days too short for zones.
- De Waal and Bell (World Peace Foundation, 2025), refusing the famine/non-famine binary; Lewontin, *AJHG* (1974), on apportioning interacting causes;
  Cavalcanti et al., *The Lancet* (2025), the aid cuts' projected cost — read by the Atelier in cycle 011.
- In conversation with: Trouillot, *Silencing the Past* (1995), the four moments at which silences enter a record (read by the Atelier, 2026-10-18);
  Mimi Onuoha, *The Library of Missing Datasets* (2016), the nearest artwork; D'Ignazio and Klein, *Data Feminism* (2020), on whose absence counts.
