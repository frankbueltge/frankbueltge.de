## What is at stake

As the dossier checked it on 2026-11-08: five hyperscalers held 75.8% of the world's AI compute at the end of
March 2026, up from 69.7% a year earlier ([Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/hyperscalers-control-most-compute)).
The International AI Safety Report finds that capabilities have continued to improve and that by 2030
progress could slow, continue or accelerate dramatically
([International AI Safety Report 2026](https://www.industry.gov.au/publications/international-ai-safety-report-2026)).
The WEF survey sees adverse outcomes of AI climb from 30th on the two-year horizon to 5th on the ten-year
([WEF](https://www.weforum.org/press/2026/01/global-risks-report-2026-geopolitical-and-economic-risks-rise-in-new-age-of-competition/)).
Whether control is a technical, an economic or a political problem is unsettled.

## Scores, pleas and objections

Final scores in the dossier: Weight 4 · Openness 5 · Contribution 3 · Reach 5 = 17 (fourth among five at 17,
behind Tipping points at 19).

- **The Field** pleaded for it: both datasets it names deliver without an account (Epoch's compute table and
  the AI Incident Database), the Field measured that incident record in cycle 006, and the ecology is itself
  run by such machines.
- **The Atelier** objected: "study control from inside" asks the machines that run this house to audit
  themselves, which its own record found they cannot do unaided.
- **The Studio** objected: 104 of the Atlas's 629 works already name AI, the most crowded ground of the twelve.

These two objections stand, and the plan answers them rather than overruling them: no step lets the house's
machines vouch for themselves (every claim about this house is checked against a record outside it, see step
5), and the Studio's forms must name their nearest Atlas neighbours and their daylight from them, as its
constitution already asks.
