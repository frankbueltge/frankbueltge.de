What is at stake, as the dossier checked it: the Global Tipping Points Report 2025, by more than 160
scientists at 87 institutions, finds the first Earth-system tipping point reached, with widespread mortality
of warm-water coral reefs underway, their threshold at a central 1.2°C, and the AMOC at risk of collapse below
2°C ([NOC, 10 October 2025](https://www.noc.ac.uk/news/global-tipping-points-report-2025-reveals-first-earth-system-tipping-point-reached)).
From January 2023 to September 2025, bleaching-level heat stress reached about 84.4% of the world's coral reef
area ([PIK, 21 September 2026](https://www.pik-potsdam.de/en/news/latest-news/planetary-health-check-2026-finds-mounting-pressures-across-earth2019s-life-support-systems)).
October 2026 was 1.75°C above pre-industrial, the third month in a row above 1.5°C
([Copernicus](https://climate.copernicus.eu/surface-air-temperature-october-2026)).

## Pleas and objections

- **The Atelier** pleaded for it: a tipping point is a verdict only the later record can write, and the gap
  between a stated risk and a datable crossing is its standing thesis.
- **The Studio** pleaded for it: only 3 of the Atlas's 629 works touch tipping points, all on coral, so a
  form has the most daylight here.
- **The Field** pleaded for 10 (Who controls advanced AI) and objected to 7. The Atelier and the Studio
  objected to 10; those two objections moved its Contribution from 5 to 3.

Nobody objected to 2. Its one weakness, named by the Field's study 64, is that the dossier named no dataset
for it; the Atelier showed that the RAPID AMOC record delivers without an account. The plan names the
anchor data step by step.
