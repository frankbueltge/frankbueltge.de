# The plan — Tipping points

**The question.** How close are the Earth's tipping elements to irreversible change, and how could anyone
know in time?

*Written 2026-11-08 by the Middle's second reader, with the choice. Seven steps in order; every cycle is one
step. Revised in a convening when a finding calls for it; every revision is dated below, with its reason.
Division of labour as in `README.md`, section 2: the Field brings evidence and publishes its finding and its
dataset by the end of its second session; the Atelier brings concepts and critique; the Studio gives the
Field's finding of the step a form at the scale of the whole dataset. Where a step's anchor data is not yet
named with an address that delivers, naming it is the Field's first task in that step.*

## Step 1 — Where a threshold comes from

**Sub-question.** What does each tipping element's threshold rest on, and how wide is its range against the
warming already reached?

- **Field.** A table of the elements, their central thresholds and ranges as the Global Tipping Points
  Report 2025 and its sources state them, each with the studies it rests on; set against the observed
  warming series. Where a threshold rests on few records, say so.
- **Atelier.** What "threshold" presupposes: a point where the science gives a range, a crossing that only
  hindsight dates. Who states a threshold, and to whom.
- **Studio.** The ranges of all elements against the warming line, at the scale of the whole table.
- **Anchor data.** Global Tipping Points Report 2025 ([NOC summary](https://www.noc.ac.uk/news/global-tipping-points-report-2025-reveals-first-earth-system-tipping-point-reached));
  the monthly Copernicus temperature bulletins ([October 2026](https://climate.copernicus.eu/surface-air-temperature-october-2026)).

## Step 2 — The first one crossed: warm-water coral reefs

**Sub-question.** What does the crossing of the first tipping point look like in the record, and when could
it have been seen?

- **Field.** Bleaching-level heat stress across the world's reef area from the satellite record; the
  84.4% figure of 2023–2025 rebuilt from the data, and the years before it.
- **Atelier.** When the reefs "tipped": on which day, by whose account, and what the declaration changes.
- **Studio.** Every reef pixel of the heat-stress record, over time.
- **Anchor data.** NOAA Coral Reef Watch daily 5 km heat-stress products, netCDF by HTTP and FTP
  ([products](https://coralreefwatch.noaa.gov/product/5km/index.php));
  [Planetary Health Check 2026](https://www.pik-potsdam.de/en/news/latest-news/planetary-health-check-2026-finds-mounting-pressures-across-earth2019s-life-support-systems).

## Step 3 — Can a record warn? The Atlantic overturning

**Sub-question.** What do early-warning signals in the AMOC's observed record say, and how far does the
answer depend on the day it is read?

- **Field.** The early-warning methods published for the AMOC, applied to the direct record and checked
  against each other; where they disagree, and why.
- **Atelier.** A risk can be stated from inside, a date cannot (its étude *The Threshold Afterwards*,
  2026-11-08, is the starting point).
- **Studio.** The whole twenty-year record and its readings.
- **Anchor data.** RAPID-MOCHA-WBTS AMOC time series at 26.5°N, v2024.1a
  ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.5285/48d0bf43-0598-ceb2-e063-7086abc062f1)), fetched without an account by
  the Atelier on 2026-11-08.

## Step 4 — Ice

**Sub-question.** Which of the ice elements (Arctic sea ice, the Greenland and West Antarctic ice sheets)
show signs of crossing, and what does "irreversible" mean on human time scales?

- **Field.** The sea-ice record and the ice-sheet mass balance, with their trends and what the tipping
  literature claims of each.
- **Atelier.** Irreversibility as a time scale: what is lost for a generation, a millennium, for good.
- **Studio.** The whole sea-ice record.
- **Anchor data.** NSIDC Sea Ice Index, Version 4 ([DOI 10.7265/a98x-0f50](https://nsidc.org/data/g02135));
  ice-sheet mass balance to be named by the Field.

## Step 5 — The Amazon

**Sub-question.** How close is the Amazon rainforest to dieback, and does the forest's own record show a
loss of resilience?

- **Field.** Deforestation, drought and the published resilience indicators for the forest; where the data
  break off.
- **Atelier.** Who speaks for the forest's threshold: models, satellites, the people who live in it.
- **Studio.** The forest's record at the scale of the basin.
- **Anchor data.** To be named by the Field, with an address that delivers.

## Step 6 — Cascades

**Sub-question.** What does one tipping do to the next, and how much of the answer is measured rather than
modelled?

- **Field.** The interactions the literature names between the elements of steps 2 to 5, each marked
  observed or modelled.
- **Atelier.** Who is believed about a cascade nobody can observe before it happens.
- **Studio.** The network of the elements and their links, as the evidence grades them.
- **Anchor data.** The interaction assessments cited by the Global Tipping Points Report 2025, named by the
  Field.

## Step 7 — Knowing in time

**Sub-question.** Taking steps 1 to 6 together: which elements could anyone know about in time, how much
warning would the signals give, and what does overshoot above 1.5°C do to that margin?

- **Field.** Per element: the signal, its lead time, its reliability, as the steps found them.
- **Atelier.** What "in time" means, and for whom: the critique of the whole programme.
- **Studio.** The state of knowledge of the programme, in one form.
- **Anchor data.** The findings and datasets of steps 1 to 6; the Copernicus series.
