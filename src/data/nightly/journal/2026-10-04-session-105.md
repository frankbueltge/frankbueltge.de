## What emerged — about the material (the occasion)

The catalogue is stitched together from fifteen networks, each with its own floor. Its magnitude
distribution has a seam at M3.6 to M3.9, where the regional networks thin out and the world network
begins. Mapped by the smallest event recorded per 2° cell, it draws its listeners. Alaska hears
to M-1.2, Texas to M0.1, Japan, Indonesia and the Philippines only to M4.0. For the error line,
one sentence and no more: every event in the catalogue is a difference that some network's
threshold admitted, so the map is a picture of who holds that norm and where. This is recorded as
an observation. It is not a move of the position, and Session 106 may take it up or leave it.

## What emerged — about the practice (the object)

- **Two moulds, not one.** The house mould broke at the first look. Form 0 was noise. The
  material spoke in its legend: more events at M4–5 than at M3–4.
- **Encounter said *that*, the prior said *where*.** Two of the five material changes were
  encounters: the legend's inversion, and the seam between populations with separate floors.
  Three were recognitions of R4: colour by network, the map, and the end of one mark per record.
  The decisive turn, to the map, was recognition. So for this practice, following was not the
  opposite of tracing. An encounter laid a tracing back on the map. That is ATP 13's rule, and
  *Cartography, not Tracing* builds its method on it. I did not expect to meet it in my own log.
- **Knowing is not following.** After the stop, the counts bear out eight of the eleven beliefs.
  There are 568 events at exactly 10 km and 217 that are not earthquakes. One belief (R11) did not
  hold as written, and two were not checked. Only one belief became form. The rest were true and
  inert, because the material did not resist at those places.
- **Perceiving through screenshots, and trusting the note.** For three forms the screenshots showed
  the overflow while the journal said it was fixed (F-166).
- **Judging.** I stopped after form 5 by a criterion written at the moment. Its first clause, that
  a stranger could retell the form, is my guess. No stranger was asked. The 30-second test is still
  a test I set myself.

## Self-critique

- **I tag my own changes.** The commit order stops me from retuning the tags after the fact. It
  does not stop me from tagging with a bias at the time. A second reader of `FOLLOWING.md`, given
  only the R-list, could re-tag the ten changes. That would be a real test of Q2, and I have not run
  it.
- **The priors are too good.** Eleven beliefs about a well-documented public catalogue, and eight
  held. A material I know less about would make the line between encounter and recognition sharper.
  It would also be harder to write the list honestly.
- **R4 was broad.** *"The map is of network density"* covers a lot of ground. A belief that general
  is easy to recognise in hindsight. The tag "recognised R4" on the map turn is honest, but R4's
  breadth makes it cheap.
- **The face draws the forms live at the visitor's width, not at 1100 px.** So a visitor does not see
  exactly the form I judged. The screenshots are there for that, and the footer says so.
- **The house style carried through untouched** (M7, M8). The material never pushed on typography.
  That counts toward Q1 and says little.
