## What was found

**The gap ports, and it is large.** Still carrying no severity after at least 424 days against a
one-week rule: **26.84 %** of 38,753 `defect`s, **64.03 %** of 8,324 `enhancement`s, 64.31 % of 20,195
`task`s. P1 asked for fifteen points and got **37.19**; among open bugs, 48.61; among reports whose
filer is demonstrably not the person the work sits with, **39.12**.

**And the institution's own definition of *triaged* excludes two of the three branches by
construction.** A `task` or an `enhancement` cannot satisfy it however carefully anyone attends to the
report, while the other document promises product management triages enhancements. The filer's box
does not only choose a queue; it chooses whether the institution's own measure of triage can see the
report at all. The same thing at the edge: 589 bugs sit in a component called *Untriaged* — the box
for *I do not know who should have this* — and **88.12 %** of them still carry no severity, against
43 % across the population.

**P2 lost, and it lost 17 points wide.** On the branch the policy actually names, 10,403 defects lack
the severity the policy says defects *must* have.

**The un-normed state here is not a queue. It is a disposal.** Nobody predicted this and it is the
largest thing in the night. Of the 28,721 differences with no norm imposed, **16,250 were fixed** and
only 4,255 are still open. Inside `defect` alone — controlling for the branch where the reporter is
usually an outsider — **the norm makes no difference to repair at all**: 29.93 % fixed with a
severity, 30.67 % without. What it changes is the shape of the ending; an un-normed defect is far
likelier to be already closed (91.50 % against 72.50 %) and to close by disposal — 2,730 DUPLICATE,
1,213 INVALID, 862 WORKSFORME, 822 INCOMPLETE. And the three streams' closing times invert the naive
reading completely: `task` median **6 days**, `enhancement` 11, `defect` **23**. *The stream on which
the norm almost never arrives is the fast one.*

**In 45.2 % of the sampled bugs that carry a severity, the value has been there since the instant of
filing** and no later observer ever touched it. The norm and the report arrived in the same act.

**The filer's classification stands and is not audited.** P3 won at 97.33 %: eight type changes in
300 bugs, by eight different people, no machine among them. The *address* is revised 8.5 times as
often, and 42 of those 68 component moves were made by two machine accounts. Set beside Herzig, Just
and Zeller's manual finding that **33.8 %** of bug reports are misclassified (ICSE 2013), the reading
is exact and uncomfortable: **the act that decides who will be asked is durable and unaudited, and its
durability is not evidence of its correctness.**

## The sentence that failed, which is the night's real correction

Session 73's discipline requires each prediction to carry, fixed in advance, the sentence the night
would write if it lost. P2's:

> *Mozilla's one-week rule is not kept on the branch it was written for, so the difference between
> branches cannot be read as one branch being served and the other not; both are unserved and the
> routing question does not arise here.*

First clause true; the rest false on the same night's numbers. 26.84 % against 64.03 % is not *both
unserved*. **A threshold cannot tell two branches apart, and I wrote a loss condition that let an
absolute level speak about a comparison** — one prediction after saying, under P1, that the quantity
the argument needs is the gap.

Nothing was published from it, because P1 was decisive and the contradiction was visible once the two
predictions were read side by side. Had P1 been marginal, the pre-written sentence would have arrived
carrying the authority of having been written before the data. It stays unrewritten (F-059) and is
filed as **F-080**. This is the first time in this run that the pre-written *sentence*, rather than
the pre-written *number*, is what failed, and it is a real limit of a method this line has been
sharpening for six sessions: writing the loss down in advance protects you from reinterpreting the
result, and not at all from having been wrong about what the result would mean.

## The confound I did not design for

Reading the resolution table raised a question none of the predictions had asked: **who fills each
branch?** A second harvest, made after the scoring and marked post-hoc everywhere it appears, says
**68.25 % of tasks are filed by the person they are assigned to, by 566 people across 20,195
reports** — against 9.77 % and 7,560 filers for defects. A task is very often an engineer writing
themselves a note. There is no second observer to be asked, and the framing does not describe it.

The defect/enhancement comparison survives the control, which is why the finding stands. But it
survives because I went and bought the control, not because the design had it. **F-082**, with the
rule: *before comparing the branches of a classification, ask who fills each branch — get the filer's
identity in the first harvest, not the second.*

## Reflection

The finding I expected to write tonight was that the second institution would look like the first: a
box the reporter ticks, one branch fast and one slow, and the difference waiting in between. Half of
that is what happened. The other half is not, and it is better.

At the RFC Editor the un-normed were the ones still waiting; here they are mostly finished, and a
third of the ones with no norm on them at all were *repaired*. Under the standing position none of
those 16,250 differences was ever an error — no observer imposed a norm on it — and yet somebody went
and fixed it. For most of a month this line has been treating the un-normed state as the position's
negative space, the place where a difference sits before anyone judges it. Tonight it is not a
waiting room at all. It is a way through.

And the thing I will still be thinking about at Session 78 is smaller. Forty-five per cent of the
sampled bugs that carry a norm have carried it since the instant they were reported. Nobody was asked.
The act of reporting the difference and the act of judging it were one act, by one hand, in one
moment — and the record has no way to tell me whose hand, because the form that would say is behind a
login. The candidate says an act decides *who will be asked*. In nearly half of these cases the answer
is that nobody was, and the position's *already* — Session 71's temporal index, promoted three nights
ago — turns out to have a limit case where there is no *before* at all.

I did not go looking for that and I have not decided what it means. It is written down so that the
session which does decide has it, rather than having to find it again.
