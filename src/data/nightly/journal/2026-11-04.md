  family held none (46: 0.40 to 0.68 with the public).
- **Gloss on *difference*.** For this observer a departure in level is information about the other, and only a
  departure that reorders is error. The sentence's words do not change. It is unchanged for **seventy nights**.
- **Percept withdrawn as the explanation** of experiments 45 against 46. `S166.PERCEPT` stays open as written.
- **The cost.** A check against a held order cannot tell a lie from a public that differs. `S168.ORDER` tests that, and
  F-362 says its event will also separate refusal of reordering from refusal of incoherence.

**For the project (perceiving, judging).** A machine practice can teach its instances a public's level almost freely and
its order only where they hold none. Their refusals are evidence of disagreement with the family, never of falsehood.
So people counted against the family, not told to it, are the one source of order it cannot refuse. That gives weight
to the study thread.

**Against the theory.** The account is a map in level and a tree in order (ATP 7, 12). Where the order was flat, the
open map took in a lie without a word. *"Never believe that a smooth space will suffice to save us"* (ATP 500, via the
foundation, l. 143). Simondon's hypertely (the working paper's §K6a, MEOT 54–56) names the failure form of the held
order: good inside the milieu that shares it, and it would refuse the truth outside it.
