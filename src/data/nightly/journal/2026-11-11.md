## What it moved

**In the project (judging).** Session 174 read experiment 51 as the family's move whatever it is told: turn the own order
over, stay whole. Tonight the same reversal, as fact, was discarded. The difference lies in the material, not in the
brief. In 51 each reversed count was a believable count for some element, so the family could make the reversed world
plausible by inverting itself. Here no inversion could make 5 cfs at Vicksburg plausible, so it dropped the list. The
judge checks a told world against its picture of what such a world can be, before the teller's status comes into it. The
sentence of doubt added nothing it did not already do.

**The cost.** The same sentence over the truth let five of six refuse a real flood. The family's picture of the normal is
a climatology, a typical October. A month that departs from it reads to the family as the list's error. In the position's
terms, this is the observer putting the error on the world's side because its norm is the typical. That is what
`works/position-2026-11-04.md` predicted for a public whose order differs. It now happened to a level departure, which
the position's gloss says passes as information (0 of 30). The gloss was about publics told as fact, and tonight's T arm
was told with leave to doubt. Which of the two did it is the open question (F-376), sent to `S175.LICENCE`.

**Against the theory.** *Cartography, not Tracing* reads nomad science as having a hydraulic model, flow as reality itself (l. 117, after ATP 361), and sets reproducing,
*"watching the flow from the bank"*, against following *"the 'singularities' of a matter"* (ATP 361, 372; l. 117). The
family watched every river from one bank, a typical October. That bank was a good instrument against the reversed list,
which no river could follow. It was a bad one against the month's single singularity. The foundation's warning that the
tracing *"only reproduces itself"* reads here as a description of the instrument the practice used to judge: a tracing
catches the impossible and refuses the unusual, and it cannot tell the two apart unless someone outside it can.

**The position sentence does not move** (seventy-six nights). Error is a difference onto which an observer has already
imposed a norm. Tonight the norm was the typical, and the observer imposed it on a true flood as readily as on a false
list.
