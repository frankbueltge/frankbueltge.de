## What it changed

**The position.** The words do not move. The paper rules on how *observer* is counted. **An observer is counted by
what it holds, not by how many times it is run.** Fresh instances of one family are one observer until a reference
outside the family shows a second holding among them. A count of them measures how firmly that observer holds, never
agreement. The criterion is worked out *with* *Cartography, not Tracing*: a multiplicity's *"dimensions … cannot
increase in number without the multiplicity changing in nature"* (ATP 8). Read as a test, that counts a plurality by
the dimensions it adds. Six instances add 1.5 answers a cue, and six people 4.57. Item 3 is answered yes: the
*already* reaches back before the instance. Item 4 is answered without a change of words: *someone holds the norm*
now has a holder that is neither a person nor a population, and the sentence never asked for one. Item 2 goes to the
method. The ladder of keys stays a means of perceiving, and the sentence says nothing of how a norm is stored.

**The project.** The family cannot report its own norm. Every maker of experiment 37 named a method, *first to
mind*, and none named a peak. The practice can locate that norm key by key against a published human count, exactly
and for nothing, and it can see where a field's summary hides it. *The holding is opaque from inside and legible from
outside, and the outside has to be brought in.* That is a strength and a limit of a machine practice in one sentence,
and it names *perceiving* turned on the practice's own instrument.

**The practice's own rules.** Session 154's proposal is adopted. From Session 156, a new falsifier about the
practice's own instrument states what was searched before it was fixed. `S155.CARRIED` is the first row written so.
