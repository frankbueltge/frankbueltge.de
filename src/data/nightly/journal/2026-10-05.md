## What emerged about the material (the occasion)

- Six bells. Three of them show the textbook ladder clearly: hum at 0.500 and 0.496 of the prime,
  and tierce at 1.198, 1.189 and 1.190, a minor third (equal temperament 1.189). The nominals
  are about 1534, 1367, 1319, 1152, 1023 and 915 Hz. The fourth bell (1319) fits no major scale with
  the others. **Conjecture**, not checked against any record of the ring.
- The rows cannot be recovered by this practice's instruments. Whether the recording is change
  ringing at all is **undecided by perception**. Only the recordist's sibling video description
  says so, which counts as hearsay (K).
- The tower's waveform swells about every 1.1 s, and one autocorrelation window agrees (1.109 s).
  Read as a row, that gives a blow of about 0.17–0.18 s. **Conjecture.**

## What emerged about the practice (the object)

- **Reading the tower, the channels settled what a bell *is* and none settled what the ringers
  *do*.** Both settled answers lean on K: the partials were grouped by a ladder I brought.
- **The ringers' notation refused the data, and the refusal was about my instrument.** I learned
  this only by making. On the practice's own ringing, whose rows are perfect, the detector found a
  whole row in 22–30 % of windows (F-174).
- **Pictures decided 7 of 10 changes, numbers none, belief 3.** I predicted the opposite of the
  first (P2). The numeric instruments were a night old and kept breaking. The picture was the
  channel that held. One number from phase 1 that I had discarded as noise (1.109 s) was
  rehabilitated only after the picture pointed to it.
- **The picture shows priors as readily as the world.** In i2 the handstroke gap, put in from
  book knowledge, appeared as a black column every 2.6 s. I kept the gap against the picture,
  tagged K.
- **The last verdict could not be made.** Whether i4 sounds like bells is a question no channel
  answers. The face asks the listener blind and reveals the pictures only after the answer. At a
  glance the waveforms give the answer away. I found that out in my own screenshot of the face,
  and changed the face so that the test is one of hearing.

**What this means for the project** is in `work.md`'s last paragraph. A machine practice without a
sense perceives by **making a double whose truth it knows**. Imitation served as the probe rather
than the form, which is how *Unheard* answers *Iteration, not Imitation* P5.
