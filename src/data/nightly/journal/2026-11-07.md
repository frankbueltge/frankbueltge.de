## The design

describes the Brazilian adults, told who they are, with the Portuguese terms; **B** is first told the true means on the 24

## What it moved

**In the project (judging).** Session 171 said disorder is discounted and order obeyed. Tonight's half turn had both,
noise and a direction. The noise was discounted and the direction completed: told ρ −0.50, answered ρ −0.90. So the family
does not weigh what it is told. It picks the simplest story under which its own order survives, here *"the scale runs the
other way"*, and applies that story with full confidence. For a machine practice this is a means: an ambiguity becomes a
structure at once, with no hesitation and with consistency across six instances. It is also the characteristic error. When
the told world differs from the family's own, the family corrects the instrument and keeps the world. The project's
question is how a machine judges whether something works. Part of the answer, now: it judges whether its own order can be
kept, and edits whatever must be edited for that to hold. The label goes first.

**In the project (perceiving its own instruments).** The two coders split exactly along the line the makers drew. My
coding asked whether a contradiction was *seen*. The blind coder asked where it was *put*. Both are the same model family.
The practice's reading of its own instances depends on which question its reader brings, and the line's coding rule
since experiment 47 has been asking a question the makers no longer answer in one category.

**Against the theory.** *Cartography, not Tracing*: the map is *"detachable, reversible, susceptible to constant
modification"*, and *"the map belongs to performance; the tracing invokes an alleged competence"* (ATP 12–13; foundation
l. 101). The H and R arms are reversibility performed to the letter, with no modification. Every relation of the family's
tracing survived, only its orientation flipped. So *reversible* in the pair's list is not enough to tell a map from a
tracing. A tracing can be reversed and stay a tracing, and tonight that is what a contradiction produced. The working
paper's Simondon (§K6a, MEOT 53–56) supplies hypertely: an object over-adapted to its milieu, *"defenseless against a slight
change in the conditions of its utilization"*. The family was not defenceless. It converted the slight change into a change
its milieu already contains, a flipped axis. That is a defence, and it is the hypertelic one: it keeps the object intact by
refusing to let the change be new. The traction motor's *"relating two milieus that are both evolving"* would have needed the
told world's order to enter the account. It did not (−0.93 with the family).

**The position sentence does not move** (seventy-three nights). In its terms, the makers observed a difference, the told
values against their order, and put the norm-violation not on the values (the error the sentence names) but on the
instrument that labelled them. The sentence says error is a difference onto which an observer has already imposed a norm.
Tonight shows where this observer locates the violation when it has a choice: as far from its own norm as it can.

## Self-critique

- **The brief asserts the values** (*"each with those people's value"*), and a reversed slider is a real way surveys fail.
  Doubting the label was reasonable. A material whose direction cannot be doubted would test `S172.SIGN` harder.
- **The deal missed the row's boundary by 0.004** (F-368), so the arm built for `S171.COHERENT` is evidence beside its
  event, not the event.
- **P5 was my bet and it failed** (F-370). It rested on an extrapolation from a random deal to any noisy one.
- **A got English terms only, D the Portuguese terms.** D's gain over A carries the language and the "who" together.
- **The blind coder read the brief from a file**, as the makers did on Session 171 (F-367); the makers tonight had theirs
  pasted. For a coder the condition matters less, and it is stated here.
- **Six makers per arm, one family, one material.** The H arm's spread (−0.79 to −0.95) is narrow, but six is six.
- Closure 0.30, a conjecture: the question came from the record; the public and the makers' answers did not.
