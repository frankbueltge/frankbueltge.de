## What emerged about the material (the occasion)

- GB frequency in August 2026 stays within ±0.2 Hz for 99.976 % of seconds and within ±0.125 Hz for
  94.1 %.
- **A half-hour comb.** The mean deviation by second within the half-hour has a range of 21.7 mHz,
  against 6.8 for a 1,700 s control. It climbs to about +10 mHz in the last five minutes and drops at the
  boundary. Rydin Gorjão et al. 2020 see dispatch actions in GB *"every 15 minutes"*
  (arXiv:2006.02481, Results, "Data overview"). The half-hour is not named there.
- **A daily cycle of level.** The hourly mean ranges over 56.8 mHz. The highest hour is 20:00 UTC
  (+33.6 mHz), and the 8-minute floor runs above 50 Hz around 20:40–21:20 UTC.
- **A once-a-minute line, dashed by night.** The 60 s peak stands 0.9–1.4× its neighbourhood at 00–04 UTC
  and up to 5.3× at 06 UTC. Hartmann et al. 2025 (arXiv:2510.09862, abstract) report *"a persistent
  one-minute oscillatory pattern"* traced to *"the energy management systems of battery electric storage
  systems"*, whose amplitude *"has increased substantially in the Nordic and British grids"*. **Why it
  fades at night is a conjecture I do not make.**
- **A nightly quiet.** The energy of the 11 s – 2.5 min wobble at 00–03 UTC is 0.37–0.47 of the median
  hour.
- Timestamps: NESO's notes say *"Time values are all given in Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) starting
  January 2020"*. I read them as UTC.

## What emerged about the practice (the object)

- **Strictly, 11 of 16 blind tags matched the checks.** P3 asked for 70 % and failed by one claim.
- **Every claim tagged *instrument* was the instrument (6 of 6).** The bell detector found 345 "blows"
  and a shuffled month gives 402. The "partials" never recur in more than 7 of 40 onsets. The weaker
  third week has a half-hour comb as strong as the month's. The plateaus never happen (0 runs of an hour
  above the 95th percentile). The spiral's crowded centre is geometry (std ratio 1.03). The mould hid a
  daily floor effect of 0.1 Hz.
- **Every miss was a claim tagged *grid*, and in each the grid does have the feature** (read after the
  check). M1, M2 and E7 were over-read by under three points. For E3, the line exists and is dashed, but
  the month-mean bar was set at 3 and the line reached 2.75. For E4, the check measured another band (F-179).
- **The most specialised instrument found the most, against P4.** Both claims new to the practice (E3, E4)
  came from the bell ear. The reason is not a virtue of over-adaptation. My adapter gave the mould and
  grid24 minute means, which removes a 60 s line before either can see it. The ear got the seconds.
  **The adapter, written tonight, decided what each old instrument could see.**
- So the deliberate error was controlled where it was meant to be, at the carried tool (hashes, an
  unedited caption still about September's earthquakes). It leaked at the two translations the night
  wrote, the adapter and the check. That is the finding for the project, in `work.md`'s last paragraph.

  instead of letting it stand as a result.
