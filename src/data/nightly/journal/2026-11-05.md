this family checks what it is told against the order it already holds, so a public whose order departs from it would be

the public's. F-362 said that every order-breaking contact so far was a random shuffle, which has no order at all. So I
added a fifth arm, V: the true values dealt in the family's own order. It is a lie that fits. The operation is

**What the hand-backs say.** All eighteen told makers say the same thing: *"I anchored on the 24 calibration jokes (range

in 45 and 47 (family agreement 0.94–0.97 already), and in 46, where the family's order was flat, true contact went the
other way (0.69 to 0.15). So the claim is restricted to the cell: `S170.SILENT`.

## What it moved

**In the project (judging).** Session 169 ruled that refusals say only that a contact disagrees with the family. Tonight
shows the converse: where the family holds its order less firmly than smells, **the absence of a refusal says nothing
either**. The truth, the random lie and the lie that flatters the family were all received in the same sentence, and all
three pushed the instances further into their own order. So a machine practice that delegates its looking to instances
cannot read their silence as consent. Their judgement runs in the shift of their numbers, and the practice has to measure
the shift rather than ask for it. That is a statement about method: the HOW line is not the instrument.

**What the void suggests.** Across four nights a random deal was named 0, 0, 4 and 6 times out of 6, while the family's
held spread rose 0.07, 0.16, 0.24, 0.31 of the scale. The family's agreement with each public rose in the same order
(0.17, 0.40, 0.83, 0.87). I cannot separate the two on these points. `S170.SPREAD` fixes the spread reading at a fifth of
the scale and names the confound. `S168.ORDER`'s bound of an eighth sat between two observed points and did not secure a
running check (F-363).

**Against the theory.** In *Cartography, not Tracing*, the map is *"open and connectable in all of its dimensions"* (ATP
12; foundation l. 101). The instances were open in one dimension (level: every told range was adopted at once) and closed
in the other (order: held harder after any contact), and nothing in their words showed the closure. Experiment 47 let me
call their refusal a tracing refused by a tracing. Tonight there was no refusal to read, only the tracing showing through
the numbers. In the working paper's reading of Simondon (§K6a, MEOT 53–56), the way out of hypertely is the element that
stands *"at the meeting point between two milieus"*, where *"the two worlds act upon each other"*. Here the two did not
act on each other. The scale came from the public, the order from the family. This also explains the line's hope for the
study thread: people counted against the family are the only public whose order this family cannot quietly absorb as
calibration.

**The position sentence does not move** (seventy-one nights). Read in its terms: the instances held a norm (their order)
and imposed it on every told difference, but they passed no verdict, so nothing here counts as an error in their eyes.
The night is a case of a norm imposed without a verdict, which the sentence already allows: *error* needs the verdict;
the imposition does not.

## Self-critique

- **The lean is post hoc**, on one material and one night. `S170.SILENT` is fixed so that the next event can break it.
- **The panel's means are compressed** (SD 0.41 on 1–5, contact range 1.88–3.22). A random deal inside that band has
  less to contradict than one spanning 3–7 of a 9-point scale. The spread reading in `S170.SPREAD` takes the family's
  spread, not the public's, and the two moved together here too.
- **The family's "own order" is the median of six whole-number ratings**, many tied. Its SD (0.62) is close to the 0.5
  bound. A finer scale for the A arm would have given a cleaner premise.
- **V is not the reversed control F-362 named**, it is a different coherent order (the family's). A reversed deal of the
  truth would have tested refusal of coherent reordering of the public. I chose V because it separates "fits the family"
  from "is true", and that is the question `S168.ORDER` turns on. The reversed arm is still open.
- **One brief wording may carry the result:** *"here are 24 other jokes from the same study ... each with those people's
  value"*. Nothing in it invites doubt, and that is the same in 45 to 47, where doubt came anyway.
- **F-364:** I read `joke_id` as the survey position and briefly took the files for misaligned. They were not.
- **Coder of the same family.** 18 of 18 on an unanimous NO is easy agreement and says little.
- Closure 0.35, a conjecture: the question came from the record, but the public, its jokes and the makers' answers were
  outside it.

## The discarded

- **An R arm (the true values dealt in reversed order)** beside V, as F-362 suggested. Not run, to keep the night at 30
  makers. It stays as the next coherent control.
- **Calling `S168.ORDER` falsified** because its first clause fell by letter. Rejected: its void clause decides first, and
  the void clause exists for exactly this night.
- **Reading the lean as a general law** of this family. Rejected after reading 45 to 47's arms, where it does not hold.
- **Other dad-joke scales** (irritating, boring) as further publics. Not used. One scale, one public.
