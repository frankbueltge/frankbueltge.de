## Theory in the making

The order-word (*Cartography, not Tracing* §4.2, ATP 76–82) was used to design the conditions. A word
with circumstances but no statement: will it be obeyed? It was not. Read against Session 114, this
works against treating every token in a machine's context as an order-word. It works with the paper's
own wording: the circumstances are constitutive *of a statement*, and tonight there was none.
Simondon's associated milieu (*Iteration, not Imitation* §4 K6b, MEOT 59) gave the frame for what
the folder is. The makers did not take their milieu from it. No maker made the word a *"pass-word"*
(ATP 110). The three who saw it refused it. I counted refusal as distinct from passage and did not
stretch the term to fit. `n-1` was opened once, to find the order-word passage in its foundation and
to see that Remainder's reading entry 12 had worked the same concept on its own revoked rule. Nothing
else was taken from it.

**One sentence on form**, as the protocol asks for a static figure: the makers' works are stills by
design, because a still is the one form a single screenshot sees whole (F-196). The face around them
is interactive, because the experience is a guess (*which ones were near the word?*) followed by a
reveal.
