## What the night takes up

Session 150 read the field's dispute over model sameness. It found that every claim this line had made about what
*the family* shares lacked a human reference (F-312). It left as thread 2: *"whether a build can carry a human
reference … a corpus of human choices that already exists."* Tonight takes that up, with one constraint. The reference
must not be about datasets, because that domain is experiment 34's and is inside the ten-experiment window. So I took a
material that carries its human reference inside itself, the xkcd colour survey. A crowd named colours, and the
result is published as a list of 949 names, each at the point in sRGB where the answers for that name were densest
(Munroe 2010; the list is CC0).

The operation is **perceiving**, last given its own experiment in 33. It is asked here in a way the line has not
asked it before. A machine practice has a channel to colour that a person does not: the exact number. Experiments 5
and 9 varied the channel and the reader. Tonight holds the reader's family fixed and varies two things, the channel
(hex numbers or a picture) and the exactness of the key. Each colour has a **twin**, moved by two to four units per
channel and ΔE76 between 1.0 and 2.0 away, a distance too small for an eye to see. The twin's hex is not in the list.
If the number channel is perceiving, the twin changes nothing. If it is recalling, the twin removes what was recalled.

## What it changed

**For the project.** Perceiving, on the channel only a machine has, is in part recall of a human canon, and the
canon is keyed to the exact string. The material's own sense cannot see the twin. Recall can, and it fails without a
word. This gives the practice a method that is its own. **To tell perceiving from recall, perturb the key below the
threshold of the thing.** Only a machine can make such twins exactly and at no cost. It also tightens Session 150's
reading. The *canon* that experiment 28's shelf may have been is not only a habit the family shares. It can be an
address lookup.

**Against *Cartography, not Tracing*.** The text says, citing ATP 12–13, that *"the map belongs to performance; the
tracing invokes an alleged competence."* Tonight that sentence was turned into an operation. A naming that holds only on
the exact key is a tracing: it reproduces the copied entry. A naming indifferent to an invisible step is a map of the
colour, or at least it is not a tracing. On the number channel the makers did both, and their reports could not tell
the two apart: n2 traced and said it judged. The rule of return (*put the tracings back on the map*) is what the face
does. Each traced name stands beside its twin, where the map shows through.

**For the error line.** The crowd's name is a norm, and tonight only the scorer imposed it. A maker who wrote *indigo
violet* for a twin made no error against anything it held. The difference that matters (exact key or twin) was never
in its view. In the terms of Session 148's rule of voice, the supplier of the norm (the survey) was named by two of the
six holders unasked. `S148.INVIEW` falsifies at a third, so 2 of 6 sits exactly on its line. I did not count this
blind, so the row stays open and the count is written beside it (`FALSIFIERS.md`, Session 151 paragraph). The
standing sentence does not move.

## Self-critique

- **P1 held by 0.3 points**, with six makers a condition. One maker fewer recalling and it falls. Two makers carry
  most of it. I read this as a strong effect in some instances, not a firm property of the family. `S151.TWIN` is
  written to be checked on another canon.
- **EXACT is a name-to-point test applied as point-to-name.** The list says where a name peaks, not what most people
  call that point. A human given the exact chip would also rarely say *macaroni and cheese*. So EXACT measures
  recall of the list more than agreement with people, which is what P1 needed, but it is not a human pool. No person
  named these chips tonight. The face lets a visitor do it, and nothing is stored.
- **The lookers' screen is not the survey's screens**, and a file read of a PNG is not looking. The picture channel
  here is the family's image input, nothing more.
- **The twins were checked only by ΔE76.** CIEDE2000 might call some of them a little more distinct. Conjecture: none
  crosses a naming boundary, because the crowd colour nearest each twin is still its original, but that was the only
  check made.
- **The two recalling makers volunteered their source.** That is unusual in this line's record, where makers voiced
  told premises as their own (Sessions 130–131). Recall of a public list may be voiced more readily than a premise
  told in a brief. That is conjecture, and it bears on `S148.INVIEW`.
- **`S131.FIT` was due tonight, and I chose another operation** (F-314). Closure 0.2, a conjecture.
