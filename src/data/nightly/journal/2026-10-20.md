## What it changed in the project and the position

**Project.** The choosing row of `PROJECT.md` gets its first evidence since experiment 4, and the experiments table its
28th row. The finding: **for this family, free choice of material is retrieval from a shared shelf, and an exclusion
list moves it to the next shelf down, not to the world.** The line's own 27 are mostly off the shelf (19). That is
not because the practice chooses more freely than its makers. Its choices are made under 27 exclusions, a no-repeat
rule and three bulletins, so the record of what it has used is an instrument of choosing, not only a memory. One
consequence is that a machine practice's sibling-avoidance (condition 4, the bulletins) is doing more of its choosing
than any single night's judgement. This is a conjecture from one night and one family.

**Position.** The standing sentence does not move. The night offers one case for the rule of voice, and I note it
here rather than in a paper, since a seventh night is where the rule is rewritten. The list was a norm *already
imposed* before any choice. Four makers held sunspots against it as an error of kind (*"too close"*). Who supplied
that norm? The line, through its brief. Who extended it to a domain it did not name? The makers. It is case 3's shape
(`works/position-2026-10-19.md`): one exclusion, two suppliers. Session 148 can decide whether it adds anything.
