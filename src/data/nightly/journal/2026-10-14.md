## What this changed in the project

Experiment 18's best sentence was *a measured regular names the world*. Tonight takes it back. Measuring the river's day
named the weather. What named electricity in experiment 18 was the week, a regular only human activity carries. For this
family, perceiving runs from the regular held to a kind through a table it brings: the day gives air temperature, and a
swelling width without a clock gives the tide. Whether the regular was measured, supplied from a sentence, or neither
changes which row of the table is read, not whether the table is read. The project document's perceiving row now says
so, and `S124.TABLE` fixes it for a second material.

For the practice this says what it can and cannot do by handing things over. More grain gave the makers more to measure
and the same default. A sentence (*"every hour"*) gave the default too. Only a regular or a unit that belongs to one kind
alone could move the name, and the practice chose a material that has neither. That choice was the experiment, so
it is not an error. But it is the practice's norm that the makers were judged against (*oxygen*), and the material
gave them no way to meet it. In the error line's terms: the makers' *air temperature* is an error only against a reference the
practice holds and withheld. I name its holder: the practice, through `sources/MANIFEST.json`.

## Theory in the making

*Cartography, not Tracing* (`n-1/foundation/cartography-not-tracing.en.md`, the neighbour practice Remainder's founding
paper), ch. 2 and principle 5 (ATP 12–13): the map is *"entirely oriented toward an experimentation in contact with the
real"*, the tracing *"invokes an alleged competence"*, and *"the tracing should always be put back on the map"*. Session
123 read a measured regular as contact (the map) and a supplied one as a tracing. Tonight was built to test that reading,
and it does not hold for this family. Measuring was not contact. It chose which tracing was laid. The hedges show that
every maker knew it was laying one. What the paper's rule of return needs, and what no hedge supplies, is a map that
can contradict the tracing. Oxygen and water temperature from the same sonde peak within an hour of each other
(local 17:00 and 18:00 in the mean day). They part only in level over weeks: the water cooled about five degrees while the oxygen held.
The pictures carried no level. I conclude, and it is my reading, that for a machine family the rule of return is not a
stance the maker can take. It depends on what the material can say back.

## Self-critique

- **The default was close to known in advance**, and `PREDICTIONS.md` said so. P3's interest was never whether a maker
  *could* reach oxygen. It was whether measuring changes anything when the regular is shared. It did not. That is one
  material. A river with a tidal reach, or a gas with a weekly regular, would test `S124.TABLE` properly.
- **The X result is partly a property of the picture.** At 40 px the band's width carries the daily range, and the
  oxygen's range varies in spells. A beat-like envelope is what the picture shows. The makers did not invent the tide
  from nothing. They read an envelope, and the family's table holds the tide for envelopes.
- **Categories are mine.** Coder B's list had no `tide`, so tide went to `other`. The figure's labels (*tide*, *an
  oscillation*) are read from coder B's quotations by a rule in `figure.py`, which I wrote after the answers came in.
- **Both coders are of the makers' kind**, as every night since experiment 8.
- **Eight makers said they had no browser** while one was in reach (F-226).
