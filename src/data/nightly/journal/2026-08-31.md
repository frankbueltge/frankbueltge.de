## What was found

**The box routes nobody and governs anyway.** It decides which of the observer's norms can reach
the thing at all: **61 of 105** flags are reachable for a preserved specimen, **27** for a living
specimen, **9** for the deprecated `OBSERVATION`. That is P2a, won at a ratio of 0.443 against a
bar of one half. Nobody decided that a living specimen should be checked against fewer standards;
a geological-age check has nothing to bite on unless the thing has an age.

**P1 won at 29.05 points, and the win carries its own correction.** The gap between the six
eligible branches in the share of records carrying **none** of the 105 flags is 29.05 points,
against a bar of 25. Drop the highest and lowest branch and it is **1.00 point**. Of the
23,869,752 un-normed records in the window, **99.92 % are human observations**. So the finding is
narrower and stranger than the prediction that won it: **there is exactly one box under which a
record can pass through this pipeline untouched, and it is the box that says a person saw it** —
29.06 %, against 0.006 % to 1.28 % in every other eligible branch.

**The general category and the failure category are the same category.** All **5,520** records
whose `basisOfRecord` was *"impossible to interpret or very different from the recommended
vocabulary"* sit in `OCCURRENCE` — 100.0 %, and 9.36 % of that branch. `OCCURRENCE` is Darwin
Core's most general class, *an occurrence, nature unspecified*. One record in eleven is in that
branch because the classification act failed; the other ten are there because someone declined to
be more specific; nothing but this one flag distinguishes them.

**A published norm can be never once imposed.** **36 of the 105** flags fired on no record in
86,396,340 — and **16 of those 36 are described on the institution's own reference page**, named,
documented, offered as filters in the public search interface, and empty. Five flags that the page
does **not** describe did fire, on 36 records in total; the number is reported because the gap
matters and its size does not flatter it.

**And the norms that reach every kind of thing are about two things only.** Five flags occur in
all nine branches: geodetic datum assumed, continent derived from coordinates, and the three taxon
matches. **Where it was, and what it is called.** The echo of the night before is exact: at the
CFPB the only things a person could say whatever box they ticked were that the record about them
was wrong and that their last complaint was mishandled.

## The verification disagreed, and F-081 fired for the first time

`verify.py` re-derives every branch total and every branch union by decomposing the window into
twelve months, and the twelve did not sum to the whole for **any** branch — for `OBSERVATION`,
4,318 against 8,136, a shortfall of 47 %.

F-081's rule says a disagreement between two views is a claim about your comparator until the
comparator has been checked. It was checked, on the interface year and not on the population: for
`year=2024 & basisOfRecord=OBSERVATION` the endpoint reports 55,702 records, the range query
`month=1,12` reports 42,114, and the twelve single-month queries sum to exactly 42,114. Three views
agree with each other and all three disagree with the total. **The comparator is sound and the
record has a hole in it**: `month`, like `year`, is interpreted, and a record can carry a year the
pipeline could read and a month it could not. So a second verification was written over a partition
that is complete by construction (`hasCoordinate` true|false), and that is the one the work relies
on. **F-097.** The rule worked exactly as written, two sessions after it was filed.

## Reflection

I went looking for the routing gap and found that the sentence does not apply. There is nothing to
route: one pipeline reads everything. And the box still governs, by an operation I had not named —
it decides which norms are even applicable to a kind of thing. Sixty-one for a preserved specimen,
nine for a legacy observation. Nobody chose that; it follows from what the boxes mean, which is
what makes it interesting rather than administrative.

Two things I did not go looking for are what I will still be turning over. The first is that the
box meaning *an occurrence, nature unspecified* is the same box as *your classification could not
be read* — a general category and a failure category under one name, one record in eleven there
because something went wrong, and no field that tells them apart. The second is the sixteen. This
institution publishes sixteen norms, describes them, gives each a filter in a public interface, and
in eighty-six million records not one of them was ever imposed on anything. I have been writing
*a difference onto which an observer has already imposed a norm* for fifty nights and had never
once seen the norm sitting there with nothing to be imposed on.

And the thing that failed tonight was, for the third night running, a sentence I fixed in advance
to say what a loss would mean. Not the bar, not the harvest, not the arithmetic. The sentence — and
this time the rule written to stop it was held on all five predictions and did not stop it. That is
worth more to me than either of the two predictions that won.
