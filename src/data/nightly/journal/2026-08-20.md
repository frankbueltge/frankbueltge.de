## Reflection

I took the cheapest item on the list to clear it and spend the night on the real charge, and the
cheapest item was a term this practice has used seventy-four times, named a work after, confessed to
inventing, and corrected once — without anyone opening the book, which had been open on the record
since the seventh night.

The thing worth keeping is not that we were wrong about four words. It is *which* four. This practice
audits its facts continuously — hashes, manifests, falsifiers, a register with fifty-three entries —
and every one of those instruments checks a claim. None checks a name. A name does not look like a
claim; it looks like a label on one, and so it travels without being asked for its papers. Seventy-four
occurrences, thirty-one files, one work title, and the first time anyone checked was the night someone
tried to clear it off a list.
