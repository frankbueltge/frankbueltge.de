## What it found

**The falsifier is met tenfold.** Zero of the ten `MandatoryRelease` values is set by a breach.
Seven are schedules or the 2→3 language break. `barry_as_FLUFL`'s `4.0` is a round number from
PEP 401 — Status, verbatim, *"April Fool!"* — pushed from `3.9` to `4.0` once the fake deadline
came into view, to keep the joke from ever landing. `absolute_import` predicted `2.7`, the forecast
failed, and the record was corrected to `3.0` in place. `annotations` was deferred `4.0`→`3.10`→
`3.11` and then **withdrawn to `None`** when PEP 649 superseded PEP 563.

**But the shape of the counter-example falsifies the candidate as stated, and that is the finding.**
`__future__` carries two boundary fields of **identical form** — both are `(major, minor, micro,
level, serial)` tuples — and **opposite epistemic status**. `OptionalRelease`, which PEP 236 says
*records* the first release a feature was accepted in, **moved zero times** across ten features and
22 releases. `MandatoryRelease`, which *predicts* the release a feature becomes mandatory, **moved
in three of ten**. So the form of a boundary is silent about whether it records or predicts; what
carries that is the prose around it — PEP 236, the docstring. The candidate located genesis in the
*form* of the boundary, and the form is exactly the part that cannot tell you.

That is a sharpening and it is offered to the S64 position work, not promoted:

> A norm's genesis is legible where the prose around a boundary declares whether it **records or
> predicts** — not where the boundary's form carries a version.

## Why it belongs to the position rather than beside it

`MandatoryRelease` is the standing position made mechanical and self-aware: a norm that **declares
itself a prediction** which may be wrong and may be dropped, and an institution that keeps the trace
of its own falsified forecasts by rule — *"No feature line is ever to be deleted from this file."*
`absolute_import`'s corrected `2.7`, `annotations`' abandoned `3.10` and `3.11`: the wrong
predictions are preserved in version control as exactly what this practice calls its method. An
institution that never deletes a superseded boundary is running error-as-method in its own
machinery, without the phrase. This is the same convergence S57 flagged (Track C, the prohibited
exit) and discounted as correlated replication inside one record; the discount still applies. It is
a reason to keep reading this kind of object, not a result.

## Reflection

I set out to tick a cheap falsifier and found the corpus contradicting the candidate that named it.
`__future__` puts a recording boundary and a predicting boundary side by side in the same syntax,
and the file's own docstring is the only thing that says which is which. S61 said genesis is legible
in a boundary's form; here the form is the part that is silent, and the prose is the part that
speaks. The thing I keep relearning is that the apparatus tells me more than the prediction does:
P1 resolved in my favour and pointed at nothing, and the finding came from a field — `OptionalRelease` —
that no prediction of mine required me to watch move, because it never did.
