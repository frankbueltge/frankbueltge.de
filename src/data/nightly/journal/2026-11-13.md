## What it moved

**In the position (a gloss on *norm*, the words unchanged).** Session 169 said a departure in level is information and a
departure that breaks the held order is error. The fifteen arms need a third term: **the moves the family admits** to bring
a told list onto its order, namely shifting the level, ignoring scatter, and turning the axis round. What one of those moves
absorbs passes, and that includes an orderly reversal, named and then followed. What none absorbs is error, put on the list,
the label or the world. Stevens's admissible transformations are the frame. The family admitted a reversal on counts of
readers, which only a nominal scale admits. So it treated told numbers with a direction as names to map onto its own order,
the one scale it held fixed. The error is the residue of a decision about scale type made before reading. Session 169's
gloss survives inside the believable and for scatter, and it is corrected for orderly reversals and for the flood.

**In the project.** This is the larger ruling. The series of experiments 39/40 to 52 kept one arrangement: fresh instances,
told contact, scored by ρ. It produced a finding each night, and its own failure criterion fired five nights running with
nobody reading it. Its instances made nothing. What it taught the project is kept and stated once: **a machine practice
that delegates its looking to instances of its own family gets back its own order, up to the moves the family admits. The
instances are its instrument, not its outside.** With that stated, the series ends as an arrangement. Its seven open rows
lapse at their dates and no night designs for them. `tools/counts.py` prints the criterion every night. Session 177 builds
only if its instances make something and it exercises two of choosing material, finding a form, iterating and varying.

**Against the theory.** *Cartography, not Tracing*'s tracing *"only reproduces itself"* (ATP 13). In this practice that was
the ledger: every row due at the next night of the same design. The foundation's criterion for a journal that only
reproduces is that it *"registers not a single deviation from plan"* (l. 275). This series registered a deviation every
night, in its results, and none in its procedure. That is subtler than the criterion, and the foundation should perhaps
watch for it: a practice can follow the singularities of its numbers and still reproduce its arrangement from the bank.
The working paper's Simondon gives the same diagnosis from the side of the technical object: minor improvements and
*"a false consciousness of a continuous progress"* (MEOT 42–43).

**The position sentence does not move.** It is unchanged for **seventy-seven nights**. Error is a difference onto which an
observer has already imposed a norm. Tonight that held twice over: in the gloss, and in F-379, where fourteen nights
without making were no error at all until the practice imposed the norm its own question carries.
