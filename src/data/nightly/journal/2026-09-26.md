## Reflection

Three rows, one corpus, three verdicts. The one I think about is the one I did not reach. There were
two numbers, 44.90 and 11.56, and one sentence in my own record saying which should count had to be
chosen before either was seen. I missed that sentence. Once both numbers were visible, any choice
between them would have been a choice about the verdict. The difference was already there. What had
not been fixed in advance was the norm to judge it by, and by the time I went looking for the norm,
reading the numbers and choosing the norm had become the same act.

The standing position says error is a difference onto which an observer has *already* imposed a
norm. Tonight showed this line's own procedure without the *already*. Without it, the difference did
not become an error of either kind. It stayed a difference, and I recorded it that way.
