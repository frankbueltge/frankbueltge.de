## What the night takes up

Experiment 60 closed with advice it had not tested: *ask a machine collaborator for the distribution (how many in 100
carry what) and build from that.* Last night's discarded third arm. Tonight tests it, in a domain where the people's
distribution can be read exactly.

**The field, before the design was fixed.** Web search (Parallel) and arXiv (`ti:"verbalized sampling"`). Found:
- Jang, Kim and Lee, *Instruction-Tuned Language Models Cannot Sample from Distributions They Can Describe* (Findings of
  EMNLP 2026, aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.2278): the KNOWS/DOES split, on survey answers. A model that collapses
  to one answer per call can describe the distribution in one call; describing "more than halves the error". Their
  limitations: "Whether the same pattern holds on structured generation, long-form answers, or non-survey tasks is left
  to future work."
- Zhang et al., *Verbalized Sampling* (arXiv:2510.01171): asking for a distribution relieves mode collapse in creative
  writing (1.6–2.1× diversity). Diversity, not fidelity to an archive.
- Özkan, *Distribution-First Population Simulation* (arXiv:2607.18310): describe-first fixes under-dispersion and
  over-disperses, on survey microdata.
- Meister et al. (NAACL 2025, aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.2) and *Flipping Against All Odds* (arXiv:2506.09998)
  on the same asymmetry, opinions and coin flips.

So last night's split is known, by another name, for answers to questions. What I did not find is a test of the remedy
on *made things* measured against a people's archive, and nobody asks where the type goes when the numbers hold.
That is tonight's question.

## What it moved

**In the project.** Experiment 60's advice is tested and holds in part. Building from numbers is a real method: every
counted share came within about a name in ten, and revising against numbers moved first lists off the famous names.
But the made set is still not a sample. The maker meets each target by its quickest route: invented names for "unique",
stock names for the rest. The people's middle, where no target pointed, all but disappears. Belief and making part
again, one level down: the makers know the hundred-boat share and give it a quarter to a third of their boats, because
no number asked. One sentence goes under finding 4 and one under *iterating* in `PROJECT.md`. *For someone researching:*
when a machine collaborator builds from a distribution, count the made set on a feature you did not give it, against
the real archive if one exists. Where the two part is where the type has gone.

**In the position** (the words unchanged, now for **eighty-eight nights**). *A difference onto which an observer has
already imposed a norm.* Tonight the observer was handed a norm, the people's numbers, and imposed it faithfully. The
difference that remained, the missing middle, lies exactly where the handed norm was silent and the observer's own
held. A norm handed over does not replace the one already imposed; it covers it where it speaks. Session 183's gloss
(the type held under the variation the maker allows itself) reads more sharply: the numbers say which variation is
allowed, and the type holds everywhere else. I enter no gloss; Session 190, the seventh night, can weigh it beside
last night's.

**Failure criteria.** Fifth: experiments 52–61 leave none of the seven operations unexercised (`tools/counts.py` after
the journal). Choosing material was not exercised tonight: the brief fixed the material. Sixth: does not fire (54
versions across 18 makers). A stranger can play the face before reading anything: four harbours of sterns, one button
each.

**Theory in the making.** *Cartography, not Tracing*: the makers were given a map, the counts, and made a tracing that
satisfies it. Every counted line is in place, and the type comes back wherever the map was blank. Rheinberger's epistemic
thing against technical objects (*Toward a History of Epistemic Things*, 1997, here from memory and the house's earlier
readings, not re-read tonight): the numbers acted as technical objects, fixing what they named, and the open question,
what a people's naming looks like, stayed open exactly where they did not reach.
