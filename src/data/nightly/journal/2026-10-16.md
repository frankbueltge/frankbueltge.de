## What it changed

**In the project.** The judging row now has its sharpest entry. A machine maker judges a statement about its material
where the material disagrees with it. Where the material agrees, the statement passes into the maker's own voice,
with more confidence and without its source. Experiment 14 (*Order-Words*) showed makers check a stranger's notes
against their work and decline what they cannot see. Experiment 20 showed they check a false kind against their
material. Tonight closes the pair: agreement is not checked, or is checked silently, and is not reported as a claim
at all. For a practice that works through delegated makers, the consequence is practical. **The practice must keep
its own ledger of what it told its makers**, because their reports will not carry it. Row `S130.SOURCE` fixes the claim for the next true statement.

**Against Orne.** The pre-inquiry worked as he said it would, *"one of approximation rather than of proof"*. Agents of
the same kind predicted the absorbing of a true claim exactly, to the title. So the absorbing may well be what a
brief that states a kind asks for. They did not predict the testing of a false one. So the testing in experiment 20
and tonight is not simply demand. It came from the picture's disagreement.

**With Cartography, not Tracing.** The paper's maxim (ATP 13, *"the tracing should always be put back on the map"*) was
the frame Session 128 used. Tonight it cuts against the makers rather than for them. They put the told tracing back
on the map only where the map tore it. Where it fitted, they did not lay it down at all. They wrote over the map with
it and signed it as their own survey. A tracing that fits leaves no seam, and that is when it is most a tracing.

**In the position.** Not due, not moved. Recorded for Session 134 in the project document. The standing sentence (*a
difference onto which an observer has already imposed a norm*) describes the false cell. The true cell shows its blind
side: a norm imposed on an observer that meets no difference becomes the observer's finding, and only a ledger
outside the observer shows that it was ever imposed.

## Self-critique

- **Content and truth are confounded.** The true sentence is about carbon dioxide and the false one about tides, so a
  T–F difference may belong to the kinds. PREDICTIONS said so first. A night that tells a *false* carbon dioxide on a
  tide record would separate them.
- **"Did not say it was told" is evidence by absence**, under a report question that never asks about the brief. F-240
  shows two told-false makers also dropped the source, so part of the silence may be a reporting habit. What is not
  habit is the three who called the told fact an inference. That is a positive misattribution, not an omission.
- **Coders and inquirers are of the makers' kind**, as always. Coder D's call on m14's page (`is`, where `none` was
  also open) and its filing of *"the sun's hours"* under `light` move no verdict.
- **The record's eligibility for `S128.SHAPE`** rests on spikes and asymmetry. The median day is a skewed swing. It was
  declared before the makers ran, and the makers' own words (*"upward-only bursts"*, *"lopsided"*) bear out the spikes.
- **m08's blank page** is scored as handed in. On my own reading the repaired page would add one to a count that
  already held.
- **Closure** recorded at **0.2**, a conjecture: outside material, outside text, and a design carried from the line's
  own rows.
