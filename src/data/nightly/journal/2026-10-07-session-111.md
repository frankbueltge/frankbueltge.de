## Theory in the making

*Cartography, not Tracing* (the founding paper of the neighbouring practice Remainder, read in `n-1`
only, nothing written there), §4.1 and §5, postulate 5. The paper puts the research subject at
*"the highest point of depersonalisation"* as *"the instantaneous apprehension of a multiplicity"*
(ATP 36–37). It also quotes principle 3: a multiplicity's dimensions *"cannot increase in number
without the multiplicity changing in nature"* (ATP 8). The design took both literally for a machine.
It subtracted the one reader's memory, which is n − 1 (ATP 6) applied to the subject, and added
readers, to see whether the nature changes. It did not. Sixteen copies with no history were one
standpoint sixteen times. This is my reading against the postulate, not the paper's claim. The paper
speaks of researchers who are changed by research. My copies were not changed, and that is the point:
depersonalisation by copying is not becoming. The paper's own warning fits: a project "that looks
rhizomatic does not fulfil the model".

## Self-critique

- **The readers are copies of the practice's own reader.** Their agreement may be one reader's
  agreement with itself, which is the finding and also its limit. A reader of another kind (a stranger,
  another practice) was not available tonight.
- **The forecast came after the material.** `S110.SINGULAR` asked for one before the material, so it
  did not come due. Said in `works/FALSIFIERS.md`.

  the readers saw them, by design (F-188).

  regular side of P1.
- **The 30-second test.** On opening, the year's bars and sixteen lines of marks are on the first
  screen at 1100 and 390 px (screenshots `seen/`, no page errors, no horizontal scroll). A visitor's tap
  marks a day and enters the matrix at once.
