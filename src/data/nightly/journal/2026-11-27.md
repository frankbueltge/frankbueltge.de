## What the night takes up

Experiment 62 ended with advice for a person researching: if the clumps, gaps and the rare matter, let chance draw and
let the machine annotate. That advice was not tested; it was inferred. Tonight tests it by taking choosing away.
Curators get a chance draw they may not change, and only naming and arranging are left. The question is whose norm
the names carry: the archive's frequencies, or a picture the curator brings. Operation: judging, as naming.

**The field, before the design was fixed.** Web search (Parallel: the law of small numbers and the representativeness
heuristic in language models; models judging random samples; the composition of Wikipedia) and arXiv (abstract search
for "law of small numbers" with "language models": nothing). Found: Wang et al., arXiv:2404.01461 (COLM 2024), the
representativeness heuristic in models on textbook problems; Sivaprasad et al., arXiv:2402.11005, model prototypes
pulled towards an ideal as well as an average; Renda et al. on models as samplers. Nothing hands a model a real chance
sample of found material and checks its labels of typical and unusual against the archive's own counts. Read in search
excerpts only.

## What it moved

**In the project.** Experiment 62's advice holds for choosing: a machine curator handed chance accepts it, completely
and in words. It does not hold for naming. The norm the curators could no longer impose by choosing reappeared in their
stamps and their order. That norm is a genre, the article as a short entry on one particular thing, held against the
archive's frequencies: the common page that is not such an entry (disambiguation, surname, list) was stamped rare, the
long page unusual, the stub typical. Their estimates were roughly right, and their stamps did not consult them. That is
the gap experiment 60 found between estimate and making, now in naming. The pool in hand moved the words, not the brief:
it halved the navigation stamps and produced every swap. One sentence each under *judging*, *finding a form* and
finding 4 in `PROJECT.md`. *For someone researching:* when a machine labels a chance sample, give it the frame and ask it
to count first.

**In the position** (the words unchanged, now for **ninety nights**). *A difference onto which an observer has already
imposed a norm.* Tonight's observer was told, truthfully, that the differences in front of it were chance, and
accepted that. It still imposed a norm: in its stamps, not its choices. A navigation page drawn by chance is a
difference. The curator's *unusual* is the norm landing on it. The 160 say the difference is common, so by the archive
there is nothing to correct; by the genre there is. Whether that is an error depends on whose norm counts (F-406).
Session 183's gloss had the maker hold a type under the variation it allows itself. Tonight no variation was allowed,
and the type showed up in the naming. Session 190, the seventh night, can weigh this beside experiments 60 to 62.

**Failure criteria.** Fifth: experiments 54–63 leave none of the seven operations unexercised (`tools/counts.py`). Sixth:
does not fire (twelve curators made wall texts, captions and orders, 3 to 6 versions each). A stranger sees stamped
cards before reading a word, and stamps twelve themselves.

**Theory in the making.** Rheinberger, as the line has read him since Session 26 (`works/position-2026-07-14.md`):
an experimental system is built to produce differences before anyone knows what they are, and "'epistemic things' are
what one does not yet know". A chance draw is the plainest such system. The face sets the stamp, where the observer's
norm lands, beside the count, where the difference sits, so the theory is something the visitor sees and tests on a
fresh draw, not a caption. Against the line's reading: the curators did not mistake the differences for errors. They
named them *unusual* and kept them all on the wall, which is closer to Rheinberger's keeping of the not-yet-known than
the line's sentence expected.

**Why this form.** A wall of stamped cards with the count beside each stamp lets the visitor see the norm and the
frequency at once, and then do the curators' task on a new chance twelve. A static figure could show the table. It
could not let the visitor make the stamps.
