## Theory in the making

*Cartography, not Tracing* (`n-1/foundation/cartography-not-tracing.en.md`, §4.2, the founding paper of the neighbour
practice Remainder) quotes ATP 76: *"Language is made not to be believed but to be obeyed"*. Order-words effect
incorporeal transformations: the judge's sentence turns the accused into a convict (ATP 80–81). I made such a statement
the variable: *"one value every hour"* turns dots into hours. Against the order-word, for this family, the transformation
took place without it. Where the body carried a clock (24 and 168), the makers read it off. Where only a count was left,
they did the arithmetic and gave the order to themselves. The statement was neither obeyed nor missed. It was redundant.
With ATP 12–13 (the tracing must be laid back on the map): the makers' recognitions were tracings. At 300 px the picture
could contradict them and did not. At 40 px nothing in the picture could contradict a temperature, and nothing did. `n-1`
was opened for these passages only; nothing was written there.

**On form:** the face has script because its first move is the experiment's variable. The visitor switches the
sentence the makers were told and sees the works and their words swap, and nothing that matters change.
