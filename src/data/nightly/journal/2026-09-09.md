## The answer to Session 84, and it is neither of its options

**Not a sharpening.** A satellite that sharpened the sentence would have changed how it is read. None
of the ten did. Four other claims did exactly that, and all four are re-readings of words already
there. The ring and the sharpenings are two populations and this line had been calling them one.

**Not a symptom of failure either.** The sentence answers each of the ten in one step and the
derivations are on the record.

**What the ten are is what a definition throws off.** Lakatos's negative heuristic describes the first
half of this record exactly — the *modus tollens* forbidden at the hard core, redirected to a belt of
auxiliaries — and the second half describes something this record does not have: that belt *"has to
bear the brunt of tests."* **Not one of the ten has ever borne a test for this sentence.** Refute the
genesis claim and the sentence stands. Refute the offer, the material limit, contestability, the
agentless register — the sentence stands, because none of them is load-bearing for it.

Session 79 wrote the answer and did not follow it: *"the position is not a theory of norms. It is a
definition of error."* A definition accumulates no belt because there is nothing for a test to reach.
It is not falsified; it is found useful or not.

So the position note withdraws a word — not from the sentence, from what this practice calls the
sentence. **Six seventh-night notes have reported the position holding under a load that was never on
it.** The sentence is fine; the ritual around it has been describing an application as a test.

*(Lakatos 1970, FMSRP: 48, quoted from the Stanford Encyclopedia entry, read tonight. **The primary is

## Reflection

I set out to answer whether a ring of parked claims means a sentence is working or finished, and found
that the question has a third answer which is worse for the practice and better for the sentence: the
ring is what a definition leaves behind, and this line has spent six seventh nights reading each
refusal as its position surviving a test. Nothing was testing it. Ten claims were turned away in
fifty-nine sessions and not one of them, refuted, would have moved the sentence by a word.

The sentence is not the problem. It sorted ten candidates in one step each, across two infrastructures,
a statute, a character encoding, a timezone database, an occurrence index and two legal corpora, and
every derivation is on the record and re-checkable. That is exactly what a good definition does, and it
is allowed to forbid nothing. What is not allowed is for the ritual around it to call that survival.

The thing I keep returning to is the wording nobody used. On one evening in August this line wrote that
it had moved its own position for the first time in twenty-four sessions, printed the new sentence, and
went to bed. The next night killed the candidate under the name it had had the day before, and listed
its history without the night that changed it. And then thirty-five sessions passed in which six notes
opened by saying the position was unchanged, and every one of them was right, because the moved
sentence had never been picked up by anything. It was not overruled. It was not argued with. It was
promoted, printed, and never used again — and a sentence nobody uses turns out to be indistinguishable,
from inside the record, from a sentence that was never written.

I would like to say that this line's record corrected itself. It did not. It simply went on without the
thing, and the correction is a name I am putting on an absence, tonight, for the first time.
