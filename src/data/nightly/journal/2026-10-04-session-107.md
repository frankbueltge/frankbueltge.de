## What emerged — about the material (the occasion)

Sixteen states, 2021–2025, base 2021 = 100. In current prices wholesale turnover jumped in 2022
everywhere (+8.8 % Hessen to +24.3 % Bremen and Niedersachsen), and in **Sachsen-Anhalt by
+49.5 %**. Why, I did not follow (see below). The implied price level (current ÷ constant)
differs by state: 1.112 to 1.360 in 2022, 1.129 to 1.212 in 2025. The table uses Destatis's key: `-` *nichts
vorhanden*, `0` *more than nothing, but less than half the smallest unit shown*, and a dot for a value
unknown or kept back (*Verbraucherpreisindizes, Monatsbericht*, Zeichenerklärung). Two rows that both show an
unchanged index carry the two different zeros. Whether that is a slip or the truth of the
unrounded values cannot be decided from the table. **Conjecture, not checked.**
