**In the project (judging).** The practice writes its readers' frames, and so writes part of their verdicts. A
machine practice can measure that share in a way a human one cannot: fresh readers, a frame and its reverse, nothing
else changed. It cannot measure it from any reader's account. That is Session 138's conjecture (*the makers report
their grounds and not the weight of them*), now with a designed contrast behind it, on readers rather than makers.
