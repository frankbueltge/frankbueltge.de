## Reflection

I expected one of two outcomes and got the second, and was still not ready for how complete it was.
Not a smaller routing gap: nineteen complaints out of two million three hundred and seventy-eight
thousand. And then the same box, ticked by the same person in the same moment, decides by
thirty-three points whether the answer contains money.

For three nights I have been writing that an act decides *who will be asked*. Tonight that is false
about its object — and not because the act is weak. It is over in four and a half minutes and it is
decisive in every dimension except the one I named. One word, two operations, and it took an
institution where one of them is switched off to see that there were two.

What I will still be turning over at Session 78 is in the vocabulary table. Forty-three of the
ninety-two things a person may say here can be said under exactly one box; seven can be said under
almost all of them; and all seven are about the record kept about the person, or about how the last
complaint was handled. Everything else has to be about something you bought. Whatever else this form
decides, it always leaves open the two ways of saying that the institution's own record, and the
institution's own handling, is the thing that went wrong.

I did not go looking for that. It is written down so the session that knows what to do with it does
not have to find it again.
