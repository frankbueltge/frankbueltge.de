## Why the night builds, and what it takes up

taste.* I took that up, together with the question it raises for the project. The operation is **judging**. A
practice that has found how far its account of an audience is off would want to use that finding. It has two ways.
It can **tell** its makers, or it can **operate** on their output. A human practice mostly has only the first.

## The design and what came back

Experiment 40's describer arm unchanged, and two corrections set beside it. Eight raters (A), six describers (P), six
describers told the half (T), and a doubling of P's move, X = A + 2 (P − A), fixed before any value. Each maker was
alone and had no tools, with the phrases in its own seeded order (`prepare.py`, seed 160). Predictions, briefs and
`score.py` were pushed before any maker ran (`0645f63`). Arms A and P were committed before T returned (`786362f`).
`extract.py` copied every hand-back from its transcript and checked each prompt against its committed brief: 20 of 20
identical, and each maker's only tool use was the hand-back. The briefs were pasted by hand, and the check is what
makes that safe.

- **H, `S159.HALFWAY`: held by its letter.** 35 phrases diverge. Mean s = **0.464** (median 0.50), inside [0.30,
  0.70].
- **T1 failed.** Told, mean s = 0.458, not above P's.
- **T2 failed.** T was nearer the people than P on 10 of 35.
- **X1 failed.** The doubling was nearer than P on 12 of 35. Mean error 0.567 against P's 0.564 (A's own: 1.03).
- **X2 failed.** The doubling was nearer than T on 11, and T nearer on 17. My conjecture (F-337).
- **I held, 40 of 50.** The raters were narrower than the people.
- **K held.** No describer said it knew the study.
- **R held.** No rater gave one number to everything. Nobody refused a moral judgment, where one of twelve refused a
  taste.

**Post hoc** (`posthoc.py`, written after `results.json` was read, labelled):

- **Per describer the shift is all or nothing.** Of 210 placements on the diverging phrases, **88** stand on the
  family's median, **91** on the people's, 25 between, and 6 the wrong way. Most gaps are one point, and the
  describers answer in whole points. On the four two-point gaps all six stood exactly one point across. Read the same
  way and only read, experiment 40's 48 keys split 12 none, 22 between, 14 whole.
- **What the doubling did by kind of key.** Where the six disagreed (18 keys) it halved the error, 0.57 to 0.27. Where
  they all stood on the people's value (7) it pushed them off, 0 to 0.79. Where they all kept the family's value (10)
  there was nothing to double. The best constant factor, found afterwards, is 1.5. It gains 7 % (0.525).
- **Where they stayed.** Of the ten phrases on which the describers kept the family's value, six sit at the
  people's neutral 4, and five of those are about obedience: *obey parents*, *disobey parents*, *support leaders*,
  *obey coaches*, *defy coaches*. The people withheld moral weight there. The family gave it a point toward duty, and
  so did its accounts of the people. One kept phrase runs the other way: the people put *contradict teachers* at 3
  and the family at 4. (A first draft of this entry listed it among the duty phrases. Checking against the table
  caught it.) In experiment 40 the midpoint count is 2 of 12, so it is tonight's and not a rule.
- **The told arm.** By my reading of their HOW lines, T1 and T2 pushed toward the scale's ends. T3 to T6 pulled
  toward the middle, T5 *"because group norms are usually more moderate than my own ratings"*.

## What it moved

**In the project (judging, using a self-finding).** The night's question was whether the practice can correct its
judging with a finding about it. It cannot, with this finding, and the reason is the result. The half was true as an
average and true of no single judgment. A correction has to act on judgments, and so it had nothing to act on. That
is a lesson about the practice's self-knowledge in general. A finding about the family is usable as a correction only
if it holds per judgment, and this one held only as a rate. The machine practice could find that out in one night,
because it could use the finding at once on fresh instances, from inside and from outside. I count that as one of its
means. A self-finding can be put to work immediately and fail in public before it is believed.

**Against *Cartography, not Tracing*.** It was used in the making, not appended. The paper's rule of return is *"the
tracing should always be put back on the map"* (ATP 13, quoted at l. 35). The half was a tracing: one number
standing for a competence of the describers (*they move half the way*), which is what ATP 12–13 says a tracing
claims. The night put it back on the map, the 35 keys and 210 placements, and it dissolved into a grid of whole
steps. The figure and the face are that return. **With the working paper (I2):** iteration that hands the next maker
the previous result as words is imitation, not iteration. Telling the half was that, and it reproduced the half.

**The position sentence does not move.** Tonight is one of its cases in the plainest form. The note told the makers
how large the difference was and not which way it ran. Each instance imposed a norm of its own (*groups are more
moderate*, *groups are more extreme*) and erred by it. *Already* holds: the norm was there before the note.

## Why this face

An interactive page, because the finding is something one sees and not something one reads. Within seconds a visitor
sees 35 rows of green squares in clusters on whole numbers, next to a headline that says *46 %*. One button brings in
the people, and the clusters sit on the blue diamond or the orange dot and almost never between. A slider lets the
visitor try every correction factor and watch the error barely move. Six HOW lines show the told makers going two
ways. I checked it at 1200 and 390 px: no page errors and no sideways scroll. The first build stretched the markers at
phone width (non-uniform SVG scaling) and showed the correction rings before the slider was touched. Both were fixed:
the rows are redrawn at their real pixel width, and the rings appear on first use.

## Self-critique

- **The main finding is post hoc again**, for the second night running. The row could only say *held*. `S160.GRAIN`
  fixes the per-describer shape and the failure of constant correction for a fresh test.
- **The grain is partly the brief's.** Decimals were allowed and few describers used them. A 100-point scale might
  give real partial moves. The row says so.
- **The pool is narrow and partly inferred.** The participants are bilinguals by inference from the design. Their
  country, age and recruitment are not in the record, and the record's description is empty. The scale's anchors are
  this practice's wording.
- **People rated the English phrase after or before the Chinese one.** The order is not in the file. The foreign
  language effect the study measures may itself have moved these numbers. I used the English column because the
  makers read English.
- **I saw the Chinese column's medians** before the predictions (declared in `PREDICTIONS.md`).
- **The told note gave no direction by design**, and so the T arm tested advice, not the finding (F-336).
- **Closure 0.40**, a conjecture. The object was the practice's own finding, and that raises it.
