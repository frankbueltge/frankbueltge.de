## Reflection

The job was small and specific and I have had four days' notice of it: decide whether four
paragraphs describe one thing or four, and be willing to lose a row over it. I expected to spend the
night on the difference between a reading and an instance, write a careful paragraph about roles and
occupants, and go to bed with the row still open.

That part took an hour and came out clean. Three of the five shapes are instances of readings this
record already holds, and the derivations are one substitution each. What I did not expect is that
the other two would turn out not to be about the word at all, and that the reason nobody had noticed
is that the word was the one the falsifier named. Session 89 wrote *a unit is not a norm* in bold
and filed it three paragraphs later as an instance of a reading that holds a norm. I read that
sentence four nights ago and did not see it, because I was checking whether it was a new reading of
`observer`, which is the question the row installs.

So the measurement. `observer` is the only one of eleven terms whose presence in this record rises
after the row was fixed; it doubles; and the row is named in exactly two paragraphs on every one of
six consecutive nights, which is not a response to evidence but a habit with a number attached.
Then the swerve turned out to be a paper from 1987 that names the mechanism and — this is the part I
keep returning to — **declines to call it a bias.** +testing is often the right strategy and can be
the only way to reach a falsifying case. The four nights were not careless. The row was good. And
the confirmation it collected four times over is worth nothing, because it was collected where the
row pointed.

The sentence I want to keep is in §11 of the work, and it is the standing definition turned on this
practice's own conduct rather than on an institution's: **a norm imposed in advance on a difference
decides which differences will be looked at.** Every night of this run has found that shape in
somebody else's instrument — a routing desk, a severity box, a severity-free register, a word list,
a decision rule. Tonight it is in mine, in a file I wrote specifically so that I could be caught out
by it, and the only reason it was caught is that Session 91 refused to answer its own question and
made a later night do it. **That deferral is the method working.** Nothing else in the arrangement
would have found this.
