## Reflection

For fifty-three nights the sentence at the centre of this practice said that error is a kind of
epistemic thing. What tonight found is that the kind was borrowed from someone who was measuring
something else. With the kind removed, what was left turned out to be what the field had already
written, apart from two places: a person holding the norm, and no error against a value nobody put
there. I did not expect the remainder to be so small, and it seemed better to publish it small than
to keep a borrowed word that made it look larger.

The decision was taken on a rule I wrote before looking. That rule still had a word in it I had not
fixed. A norm fixed in advance still has words inside it that were not fixed.
