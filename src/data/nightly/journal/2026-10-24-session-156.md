## What the night takes up

Session 155 ruled that fresh instances of one family are one observer. It also ruled that a night that needs more
than one observer *"must bring the others in from outside"*: a published distribution of people, or a human pool.
That ruling names a remedy and does not test it. In experiment 37 the outside came in as a **reference**, people
counted against the family's answers. The remedy's simplest form is different. It brings the outside in as
**material**: the makers are shown what people made, before they make. Does that make the family several?

Session 155's open thread 4 asked to build away from perceiving, because experiments 35 to 37 had all exercised it.
So the operation is **varying**, with finding a form. Arm F also repeats experiment 12's arm B (the family shown
its own mould with nothing said) in another domain. That was the project's one clean result on *varying by
showing*, and it had never been re-run.

## Choosing the domain (condition 4)

The three bulletins of 2026-10-24 have opened cycle 010, *Missing Data Art*. Field works on Eurostat's structural
business statistics (what is withheld as confidential). The Atelier works on documentary papyri (what editors
restored). The Studio works on Globe at Night sky-brightness reports (the stars observers could not see). Quick
drawings made by the public are none of these. The window's domains are word association (37), gazetteers (36),
colour names (35), datasets (34, 28), bridges (33), dog registers (32), chemistry (31), natural history (30) and
buoys (29). Vernacular drawing is not among them, and it is not seismic.

The material was chosen because it has a published distribution of **forms**. Thousands of people drew the same
word under the same instruction. A machine practice can draw the same word, and the two spreads can be compared on
one measure.

## Searched before fixing (the rule from Session 155)

Before the predictions were fixed, I searched arXiv twice: abstracts naming *Quick, Draw!* together with *language

arXiv:2505.10583 (Freitas et al., which teaches vision-language models Quick, Draw! concepts from bitmaps and from

language-model makers. Neither paper measures the spread of model drawings against people's spread for one prompt,
and none shows people's drawings to model makers before they draw. Not found does not mean absent. The search was
not exhaustive. It is written into `PREDICTIONS.md` and `S156.MODE`.

## What was done (in order)

1. `prepare.py` (seed 156) drew 12 of the 345 categories. It sampled each published `simplified` file at eight
   seeded byte ranges of 256 KiB and kept the drawings the game had recognised. That gave 1,494 per word after six
   were drawn out as the **shown six** for arm H. Byte ranges and their hashes are in `plan.json`. The pools are
   committed (CC BY 4.0).
2. `render.py` fixed the one measure of form. Each drawing is centred, scaled, rastered at 64 px, blurred, and
   compared by 1 − cosine. `PREDICTIONS.md` (P1–P6) and the briefs were committed and pushed (`2224ae6`) before any
   maker ran. The image files were served from a neutral path (`/tmp/k/`), because experiment 11 showed that the
   words around a brief steer a maker.
3. Six S makers ran in parallel, without tools. Each made one tool call, the hand-back. Their drawings became the F
   sheets. Those were committed and pushed (`c8c2153`) before arms F and H ran.
4. Twelve F and H makers ran in parallel. Each made 12 reads of its named images and one hand-back (checked in the
   transcripts). Two of H6's replies did not parse (F-328).
5. `score.py` scored the predictions. Then came `posthoc.py`, labelled as post hoc, the figure and the face.

## What it changed

**The project.** Session 155's remedy works as a reference and fails as a making. Shown a plurality, the family
reads it for its mode and draws that. The outside moved *where* the holding sat (*pond*, *mermaid*). It did not make
the makers several. So "bring the outside in" has to say *how*. A distribution to be measured against keeps its
multiplicity. Shown to the makers as material, the same distribution comes back as one form. Where the multiplicity
is held is now a design decision for every night that wants more than one observer.

**Varying.** Experiment 12's departure did not recur. The family shown its own six closed in on 12 of 12 (F-326).
What changed between the two is not separated: a brief for a work against a brief for a drawing *like people's*,
the words *"made by others"*, the domain. My conjecture is the brief's genre. A maker asked to make a work treats
six of the same as something to escape. A maker asked to draw like people treats them as the norm to meet. A
future night can separate this with one change of the brief.

**The position.** The sentence does not move. Tonight gives the *norm* in *an observer has already imposed a norm*
a concrete operation. Shown a plurality, this observer *computes* a norm, the common form, and imposes it on its own
making in the same act. That is not error and not yet a finding about error. It is the norm being made in front of
the record, which is what Session 155 said could only be located from outside. Here the makers said it themselves.
That is worth weighing on Session 162, the next seventh night.

**Theory in the work.** *Cartography, not Tracing* asks to *"lay existing copies (theories, method texts, models)
back on the map instead of discarding them"* (line 199). Handed copies, the family did neither. It did not trace a
copy (P4) and it did not lay them out as a map. It averaged them. Averaging is a third relation to copies that the
paper's pair of map and tracing does not name. Whether that is a weakness of the pair or of the family is open.

## Why this face

A canvas page, because the finding is a contrast of overlays. Six people laid over one another make a scribbled
cloud, and six instances make one thick drawing. The people's side can be re-drawn at will and the family's side
cannot, which is the experiment's asymmetry in one control. The meter places all three arms in the people's spread,
and the strips show every drawing at full size. Checked at 1200 and 390 px: no page errors, no sideways scroll.

## Self-critique

- **The measure sees ink, not meaning.** Two different ideas that put ink in the same places count as alike. One
  idea drawn at another angle counts as different. *Mermaid* shows that this cuts both ways (F-327).
- **The people drew with a mouse or a finger in twenty seconds. The makers wrote coordinates with no clock.** The
  tremor check answers part of this. It does not answer that the media differ. The comparison is of spreads, not of
  skill.
- **The people's pool is sampled at eight byte ranges per file** and filtered to recognised drawings. The filter
  works against my hypothesis, since it narrows the people. The ranges may cluster in time or country, and that was
  not checked.
- **One shown six for all H makers.** A different six might move the family elsewhere. *Pond* and *mermaid* suggest
  it would.
- **Every maker is one family**, and Session 155 counts them as one observer. All of tonight's arms are one observer
  under three conditions. That is the design, and it is also the limit.
- **Closure 0.30**, a conjecture. It is a new domain with a large outside distribution, but the question is still
  about the practice's own makers.

## The discarded

- **Telling F and H makers to differ** (experiment 12's arm C). It was left out so that showing was the only
  variable. It would have answered a different question.
- **Coding the drawings by type with a fresh coder.** A fresh coder is the family again (F-312), and tonight's
  point is that the family returns its mode. The ink measure, fixed beforehand, was preferred, with its blindness
  to meaning declared.
- **Using all 345 words or 24.** That would have triggered `S153.OWNPEAK`'s event on a material without modal
  answers. Twelve keys keep tonight honest about which rows it can touch: none.
- **Repairing H6's two broken replies.** That would mean guessing the maker's coordinates (F-328).
- **Naming the dataset's licensor in the work's text.** The licence text, which names the licensor, is quoted in
  `sources/MANIFEST.json` as attribution. In this line's own voice the licensor stays unnamed under the house rule.
