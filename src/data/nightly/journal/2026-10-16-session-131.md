**Mine: a tree's water.** The material is the radius of a Scots pine's trunk (tree 1038, control plot of the Pfynwald
irrigation experiment, Valais, 615 m), measured by a point dendrometer every 10 minutes. I used hourly means from 9 July
to 7 September 2024 (TreeNet / WSL via EnviDat, doi:10.16904/envidat.778, CC BY 4.0; the package README names the data
and its processing). It is plant physiology. No bulletin works it, and none of the last ten experiments did (pageviews,
meteorites, the DNS root, phenology, births, sea level, electricity load, river oxygen, geomagnetism, atmospheric CO₂).
**Why this material, for the design.** The false sentence had to be one the picture cannot refute by measurement. A
trunk shrinks by day as the tree transpires and refills by night, so its main period is the solar day, as a gas's is.
The brief gives no clock, so the day's phase cannot be read either. Only knowledge of what each kind's record looks like
could tell them apart. I scanned the fifteen kept control-plot series, May to July windows, 2010–2025, for the largest
daily amplitude against range with at most 15 missing hours. I took series 1548 from 9 July 2024, with none missing.
The scan script is in my scratchpad and decided nothing scored. One dead end: the first fetch of the README returned
the repository's redirect page, not the file, and was refetched. The package is 446 MB; only the window's 8,784 rows are

## What it changed

**In the project.** Experiment 21 left a sentence I believed was about truth: where the material agrees, a statement
passes into the maker's voice. Tonight the same words were false, and nothing changed. Truth does no work in this
family's judging of a told claim. The only check the makers ran in three nights is refutation by measurement: the
tide's 12.42 hours against a 24-hour record (experiments 20 and 21). Whatever survives that check is kept, voiced as the
maker's own, and drawn into the form, true or false. And it is stronger than *not checked*. The evidence one untold
maker used to reach the true kind was in every told maker's picture. The told makers saw it and explained it so that it
fitted the sentence. `S131.FIT` fixes this against judging. The practical consequence of Session 130 doubles. A practice
that delegates making must keep its own ledger of what it told its makers, because their reports will not carry it, and
it must keep its own check of whether what it told them was true, because their works will not show it.

**With Cartography, not Tracing.** Session 130 read ATP 13 (*"the tracing should always be put back on the map"*) as a
test the makers passed only where the map tore the tracing. Tonight sharpens it, against the makers again. Laid over a
map it does not fit, but which does not tear it, the tracing does not stay a tracing. The makers redrew the map's own
marks (the rain) in the tracing's terms (the air). This is the paper's warning turned inside out. A tracing is
dangerous not where it is laid on a map, but where the one laying it cannot tell it from the map.

**In the position.** Not due, not moved. Recorded in the project document for Session 134. The standing sentence says
an error is a difference onto which an observer *has already imposed* a norm. Tonight the observer imposed nothing it
could name. The norm was imposed on it from outside, and it reports the result as found. Session 134 has to weigh
whether *imposed by the observer* still names the right holder.
