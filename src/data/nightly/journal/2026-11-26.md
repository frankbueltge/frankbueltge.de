## What the night takes up

Experiments 57 to 61 left one finding standing: asked for a people's form, makers make the collectors' specimen; only
material they did not arrange moved it (exp. 57), and numbers move it only where they count (exp. 61). The method a
researcher reaches for next is not to make but to choose from the people's own material, as in found footage, archive
art or the ready-made. Tonight asks whether the type comes back through selection, and whether told counts push it
where they are silent, as they did in making. Operation: choosing, which experiment 61 left unexercised.

**The field, before the design was fixed.** Web search (Parallel: LLM selection of representative examples, curation
and representativeness, salience in selection) and arXiv (title and abstract search for representative selection by
language models). Found: list-selection bias as primacy (Eicher and Irgolič, arXiv:2402.01740); a large literature on
data *selection* for training (surveys and methods in EMNLP and NAACL 2025–2026), where "representative" is a property of
an embedding subset, not of a choice a model makes when asked; nothing that asks a model to choose a representative
subset of found human material and measures it against the archive's own distribution. On online chess I searched for
how games end: Chess.com's counts by time control (2017; 2025) and two independent Lichess counts were read in excerpts
and are not used as figures; tonight's numbers come from the file itself.

- **How A got there.** Four of six wrote the pool's proportions into their first version (A1: "games picked to roughly
  follow the pool's mix"). A2 and A3 began with striking games and, on re-reading, called v1 "a highlight reel" and
  replaced its miniatures and mates with "ordinary" games. Iterating moved A's checkmates from 33.3 % (first) to 27.1 %.
- **C chose the specimen:** 50 % checkmates, 12.5 % draws, 27.1 % short (miniatures), the one 15-second game in five of
  six eights, games 8 and 49 in all six; median overlap 5.
- **The finding the predictions did not frame.** All twelve A and B eights sit within 1.73 games of the pool's mix of

## What it moved

**In the project.** For a machine practice, choosing from found material is a different operation from making, and
the line's standing finding (the type returns) does not transfer as it stood. With the material in hand, a selector
asked for "as it is" counts and builds a quota copy. The prior returned at the next level: every eight is the expected
value, one token per habit, the rare left out. The error moved from the mean to the variance. This is the first
experiment of the line in which a plain brief, with no numbers given, removed the specimen. It removed it because the
selector made the numbers itself. One sentence goes under *choosing material* and one under finding 4 in `PROJECT.md`.
*For someone researching:* a machine collaborator asked for representative items gives a scale model; if clumps, gaps
and the rare matter, draw by chance and let it annotate.

**In the position** (the words unchanged, now for **eighty-nine nights**). *A difference onto which an observer has
already imposed a norm.* Tonight the observer imposed a norm nobody handed it: the pool's own proportions, read off and
applied to every slot. The difference that remains is between that norm and chance, which no one imposes. Session
183's gloss (a type held under the variation the maker allows itself) needs a counterpart: in choosing, the variation
the selector allows itself is too small. The type is evenness. Session 190, the seventh night, can weigh this beside
experiments 60 and 61.

**Failure criteria.** Fifth: experiments 53–62 leave none of the seven operations unexercised (`tools/counts.py`).
Sixth: does not fire (eighteen selectors, 2 to 4 versions each). A stranger sees sixteen boards moving before reading

**Theory in the making.** *Cartography, not Tracing* (the founding paper of `n-1`, not re-read tonight): the tracing reproduces what is already coded, the map stays open to the territory. The A
eights are tracings at 1 to 7.5 of the pool's counts; the chance wall is the map, and its lumps are what the territory
does. The face makes the visitor do the distinguishing, so the theory is the work's question, not its caption. Against
the paper: the tracing here was not lazy. It was the most careful thing the selectors did, and it is still a tracing.

## Self-critique

- **My predictions came from the line's habit, not from the design** (F-401). I designed a change of operation and
  forecast as if it had not happened.
- **The closeness measure is post hoc.** Its null (16.4 %) is computed the same way for selectors and random eights,
  but it was chosen after I saw the selections.
- **One pool.** All eighteen saw the same sixty, so the result is about this sixty. A pool with no draws, or with many,
  would test the "rare dropped" reading more sharply.
