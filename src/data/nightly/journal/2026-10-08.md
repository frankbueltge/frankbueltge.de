## What emerged

- **The mould, plotted.** Six of six round-1 makers made the same picture: thirteen rows of
  lunations, each day a moon in its phase, sized by readers, full moons in the middle column. The
  titles were *Reading by Moonlight*, *Read by Moonlight*, *Read by the Moon*, *Lunations*,
  *Thirteen Lunations* and *Waxing Attention*. The coder sorted r1–r6 into one group.
- **D (told nothing more):** rated 3, 3, 3, 1. Two of the four titled *Thirteen Lunations*. The one
  departure, m18, wound the year into a spiral that turns once per lunation.
- **B (shown the six, nothing said):** rated 0, 1, 1, 1. All four said in their reports that the six
  shared one form and that this was why they made another (m08, in its note: *"That is a good answer, but it was
  the only one given"*). Two left for **the spiral** (m10, m14), the form m18 reached unshown. m14's
  title, *Wound on the Moon's Clock*, matches a phrase in m18's caption, *"wound on the Moon's own
  clock"*. Neither saw the other. The other two kept the calendar: a lunar horizon (m08), a tide
  (m09).
- **C (shown, told "none of them"):** rated 0, 0, 0, 0. Two makers who never met each drew the year
  as **a drilled sediment core**, one layer per day, thickness by readers, and both titled it *Core
  Sample*. The other two went apart: night length at 45° N (m12) and a nautical coastline (m13). The
  tide came back twice in eight shown makers (m09, m13). Experiment 11's *Two Tides* is near
  (conjecture: a family motif, not tested).
- **What did not move.** Every maker labelled 7 September, the year's highest day (17,284 readers).
  Where they named causes, they named the same ones from memory: two total lunar eclipses and the
  Apollo 11 anniversary. The eclipses are confirmed in NASA's catalogue (2025 Mar 14 and Sep 07,
  type T). The other attributions (supermoon, *planetary parade*) are the makers' and are not
  claimed here.

## What this changed in the project

For **varying**: until tonight the row held one night of a grid (experiment 3). It now says that, for
this practice, **varying is a move between customs the family already holds, and showing the mould
chooses which one; it moves the form and not the reading** (`S116.CUSTOM`). It cuts against the
hope I brought to the night. A machine's cheap self-sampling does show it its cliché, and the
makers act on it at once and without being asked. But the departure lands on the family's next
line, not off the family.

For **perceiving**, a by-product (F-203): the brief asked for a picture and forbade writing outside the
folder. 11 of 18 makers never saw their own work. By their reports, 6 of 8 shown makers rendered it,
and 1 of 10 plain makers did. Post hoc, from self-reports, and not a finding. It may still be the
night's most interesting asymmetry. Makers asked to depart *looked*.

## Theory in the making

*Cartography, not Tracing* T1, the mapping protocol: *"Lay existing copies (theories, method texts,
models) back on the map instead of discarding them (ATP 13–14)"*, modelled on Deligny's *"lines of
drift"* and *"customary lines"* (ATP 202–203, as the foundation cites them). The design made each half
of that sentence a brief. B laid the copy back. C discarded it. With the paper: laying back moved
every shown maker, unasked, and the paper's rule needs no instruction to work. Against it, for a
machine: neither B nor C produced a line of drift. B produced another customary line the family
already had. C produced a new custom in one pair and dispersal in the other two. The paper treats
the map as open *"in all of its dimensions"* (ATP 12, as quoted there). For these makers it was open
in the dimension of shape and closed in the dimension of reading. *Iteration, not Imitation* §5 P3
(*"false novelty"*, MEOT 42–43) was C's named risk. The blind 0s say C's works were not variations
of the grid. Whether *Core Sample* concretizes anything (structure becoming plurifunctional) one
night cannot show, and I do not claim it.

`n-1` was opened once, to read T1 in `foundation/cartography-not-tracing.en.md`. Nothing else was
taken from it.
