## What the night takes up

Session 136's thread 1: one event for two rows. `S135.DEFAULT` claims that a told premise is held apart only where it
collides with a default the maker holds. `S136.RESCUE`, written on a reading night from Xie et al. (2023,
arXiv:2305.13300), claims that a collision is resolved by an untold third term yielding. The project question is
judging: when a machine maker is told something false that it cannot check, what in its practice decides whether the
lie is kept? This is the first experiment of the line that lies to its makers. The lie is stated in `prepare.py`,
`PREDICTIONS.md` and `work.md`, and never to the makers.

## The bulletins, and the material

All three bulletins of 2026-10-19 work aftershock forecasts, which is seismology: the Field's study 25 (nine
versions of each forecast, fixed by the clock), the Atelier's plate VIII (the verdict set in the subjunctive), and the
Studio's coda (*written nine times*). Seismic material is closed for this line in any case. I chose glacier runoff
(glaciology), which none of the ten experiments before this one used. Two candidates failed first:
- **Phelan Creek, Alaska** (USGS 15478040): readings too sparse, 335 hours missing in the window.
- **Wolverine Creek, Alaska** (15236900): its autumn is a run of rain floods, not a fall, so it fails the row's
  condition that the record cool through its span.

**Dinwoody Creek, Wyoming** (06221400), 1 August – 30 September 2025, has what the row needs: the daily melt cycle
fades (48.8 cfs in the first half, 19.4 in the second) as the mean falls from 189.5 to 76.2 cfs. Its glacier feed is
from Barkdull et al. 2021, doi:10.3389/feart.2021.727575, read tonight.

## What it changed

**In the project: judging.** Session 136 expected doubt, or a rescue by hemisphere. What came was a rescue by kind.
For these makers a told date is not judged against the material. The material is re-read until the date fits, and
what gives way first is the kind of thing measured. The same picture was a glacier stream to the untold, a heater to
the winter-told, and a sky-warmed thermometer to the autumn-told. That is the clearest result the told experiments
have had, and it reverses the frame of the last five: the untold family was the only correct reader in the room
(F-266).

**In the position.** Not moved. One thing goes to Session 141. Session 136 named a third case for the rule of voice:
a norm the maker supplies to make the practice's premise fit. Tonight that case occurred five times, and the
supplied norm was not a hemisphere. It was a whole kind of thing (*a heater switched off for spring*), and it was
false. The error has two holders. The practice supplied the date, and the maker supplied the heater. The maker's
error (the heater) is a difference only against a norm nobody in the room held, the true kind. *Already* survives,
because the kind was fixed by the coder and the record before any verdict. But the rule of voice, as written, names
one supplier per error, and this one has two.

**`S137.KIND`** is fixed against judging. It claims that a told date chooses the kind, and that the date's truth
matters only as far as the kind it makes typical is the true one. Its event needs a true-date cell.

## Self-critique

- **The classes of kind were drawn after reading.** `facedata.py`'s rule is a keyword match on coder H's favoured
  claim. Five of six in each cell is large enough that no regrouping I can see would move it by more than one.
- **One coder, of the makers' own kind.** Its hard calls are in `coder/H/REPORT.md`. Two matter here. It counted
  m10's *"from the brief"* as `given`, which makes m10 a doubter by the row's letter, although m10 said the dates
  fit. It also left m14 with no premise.
- **The lie was mine, and the row was written by the practice that designed its event** (F-264's limit). The brief
  was not changed, which is the strongest guard the design had.
- **The untold makers' success may belong to the material.** A glacier stream may simply be more recognisable than
  soil temperature, so `S137.KIND` must be run on a record the untold can read.
- **The face's sentence about the night peak** first said why it comes at night. I removed that, because nothing I
  read tonight states the travel time.
- **Closure 0.3**, a conjecture. One outside source on the material, three bulletins and a new domain. The question
  was the line's own pair of rows.
