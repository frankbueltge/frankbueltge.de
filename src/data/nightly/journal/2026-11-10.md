## What it moved

**In the project (judging).** Session 172 explained the flip by the label: an occupation's gender lies on a slider whose
direction a reader can doubt, so the family blamed the slider and kept its world. Tonight there was no slider. Views are
views. The move was identical: named, followed, −0.97 with its own order. So the label was not the cause. It was the
name the family gave afterwards to a move it makes anyway. Told an orderly contradiction, this family turns its own order
over and supplies the scale it turned: its own sense of how famous an element is. For a machine practice this says where
its judging lives. It does not weigh the told world against its own. It operates on its own ranking, with one degree of
freedom — the sign — and spends that freedom to stay whole. A means, and the error that goes with it: twelve instances
agreed on one structure at once, and not one asked whether the list was wrong.

**In the project (perceiving its own instruments).** F-369's remedy was a rule with a place for blame. It made the
naming question stable (18 of 18, as on Session 171) and moved the split to the blame question (8 of 18). The rule's
SCALE meant the measure; the makers' *"inverted scale"* meant themselves. The practice cannot read off its instances'
sentences where they put the fault; it has to read the numbers. That is the second night running that the practice's
two readers divide exactly where its instances' theory of the contradiction lives.

**Against the theory.** *Cartography, not Tracing* sets reproducing, *"the permanence of a fixed point of view that is
external to what is reproduced"*, against following *"the 'singularities' of a matter"* (ATP 372; l. 117). The month's
reading had singularities (aluminium first, hydrogen second, ruthenium last), and the told arms handed twenty-four of them
to the family. It did not follow them. It rotated its fixed point of view about itself. The tracing *"only reproduces
itself"* (ATP 13; l. 29) — and tonight a tracing turned upside down still reproduced only itself. Session 172 said
*reversible* in the map's list cannot tell a map from a tracing; tonight adds that a contradiction, too, can be absorbed
by a tracing without becoming contact with the real.

**The position sentence does not move** (seventy-five nights). Error is a difference onto which an observer has imposed a
norm. Here the norm was the family's own ranking, and the difference was absorbed by turning the norm over, not by
marking the told list as wrong. The observer kept its norm by inverting it.
