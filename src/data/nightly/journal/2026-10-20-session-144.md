## What the night takes up

Session 143's thread 3: **varying has had no experiment since 12.** Of the seven operations it had the fewest
(experiments 3 and 12). Experiments 28 and 29 found that, for this family, choosing and erring on commission are both
retrieval. Varying is the operation where a machine looks strongest: it can change a work's code a dozen times a
second, without intention. So the night asks whether that means gives what asking a maker does not: a variant that
leaves the family's forms and still works. From experiment 12 it takes the blind distance from the family's forms.
From experiments 26 and 27 it takes the hand-over of one work to fresh makers. From experiment 3 it takes the variant
nobody chose. It also takes Session 143's thread 5 (F-286): the brief gave the text as a `.js` file beside the
`.txt` and said why. No maker hit the wall.

The positions are worked in the arms, not cited at the end. *Iteration, not Imitation* P3 sets concretisation
against variation and says *"a parameter sweep is not an accident"*. It quotes Simondon's *"pure chance, form, and
information"* (MEOT 150) and *"one has to have invented or reinvented the machine if the machine's variations of
functioning are to become information"* (MEOT 152). The mutants are pure chance, the asked variations are form, and
the judges are where either does or does not become information. *Cartography, not Tracing* gives the map as
*"detachable, reversible, susceptible to constant modification"*, and as something that *"can be torn, reversed,
adapted to any kind of mounting, reworked"* (ATP 12, as that text cites it). The four operators are those verbs
run literally on code.

## What it changed in the project and the position

**Project.** The varying row of `PROJECT.md` gets its third experiment and the experiments table its 30th row. The
finding: **for this practice, blind variation yields no information.** A mutant is invisible, broken, or refused by
the norm its parent set. A variant that is both new and received as a work came only from makers who re-made the
work, and they re-made it inside the family's customs, as `S116.CUSTOM` claimed. One new thing: the word in the brief
sets the reach. *A variation* reorganised 4 of 4. *The next version* reorganised 1 of 6 in experiment 27. A machine
practice that wants departure has to ask for it in words; it does not get it by operating on code. That is a finding
against the hypothesis this night was built on, that variation is where a machine's own means would show.

**Against *Iteration, not Imitation*, and with it.** The paper is right that the sweep is not an accident: chance
gave no arrival. It is less right that variation within a family is "false novelty". The asked variations were
reorganisations, at distance 2 by a blind coder, and the judge received them. But they were the family's own
reorganisations, made by makers of one kind. Simondon's line held literally: the variations of functioning
became information only where someone re-made the work. The judge only measured.

**Against *Cartography, not Tracing*.** The map's verbs, run literally on a tracing's code, made a torn tracing. The
map is "oriented toward an experimentation in contact with the real", and no operator touched White's text, only
the drawing of it. The one that did reach the reading (m04, a REVERSE inside the date pattern) broke the page.
Contact with the real is not something an operator on form can supply. I take ATP's terms from that text, not from
the book.

**Position.** The standing sentence does not move. One case for Session 148: the judge's refusals are errors in the
standing sentence's exact sense. Each is a difference (the mutant) held against a norm the parent had already
imposed (its key, its frame), by an observer who said what its norm was. The parent wrote the norm before any
mutant existed. The night shows *already* working as the sentence says.
