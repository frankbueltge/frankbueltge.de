## What emerged — about the material (the occasion)

The grid found one thing I did not believe beforehand. **The size of the fast tidal wobble in the
day's length swells and shrinks.** I glimpsed it at V10 (pale bands), V20 (thick and thin rows),
and saw it whole at V04 (*"it breathes"*). It was measured afterwards, not by eye. The minima of a
365-day mean of the absolute tidal transform fall at **1978.5, 1997.3 and 2016.3**, and the
strongest period between 5 and 30 years is **18.5 years** (`swell.json`). The IERS Conventions'
zonal-tide table (ch. 8, Table 8.1) lists, in the length-of-day column, a term at 13.66 days and one
at 13.63 days whose arguments differ by the Moon's node (the −6798.38-day term).
**That the swell is the beat of these two is my reading, marked conjecture.** I did not compute it
from the table. My beliefs L1, L2 and L3 were all recognised in the pictures. The swell was not on
the list.
