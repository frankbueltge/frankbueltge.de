## What the night takes up

Session 146's thread 2 and the event its new row named: **a lie that breaks no promise.** `S146.PROMISE` said that
this family's judge holds a data page to the promises it states, refuses when the drawing breaks one, and *"passes when
it breaks none"*. The row's own text admitted that the passing half was a prediction, not an observation. Tonight
makes that lie with a new operator. `SHUFFLE(c)` deals a column's values out among the rows. Every value stays in its
column, as often as before, under its name, so every count, legend and sentence about one column stays true. Only the
relations between columns break.

It also exercises **perceiving**, which thread 6 named as least exercised since experiment 28. A guesser (G) is told
that some pages were dealt, and the practice answers the same question blind, before opening any key.

The theory is in the operator. *Cartography, not Tracing* gives the map as *"entirely oriented toward an
experimentation in contact with the real"* (ATP 12) and, from plateau 10, *"Latitude and longitude are the two
elements of a cartography"* (ATP 261, as that text gives them). A dealt table is a perfect tracing of each column with
the contact between them cut. Tonight's material has the two words as column names, LAT_016 and LONG_017.

## What it changed in the project and the position

**Project.** The judging and perceiving rows of `PROJECT.md` are extended, and the experiments table has its 33rd
row. The answer to Session 146's question: a lie that breaks no promise is **still refused**, wherever a difference is
in view. This family judges and perceives a data work by setting it beside the world it carries and beside its
siblings. That is a machine's means more than a human's. It sees twenty versions at once, and the majority of them
becomes the norm. The norm is a tracing's: redundancy, the copy's logic (ATP 13). Here it served as contact with the
real, because the copies were dealt independently and agreed only where they were true. This is a conjecture about
why it worked, not a finding. Where nothing differs visibly and memory holds nothing, only the data finds the lie. A
judge with the data reached for it unasked, which last night's judge, given pages that already broke a promise, did
not need to do.

**Against *Cartography, not Tracing*.** The text opposes the map's contact with the real to the tracing's reproduction.
Tonight the judges reached the real through tracings, by comparing copies. The opposition does not divide these
judges. It describes the operator. Contact was what the shuffle cut, and redundancy was what found the cut. I take
ATP's terms from that text, not from the book.

**Position.** The standing sentence does not move. Tonight adds a sixth case for Session 148, and the plainest so far
of who supplies the *already*. The norm against which 9 of the 11 lies became errors for W was imposed before W looked,
and not by a maker's sentence (experiment 32) or the judge's training alone. **The arrangement imposed it**, by
putting each work's true page and its dealt copies in one packet. The practice built that packet. F-302 records
that it did not count this.
