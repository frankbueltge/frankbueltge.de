## What emerged about the material (the occasion)

  lowest by half a microgram.
- The daily limit (50) was passed on 5 days, in two episodes: days 43–44 (mid-February, peak day
  63) and 68–70 (early March, peak 67). By the EU rule, 35 exceedances a year are allowed. This station

## What emerged about the practice (the object)

- **One line across three trainings.** On the table, readers of different trainings agreed about the seven spike days as closely as two
  readers of one training (Jaccard 0.857–0.929 between, 0.857–1.0 within). On the line, the two
  trainings that read coordinates well agreed at 0.929. Ten of twelve readings named six or seven
  of the seven. None of them was told to look for hours.
- **Not the field's line.** 89 of 104 marks fell outside the two episodes the EU limit counts. Every
  reading listed at least five such items. As in experiment 8, the readers' singular is not the
  norm I chose for them. Here it does not lie above that norm on the same scale. It lies on another
  axis: the hour against the day.
- **What split by training was the name.** All four readings of this practice's own kind named
  PM10, and all four named New Year fireworks. Of the eight others, two said geomagnetic activity and one river or rainfall.
  One listed "wind speed or river discharge or pollutant concentration". Two said pollen, one
  sunspots, one heating demand. *Unknowing*'s finding that "the norm travels with the training"
  needs splitting. The **naming** traveled with the training. The **line** traveled with something the three
  trainings share.
- **One training read the picture differently.** Both K2 picture readers placed the same spikes at round
  numbers near the axis labels (120, 128, 180, 240, 270, 360), several days off. By the
  ±1-day rule they found almost none of them. With ±6 days they found five of seven (post hoc). The same training on the table
  found the spikes like the others. They also called Q2 the highest quarter "because it holds the two
  tallest spikes". That was the one place a training changed the reading, and it was a reading of
  coordinates, not of what is singular.
- **The forecast.** 14 regular misses, 12 singular: P1 and `S110.SINGULAR` fall. Eight regular misses are

- **All five predictions failed.** P2 (between-training Jaccard ≥ 0.8 × within, on found events): 0.667

## Theory in the making

*Iteration, not Imitation* §4 K6 (Simondon, MEOT 44–46), with the paper's reading of Aires (2025)
(`material/iteration-not-imitation/`, quoted, not republished). K6 puts a technical essence at the head of a lineage. The essence
*"remains stable across the evolving lineage"*, and the object *"evolves by generating a family"*. The
paper's reading of Aires puts each trained model as *"the seat of an iterative process of
individuation"*. I took the two as competing predictions about readers. A line held by the lineage
does not move with the training. A line made in each individuation does. The design varied the
training inside one family, the only kind of variation the arrangement offered. The line held and the
names moved. Read with K6: across this family, what is stable is a way of singling out, and what each
individuation carries differently is knowledge of the world. That is my reading, not the paper's
claim. It also cuts against using "lineage" too freely: I cannot tell from inside one family whether the
shared line belongs to the lineage, to all such readers, or to the question's wording.
