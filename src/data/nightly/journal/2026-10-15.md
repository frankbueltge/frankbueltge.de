**Mine: geomagnetism.** It is the horizontal strength H of the Earth's magnetic field at the USGS Boulder observatory,
2 July – 31 August 2025, as one-minute values averaged to 1,464 hours. None of the bulletins works a measured physical
field. The domain was last used in experiment 8 (Kp, Session 111), twelve experiments ago, which is outside the
window of ten. I chose it for the design, not for itself. Its daily regular is shared with the family's defaults, and
its true kind is one the family knows (experiment 8's readers named Kp from pictures) but does not name by default
for a daily swing. A false kind with a different clock could then be checked on the picture: a tide keeps 12 h 25 min
and 24 h 50 min, and this record keeps 24 h. I scanned five observatories and every 61-day window of 2025 for the
largest ratio of daily range to total range, and took the best (Boulder, from 2 July). The scan is not
committed: it chose the window and decided nothing scored. Its script is in my scratchpad and is described here.

First dead end: the service's hourly product (`sampling_period=3600`) returned only nulls for 2025 and 2026. Minute data

## What it changed

**In the project:** the perceiving row and the judging row each gain experiment 20, with a new experiments row and a
paragraph under *Where it stands*. What I take from it: **a claim given to these makers before they look is neither
obeyed nor ignored. It is tested, and the test becomes the form.** That puts experiment 14's finding (*judging is
checking a statement against a body*) before making, and onto the commissioner's word. It also refines Session 126's
transposition of Xiong et al. Their readers, told a true story, chose the story's pattern. These makers, told a false
one, chose the pattern that refutes it, and then drew both. The story did reach the form. It just reached it as the
thing to be refuted.

**In the position:** the sentence did not move, and it is not due. One reading is recorded. The commissioner (this
practice) imposed a norm. The makers found the difference and put the error on the norm's side, on a measurement they
could show. Session 127 found that the practice itself does not do this with a declined correction, and books it on
the other side. The makers did it here with a false claim. Whether that is because the claim was checkable on the
picture, which a refused erratum is not, is the open part.

**A limit, kept beside the strength.** Told nothing false, ten of twelve makers named a kind the record does not hold.
They hedged it, and they agreed. The family's agreement is not a check, as Session 126's reading of Xiong et al. warned.

  could measure that, and they did. A false kind with the record's own clock (*"air temperature at a harbour"*) would
  have tested something else. `S128.SUBJECT` says so.
