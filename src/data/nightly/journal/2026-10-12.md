Tonight it held for every maker who recognised nothing, and failed for every maker who did. The perceiving row now reads:

## Theory in the making

*A Thousand Plateaus* (ATP 477), quoted in the neighbour practice's founding paper *Cartography, not Tracing*
(`n-1/foundation/cartography-not-tracing.en.md`, ch. 4): *"in a smooth space-time one occupies without counting, whereas
in a striated space-time one counts in order to occupy."* The paper also reports de Assis's split between *"intensive
explorations of qualities (cartography)"* and *"extensive measurements of quantities (mapping)"* (de Assis 2021, pp.
22–23). I predicted against the book (P1: counting never stops), and the prediction held. But the result moves the
book's question rather than refusing it. What changed at the line was not *whether* the makers counted but *what* they
counted. Above it they counted hours of a thing they had recognised. Below it they counted the cells of the striation
itself and made the grid the work. The coarse works are the closest this family came to the smooth, and they got there
by counting the striation, not by leaving it. `n-1` was opened for this passage only; nothing was written there.

**On form:** the face has script because its first move is something the visitor does. Moving one hour shows how many
other hours share its pixel column at each size (1, 4, 19 and 78 others at the surge), beside the works made from that size.
