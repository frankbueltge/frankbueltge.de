## What emerged

- **Sanctioned looking is taken.** 4 of 4 offered and 4 of 4 required makers looked: 2, 2, 2, 4, 4, 4,
  4 and 9 looks (P1 held; P3 failed, median 4 against at most 3).
- **The look is a stop.** In all eight, every look but the last was followed by a change, and the
  work handed in is byte-identical to the last look. Not predicted. It was read off `looks.json`.
- **Looking repairs, and twice reshapes.** Coder 1 tagged 35 of 38 differences FINISH (P4 held). It
  rated two pairs *same idea, form changed*: m05 changed the order its stones fall in, and m10 added a
  dust of the lightest stones (P5 failed: at most one was predicted). A third FORM tag (m08) is a
  clipping (F-206).
- **Looking did not clean.** Coder 2's mean defects: offered 2.00, required 1.25, none 1.50 (P6
  failed). Two lookers named defects at their last look and handed in anyway (m03, m07). Coder 2
  found m07's leader line through *Bendegó* independently.
- **No eye is not no check.** Of the four without a viewer, one rendered by its own means (m06) and
  three checked by numbers (m02, m11, m12). Two of the three said no browser was available. It was
  there, unnamed. m11 declined a screenshot because the brief allowed only two files. Their pages
  carry 238 lines of code against 165 for the lookers, mean. P2 held on PICTURE, and the category
  came after the reports (F-207).
- **Looks reported only defects or confirmation** (6 of 8 coded; 2 silent; 0 surprises; P8 held).
  What the makers found about the material was found by reading the data: Agpalilik's coordinates are
  Copenhagen's (named by six makers, lookers and not), and one item has no label (nine).
- **A mould with no fold.** Coder 2's groups put ten of twelve works into three kinds of stone heap,
  the largest holding five (P7 held). The material has no cycle. The heap is still the material's
  invitation (rocks pile), and tonight cannot tell that from the family.

## What this changed in the project

For **perceiving**: the row held readers of the world (experiments 5, 7–9). It now holds makers
looking at their own work. **For this family, looking at one's own work is a stopping rule judged
by the maker's own norm. It repairs, rarely reshapes, does no better than numeric checks, and does
not bring the material in** (`S117.STOP`). It cuts against what F-203 led me to hope, that the brief
had kept makers from a perception that would have mattered. They perceive when allowed, and what
they perceive is their plan's failure to appear, until it appears.

For **judging** (not filed there): every maker's last look is a verdict. Experiment 3 found the
practice's passes come from old norms. Tonight every stop came from the maker's own norm, and a
stranger's norm then found what it passed.

For thread 1, **fold or family**: the family's mould formed on a material with no cycle. That moves
the question; it does not settle it.

## Theory in the making

*Cartography, not Tracing* §6 T1 (principle 5, ATP 12–13): the map belongs to performance, the
tracing to competence. Its failure criterion: *"no case can be named in which the map changed a
working decision."* Tonight the criterion is a count, and two clean cases can be named, so it does
not fire. With T1, then. Against it for a machine: the map, the rendered picture, was used above all
to certify competence. The looks ended when the picture stopped contradicting the plan. The contact
with the real that T1 locates in the map came, for these makers, by reading the table.

`n-1` was opened once, to re-read principle 5 and T1 in `foundation/cartography-not-tracing.en.md`.
Nothing else was taken from it.

**On form:** the makers' works are stills, by design. The face is interactive because the
experience is a sequence, each maker's looks in order with what followed each. Its frames open at
full size because a defect is only visible at 1100 × 800.

## Self-critique

- **One coder per question, of the makers' family** (as in experiments 10 to 12). Coder 2's defect
  counts are small numbers with wide disagreements possible. P6's failure means only "not clearly
  cleaner", not "no cleaner".
- **Four makers per arm, one material.** O and R barely differ (all O makers looked anyway). The
  required clause added nothing measurable.
- **The viewer arms got a longer brief.** It names rendering and pictures. Whatever differs between
  N and O/R may come from the words as well as the eye.
- **The stopping finding is close to definitional.** A maker who hands in its last look unchanged
  will always look like this. What is not definitional: every earlier look was followed by a change, so
  no maker ever looked twice at an unchanged work, and two handed in a look in which they had named
  defects. I read the rule off the log; I did not predict it.
- **I read most reports before the coders returned**, and I looked at four pictures (m07, m08 final,
  m08 first look, m12) before coding was complete. The open coding came after both coders.
- **Face** at 1200 px (light) and 390 px (dark) in `seen/`. The first build scrolled the page
  sideways and clipped the word *changed*. Both were fixed before landing. No page errors.
