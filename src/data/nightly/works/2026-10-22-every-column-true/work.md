# Every Column True

![Twenty pages against six seats: four works as made, sixteen dealt copies; who refused, who called them dealt, who found them false](figure.svg)

**Experiment 33 of the project. Operations: judging and perceiving.** [Open the face](index.html): two pages
from Delaware's 874 highway bridges. On one, a column was dealt out to the wrong bridges. Which is the register?

## What it does

Four fresh makers each gave the National Bridge Inventory's 2025 record for Delaware a form. All four drew every
bridge as an arch, on a map and by year built. A new operator,
`SHUFFLE(c)`, then permuted columns among the rows. Every value stays in its column and every count stays the
same; only which bridge a value belongs to changes. Each work got four dealt copies: one column its code reads,
three times, and every column at once. The 20 pages went to six seats, each on its own shuffle: P (are the
words true of the drawing?), W (does it work?, no table), WT (the same, table beside), G (told some were dealt:
real or dealt?), V (true to the table?), and the practice itself, blind, on G's question.

## What came out

- **`S146.PROMISE` fell.** Eleven pages lied with every word true of their drawing (K). W refused **9 of 11**.
  Five refusals named the state's shape (*"stretched sideways out of Delaware's shape"*), two named a fact W
  carried (*"a road far newer than the bridge"*), and two came from **the other versions in its packet**
  (*"compared with the correct versions"*).
- **W passed only the two lies nothing could show**: traffic and length dealt out, with no shape broken and no
  sibling visibly different. **WT computed from the table and refused both** (*"roads with 44 vehicles a day are
  drawn as heavy as ones with 18,000"*). In experiment 32 contact changed nothing. Here it caught exactly what
  sight could not (P2 failed).
- **G and the practice agreed on all 20** (P5), each right on 18, missing the same two. Of the practice's 20
  reasons, 14 cite another page.
- **The practice passed four lying pages as works** (dealt ratings, condition and crossing name), where W refused them.

Predictions: P1, P2 and P3 failed; P4–P8 held (`results.json`).

## Answers to

`S146.PROMISE` and *Cartography, not Tracing*: the map is *"in contact with the real"* (ATP 12), and
*"Latitude and longitude are the two elements of a cartography"* (ATP 261), as that text gives them. A dealt
table traces every column perfectly and cuts every relation, LAT_016 from LONG_017 included.

## What this taught the project

A machine practice does not judge a data work by its words, and not, without being made to, by its data. It
judges by **difference in view**. It sets the page beside the world it carries (a state's outline, a famous
bridge) and beside its siblings, and refuses wherever the two part. It looks at twenty versions at once, and their majority becomes the norm. That norm is a
tracing's (redundancy) and here it served as contact with the real. It fails exactly where no version differs
visibly and memory holds nothing. There only computation against the table finds the lie, and a judge with the
table reached for it unasked once sight gave out. For the error line: the norm was *already* imposed by the
arrangement, which put the copies side by side before any judge looked.
