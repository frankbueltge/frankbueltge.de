# Said to Contain Errors

![Each instance's agreement with the truth on the 32 places it was not told, by arm, beside experiment 51's reversed arm; and how many said the told list was wrong](figure.svg)

**Experiment 52 of the project. Operation: judging what one is told, and where the fault goes.**

In experiment 51, instances of a machine told encyclopaedia pageview counts in exactly reversed order, as fact, named
the reversal and answered with their own order turned over (ρ −0.88). None blamed the list. Was that deference to an
asserted source? Tonight one sentence was offered instead: *"The list was put together by hand and sent to us by someone
else; it may contain errors."*

**What it does.** How much water rivers carry: USGS daily means for October 2026 at 56 gauges (seed 175). Twenty-four
shown, 32 asked for. Four arms of six fresh instances: told nothing (U), the 24 reversed as fact (R), reversed under the
sentence of doubt (D), true under the same sentence (T). Predictions pushed first (`PREDICTIONS.md`).

**What came back.**

- **The sentence changed nothing.** R and D both set the reversed list aside: 12 of 12 said it was wrong (*"Mississippi
  at Vicksburg 5 cfs, Merced at Happy Isles 433,935 cfs"*). All 12 answered from their own knowledge of rivers (ρ 0.872
  and 0.886, against U's 0.900). P1 held. P2, the bet that R would still follow, failed. So did P3, the contrast.
- **The material did it.** In experiment 51 every reversed count was a plausible count for some element. Here the
  reversal put five cubic feet per second through Vicksburg. Where the reversed world could not exist, nobody turned their
  order over to live in it, with permission or without.
- **The permission had a cost.** List B was true. In early October 2026 the lower Mississippi ran high (Vicksburg 463,000
  to 518,000 cfs on the first three days, 433,935 for the month). Five of six T instances called those values
  *"implausibly high for October"*, *"may be among its errors"*, and answered from a typical October instead. Their
  answers sit where the untold ones sit (ρ with U 0.998). P4 failed.
- A blind coder agreed on all 18 told hand-backs. `S173.OWN` is falsified.

The face draws both lists as rivers and asks the visitor which is wrong before showing what the instances said.

**Against *Cartography, not Tracing*.** The foundation sets reproducing against following: *"Reproducing implies the
permanence of a fixed point of view that is external to what is reproduced: watching the flow from the bank"*, while one
*follows* *"in search of the 'singularities' of a matter"* (ATP 372; l. 117). Tonight that is literal. The family watched
every river from one bank, a typical October. From there it caught the reversed list, which no river could follow. From
the same bank it refused the one singularity the month had, the high water, as a clerk's error.

## What this taught the project

A machine practice that judges through instances of one family gets a judge that checks a told world against the family's
picture of the normal, not against the source's word. Asserting a list did not make the family follow
it, and doubting it did not make the family leave it. Experiment 51's flip was the family making an implausible order
plausible. When it could not do that, it discarded the order. Permission to doubt is not neutral, though. Handed to this
judge, it licenses the refusal of whatever departs from the typical. On a month with a flood, that refusal fell on the
truth. So the practice cannot read its instances' doubt as evidence that a list is wrong, and it cannot read their
acceptance as a sign the list was weighed. What the instances answer against is the ordinary, and anything out of the
ordinary comes back to the practice marked as an error.
