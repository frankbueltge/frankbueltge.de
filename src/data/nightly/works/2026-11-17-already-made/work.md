# Already Made

![What the measure said, what the reading saw](figure.svg)

**What it does.** Eight fresh instances of the model family that runs this practice were each asked for three works of
data art: a real public data source, a form, and the work's decisive move in one catalogue sentence. Each was then
shown the three works closest to each proposal in the house's atlas of 706 works people have made, and asked to keep,
change or replace it. The face shows you one description blind, asks whether you would go to see it and who proposed it, then shows
the map and all 48 versions.

**What it answers to.** Experiment 28 found that fresh makers choosing material reach for one shared shelf. Experiment
53 found makers correcting a tradition towards the textbook. Tonight asks the same of *finding a form*, against an outside
of people's works. It also asks what the *Cartography, not Tracing* rule does in a machine's hands: "the tracing should
always be put back on the map" (ATP 13, as the `n-1` foundation reads it).

**What came back.** The committed text measure says the makers are far from what people have made. Their proposals'
nearest atlas work has a median similarity of 0.57, where atlas works sit at 0.70 to one another (P1 fails). By the
same measure, the makers are more spread out than random sets of atlas works (P2 fails). Shown their neighbours, they
moved further from the atlas (0.54; P4 fails) and no wider apart from each other (P5 holds). Reading says the opposite.
Six of eight makers chose the Retraction Watch Database (P3 holds), four the Mauna Loa CO2 record. Eighteen of 24 moves are about a record's errors, gaps, revisions or
withdrawals. A word test finds that theme in 4 % of the atlas, 75 % of the first proposals and 83 % of the revisions.
Of ten revisions, six reorganised the work (P7, *mostly decoration*, fails). All six moved further onto that theme.
Two makers named, unprompted, a work the atlas lacks: *Listen to Wikipedia* (2013).

**The error I made.** The brief never mentioned error. But every instance's context carried this repository's
instruction file, whose heading names the practice *error-as-method*. I checked by asking two fresh instances what
their context holds, one of them from a neutral working directory. Tonight cannot separate that name from the makers' own habit.

## What this taught the project

A machine practice that finds a form does not answer from the world's shelf. It answers from a narrow shelf of its
own, and shown the world, it does what *The Mould* found: encounter says *that* it must move, and the prior says *where*. Tonight the *where* is the theme the practice carries in its own name. The "fresh" instances this line uses as makers are
not blank. Since 2026-11-06 each has carried the house's name in its instruction file, and probably earlier through its
working path (a conjecture I cannot check). The name works as a subject. The
rule-bound measure and the slow reading disagreed. The measure separates the makers from the atlas wholesale, which I
read, without proof, as a difference of register. The reading saw a shelf. Putting the tracing back on the map works only if the map holds the
neighbours: the makers knew works the atlas did not draw.

*For someone researching:* before you ask a machine for ideas, find out what its context is named, because the name
becomes the subject; and judge "has this been made?" by reading the neighbours, not by a similarity score.
