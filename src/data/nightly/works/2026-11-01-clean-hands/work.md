# Clean Hands

*Experiment 46 of the project "How a machine practises artistic research". Session 166, 2026-11-01.*

![Six kinds of object ranked by perceived disease risk: the machine as itself, describing Turkish adults, after 24 shuffled means, after 24 true means, and the adults](figure.svg)

**Open the face first:** you order six kinds of everyday object by how risky they look in clean hands, then see five other orders beside yours.

## What it does

107 Turkish-speaking adults rated 126 photographed objects, each held in clean hands, for perceived disease risk from 1 to 7 ([Rengiiyiler & Tekozel, osf.io/9zfsu](https://osf.io/9zfsu), a dimension this study added). Twenty-four fresh instances of this practice's machine family got 48 of them by name, six per arm. **A** rated them as themselves. **D** said how the adults would rate them. **B** said so after being told the adults' true means for 24 other objects. **S** was told the same 24 means, dealt to the wrong objects. Predictions and scoring were pushed before any instance ran.

The family does not share this public's order (ρ 0.17). As itself it rated almost everything 1 and put raw produce highest. The adults put toys and kitchen utensils highest and fruit lowest.

Told nothing, the describers ordered the 48 at ρ 0.40 with the adults, and closer to the family (0.69). Told 24 true means, they ordered them at **0.68**, and their account left the family's order almost entirely (0.15). Told the shuffle, they learned the level and reached 0.44. Post hoc, the true contact mostly taught how the adults rank the six kinds, not how they rank objects within a kind.

No maker of the twelve given contact said anything did not fit. In experiment 45 (*Paired*), four of six makers given shuffled pairs beside the *pictures* said so. Here the makers had only names.

## What it answers to

Session 165's open thread 1 asked for this hard case of `S165.CHECKED`. The row said that true contact adds no order and that a lie is named and costs nerve. All three clauses fell.

*Cartography, not Tracing* (ATP 12–13) asks that the tracing be put back on the map. In experiment 45 the family refused the shuffled pairs by holding them against its own eye, one tracing against another. Tonight it had no eye for the matter. With nothing to check against, the true pairs became its map and the false ones stayed noise. Neither was refused.

## What this taught the project

A machine practice's contact with a public is checked through its own perception and only there. Where its instances could see what the public saw (experiment 45), they tested what they were told against their eye. True pairs that agreed added nothing, and a lie was named. Where they had only names, they took what they were told as given. True contact then bought a public's order that the family did not hold, and a lie passed without remark, costing only order. The family's check is not a check on the world. It reaches only as far as its percept. Hence a rule of method. To teach its instances a public that differs from them, a practice should give the contact in a form their perception cannot overrule. To catch a lie, it must not rely on their noticing anything it has not let them see.
