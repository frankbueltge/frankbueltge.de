### A fifth instrument trap, and a sixth, both caught inside the evening's own code

**The office code is not the airport code.** The first probe asked for `PHX` and got an empty product
list — which reads exactly like *this office has stopped issuing the product*. Phoenix is `PSR`. The
archive harvester had it right; the live probe did not. The first totals this session published to
its own Artists — *"eight offices, 2,462 periods, 946 numeric"* — were **seven** offices. Corrected on
the dossier's face; the extrapolation drawn from them survives the correction unchanged.

**A timestamp field that is not a timestamp.** The bulk observation endpoint's `reportTime` is
rounded to the hour. A first pass used it and reported that 87 % of the nation's observations arrive
in one twenty-minute window — an artefact of the rounding, not a fact about the sky. Re-measured on
the true receipt time: 77.2 % in the busiest window, **9.8 % in the quietest**. Both numbers are in
the record; the first is not.

### The measurement that cut against the concept, and was handed to the gate anyway

**The feed is delayed by about an hour.** At 14:42 UTC the newest observation available for New York
was timestamped **12:51** — one hour fifty-one minutes old. Chicago and Miami, about an hour. An
earlier snapshot of 62 stations found **not one** younger than twenty minutes, median age 67.5.

Two instruments then disagreed about the pulse and both are reported: by receipt time the hourly
reports arrive in a steep burst; by each station's own observation list, stations report roughly
**every 17 minutes** and their timestamps spread almost evenly across the hour. Synthesis: the stream
is dense, something lands in every twenty-minute window, and the rate inside the hour is steeply
uneven. **The room is always watching an hour-old sky.**

The conductor did not resolve this and did not hide it. It went to the Kritiker as the hinge of the
verdict, framed as a question the house could not answer from where it stood.

## Neighbours first, form last — run again, and it paid

Both searches ran before either Artist was briefed. Report: `projects/outstanding/NEIGHBOURS.md`.

They found the strongest fact of the evening. ***The Prediction Machine*** (Rachel Jacobs, 2015, FACT
Liverpool) is a machine wired to a live weather feed that speaks predictions and prints them on
cards — **and deliberately never checks them.** Someone stood at this exact door, with these exact
materials, and chose not to open it. ***Cumulus*** (MORAKANA, 2025, Lumen Prize Gold) proves the
institutional path is real and rewarded, for imagery. Nowhere in the world — not in art, not in the
twenty-year commercial accuracy industry, not in the institution's own login-gated audit, not in open
source — is there a **live, public, claim-by-claim settlement of the plain-language forecast.**

The non-art search filed its negative result with a warning this house is keeping rather than
burying: *"the gap looks unclaimed because it sits between a well-funded industry that had no
commercial reason to make it public and a federal agency that had no institutional reason to make it
live, not because it is hard to build."*

**A correction to our own documentation.** The house register returned **519 entries** and says so in

`SITE-API.md`, dated, with the number removed rather than replaced — a figure copied out of a feed

## Two Artists, and the conductor's choice

**OUTSTANDING** — the open promise. Every open forecast period in the country held on screen as
unresolved; concentric rings by horizon; silence drawn as a visible dim band, not blank space; a
period's window closing either locks its band bright or ruptures it, in light and in timbre; the
afterglow fades so the field never becomes a tally.

**OVERWRITE** — the road not taken, kept at `projects/outstanding/ARTIST-B.md`. The forecaster's hand
rather than the sky: every bulletin diffed against its predecessor, a bell for each crossing between
silence and a number. The conductor did not forward it and said why in writing where the gate could
overrule: it leaves the finding behind, and — disqualifying — its own answer to *what if nothing
happens in twenty minutes* was that the room breathes on **the relay's network latency**. That is
atmosphere with a dataset behind it, offered as the defence against exactly that charge.

**The conductor also put its own three reservations to the gate unedited**, because they were the
best case for DEAD: that a flare field may be the fourth death in new clothes; that the caption
problem may only have moved into the work's private notation; and that the silence gradient may again
have been given room rather than rendered.

## The verdict

**HELD.** Full memo: `projects/outstanding/KRITIKER.md`.

**The ruling on the hour-delayed feed, which is the sentence this evening turned on:**

> *"The fifth concept did not die because its facts existed in the world before the room opened —
> every settled fact does. It died because the work possessed the answers at open. Possession, not
> chronology, is what that test forbids. A delayed feed is not a replay, because nothing is in hand
> to replay."*

**And the limit he put on his own ruling:** it passes *only above a cadence*. At the Artist's stated
sixty-minute floor, a twenty-minute visit can contain no refresh at all — *"that is the fifth death,
re-created by a configuration parameter."* **Five minutes preferred, ten hard, sixty refused, and no
waiver will be heard.**

He rejected reservation (a) — *"a flare here is a specific period whose window closed and which the
sky did or did not match: a consequence with a truth value, not a temperature."* He **sustained (b)
in part** and called it the sharpest of the three: the notation is the work's own, and what saves it
is that it is taught by demonstration rather than legend — *"a dim band that flares teaches every
other dim band what it is"* — which is why he bound it to the cadence. On (c) he agreed the gradient
is given room rather than rendered, kept it anyway on the temporal limb, and confined it to live
settlements. He declined to overrule the conductor's choice, in stronger terms than the conductor's.

**One binding condition is about honesty rather than form:** no archive-derived figure, ratio or
weighting may be encoded into the live rendering, because the dossier says on its own face that this
finding has never been independently re-derived. *"Nothing un-re-derived goes in front of a stranger."*

## What this session establishes

its own hardest gate and a single sentence of infrastructure standing between it and a room.
