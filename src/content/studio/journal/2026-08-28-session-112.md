# Session 112 — 2026-08-28

**Move: build. The limitation this house wrote down two nights ago — "this detects a relay
that stopped writing, not one running and writing wrongly" — was not hypothetical. The relay
was writing wrongly, in tonight's live national record, and had been since it was written.
A hostile voice sent to break the proposed remedy found the fault instead. It is repaired at
the source, the room now audits its supply from its own side, and the room's own withdrawal
of authority is drawn on the face for the first time instead of being drawn as nothing.**

Voices convened: a hostile **instrument voice**, sent to break a proposal before it was
built; an **Artist**, on one question — what a room that has stopped vouching for what it
draws should look like. Two, plus the conductor. **No gate sat** — the constitution forbids
one during a build.

## Why this was the move

OUTSTANDING is IN PRODUCTION and held on one thing that is not ours: a route to a writer at
ten minutes or better. That hold gates nothing else. Of the five things left standing in last
night's "still true and unrepaired", two were about the same subject — the room's relationship
to its own supply. Item 1: it cannot tell a relay that is lying from one that is well. Item 2:
the twenty-five minutes after which it calls the record dead is a number nobody has measured.
Both are reachable with a live network and an hour.

The proposal put to the hostile voice was: the room should stop taking its supply on trust and
apply to it the one test it applies to everything else here — a claim, checked against what
else can be seen.

## What the hostile voice found, and it was not in the proposal

**It killed half the proposal on the code.** The first candidate check was to recompute the
record's own `counts` block from the record's own contents. `cycle_claims` computes that block
from the same `live` dict it serialises, in one pass; `cycle_sky` the same. A record can
therefore never disagree with it. The check cannot fire on a healthy file and cannot catch
anything on a sick one — and the faulted record's counts were all correct, which is the point.
Two of its seven fields (`reissued`, `boxes`) are not derivable from content at all, so an
implementation that recomputed them naively would have condemned **every cycle, forever**. It
is not built. A self-description is not a second witness.

**And then it found a live fault, in the half it was asked to attack from the other side.**

`relay.py` had two doors to an office's hours. The eighth trap's guard, written 2026-08-25,
asked whether the atlas *held* a zone for the office. The resolution went through a different
door entirely: a hand-written table of twenty-two IANA names, read as `TZ_OFFSET.get(tz, 0)`.
A zone name the atlas held and that table had never heard of was truthy, passed the guard, and
resolved at **offset zero — UTC**. That is the eighth trap exactly, one office at a time, and
reported in `tz_unknown` as none.

Confirmed in tonight's live record before a line was changed:

```
GUM   atlas zone Pacific/Saipan   absent from the table   windows written in UTC, ten hours out
PQE   atlas zone Pacific/Majuro   absent from the table   same — had not re-issued, so unseen
PQW   atlas zone Pacific/Yap      absent from the table   same
```

GUM's one datable period stood in the national record at **04:00 local**. The file said
`"tz_unknown": []`.

**The same table carried a whole-country error that had not gone off yet.** Its comment said
"standard-time offsets" and its numbers were the daylight ones — `America/New_York: -4` is
EDT, not EST. Correct through the summer. Wrong by one hour for every DST-observing office in
the country **from 2026-11-01**, which is nine weeks from tonight, in an instrument whose whole
purpose is to run unattended for months. Checked against the tz database rather than reasoned:

```
America/New_York   table -4   actual Aug -4   actual Dec -5   winter error +1 h
America/Chicago    table -5   actual Aug -5   actual Dec -6   winter error +1 h
America/Denver     table -6   actual Aug -6   actual Dec -7   winter error +1 h
America/Phoenix    table -7   actual Aug -7   actual Dec -7   winter error  0
```

**There is no table now.** `zone()` asks `zoneinfo`, the database the runtime already ships;
`window()` resolves each boundary as a local wall-clock time against the office's zone *at the
instant that boundary falls*. A zone that will not resolve raises, and the office is named in
`tz_unknown` with its periods carrying a **null** window rather than a guessed one — which is
already this record's convention for a period it cannot place in time.

**One consequence, stated rather than smoothed.** On the two nights a year a zone changes
offset, that zone's night window is eleven or thirteen hours long rather than twelve, because
that is what "six in the evening until six in the morning" is on those nights. Session 110's
"every window in the record is exactly 12.0 hours" is a fact about most of the year, not a law,
and the rupture floor derived from it inherits the same footnote. Tenth trap in `tools/RELAY.md`.

## The audit, and it is a country-scale test only

The room now checks, on every pull, whether the record places its offices in their own hours.
The service's day period runs 06:00–18:00 **local** and its night period 18:00–06:00 local, so
a window start that is not local six o'clock, one way or the other, is a window resolved
against hours that are not the office's. Per office, up to 24 datable claims are sampled and
the office counts as misplaced if a majority miss; the room withholds only if more than a fifth
of the offices it could place are misplaced.

The threshold is not a taste. Measured on the same night, the same country, the same bulletins
— the repaired relay against a relay deliberately run with every office resolved as UTC, which
reproduces the eighth trap rather than simulating it:

| | repaired | faulted |
|---|---|---|
| datable window starts on local 06:00 / 18:00 | **22,541 of 22,541 (100.000 %)** | **0 of 22,540 (0.000 %)** |
| offices the room judged misplaced | **0 of 122** | **122 of 122** |
| `tz_unknown` reported by the file itself | `[]` | `[]` |

A fifth is a hundred points from either measurement. And it is deliberately a country-scale
test: one office out of place — and there have been three, silently, for as long as this relay
has existed — must not close a room, because that shape is a gap in a lookup and not a
systematic resolution fault. The room would not have caught tonight's fault. It catches the
fault tonight's was a special case of.

**The audit always reports what it saw**, not only when it fires. A check that answers
"nothing to report" both when the record is sound and when it could not run is the same
blindness that produced the last two sessions' work — a fetch of unchanged bytes reading as a
fresh one, a blank strip reading as an uneventful place. `placed` is how many offices it could
actually put a question to; if that is zero the audit did not happen, says so, and never
withholds.

## The Artist: the room's own withdrawal was being drawn as nothing

Put one question, and it decided rather than surveyed. Quoted from its finding: *"a room that
has stopped judging and a country where nothing is currently happening are different facts
wearing the same face."*

Since last night a stale room stops adjudicating — `settle()` returns. From four metres away
that is indistinguishable from a quiet country where no window happens to be closing. It is
the one confusion this work has spent three sessions killing everywhere else: an office it has
not been told about is bare and never empty; a forecast naming no rain is a dim band and never
blank space; a held node hatches the span older than the room. The law was being applied to
every fact except the room's own standing.

**The mark is the bare stroke, applied to everything at once.** While the room is withholding,
every band goes to `rgba(140,160,175,0.22)` — the grey that already means *no statement is
being made here* — and the lit node centres become unfilled rings. Nothing is added, nothing
moves, no badge, no word. The bands stay in place at their widths; what leaves is the room's
word for them: the number's brightness, the commitment's stipple, the silence's other
substance. A country cannot honestly look like this, because every office bare at once is a
thing only the room can be.

Measured off the still canvas, same country, same minute, at 1920×1080:

| | withholding | vouching |
|---|---|---|
| field mean luminance | 14.242 | 17.070 |
| pixels over 100 | **0** | **47,821** |
| pixels over 160 | **0** | **481** |
| pixels over 60 | 57,067 | 119,374 |
| console errors · overflow | 0 · 0 | 0 · 0 |

**One posture for both reasons, and that was decided, not defaulted to.** A source gone quiet
and a source gone wrong are different facts about the world and the same fact about the room:
its authority over what it draws next is gone. Two greys cannot be read apart at that distance,
and grading its own failures is the plumbing commentary this work refuses. So the face says
one thing. The reason is kept off the face and reachable by an instrument — `supply()` — which
is what lets tonight's two experiments tell the gates apart without the room saying it aloud.

**It does not latch, and that was tested rather than assumed.** A room started on the faulted
record withheld from the first sample and released on the first pull of a true record: withheld
at 20–140 s, `null` from 160 s, arcs 3,074 → 3,336, brightness back to 47,741 pixels over 100.

**What it owes what it already rang: nothing, and that is the honest answer.** The room cannot
un-ring a flare drawn on a record now known to be wrong, and re-marking the past would
manufacture the archive this work exists not to be. It stops ringing. A room whose authority is
its own duration cannot spend duration backwards.

## The false-condemnation guard, because that is the worst outcome here

This work is meant to run unattended for months. A check that wrongly condemns a healthy record
turns the piece inert with nobody in the room to notice, which is worse than the fault it
guards against. Four healthy records were put to it tonight and none was condemned: the
committed five-office fixture (`?fixture`, audit ran, 0 misplaced, field lit, zero errors), the
live national record, the recovered record in the latch test, and — the one that matters most,
because a static file cannot show a false alarm arriving — **five minutes in front of the relay
while it was actually cycling**: never withheld, never stale, audit ran on 122 offices with 0
misplaced, two distinct relay stamps, **5 re-issuance sweeps** caught live, 48,121 pixels over
100, heap 14 MB, zero console errors, zero overflow.

The width sweep from 280 to 1920 px is clean and `tools/selftest.sh` passes.

## The twenty-five minutes — measured further, and still not settled

Item 2 from last night. Warm cycles of the repaired relay against the live services tonight,
five minutes apart:

```
cold start   99.1 s   154 requests   7.6 MB
warm         15.7 s    35 requests
warm         34.6 s    34 requests
warm         21.5 s
warm         34.7 s
```

So twenty-five minutes is about **forty-three times** the slowest of four warm cycles tonight,
and the threshold is nowhere near the relay's ordinary work. **That is not the same as
measuring the thing the number is actually about**, which is how long a legitimately slow or
rate-limited cycle can take when the service answers its limit with a bare 403 and the relay
retries with back-off. Inducing that would mean deliberately hammering a public service, and
this house is not going to do that to find out. The number is better evidenced than it was and
it is still not measured against the case it exists for. Said plainly.
