## Why this was the move

OUTSTANDING is held on a hosting decision that is Frank's and has been open seven days. Of

## The strongest thing that happened, and it was not predicted

Cohort A's readers, cold, without labels, a legend, a colour key or a single tone, **tracked
what the room actually decided**:

- All three place the sequence's first change at **frame 10** and name the new light's
  position as the **Pacific Northwest**. The room's instant α fired **SEW — Seattle** — and
  BIS, immediately before frame 10.
- Reader 3 names **frames 16–19 as "an apparent lull", no strongly highlighted node**. The
  room's own log: **fading 0 at frames 16, 17, 18 and 19.** The lull is real and is exactly
  four frames long.
- All three name **frame 20 as a sudden multi-site burst**, and reader 3 counts **five
  simultaneous highlights**. The room fired **five offices** at that instant.
- Reader 2 places one of frame 20's lights **"further east, around Tennessee/Kentucky",
  differently shaped from the rest**. That is **HUN — Huntsville, Alabama — with three bands**,
  the smallest signature in the instant, and the only one east of the Plains.

This is the machine advantage doing exactly what §2 of the concept claims for it, observed
from outside: hundreds of independently live places, and a stranger can see which ones the
sky has just answered without being told anything at all.

**And one thing they got wrong, which is ours.** All three read the *static* bright node in
frames 6–9 as ongoing activity. Nothing was fading there. In stills, a bright standing claim
and a decaying verdict are the same object; in the room, motion and sound separate them.

## The finding that cuts, and its bounds

**Three of three trough readers could not tell a live room from a still picture.** Inside their
ten minutes the room re-issued ten times and its drawn arcs moved twice, by 89 and then by 6.
Reader
1: *"the screen looks static throughout."* Reader 3: *"I can't tell that anything changed."*
Reader 2 went furthest and named the alternative outright: *"or the screen was simply
static/paused for the entire ten minutes."*

That is the exact confusion session 112 spent an entire session killing at the other end — *a
room that has stopped judging and a country where nothing is happening are different facts
wearing the same face* — returning at visit scale. The withholding posture makes a **still**
distinction and works: a withheld field and a vouching field are different pictures. A live
trough and a frozen screen are **not** different pictures. Everything that separates them in
this work lives in animation under two seconds: a 1-pixel heartbeat line at 8 % opacity for
900 ms, a sweep that crosses a node in 1.6 s, a ring that blooms in 2.2 s.

**The bound, stated rather than smoothed:** the panel could not carry motion, so it does not
establish that a visitor standing in the room during a trough sees a dead screen. It
establishes that the still field alone does not carry proof of life. **The Artist's decision of
2026-08-29 — that the trough owes nothing — is not overturned by this and is not confirmed by
it.** The question it leaves is now well-formed and measurable, and it is the next thing this
work owes itself: *at the room's real viewing distance, is the heartbeat legible?* A 1 px line
at 8 % opacity on a projection at four metres is a measurement, not an opinion, and no session
has made it.

## What this establishes about how the work can ship

The concept's §6 says a stranger *"without reading anything, understands two things at once"*.
For the still channel, that is now evidenced false, by six readers, with the prediction on
record beforehand. §4 already said why: *the sound is what lets the piece work at the room's
real viewing distance with no text*. Tonight makes that a **load-bearing dependency instead of
an assumption** — and therefore a condition on installation, not a preference. A venue that
mutes this work is not showing a quieter version of it; it is showing the version six severed
readers called a network operations dashboard.

It is also a condition on this house: **the ship gate for this work cannot be run on stills.**
Any panel that decides whether OUTSTANDING ships has to carry sound and motion to its readers,
and building that is a session's work in itself.
