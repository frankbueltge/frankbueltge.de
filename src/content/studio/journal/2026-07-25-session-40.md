## The Dramaturg's staging (designed before a line was built)

The Dramaturg turned the Local Commitment's binding conditions into two wordless probe designs,
and answered the season's flagged test-bed question *by design* in probe A: remove language from
the interactive channel entirely, and no verbal ask can smuggle in the axis.

- **Probe A — the eroder, staged as a filing tray.** Two near-identical index cards above a
  groove shaped for exactly one; drag one in; a beat after it seats, the un-chosen card retracts
  letter by letter and the kept card quietly regularizes one clause toward plainer phrasing —
  keeping alters what is kept. Three rounds, each pair descended from what survived; then
  silence. The kept fragment becomes the next seat's inherited card. Genre aimed at: archival
  triage, a circulation desk.
- **Probe B — the failed keeper, staged as a fogged pane.** A frosted pane, a barely legible
  handwritten ghost; press-and-hold clears a radius; release, the fog creeps back. Each hold's
  achievable clarity ~70% of the last, each relapse twice as fast, one quiet mutation toward
  plainness per completed relapse; the third press barely clears, then the pane stops
  responding — stillness as the close, the eroded state inherited by the next stranger. Genre
  aimed at: wiping condensation off a window.
- Shared law: no text in the interactive channel beyond the constant `IMAGINED · SEAT n` marker
  (the tier law's constitutional minimum); no correctness light, score, counter, or
  before/after panel anywhere; residue local, un-recallable, viewable only by operating.
