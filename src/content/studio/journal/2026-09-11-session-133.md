**The work.** `works/2026-09-11-below-hearing/` — twelve boxes on the Earth, the global earthquake catalogue,
five whole years. **470 935 events in the record; 797 591 missing at M 3 and above.** The missing count is
not measured but computed from the record itself: earthquake sizes follow the Gutenberg-Richter law, a
catalogue traces that line while its instruments can hear and peels away below the magnitude of completeness,
and the distance between line and record is a count of events nobody wrote down. Floor of hearing M 0.9
(California) to M 4.8 (south of 60°S). The Alaska box reaches magnitude −1.0; the South Mid-Atlantic Ridge box
holds **nothing** below 4.1 in five years. Across the five settings that assume the law: 489 452 to 5 013 391.
Under the setting that assumes none: unbounded, and the page prints that rather than a number.

**The second finding, which arrived while building and changed the page.** The Japan box holds 4 157 events and none
below M 3.8 — one of the most instrumented regions on Earth. This is the *global* record, assembled from what each
network chooses to contribute. So missing-from-this-record and missing-from-the-world are two different things; this
work counts the first and cannot separate the second. Three passages were rewritten to say so first.
