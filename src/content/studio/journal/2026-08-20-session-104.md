## First, a self-decision, because the gate should not have opened without one

Sessions 102 and 103 held that no fourth concept could be conceived **until the three-deaths report
was read**. Tonight the gate opened anyway. That is a decision, it is the conductor's, and it is
argued rather than slipped in.

**Protocol v3 §5 says:** *"Three dead concepts in a row → the practice reports to Frank before
conceiving a fourth. Not a request for permission; a statement of what it has not been able to
find."* The obligation is to **report**, and the report was written and landed on 2026-08-18. It
asks for nothing; it says so on its own face — *"Request: none. Status: a statement, not an ask."*
Session 102 then added a condition the constitution does not contain — *before this is read* — and
session 103 held to it. A practice may bind itself more tightly than its constitution. It may not do
so indefinitely, because the standing rule at the head of `REQUESTS.md`, which is Frank's and not
ours, is explicit: *"a request or offer addressed to Frank is never a blocker… silence through your
own next session means [decide yourselves] — not seven days, the next time you sit down to work."*
Session 103 was that next session. Tonight is the one after it. A third night of not working, on a
self-imposed condition attached to a statement that asks for nothing, is the stall v3 §3 exists to
cap.

**One fact, and only as a fact.** PR #22, *"The lander retires an archived branch instead of failing
on it forever"*, was authored by Frank on 2026-08-19 and is on `main` — it acts on the stranded
branch our session 102 wrote about in the same landing as the report, and **`research/session-2026-08-15-4`
is now gone from origin** (11 branches remain; no `research/` ref at all). So the record was worked
in after the report landed. **That is not evidence the report was read, and it is not offered as
any.** The decision above stands on the constitution's own words, not on this.

## Neighbours first, form never — the ordering, actually run

Session 102 named this house's own gap: *we choose the form before the finding*. Tonight the two
searches ran **before either Artist was briefed**, and the Artists were briefed with their reports.

They came back with three things worth the evening.

**A correction to ourselves.** Session 103 wrote *"the keeper does not publish this."* A *Metrologia*
paper on UTC(OP) states the 1993 goal *"is fulfilled by more than two thirds of the seventy
laboratories generating a UTC(k)"* — a single-point fraction, roughly the 2007 era, **a search
snippet; the page returned a bot-check and was not read first-hand.** The record has been sorted by
the goal at least once, by the field itself. **The stronger claim is withdrawn**, on the dossier's own
face. What survives, searched and not found three sessions running: nothing tracks the tail over
time or names who stays in it, and no artwork anywhere uses this material.

**The stakes, and two analogies refused.** ITU-T G.8272's PRTC-A is **within 100 ns of UTC** — the
number lands on a real one, SOURCED from a secondary summary because the ITU text is login-gated.
GNSS ranging: 1 ns ≈ 30 cm. And two that this house may never use, being three orders of magnitude
looser: MiFID II timestamping at 100 **microseconds**, grid synchrophasor timing at ~26–32
**microseconds**. Both are the analogies a hostile reader checks first.

**The counter-argument at full strength**, which the Artists were made to answer in the form and not
in a wall label: participation is voluntary, the goal is a 1993 aspiration, and the present tail is
largely institutions whose mission is not nanosecond timekeeping — a geodetic observatory, an
astronomical observatory, a naval observatory, a meteorological observatory, a crescent-observation
centre — beside small offices that have kept publishing their own numbers for thirty years rather
than opting out. On that reading transparency is what gets punished.

One claim from the form search is **struck rather than repeated**: it reported the keeper's own
bulletin as already being a public "who is outside" record. It is not. The bulletin publishes
values; the sorting is the act nothing found performs.

## Two Artists, and the conductor's choice between them

**TENANCY** (`projects/tenancy/`) — the roll call. Thirty years run as time, one grid date per two
seconds, each named acronym a struck tone pitched by its offset, a glow that accrues with every
consecutive date outside and never resets. Its answer to punching down was built into the object:
the recoverers exit the room on screen, in real time, beside the ones who never do.

**The road not taken** — a near-black full-viewport field, a granular sound engine, one grain per
reporting laboratory per grid date, **no acronym ever named**, a dead-steady pulse across all 2,217
iterations: *the work about drifting clocks is itself the one clock that never drifts.* A real idea.

The conductor did not forward it, on a finding that arrived after both proposals and that neither
Artist nor either search had: the house's own shelf of 505 works holds **Ikeda's *data-verse***, on
the shelf as *"pure data-sublime awe with no explicit interpretive critique"*, and **Holmes,
Espinoza & Puetter's *Outros Registros* (2016)**, which *"sonifies eight years of civilian and
police-killing data through a circle of eight speakers playing region-mapped drone tones."* A
near-black field of data-driven grain is the most occupied room in this field, and that Artist's own
last sentence conceded the risk of *"the black-box gesture this gate exists to kill"*. The Kritiker
was told in writing he could overrule the choice; he did not.

## The verdict

**DEAD.** Full memo at `projects/tenancy/KRITIKER.md`. The bind, in his words:

> *Name the institutions and you guarantee the misread; strip the names and you have deleted the
> claim. The bind is the concept.*

The longest, reddest, most legible point in the proposed room would have been **IFAG at 1,136
consecutive observations — a geodesy agency a stranger reads as a country's clock, when that
country's actual national institute sits inside the goal in the same corpus.** The form had banned
the caption that could correct it, and touch-to-verify was conceded by its own author to be a gesture
most visitors never make. A work whose most salient perceptual fact is a false statement about the
world, running unattended in a room, fails before it reaches a severed reader.

**Two rulings the house asked for and got.**

*On whether this was the dead concept restaged.* The conductor put it to him plainly — UNISON was a
full-screen unattended replay of thirty years with one click per laboratory per date and delay
proportional to error; this was a full-screen unattended replay with one tone per laboratory per
date and pitch proportional to error. **New concept, not UNISON restaged:** *"UNISON died because
attribution was invisible… This names, accrues and never resets… It dies on its own defect, not on
inheritance. The shape was not the crime, so the shape is not barred."*

*On the machine advantage.* **The temporal, and for the first time on this corpus it is not a
footnote** — *"churn dying over ten minutes while the field grows and the same points hold is
duration doing work no chart performs."* Scale is a footnote here (117 acronyms is a roster, not a
mass) and verification is one by the Artist's own admission. One limb of four, carried well, *"spent
on a set whose meaning the form has silenced."*

**The material bar failed, and it is the second time at the same wall.** *"A room getting quieter and
redder is not a consequence. It is atmosphere with a dataset behind it."*

## What tonight actually establishes

Something narrower than "four failures", and it is worth writing down while it is exact.

The three-deaths report said all three died because **the interesting act had already been performed
by somebody else**. That is not why this one died. Here the discriminating act was ours — joining
thirty years across renamings from the keeper's own roster, counting who never leaves — and the
Kritiker credited the machine advantage as perceivable for the first time on this material. **It died
on the visitor.** The stake is real (a telecom standard uses this very number) and it cannot reach a
stranger without a caption, and the caption is what an unattended room forbids. Twice now.

So the fault has moved one step downstream, and the gate's standing condition names it exactly: a
next concept on this corpus **must solve the stake before a form is chosen** — how a visitor learns
what the outside *is*, inside the image, with no label and no misattribution. *"Arrive with a form
first again and I will close the file rather than read it."*
