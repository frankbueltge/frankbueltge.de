## §III. THE FINDING THAT GOES TO THE WORK'S CONSTITUTIVE PROMISE — and two voices reached it independently

The work promises *the Court's own sheet, whole, never retyped.*

The Builder had to place days carrying more than one order, and implemented a **vertical squeeze**: on
2026-05-21, five orders become five bands of ~223 px, each sheet compressed **5.01×** with its width
untouched. **11 of 55 unit-days carry more than one order.** On **20 % of the days a visitor meets a
sheet, they meet a distorted one** — and the distortion destroys typography, which is the entire
evidentiary content of a work whose finding is *these two things are printed the same way*.

The conductor entered this as docket item **A7** before reading the Dramaturg's ruling; the Dramaturg
reached it independently and named the part the conductor had not: **when the sheet and the rate came
into conflict, the code chose the rate — by default, nowhere argued.** Its new binding condition 13 gives
the alternative arithmetic (313 page-heights instead of 296, a 5.7 % change, every sheet whole) or
demands the proposal say in writing that the rate outranks the sheet. **Either is answerable. Silence is
not.**

**And a second fact about the corpus, found by opening a still and verified by the conductor from the
source PDF:** the channel is **not typographically uniform**. The 21 November 2025 order is a signed
in-chambers order — *Supreme Court of the United States · No. 25A608 · GREG ABBOTT … v. LEAGUE OF UNITED
LATIN AMERICAN CITIZENS · O R D E R … /s/ Samuel A. Alito, Jr.* — set in serif with a signature block,
beside monospace order-list sheets. **Any carried-out sentence claiming sameness of wording across the
channel is dead, and any claiming sameness of typeface is dead too.** What survives is sameness of
*size* and of *disposition* — and the re-written sentence is built on exactly those, clause by clause,
each pointing at a file.

## §IV. THE MEASUREMENTS — where the house's own hopes were refuted

`projects/at-any-time/MEASUREMENTS-A4-AND-PAGECOUNTS.md`. No imaging library existed in the environment,
so the Builder **wrote a PNG encoder from scratch** and validated it by round-tripping every output
through an independent decoder.

**Last session's "most promising untested option" is measured and false.** An indexed/paletted PNG does
not shrink these pages "several-fold" — it is **~3 % larger** than plain grayscale, because every page
uses all 256 gray levels. And lossless WebP applied to the raw rendering is **2–3.7× larger** than
baseline, because the rendering's colour cast defeats its cross-channel prediction.

**The honest ceiling:** the best *fully lossless* encoding moves the wall only to **N ≈ 24–35**; the best
available encoding, accepting a bounded house-precedented correction, reaches **N ≈ 60–64** — **still
short of one full term (72).** **Encoding does not solve this.** The work needs a cap, a different
delivery architecture, or an adopted decision to accept a lifespan of ~25–35 units. None was taken
tonight; that belongs to the gate.

**Docket item 8 is closed:** **70 of 72 documents are one page; 2 are two pages**, both in the same
emergency SNAP-funding case (25A539), 7 and 10 November 2025 — **3.6 % of unit-days** the model has no
rule for.
