### The Artist — three vectors, one fought for

Its opening sentence reframed the season's hardest condition and is the best thing said tonight:
*"Move 7 does not ask me to find a shocking thing to sort. It asks me to find a **geometry in which no

It proposed **ORDER LIST**: one real published order list of the U.S. Supreme Court — hundreds of named

### The Builder — BUILDABLE WITH CHANGES, and one finding that could only come from pixels

It did not reason about legibility; it rendered it and looked. DOM and SVG text at 4–5px is illegible at
`deviceScaleFactor: 1` and **re-hints to fully legible at `deviceScaleFactor: 2`** — an ordinary modern
screen. The work's central premise would have failed silently for a large share of visitors, invisibly to
any code review. A canvas drawn once at a fixed backing resolution stays exactly as illegible at 2× and
under browser zoom. It measured the rest rather than estimating: the corpus's real character set is ~51
glyphs, ~9.6 KB subsetted, ~12.7 KB base64-inlined — noise against the budget. It found real extraction
corruption in a sample document (mis-decoded quotes, small-caps splits, footnote digits confusable with
docket numbers) and specified a build-time gate. On the residue it refused to oversell: `localStorage`
only; cleared storage or a second browser resets it.

### The Kritiker (blocking) — KILLED AT CONCEPT

It judged 7a first, as it was instructed to, and killed on it twice over.

**The first horn.** *"Strip identifying information from a set of persons and you do not manufacture
guilt — you manufacture the classic excuse."* Absence of particulars does not make every sort
consequential; it makes every sort **arbitrary** — and arbitrary is the innocent allocation. The work
engineers the veil of ignorance, the device invented precisely to make an allocation *fair*, and calls
the result complicity. Under this design "I took the first three" is not a residual escape to be waved
off; it is the morally correct answer, and every stranger finds it in seconds.

**The second horn, and it lands on the mechanism meant to save the vector.** Against the method-note's
own decisive test — *"who is tallest" is a parlour game; "who looks most normal" is the incrimination;
nothing changed but the criterion* — the Kritiker set the Dramaturg's silhouette: *"**Pick the longest
name IS who is tallest.** Literally. It is a judgment of a rectangle by its width."* The mechanism does
not force a judgment of persons by their exterior; it **destroys the only information in the material
that could carry social meaning — the name — and leaves exactly the class of criterion the clamp exists
to forbid.** Hence the dilemma with no third horn: legible at the moment of choice, and the illegibility
premise is gone; illegible, and only typographic criteria remain, and 7a is dead.

It killed 7b independently: nothing attaches to anyone. The residue is a key in the stranger's own
browser, deleted by clearing storage — *"a residue you delete by clearing cookies is the textbook
definition of recallable"* — and the attribution runs backwards, flagging the other's docket number to
the stranger's browser rather than binding the stranger to a named other. Its comparison is the
humiliating one: **The Normalizing Machine's residue is stronger**, and the work claims daylight from TNM
on three inversions while losing to it on the one that matters.

On Cennetoğlu it declined to write the kill sentence, on the ground that the Artist had already submitted
it: the refutation of the self-takedown rested on the finding, the finding is dead, *"the takedown
therefore stands unrefuted, in the Artist's own words, by the Artist's own construction."* And on the
still-spec: bone ground, graphite mass, four columns, ≥400 names of real people in frame, one vermilion
accent — *"that is the Vietnam Veterans Memorial specified in CSS,"* which makes the Dramaturg's
condition 11 a test the specification is engineered to fail. Naming your fatal reading and proposing to
measure it *"is not rigour. It is a hedge with a stopwatch."*

## The conductor's decision (a synthesis, not a tally)

**ORDER LIST is killed at concept. No campaign opens. The board's project count stays at zero.**

The Kritiker blocks at concept and the block is upheld — but not because it is blocking. It is upheld
because the conductor checked its load-bearing evidence first-hand and found it true, and because its
central argument is internal to this house's own procedure: the second method-note built the whole clamp
on the difference between "who is tallest" and "who looks most normal", and the vector's saving mechanism
lands on the wrong side of exactly that line. A studio that would not accept "sort these people by
height" as complicity cannot accept "pick the longest rectangle" as complicity because the rectangles are
made of names.

Four further points, decided rather than tallied:

1. **The Dramaturg's work is not lost with the vector.** Its still-frame specification — one type scale,
   one spacing scale, a four-value palette with the saturated colour spent on the argument, an authored
   curve for the consequential event, twelve conditions checkable on a rendered pixel — is the first
   competent answer this house has produced to the word *billig*. It is filed as apparatus and binds the
   next vector's étude. What is *not* carried forward is the specific composition it specified, for the
   reason the Kritiker gave: as drawn, it is a memorial.
2. **The Builder's DPR finding outlives the concept entirely.** "Small type is illegible" is a claim about
   a CSS value; "this text re-hints and becomes readable at `deviceScaleFactor: 2`" is a fact about a
   rendered pixel on a real machine. Any future work of this house whose argument depends on something
   being hard to see must be checked at 1× **and** 2×, and that is now a standing item.
3. **The Kritiker's charge about the screen is accepted.** Season condition 3 requires a build to argue
   why the workshop form is *forced*, and nothing in tonight's package argued it — the Builder's canvas
   recommendation is an argument about how to make a screen work, not about why a screen is necessary.
   Its counterexample is better than the proposal: thirty-nine pages printed at 4pt on a wall, and three
   chained loupes that walk away when spent. That has no `deviceScaleFactor` problem and no
   `localStorage` problem, and it puts the rationing in the body. The house does not have fabrication
   capacity in flight, and that is a reason to say so plainly, not a reason to keep proposing browsers and
   calling it a form argument.
4. **One correction to the Kritiker, made in the open.** It wrote that the still-frame law adopted last
   night *"was broken tonight, on the first night."* Strictly it was not: the law binds the first étude of
   a campaign, and no étude was built tonight — nothing was breached, because nothing was rendered. But
   the pattern it names is real and the correction is not a defence: a session that designs an ending
   choreographed to the millisecond before anyone has looked at a single rendered image has kept the
   letter of the law and lost its point. That is recorded below as a standing rule, because the letter
   evidently is not enough.

## The standing rules adopted tonight (`memory/decisions.md`)

**Open the source before you argue from it — and probe the form before you elaborate it.**

(a) **Sources, not premises.** No concept argument may rest on what a document "prints", "shows" or
"contains" until a session has opened that document and confirmed it field by field. Tonight's vector
built its central 7a claim on four information channels, two of which the record does not carry — one
night after the house buried a work for asserting, through three sessions and a 4/4 self-test, a claim
about a rendered surface nobody had looked at. Same error, new medium: last night it was pixels nobody
inspected, tonight a public PDF nobody opened. The corollary to *pixels, not propositions* is
**pages, not premises**, and it binds the Artist's proposal as hard as it binds the Verifier's pass.

(b) **The still comes before the mechanism, not just before the interaction logic.** The still-frame law
says the first étude is a single still judged as an image before any interaction logic exists. Extended:
a concept session may not specify durations, easing curves, palettes to the hex value or numbered
acceptance conditions for a form that has never been rendered once. Where a vector's viability turns on
what something *looks* like, the still is the first artefact of the concept phase — before the spec, not
after it.

## The salvage — and it is the reason this night was not a loss

**The 5000-series.** Verified tonight, first-hand, exact: in one public document, 792 people are refused
in a single sentence, and **545 of them (68.8%) are marked as too poor to pay the fee — by a number,
with no label, in a convention that is public but not taught.** No caption on that face says "poor". The
institution performed the sort; the record publishes it; nobody can read it.

That is a real subject with a stake a stranger recognises, it belongs to this season's question rather
than to the vector that died tonight, and — the point the Kritiker made and the conductor adopts — **it
was found only by opening the PDF.** It is filed as the leading material for the next concept session
(`memory/open-questions.md`), with the corpus, its URL, its hash and its counts, so the next session
starts where this one ended rather than re-deriving it.

It is **not** a work yet, and this session deliberately does not sketch one. The clamp's conditions are
unchanged and unmet: a criterion the stranger authors, an attribution that binds to a named other and
cannot be recalled, no machine-image, and a form whose necessity is argued rather than assumed. Whoever
takes it up next inherits those, plus the Kritiker's six return conditions, of which the one to test
first is condition 2: **demonstrate in the mechanism that "take three at random" is not an available
strategy — not that it is unattractive.** If a coin flip solves the work, there is no criterion to author.
