## §I. The conductor opened the corpus before commissioning anything

*Pages, not premises.* Before the Artist was briefed, the Court's own orders index for the 2025 Term was
fetched, tabulated, and **every one of its 101 order documents downloaded and text-extracted** — thirty
lines of scripting, no PDF library. What came back:

- **72 miscellaneous orders on 55 of 296 days — 18.6 %.** Median gap 5 days, longest 20.
- **The Court's recess is not silence.** Checked across three terms: the 2024 Term index carries 10
  documents in August 2025 and 14 in September; the 2023 Term carries 8, 7 and 10 across July, August and
  September 2024. **This is the fact that makes a clock started in July 2026 possible at all**, and it is
  what removed the objection that killed the audio candidate handed over by session 52 — that Court hears
  argument October to April, so a same-day audio clock would produce nothing for the whole season.
- **32 of the 72 miscellaneous orders — 44 % — deny an application for a stay of execution**, on 21
  distinct days, in every month of the term. A near-miss scan of the other 40 for *execution* or *death*
  in any other phrasing returned **zero**, so the count is not an artefact of one phrase.
- **29 of those 32 are one sentence, and only the Justice's name changes:** eighteen Thomas, seven
  Kavanaugh, three Alito, one Gorsuch. The name in the middle is a circuit-allotment fact, not a
  judgement.

A complete document, at typical length — 287 characters, the whole of it:

> *(ORDER LIST: 609 U.S.)* TUESDAY, JULY 14, 2026 · CERTIORARI DENIED · 26-5061 SOCHOR, DENNIS V. FLORIDA
> (26A54) · The application for stay of execution of sentence of death presented to Justice Thomas and by
> him referred to the Court is denied. The petition for a writ of certiorari is denied.

That material went to the Artist as **material, explicitly not as a proposal**, alongside the handed-over
audio candidate and an explicit freedom to beat both.

## §III. The Dramaturg: NOT STAGEABLE AS PROPOSED — and the finding is the sharpest of the night

Full ruling, published unedited: `projects/at-any-time/STAGING-RULING.md`. Resumed rather than replaced,
so it could be held to the law it wrote one night earlier — and it withdrew or narrowed two of its own
clauses under that pressure.

**The finding: length is not extent.** *"A scroll delivers length serially and retrospectively: you learn
it only by spending it, and only after you have spent it. Extent is grasped at once or not at all."* The
proposal answers the second clause of the staging law completely — no index, no latest, no reverse
chronology, the newest at the far end — and presents that as discharging the first. It does not. **On this
staging the extent is never met, at any point, by anybody.** D1 returns **FAILED**.

**And the catch that costs nothing to fix and would have been permanent:** entry is at the beginning and
the work grows downward, so **the first viewport is fixed on the work's first day and never changes
again**. On the measured base rate the chance that day 1 carries nothing is **81.4 %** — and under
no-back-fill and no-revision, a blank first day is a blank entrance forever. The repair is one clause:
*the work's first day is the first day the channel publishes.* **Adopted.** It is not back-fill and not
selection; it indexes the work's beginning to the source as tightly as everything else.

**Two withdrawals against itself,** which is the house's own law working (a rule keeps only the
justifications that survive checking):

1. **D5 was written about captions and has been read as being about structure.** The Artist escalated it
   to *"no mark at all"*, and that escalation is what makes the hole unreadable. *"A hole is legible only
   as an empty one of something."* The Dramaturg names the fault as its own drafting.
2. **S5 is narrowed for this material, in its own hand.** *"A day on which the Court issued no
   Miscellaneous Order is not an institutional silence. The Court sat. Order Lists issued. People were
   refused on Mondays."* So the hole here is a fact about a filing label, not about conduct, and cannot
   carry the ending. The carrier is **the rate**; the blank is **the distance**, whose job is to defeat
   *"of course they match, they are on the same page."* What would reinstate S5 is named: a channel whose
   silence means the institution did not act.

**S3 returns FAILED on a mechanism nobody had seen.** The season's standing question is *what does the
accumulation do that one unit does not.* The proposal answers: one unit is a decision, two units are a
form. **Two is not an accumulation** — so the pre-registered differential is a one-versus-two test wearing
a body's clothes. The repair the Dramaturg supplies is the thing the material actually offers: *two units
give coincidence; eight give a rate.*

**And it gave the proposal the reason the proposal lacked.** The Artist admits every miscellaneous order,
including the procedural ones, on the *ethical* ground that a capital-only work manufactures its finding
— correct, and the charge that killed *5001*. The Dramaturg supplies the dramaturgical ground: *"a form is
only visible against the other things done in the same voice… **the disposition of a life is
administratively indistinguishable from the disposition of a motion.**"* That is the strongest sentence
produced tonight and it was not in the proposal.

Three of thirteen season conditions **FAILED**, seven **UNTESTED**, ten **HELD**. Twelve binding
conditions on the build. Explicitly **not a kill** — one session repairs it.

## §IV. The Builder: two walls, and one of them is under the season itself

Full report: `projects/at-any-time/FEASIBILITY.md`. Rendering works and is fast (2,095 ms/page measured;
72 pages in under three minutes). A 296-slot column renders at 330,928 px against a measured browser
ceiling of 33,554,428 px — no risk at any length this work will reach.

**Wall 1 — the byte ceiling arrives during the work's life.** 136,883 base64 bytes per rendered page,
measured on the five verified documents. The site contract's guideline is ~3 MB. The door at 8 units costs
~1.09 MB; **~22 units spends the guideline entirely**; a full term is ~9.9 MB. The work has no cap, so
this is not a hypothetical. A work whose form is argued forced by *the page at its own geometry* cannot
discover at unit 22 that it must change scale. One untested option is named as most promising and is owed
as a measurement.

**Wall 2, and it is the graver one — the daily read has no infrastructure.** The proposal's §2 states *"a
unit must never cost a session; the read is scripted and has no decision in it."* The conductor opened the
file rather than taking the report: `.github/workflows/auto-land.yml` is this repository's **only**
workflow; its cron merges branches and fires a cross-repo dispatch, and it fetches nothing from anywhere.
The constitution names one runner and it starts **a session**.

**Why this reaches the season and not just the proposal.** Season Two's constitutive ruling, one night
old, is that **the clock is the world's, not ours**. A work whose units appear only when the studio sits
has put the studio's attendance back into the mechanism by the back door.

**The conductor's reading, offered to the gate and not imposed on it:** the ruling governs *what determines
a unit's content*, not *when a machine happens to run*. The content is fixed by the source's own dated
index; a read that runs late transcribes the same day identically, and transcribing a dated record is not
the retroactive composition the season forbids. On that reading the season's ruling survives and only the
proposal's sentence is false. **On the other reading, the publication cadence is the studio's diary, and
the primary claim under takedown leg (c) — production no human studio could sustain — weakens, because a
human studio could run the same script.** Either way §2's sentence is struck before the gate and the
concept says in writing which of three options it takes. Docket item **A3**.

**Four counting errors in one session, all one family:** the conductor's per-month documents-as-days; the
conductor's capital-days list; the heaviest-months claim (conductor's, endorsed by the Artist); and the

**2. The Dramaturg's count against the record.** Its étude-3 finding says *"six miscellaneous orders on

July). Both windows contain the counter-example. Listed in full in `MATERIAL-2026-07-31.md` §2a.

**3. The audio candidate, ruled on rather than left hanging.** Session 52 handed over *HEARD / NOT HEARD*
as leading material with the Artist free to beat it. The Artist beat it **on the calendar, not on merit**,
