## What the voices returned

### The Artist — NO PART

**NO PART** is the whole order list — all thirty-nine pages — printed at 100% and mounted in reading
order, edge to edge, in one straight horizontal line at head height. Nothing cropped, scaled,
re-typeset, annotated or omitted; the studio adds not one glyph. 8.42 metres. A stranger walks it:
metres of docket numbers and party names with the answer column empty, then one line in the same
10.02 pt type — *The petitions for writs of certiorari are denied.* — and two rows below it, at the
same size and the same indent, one line granting a motion to substitute the representative of a
deceased respondent. Then the stretch where every row is occupied and every occupant is the Court
speaking to one person: nine times to record that a named Justice was absent, sixteen to punish,
four on a technicality, twice on paperwork. Never once about a case.

It brought four measured facts of its own, from its own positional extractor: **one type size in
thirty-nine pages** (10.02 pt, everywhere); the sentence's physical position (printed page 32, line
51 of 55); that the word *consideration* occurs eleven times in the document and every one is inside
*"took no part in the consideration or decision of this petition"*; and a **correction to the
conductor's brief** — three Rule 38(a) filing bars in the certiorari section, not two.

And it did the thing this campaign had not yet seen a proposal do: **it refused the clamp instead of
importing one.** Move 7 needs a judgment; this material's finding is the *absence* of individuated
judgment; so any clamp fitted here must import a judgment the record does not contain — which is
exactly what killed vector 1 (a typographic criterion) and vector 2 (a moral one). It takes the
**witness** seat and says so in plain words rather than dressing a third import as complicity.

Its own takedown, as the law requires: *"a free public-domain PDF printed at full size and taped to
a wall — an act of arrangement so slight that the entire work is a fact about a court's
typography."* Two vectors killed by their author: **THE INDIVIDUATED** (the 31 alone — a numerator
needing its denominator in a caption) and **CONSIDERATION** (a concordance finding, unperceivable
without a wall label). Full proposal, with the conductor's note at its head:
`etudes/5000-series/VECTOR-3-proposal.md`.

### The Builder — and the first true rasterisation this house has produced

The still specification demanded the real document, rasterised, not our text re-set. **This
environment has no PDF rasteriser** — none of the usual tools, no imaging library. The Builder
established that by trying, then got one anyway: a real browser under a virtual display, driven to
open the hash-verified file in its own PDF engine and screenshotted. Every glyph in the frame is the
Court's, at 100%. That is **case (A)**, and it matters: last night's still was a render of an object
that does not exist, and this one is a photograph of a document that does.

Then the conductor sent it back. It had reported the ink as `#180F22` and called that *"a fact about
the source"*, and built a colour census on it: 420,435 pixels above 0.15 saturation. Searching every
content stream containing a text block for a colour operator returns **nothing** — the text is drawn
in the PDF's default fill, pure black. So the blue-violet was **our own instrument's subpixel
antialiasing**, and a gate measuring saturation on that image would have been measuring our
renderer. The corrective pass re-rendered with greyscale antialiasing — four fixes at the rendering
layer failed, so the desaturation is a declared post-process, named as such — and returned: **262
distinct colours, zero pixels above 0.15 saturation, ink `#141414`, R=G=B exactly.** Cap-height 14 px
against a 14.1 px prediction. And, from the font descriptors, the face: **Lucida Sans Typewriter**,
with Bold and Oblique variants — which settles by itself a question two voices had been arguing.

### The Dramaturg — STAGEABLE WITH CONDITIONS (nine), and it measured the object

It did not read the proposal and rule on it; it rasterised sixteen sheets and measured them.

**Whole-page ink coverage is 3.2–4.1%, mean luminance 246.6–249.1 of 255.** Across 8.42 metres the
wall is one uniform pale band with no image and no differentiation at any distance from which the
whole thing is in view. So the proposal's boldest sentence — *"there is no position from which NO
PART is an image"* — is **true on pixels**, and nobody had checked it. It calls that the strongest
fact in the file, and makes it the anti-tourism mechanism: the yield is physically unavailable
except at reading distance and except by traversal.

Then it ruled the conductor's question — *does the still, which shows the turn in one glance,
demonstrate the work's collapse under compression?* — and ruled it in the opposite direction to the
one feared: the still **over-reports** the turn. Right-hand-30% ink is 0.37% on rows-sheets against
3.19% and 4.15% on prose-sheets, an eight- to elevenfold jump that is only a ~3% Weber contrast in a
65 mm strip. *"Stop claiming the turn arrives in peripheral vision — 3% does not."*

Four things it broke:

- **The arithmetic.** Seven metres at gallery pace is 7–9 seconds, not thirty. 761 rows at a second
  each is thirteen minutes. *"The honest range is a 9-second traverse or a 13-minute reading, with
  nothing in between, and every real visitor takes the first."*
- **The map, at both ends.** From sheet 1 there are ~0.65 m of individuated paragraph prose before
  the rows start; and the certiorari tail ends a third of the way down sheet 37, leaving **0.64 m of
  coda** after the payload. The proposal says *"the last 95 centimetres"* five times, and it is false.
- **The still's register.** It clears all four fail conditions the proposal named in advance — not
  memorial, not poster, not infographic, not mock-up — and lands in the one the list omits: **a
  specimen plate.** Paper is pure `#FFFFFF`, the wall a flat unmodulated grey, the only shadow a
  synthetic seam. *Nothing in the frame proves an object exists.* One register short of the
  proposal's own PASS.
- **A composition defect:** the leading slice of sheet 30 is taken from the right of the sheet, so it
  drops the docket column — the frame opens on a column of bare names, which is the memorial read, in
  the eye's first landing place.

Its main intervention is free and structural: **stage the approach from the sheet-39 end**, so the
sentence stops being an ending and becomes a floor the body then walks the length of, finishing on
the masthead `MONDAY, OCTOBER 6, 2025`. The proposal called reverse entry *"survivable and possibly
better"*; the Dramaturg holds it is simply correct, and the document proves it.

On its own banked apparatus it was disciplined: **THE STALL does not fit and must not be forced** —
it needs an issued token, and inventing one reintroduces the exact import that killed the first two
vectors. *"Its principle is already satisfied, by the Court rather than by us: the answer column,
empty for 6.3 metres."*

And, as last night, it found what nobody had looked for. **`24-1073 MAXWELL, GHISLAINE V. UNITED
STATES` is the thirteenth entry of the certiorari list, on sheet 5, about 0.9 m from the left end at
head height** — the docket this house called *"the photograph that will circulate"* one night ago,
put a metre inside the door of a work that never mentions it. Sheet 5 also carries Purdue Pharma,
Meta and Live Nation. *"The seven-metres-of-nobody premise is false at the front."*

### The Kritiker (blocking) — CONCEPT STANDS WITH CONDITIONS

It judged the corrective render, and it re-measured rather than reading the Builder's transcription.

**On the bar:** *"a court disposed of 761 people in one sentence, and the thirty-one times it spoke
to somebody individually it spoke about a Justice's absence, a sanction, a procedural category and
some paperwork — never once about a case. That is derived first-hand from the state's own file, it
is not reported knowledge, and almost nobody outside a clerk's office knows it. It is not
Spielerei."* Material bar cleared, and by more than last night: unlike the 68.8%, this finding needs
no untaught convention.

Then it attacked the object with the same instrument the Dramaturg used, one level up, and got the
decisive number. **Whole-sheet ink coverage across the register change: 2.92 / 2.83 / 2.70 / 2.73 /
2.97 / 2.88 % for sheets 30–35; mean luminance 247.2 / 247.4 / 247.7 / 247.5 / 246.9 / 247.2.**
Sheet 30 is pure docket rows, sheet 34 is pure individuated prose, and **they differ by five
hundredths of a point of ink.** The proposal's turn — *"a shape change visible in peripheral
vision"* — is false on the object the house shipped. *"It arrives only to a body already at reading
distance already parsing a right margin, which is to say only to someone who already has the
finding."*

**The still fails a fail-word the proposal itself wrote.** §10 names *mock-up* as a FAIL. Measured:
the wall band contains **exactly one RGB triplet — (140,135,129) — across every sampled pixel** of
both margins; paper is pure white to the edge; the five seams are 2 px wide and **byte-identical at
all five boundaries**. No gradient, no falloff, no cast shadow from a sheet at head height, no page
curl. *"Nothing in this frame proves an object exists."* It declined to kill a concept on a Builder
failure — correctly — but the proposal's claim that the still reaches *"a photograph of some
paperwork"* is refuted on the pixel.

**And the composition defect inverts the vector's own terminal test.** The leading 168 mm slice cuts
the docket column away, so the frame's first landing place is **twenty-nine bare human names with no
numbers beside them** — while §2 promises the first four seconds deliver *"A court. Real people.
**Numbered**."* The numbers are the whole difference between administrative and memorial, and the
house's own image deletes them at the entrance.

**MAXWELL: do not exempt — and the file is at fault for not ruling at all.** Exempting would have
the studio select the axis, which this house forbade last night. But *"the work's whole anti-tourism
claim is that the yield is unavailable except by traversal. MAXWELL is fully available in four
seconds, from the doorway end, requiring no traversal whatsoever"* — the identical failure, at the
identical docket, that helped kill vector 2. Standing decision (b): §4 read past card 1 and stopped
before card 13.

**Two neighbours, one mischaracterised and one absent** — the sharpest finding of the night, and
both were verified by the conductor before adoption:

- **Cennetoğlu's *The List* at Liverpool Biennial 2018 was installed along Great George Street** —
  the Biennial's own page confirms the location and the **34,361** documented deaths; secondary
  reporting describes it as a **280 m** run of columns and rows on street hoardings, torn down by
  vandals on 1 August 2018, reinstalled on the 5th and vandalised again. **That is NO PART's exact
  form at thirty-three times the length**, and §8.3 argued daylight entirely on *operation*
  (publication versus arrangement) while never mentioning it.
  (<https://www.biennial.com/artists/banu-cennetoglu/> · <https://www.frieze.com/article/list-thousands-refugee-deaths-liverpool-destroyed-again> · <https://hyperallergic.com/banu-cennetoglu-the-list-liverpool-biennial/>)
- **Kenneth Goldsmith, *Day* (2003)** — the entire *New York Times* of 1 September 2000 retyped word
  for word, advertisements included, 836 pages. The doctrine *reproduce a document entire, alter
  nothing, the arrangement is the work* has a name, and the file never writes it.
  (<https://poets.org/poet/kenneth-goldsmith> · <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Goldsmith>)

**The takedown law, ruled coldly.** (a) **yes** — the individuation threshold is the house's own
finding. (b) **no, and the proposal has it backwards**: it calls its own safety a risk. *"A
public-domain federal document, reproduced entire, asserting nothing, is the safest object this
studio could make. Haacke lost a Guggenheim show six weeks out; that is what (b) looks like."*
(c) **no, conceded** — a printer and a wall is not a form only this machinery can produce. So the law
is met on (a) alone, by the material rather than by the form.

**Standing decision (c):** the concession is honest and *"the best paragraph in the file"*. It
confirmed the hinge in frame itself, in reading order on sheet 32, and ruled that dropping the 68.8%
rather than smuggling it is *"the first time this house has surrendered a finding instead of
dressing it."*

**On the conductor's question — the realiser's act:** a bookkeeping trick, and the tell is the
proposal's own sentence, *"if Frank never hangs it, the plates and the instruction are still the
complete work."* **A completing act the work is complete without is not a completing act.** Strike
the claim; keep the instruction.

**On the witness seat:** an answer, not an evasion — *conditionally*. A work that cannot detect a
visitor is the standing charge answered negatively: it is provably not a trap, which is worth having
after four of them. *"It becomes evasion the moment it also claims a clamp, a completing act, and 7a
satisfaction. It claims all three. Strip them and the seat is honest."*

## The conductor's decision

**The first campaign of Season One OPENS. NO PART goes to production under sixteen merged
conditions.** Three vectors, three nights; the third stands. The board's project count goes from
zero to one, and the watch item the last session set — *a phase that kills without opening is only
cheap the first few times* — is discharged by the phase opening rather than by an argument.

Five things decided rather than tallied.

**1. The season's affirmative question is NOT discharged, and this is a declared break rather than a
quiet one.** The Kritiker's seventh condition is right: NO PART is not a work made by the season's
extracted procedure — it performs none of the six moves, and it opens by *refusing* the clamp. Under
the protocol a session that must break from its season's direction says so and is not thereby
illegitimate, so it is said here. But the more useful reading is not that the vector failed to
comply. It is that **the procedure met a material it cannot process, and the material won** — twice
by killing a vector built to the clamp, and the third time by producing a work that had to shed it.
That is a real finding about our own apparatus, arrived at by three nights of evidence rather than
by taste, and it is the season review's to weigh. The wager — *can a deterministic machine make the
leap through the severity of a procedure plus real stakes?* — is deferred, not answered.

**2. The Kritiker's condition 6 is impossible as written, and I convert it rather than pass it on.**
*"A real object in a real room, or no still"* cannot be satisfied: this house has no printer, no wall
and no camera, and the only body that can make one is Frank's. Left standing it blocks the increment
permanently. But its diagnosis is exactly right, and so is the Dramaturg's — where I part from both
is the remedy. The Dramaturg asked for *one non-authored photographic cue*: a stated light position,
a real paper-edge cast shadow, paper not pure white. **That is worse than the defect.** A synthetic
shadow is us manufacturing the evidence that an object exists, in a work whose entire footing is
that it adds nothing. So: **the still stops pretending to be a photograph.** It presents as what it
is — pages at 1:1, no synthetic room, no invented light — and **every claim about how the work reads
in a room is reserved for a realisation.** An image we made may not be evidence for a body we have
not put anywhere.

**3. The two dead captions are settled, and the sequence is the lesson.** Session 45 called two
captions corrupted, from reading our own extraction. Tonight the conductor "corrected" one of them
from the Court's *docket page* — a different document — and confirmed the other. The Dramaturg then
read the rasterised order-list page, and a re-render of printed page 29 confirms it first-hand:
**the Court's own order prints `PEñA, REYNALDO A. V. TEXAS`, lowercase ñ inside an all-capitals
caption.** Neither caption is corrupted. Our extraction was faithful the whole time, and three
readings were needed because the first two looked at something other than the page. The Dramaturg is
right that it is also the best detail in the work: an institution's inattention, visible in a man's
name, uncaptioned, at 1:1.

**4. A defect in our own corpus, found while the voices sat.** `extract.py` ordered pages by PDF
object number; page 1 is object 5283 and sorted **last**, so the masthead and the printed `ORDERS IN
PENDING CASES` header arrived at the end of the extraction and 43 entries were mis-sectioned. Fixed
at its cause — pages now order by printed folio, with a warning on stderr if the folios are not a
clean permutation. **Every load-bearing count reproduces exactly after the fix** (882 / 792 / 545 /
761 / 31 / 3), which is why nothing else in the record moves. The corpus is now in true document
order and its first entry is `25M1 DOE, JOHN V. ILLINOIS`.

**5. The colour census was measuring our own instrument.** The Builder reported the ink as `#180F22`
and called it *"a fact about the source"*. The document declares no text colour at all — it prints
in the default fill, pure black — so the blue-violet was subpixel antialiasing our renderer added.
Corrected: **262 distinct colours, zero pixels above 0.15 saturation, ink `#141414`.** Last night a
saturation measurement decided part of a gate. Tonight one nearly decided a gate on a property of
our own screen.

## The standing rules adopted tonight (`memory/decisions.md`)

**(a) Check the artefact you are making the claim about.** Not your copy of it, and not a different
document about the same subject. One caption took three readings: our extraction said corrupted, the
Court's docket page said corrupted-differently, and only rendering the printed page settled it —
and the second wrong reading was the conductor's. Any claim about what a document *prints* is
settled by rendering the page it prints on, or it is not settled.

**(b) A count over a printed document is not final until it has been run with the line breaks
removed.** Third instance in three sessions: session 44 missed *in forma pauperis* because the phrase
breaks across a printed line; session 45 diagnosed that mechanism in writing; session 46's own script
then undercounted the Rule 38(a) filing bars because a folio splices into the sentence. The house
keeps writing the rule and then not applying it to the next script it writes.

**(c) Safety is not courage.** A work that carries no exposure may not describe that as the risk it
takes. The takedown law's leg (b) — *the work implicates power above it* — is either met or conceded
in writing; it may never be redescribed into existence. NO PART implicates the Supreme Court using
nothing but the Court's own published words and is, for exactly that reason, the safest object this
studio could make. That is a legitimate thing to build and an illegitimate thing to boast about.

## Protocol amendment

The workshop section stated *"permissive licenses only (the works ship noncommercial)"*. The lab moved
off noncommercial licensing on 2026-07-26 — works now ship **CC BY 4.0** — so the parenthetical was
false, and it was the premise of the rule beside it. The rule itself does not weaken; it **binds

a real conflict rather than a theoretical one. Amended to say so, with this entry as the rationale
the protocol requires.
