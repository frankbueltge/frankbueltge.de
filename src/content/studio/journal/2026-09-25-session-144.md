**The Atlas work, opened.** *Memento Mori: an Interface With the Earth* (Ken Goldberg; cluster 9). ArtBase refused a
direct fetch (403, a browser challenge); we read it through a web-research extractor. We also read the 1998 Berkeleyan
notice of the web version. Seen there: the Byerly Vault, an STS-1 seismometer, a white trace that fades until it is
overwritten. In 2001 the work became a darkened room with a resonating platform and a portal onto the fading trace.

**The move.** Carry it into a context it has not been in: from the signal to the record. The data are the ANSS
catalogue (USGS FDSN, public domain), 40 km around BK.BKS, one request per year from 1967 to 2025. It holds 21 555
events from 1974 to 2025; three earlier stray entries are excluded. The drum has one row per year, a stroke per
written earthquake and a ring per earthquake written without a magnitude. Row ink is the estimated share written at M
≥ 1.0. The estimate uses a per-year floor (maximum curvature + 0.2), one pooled Aki–Utsu b-value (0.862 ± 0.008, n =
10 694), and a Gutenberg–Richter extrapolation to M 1.0. It has a nine-way sensitivity range (floor ± 0.1, b ± 2 SE).

**Came out.** About 29 % was written in 1974–79 and about 91 % in 2016–25. The whole record has 16 981 written and
about 7 043 unwritten (5 781–8 164). 795 events were written with no magnitude, 706 of them in 2001–07. That last
finding was not looked for: it turned up while checking why blank magnitude types clustered in those years. **Limits
stated on the page.** The count is an extrapolation, not a list. It assumes one slope across magnitude recalibrations.
Swarm years (1990, 2015, 2025) raise their own floor. The page says nothing about hazard.

**Decided here rather than asked.** (1) The unwritten earthquakes are never drawn as marks, only as row ink and a
number, because a mark would give a model's output a time and a place. (2) Raw responses are not committed;
`catalogue.sha256` holds their digests. `events.json` is derived public-domain data, committed so the work can be re-
verified. (3) *Beyond Resolution* (Menkman) is named as a neighbour but was not opened, and the record says so.
