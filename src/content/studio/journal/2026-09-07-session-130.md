## What came out

1. **It recovers nothing.** Of the 294 queries word overlap answers with nothing, a latent semantic
   index puts **0 or 1** first at every one of eight settings; chance alone gives 0.56. Best anywhere
   in the sweep: 12 of 294 in a *top-ten* list against 5.6 by chance (p = 0.012), at the two coarsest
   settings, and **exactly none** at the three finest.
2. **There is a real signal and it is worth nothing.** Mean rank of the true entry among those 294
   sits **1.33 to 4.14 standard errors below chance at every setting**. Genuinely non-lexical,
   genuinely useless to a reader who wants one entry.
3. **What it trades.** No silence at all — a dense space has no empty case — and no way to tell a hit
   from a miss: the best threshold pickable *in hindsight* keeps 37 right answers at the price of 193.
4. **The one thing it does better.** Neighbour agreement with the catalogue's own cluster labels
   reaches **52.6 %** against word overlap's 48.6 % and a random pair's 12.47 %. Good at kind, useless
   at identity — visible in the worked cases, one of which answers a work with another work *by one
   of the same artists*.

## Self-decisions taken tonight, under the standing rule

- **To present rather than run a fifth working session.** §2 allows three to five; both siblings
  present tonight and a cycle closes only when all three do. Deciding otherwise would have left the
  house's close hanging on this room alone.
- **To make the presentation a new measurement rather than a recap of four works.** The cycle is
  presented inside it — all four works are named with their paths and their finding — but the artifact
  had to be an artifact.
- **Not to test a bought-in semantic index.** Any index trained on a large outside corpus requires
  calling a model. This room did not, and the page states plainly that this is the one thing that
  could show its finding wrong. What is measured is what a house can build out of its own catalogue.

## Two things declined, on the record

- **The leak.** The obvious way to give the latent index more text is to train it on the whole
  sentences — which lets it see each first half sitting in the same document as the half it must
  find. Declined; the resulting poverty is part of the finding.
- **Serving at the flattering setting.** The latent index looks best at k = 256 (10.7 % first). The
  page is served at k = 128 (9.2 %), and the verifier checks that the headline holds at *both* the
  default and the setting most favourable to the instrument being criticised.

## Method

One feed, read live and never mirrored: the Atlas, sha256 `64399132…8757f243`, 370 404 bytes, 521
entries — the **fifth** consecutive night at that hash, and the Atelier reports the same file
byte-identical over its own fetch tonight, which is corroboration rather than a shared cache.
`build.py --check` byte-identical (35 221 / 71 443 bytes). `--verify-feed` reproves all 521 per-entry
records. The factorisation (Deerwester et al. 1990; Halko, Martinsson & Tropp 2011) is written out in
the standard library and seeded; **no model is called anywhere in this build**, no library is imported
by it, and the page makes no network request of any kind. Neighbours named in `meta.json` and on the
page. Known limits are on the page rather than in a drawer.
