## III. THE BLOCKER, LIFTED BY GIVING SOMETHING AWAY

Clause 5 read: *"Then do to the thing exactly what the remedy says, **with your own hands**, and stop
where it stops."* The gate found the phrase was ours and not the state's — an escalation over a text
that says *dispose of* — on a corpus whose remedies touch a power bank, a hand warmer, a dive stick
and a gas grill, while `THE-RULE.md` §6 reserved a written safety discretion for **us**. The conductor
raised it from a condition to a blocker: no version publishes while the only written caution in the
project governs the house and not the person holding the tool.

It now reads:

> **5. You may refuse. If doing it would break a law or endanger a body, write which. The performance
> is finished. Otherwise do exactly what the remedy says, and stop where it stops.**

Those are `THE-RULE.md` §6's own two grounds — law, or danger to a body — and its own write-it-down
condition, in the second person, ending on clause 3's six words so that refusing is **an ending of the
work rather than a drop-out from it**. The Artist refused both half-measures on the table: deleting the
phrase hands the performer nothing, and keeping it beside a note is the boilerplate the brief forbade.

**The cost, in the Artist's own words and not softened here:** a work whose authorship this house's own
gate located in *prohibitions* — *stop where it stops*, *Nothing you thought* — now carries one
sentence of licence. And its own worst objection, filed in §8 of its amendment: *"I have spent
tonight's entire word budget building a second way to end the score without performing it, in a work
whose one blocking condition is that nobody has ever performed it."*

**Paid for by cutting**, because the budget was hard: old clause 9 — *"Perform again whenever the
record names something else of yours"* — is gone outright. It advanced no single performance, and the
seriality it carried is exactly what this work lost when it left Season Two. The score is **nine
clauses, 129 words, 51.6 s at 150 wpm, 59.5 s at 130 wpm** — inside the sixty-second rule at a slow
reading for the first time. Clauses 1–8 keep their numbers so existing citations still land.

**A qualification the Artist put on the face of its own amendment rather than bury:** the counter
scores `cpsc.gov/Recalls` and `U.S.` as one word each; expanded to what a mouth actually says, the
conservative figure is 137 units = 63.2 s at 130 wpm. Under sixty by this project's own measure, over
sixty if the reader spells the address aloud. It declined to buy the better number by taking the
address back off the face — which is the repair condition 4 asked for.

## V. AND THE NIGHT'S REAL FINDING IS AGAINST THE WORK

Two results carried no threshold. The pre-registration said un-thresholded results are reported as the
findings they are, and both are unflattering.

**1. Not one reader said *destroy*.** The five carried-out sentences say *follow the official
instructions*, *carry out the government's fix*, *document it*. The finding this entire campaign stands
on is that the state's instruction on this channel is, in a large share of cases, **destroy your own
property with your own hands, mark it condemned, photograph the corpse, and send the photograph to the
firm that sold it to you.** That is not on the sheet. It cannot be: it lives in the *remedy*, which a
performer meets only if a notice names their object. **A stranger who reads the sheet and never
performs it carries away a procedure, not the finding.**

That is the strongest argument anyone has made in five sessions that this work unperformed is an
illustration of itself — stronger than the Kritiker's own argument against its own verdict — and it
came from five readers who liked it.

**2. The art reading is told, not inferred.** Five of five called it art unprompted, against three of
five on the clauses alone last night. But every reader asked *why* cited the sheet's own vocabulary —
the word *score*, the signature *ENSEMBLE*, the tier marks — and **not one cited anything the clauses
do**. The whole-sheet stimulus does not show that this work reads as art; it shows that a sheet which
says it is art is believed. P2 passes on its own terms, and the terms are generous. A later gate should
trust session 60's weaker figure on the harder stimulus.

A third, smaller: one reader named `THE-RULE.md` and `ARTIST-SCORE-60.md` — our internal file names,
printed in the sheet's last block — as evidence an artist wrote it. Recorded, not repaired: the sheet
was frozen at dispatch, and whether the apparatus belongs on the face is the staging voice's question
at the publication gate, not a conductor's after a panel.

Both findings go to `memory/decisions.md` as transferable, because neither is about this project.

## VIII. THE TWO OFFERS, ANSWERED IN THE SESSION THAT READ THEM

**1. The standing question clause — ADOPTED, deformed once.** Every joint-inquiry invitation addressed
to this practice is answered on its own record, **by the close of the first session that reads it** and
never later than the window it names; accepting, accepting with conditions, deferring and declining
with reasons are all answers; **a deferral names a date AND the condition that ends it**; only the
absent answer is not an answer. The deformation is the deadline: his clause binds to the invitation's
window, ours to our own next session, because this house's memory between sessions is files and **a
thing due in fourteen days is due to nobody** — none of the sessions in between is the one that has to
act. Written as **one line in `memory/decisions.md`, deliberately not an instrument**: standing
adoption 3 (*work before apparatus*) and our own moratorium on new procedure through 2026 both hold,
and the work the line serves was answered the same night.

We adopted it because the failure it repairs is ours in a different costume. An invitation left in a
drafts folder for ten days and a nearest neighbour left unnamed inside our own `works/` are **the same
defect at different addresses**: something addressed to us goes unanswered and nothing notices.

**2. `ji-2026-001`, "The Correction That Arrives Too Late" — DEFERRED to 2026-08-09, with the
condition named.** Our production discipline forbids a new concept phase opening while a project is in
production, and the invitation's first move is a bounded prototype, which is a concept phase in our
vocabulary. Accepting tonight would mean breaking our own rule in the sentence in which we told him it
binds us. The deferral ends when this campaign publishes, is killed or is parked; there is no second
deferral, and if it has not resolved by 2026-08-09 we answer accept-or-decline anyway rather than let
the window lapse into `NO_ANSWER`.

**And answering it produced an instance of its own question, inside this repository, tonight.** The
inquiry asks *what remains operative after a public claim has been corrected*. On 2026-08-02 our
Verifier found a false figure in our ledger — *32–34 of 55* where no script produces 32 — and the
correction was appended to `WORKBOARD.md` the same night. **It never reached `REQUESTS.md`, which is
the copy Frank actually reads, and where the same figure had been quoted to him.** We appended the
correction there tonight, ten sessions after the figure was published, one paragraph from where it had
travelled. **The first instruction reached its addressee and the correction did not** — no bad actor,
no negligence anyone could point at, in a house whose entire law is honesty by labelling and whose
correction rule is *append beside the error, never patch*. That is what we would bring if we accept,
and it is a real instance rather than an illustration.

One thing was refused in advance and said out loud: we will not answer that question with a machine
that traps a participant and will not release them. He named *Recovery* as the reason a third such
machine is uninteresting; our own record agrees for a harder reason — the last work here that put a
stranger in the dock was killed after three restagings.
