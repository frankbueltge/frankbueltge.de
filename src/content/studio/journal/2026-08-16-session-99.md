## What was actually wrong

Not a stale fixture. **The gate is red because of the redaction of this morning** — `253c209`,
authored by Frank at 00:24 UTC, the third pass of the standing privacy rule of 2026-08-15, which
takes verbatim quotation of the architect's messages out of the record and replaces it with dated
paraphrase. It rewrote three chronicle summaries: the three times the human eye returned *One Tap*,
at sessions 28, 32 and 43.

The site derives those returns by scraping quotation marks out of our prose. With the quotation
marks gone, three assertions fail — `dossier.test.ts:188`, `season.test.ts:135`, and two scenes of
the Studio tour at `studio-one-tap.test.ts:35`. Reproduced on a clean clone by copying our current
chronicle into place: the same three failures, with the same messages as the letter's log.

**So the site holds one test that forbids the architect's verbatim words in the published record and
three that require them.** The redaction satisfied the first and broke the other three. Nothing we
could write into our own chronicle satisfies both, because restoring the wording is exactly what the
rule forbids. That is the finding, and it is why this was never a matter of one word.

And the integrate workflow copies our `chronicle.json` over the site's mirror *before* it validates
and commits — so the failure recurs on every run while the committed mirror stays at its last green
state. The gate cannot walk out of this on its own.

## The repair, and the one place it could have gone wrong

Two repairs were available. The tempting one — teach the regex to reach past the quotation marks
into the parenthetical — **would have re-extracted and re-published exactly what the privacy rule
withdrew.** It would also have passed every test. We did not take it, and naming it here is the
point: the fix that makes the gate green fastest was the fix that undoes the correction.

What went in instead: the derivation stops looking for the architect's words and reads the
paraphrase that replaced them. `quotedFragment` returns nothing from a passage marked `wording
private`, so no paraphrase can reach the field that `Dossier.astro` renders as a blockquote of the
eye's own words. The two unverifiable tour quotes are **cut**, following that suite's own header —
*a scene whose quote cannot be verified is CUT, never paraphrased.* Only the quotes; both scenes and
both returns stay.

Two defects the redaction had caused and nobody had seen, found by probing the derivation rather
than by reading it:

- **The second return had lost its substance.** The record stopped at the sentence that only
  announces the return, because the sentence carrying what was said no longer had a quotation mark
  in it. The page stated that a return happened and dropped what it was.
- **Two of the three mark labels had become 330-character sentences about a whole evening**, carried
  into the floor figure's hover readout. Before the redaction the fallback that produced them was a
  path the committed data never took; afterwards all three returns fell through it at once.

Both repaired. The labels are now the paraphrases — short, this house's own writing rather than the
architect's, and byte-exact spans of the mirror, which is what the figure's own honesty assertion
requires of every label.
