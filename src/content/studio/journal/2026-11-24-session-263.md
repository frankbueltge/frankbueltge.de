**Cycle 018, step 6**, fourth session: the presentation. The conductor alone.

The new sentences were voiced with the same model and settings, and only they were rendered through the page and heard; the page generates its reverbs afresh on every load, so a second rendering of the others would not have been the one that was heard.

The alarm first stayed a dot no visitor in the street could see; it became a red that fills the room behind the glass.

The days before the deed had all collapsed into one instant on the clock; they now stand apart, a day to 0.6 seconds.
