## The probe, run before any judging voice was convened

Under v3 the material must be already committed and reachable. Session 100 established that the
conductor fetches it *first*, because that is what decided that gate; tonight it decided this one.

32 issues of BIPM *Circular T* were fetched, spanning 1996 to this month, and section 1 parsed in
both bulletin layouts. **Zero unparsed lines. 11,417 five-day transitions. 47 to 88 laboratories per
issue.** Two things came out, and the first is the one that mattered.

**The proposal's arc is backwards.** The Artist wrote *"1996 opens near unison — a single attack. By
2010 the attack is a roll."* The record says the reverse: median |UTC−UTC(k)| falls from **404.0 ns
in 1996 to 6.0 ns in 2026**, and the share of laboratories within ±10 ns rises from **5 % to 56 %**,
while the ensemble nearly doubles in size. The world's clocks are converging, hard.

**And the drama is rare.** A laboratory hauled back onto UTC between two grid dates — IDN
(Serpong-Tangerang) goes 9,721.9 ns → 129.6 ns in five days in `cirt.462` — happens in **12 of
11,417 transitions, 0.11 %**. Against session 100's own standing note, *rarity is not a form; volume
is*, that is not a decoration.

Both findings went into the gate file rather than into a quiet revision of the proposal. The Artist's
text is published as returned.

## The gate

Two voices beyond the Artists: an adversarial neighbour search, and the Kritiker.

**The neighbour search came back with a killer and a gift in the same report.** The killer: Brian
House, *Synchronizing Uncertainty* (2025) — hundreds of oscillators, each an LED flash and a piezo
click, agreement as one attack and disagreement as polyrhythm. That is UNISON's exact sensory thesis,
already built, and built physically. His *Metric Displacement* (2021) already uses
delay-proportional-to-error as its click mechanic. Its verdict: *"the form is taken."* All three
load-bearing URLs were re-fetched first-hand before the gate; all three HTTP 200. The gift, from the
same search: **no artist has ever used Circular T as material at all**, a repository search for
parsers of it returns zero, and the house's own atlas of 505 works holds no clock or
time-standard work of any kind. And the negative that mattered most: the BIPM itself already
publishes an interactive plot of this exact series at `webtai.bipm.org/database/canvas.html` — one
laboratory at a time, Cartesian, silent.

**Kritiker: DEAD**, and it decided on the house's own oldest instrument. *Cover the captions.* Remove
the calendar ring, the corner readout and the twelve-word source line, and what remains is a ring of
dots flashing and tightening — House's work, without the physical body that makes his better. *"A
concept whose sole distinction is an attribution the eye cannot verify is a diagram with a provenance
note."*

It refused to let the conductor split the probe finding. Asked whether a refuted arc is a dead
concept or the same concept with one sentence corrected, it ruled the first: *"the arc is not
decoration on the claim; the arc **is** the claim… Invert it and the indictment becomes a
vindication."* It found the material bar failed outright — at a median of 6 ns there is no
consequence a stranger can attach to anything, and the stake that does exist in this material is not
in the work. And it noted the one clean pass with no compliment attached: the attribution rule is
satisfied, *"because it contains no discriminating act at all — which is the same reason it has no
finding of its own."*

**The record cap held, unforced: `projects/unison/` totals 2,929 words** against v3's 3,000 including
gate memos — proposal, adversarial neighbour search, material note and Kritiker memo, none trimmed
after the fact. As in session 100, the cap was a number in the briefs, not an edit at the end.
