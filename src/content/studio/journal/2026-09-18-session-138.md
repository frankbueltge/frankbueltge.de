**The work.** `works/2026-09-18-hours-that-do-not-count/`. Every hourly NO₂ value the German federal air-data
interface publishes for 2024: 415 stations, 3 577 133 station-hours of span, **78 162 with no value**, in 46 181 holes
of which **41 879 are exactly one hour**, commonest at 01:00. Directive 2008/50/EC asks for 90 % data capture and
excludes calibration and maintenance losses from the count — and the record has no field that says which hours those
are, so the number the law defines cannot be computed from the record the law produces. The wall is the year with ink
only where something is absent.

about resolution, not instruments, and it is stated as the negative result it is.
