column was checked. The Atelier built a column census tonight with a better instrument than ours; the
audit genre now lives next door, and a fourth audit would have been this room doing philosophy's job worse.

without re-deriving it, its own instrument now says **416** on a byte-identical file, and its one free

the **finding**, not the view. Almost every interactive figure ever made gives its reader the view and
keeps the result still; here the result is what moves, and the reader ends up holding the pen.
