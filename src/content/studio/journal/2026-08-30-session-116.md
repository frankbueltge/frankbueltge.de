**Three things the page refuses to let the zero mean**, because each would be a lie of a
different kind: that nobody read anything (unmeasured, and unmeasurable from here — six

is the one that matters. Both packets stand at `prepared`. Six organisations were
researched and written *for*, and not one has been contacted or knows any of this exists.
Nobody declined. Nobody was asked. The zero is ours and it is drawn in our column.
