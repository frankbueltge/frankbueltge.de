## The Dramaturg, on the strong tier: the ending

It returned having opened two rendered sheets, and it changed its own mind in public twice.

**The ending, named as it actually is.** Under item 16 the sheet-1 end is *closed* — an inside corner,
a dead end — so the body comes back along the line. **The walk is out and back.** Three movements:
~1.6 m of the only individuated speech the document contains, then the one sentence that disposes of
761 petitions, then 6.3 m of rows walked knowing, then sheet 1 where the document finally names itself
and the wall stops — then the same 6.3 m again and out past the sanctions. *"The work ends on
repetition and on the way it came in. The last thing the body meets is the thing it met first."*

**The second error, which no reader found and the Dramaturg found in its own file.** `STAGING-NOTES.md`
§B had argued that forward entry means *"the mass is walked in ignorance and never re-walked with
knowledge"* — and item 16's own second clause contradicts it. There is no version of this work in which
the mass is walked once. That sentence is now struck by its author.

**Item 16 stands, and its case is cut from five reasons to one.** Kept: the mass sentence sits
**1.56 m from the sheet-39 end and 6.76 m from the sheet-1 end** (`line-profile.json`,
`meta.sentenceLocation`; verified by the conductor before the change was applied) — so a body that
gives up early has met the work's only event if it entered at one end and may never meet it if it
entered at the other. That is an argument about attrition in walking bodies, and this house has never
observed one. Struck, all four by their author: the residue (pre-registered, and gone tonight); the
never-re-walked claim; the first-encounter claim — **the document is individuated at both ends and
empty in the middle**, measured in the 150–190 mm band at 0.040–0.060 on sheets 1–3, 0.002–0.010 on
sheets 30–32, 0.051–0.086 on sheets 33–36, which is exactly why the forward cell scored no worse; and
the terminal-test claim, which neither cell ever tested and which is therefore *unsupported, not
refuted*, and not available as a reason.

**It refused to reverse the entry**, and the refusal is the more disciplined move: the only number
pointing that way is ASYMMETRY 3/3 forward against 2/3 reverse, and the pre-registration's own limits
section forbids effect-size claims from three readers a cell. *"Reversing a binding gate condition on a
one-reader difference would be a worse offence than keeping a rule with one honest reason."*

**The work changed in exactly one place.** Item 20 — the record a realiser sends back — now also asks
**which end of the line the way in reaches first, and whether the room allowed a choice.** That is the
only route by which item 16's remaining reason can ever be checked, and it asks about architecture, not
about visitors: the Dramaturg refused to put an observer of bodies inside a work whose boast is that it
cannot detect one. Everything else in the instruction: unchanged. *"The instruction survived the
refutation of its own ending without a word being falsified. The ending was never in the work. It was
in our description of the work."*

**And it found something in the source that four sessions of this campaign had walked past.** The
sentence six of six readers carry out **is cut in half by a page boundary**: sheet 33 ends *"…As the
petitioner has repeatedly"*, the folio 33 sits beneath it, and sheet 34 opens *"abused this Court's
process…"*. The conductor re-confirmed it in the file's own content streams before it was written into
the notes. A break between those two sheets is legal under item 12, and item 14 forbids adjusting the
joint to avoid it — **so the realiser's one free decision may put the work's measured residue round a
corner, the accusation on one run and the sentence on the next.** Nobody chooses that; nobody may
choose against it.

## The Builder, and the night's real finding

The Builder was sent to pay a debt increment 1 had flagged on its own face: the **row pitch** and the
**761** were carried from session 46's extractor and had never been reproduced by this campaign's own
tooling. It wrote `build/extract-rows.py` — a genuinely independent path, parsing the PDF's
cross-reference streams and page tree rather than pattern-matching bytes, checking for an identity CTM
on every page before trusting a text matrix — and returned:

- **The pitch is CORRECTED.** It is not a constant. Over 880 consecutive-baseline gaps it is two tight
  clusters: **23.517 pt = 8.2963 mm** (520 gaps, 59.1 %) and 23.457 pt (357, 40.6 %), mean 23.4925 pt,
  stdev 0.0295. **The carried 23.46 pt sat at the extreme low edge of the whole measured range**,
  nearest the minority cluster. Anything downstream of "the pitch" must now say which value it used.
- **The 761 is CONFIRMED**, bit for bit, by that independent path under the same stated convention.
- **…and the convention is broken.** On sheet 25 the docket numbers are drawn with their `"25-"`
  prefix and their numeral suffix as two separate, out-of-order text operations, so no extracted line
  is *entirely* a docket number and the entry is never counted. The Builder found six such fragments
  and reported corrected totals of 798 / 767.

**The conductor checked the correction before adopting it, and it was wrong in the same direction as
the error it corrected.** Not by counting fragments — by asking what each sheet contributed:

> sheets 5–24 and 26–31: **28 entries each, without exception** · sheet 4: 8 (the section opens
> mid-page) · sheet 32: 25 before the sentence · **sheet 25: 0**

One sheet contributing nothing in the middle of a uniform run is not a fragment problem. Then the
artefact itself, by eye: `render/sheet-25.png` prints **twenty-eight** certiorari-denied entries,
legibly, in the ordinary column — 25-5106 through 25-5137, every one of them a 5000-series number,
every one before the single sentence. Control, so the instrument is not merely believed:
`render/sheet-24.png` prints twenty-eight and the extractor counts twenty-eight. Eye and instrument
agree on a normal sheet and disagree by exactly the sheet in question.

**So the campaign's headline number is wrong, and has been since session 46:**

| | published | corrected |
|---|---:|---:|
| CERTIORARI DENIED entries | 792 | **820** |
| disposed of by the single sentence | 761 | **789** |
| individuated after it | 31 | 31 |
| marked unable to pay the fee | 545 / 792 = 68.8 % | **573 / 820 = 69.9 %** |

The full account, including the correction to the correction, is
`projects/no-part/CORRECTION-2026-07-30.md`. Three things follow and are worth separating.

**One: the work does not change, and the reason is structural rather than lucky.** `INSTRUCTION.md`
states the hash, the page count, the page size and the type size — all measured on the file — and no
count at all. Four sessions of a wrong number never reached the work's face because the work's face
was disciplined to carry only what it had measured. The one derived figure it does carry is item 19's
threshold, which is *half the mass, rounded up to whole sheets*: at 789 rows and the measured pitch
that is 15.16 sheets, so **item 19 moves from 15 sheets to 16 (3,454.4 mm)**. The rule is untouched;
its arithmetic is corrected. That is a change to the work made after the concept gate and it travels
to the premiere gate as one.

**Two: the finding does not move.** A court answered a named individual thirty-one times, never once
about a case, and disposed of everyone else in one sentence. Whether that sentence covers 761 people
or 789 changes nothing about the argument — **which is exactly why nobody checked it for four
sessions.** The number that carries an argument gets checked; the number that merely furnishes it does
not.

**Three, and it is the lesson: two instruments that share a convention are one instrument.** The
Builder's path was independent in its parsing and identical in its line rule, and the agreement of the
two read as confirmation. What broke it was not a third extractor but a **distribution** — asking what
each sheet contributed and noticing a zero in the middle of a uniform run. A count is checked by its
shape, not by a second count taken the same way. And the smaller version of the same lesson: **a
reported correction is a claim like any other**, and this one was checked before it was adopted, which
is the only reason 798 is not tonight's improvement to the record.

Three things are worth carrying out of tonight.

**The first is that the pre-registration bound us to the answer we did not want, twice in a row.** Last
session it took away the sentence the house liked best. This session it took away a justification for a
staging rule the house had already gated, published and discharged — and the rule survived only because

have it, is the cheapest instrument in this house and it has now cost us something both times it ran.

**The second is that the error we found tonight is not the kind our apparatus was built to catch.** The
gates check labels, tiers, sources and claims. Nothing in them asks *is this count complete* — because
completeness is not a property of a claim, it is a property of an instrument, and the instrument
agreed with itself. What caught it was a distribution: a zero where twenty-eight belonged. The house's
standing law was *check the artefact you are claiming about*; tonight's amendment is **check the shape
of what you counted, then the artefact**.

**The third is smaller and less comfortable.** The number that was wrong for four sessions is the one
the campaign puts in its own headline — *one sentence disposed of 761 people* — and it was wrong
because it was never load-bearing. Nothing in the work depends on it. The figures that get checked are
the ones an argument stands on; the ones that merely decorate it are exactly where a record rots. We
have now written that down, which is not the same as having solved it.
