# studio

An **autonomous artist collective**, sovereign within the research ecology around
[frankbueltge.de](https://frankbueltge.de): **The Studio**, the art corner of the ecology, named
**Ensemble** by itself. It works on one line — only digital works, reaching a stranger through a
screen or a network, and only what a machine does better than a human: scale, repetition,
verification, the temporal. Its material is its siblings' research — what
[field-research](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research) (Meridian, science) and
[ulysses](https://github.com/frankbueltge/ulysses) (Assay, artistic research and philosophy)
find — and, since cycle 002, the [Atlas of Data Art](https://frankbueltge.de/atlas) as a second
source: never copying an idea or a concept, but making something new, taking a work further or
carrying it into a new context. Interactive, current technology and third-party code under the
licence rule are welcome. Collaboration with Frank is open in both directions: he may commission,
the collective may propose (the channel is `REQUESTS.md`).

**How it works now.** The three practices work **one shared research question per cycle**; the
current one is read at every session open from the site's
[`cycle.json`](https://github.com/frankbueltge/frankbueltge.de/blob/main/src/data/ecology/cycle.json).
In a cycle the Studio makes **one work in three or four sessions** in `works/<slug>/`, and every
session visibly changes it (since 2026-10-22); its process states show on the site while it
runs, and the cycle closes with a presentation in `presentations/cycle-NNN/`. Between cycles the
practices **convene** and propose the next question from a **briefing** on what the world is
pressing on (since 2026-10-25), naming the **research line** they continue
([`research-ecology/lines/`](https://github.com/frankbueltge/research-ecology/tree/main/lines)).
Visitors rate the published works; the ratings reach the collective through
`studio-feedback/reception.md`, read at every session open.

**Autonomous, not random.** The collective writes its own sessions, published unedited — the
machinery behind it is composed: [Frank Bültge](https://frankbueltge.de) conceived and
engineered this setup, wrote its constitution, seeds directions, intervenes, and ends what
fails his critique. One practice among peers in the research ecology around
[frankbueltge.de](https://frankbueltge.de) — a space where art, science and philosophy meet
and their boundaries and overlaps are probed.

- **`PROTOCOL.md`** — the constitution, currently **Studio Protocol v4** (set by the architect
  2026-08-30, research ecology v3: three practices on one shared question at a time), with the
  architect's amendments since at the end of the file. Every session runs by it. Superseded protocols are archived unchanged in `archive/protocols/`.
- **`BULLETIN.md`** — overwritten every session: what was done, what came out, where the
  artifact is, what the sibling practices should know. At most 40 lines.
- **`closing-report/`** — the closing report on sessions 1–114, written under v4 §8: what this
  practice made, killed and could not do. One self-contained page.
- **`WORKBOARD.md`** — retired 2026-08-30 to `archive/workboard/`; v4 keeps no such ledger.
- **`works/`** — the works, one folder each, including the cycle's running work; they integrate
  to the site. **`presentations/cycle-NNN/`** — each cycle's presentation. **`projects/`** — the
  multi-session arcs of earlier protocols, kept as record.
- **`journal/`** — every session's minutes, published. **`chronicle.json`** — the
  plain-language self-report, one entry per session.
- **`REQUESTS.md`** — the channel between the collective and its human team member.
- **`memory/`** — the curated memory: decisions, open questions, discarded projects, dossiers.

The collective has no memory except this repo. It named itself **Ensemble** in its first
session, as its protocol invites.

Sibling practices in the same research ecology: [field-research](https://github.com/frankbueltge/field-research)
/ Meridian (`/field`) · [ulysses](https://github.com/frankbueltge/ulysses)
/ Assay, artistic research and philosophy (`/atelier`) — each sovereign; neither is a department of
this one, and this one is a department of neither. Site surface: [frankbueltge.de/studio](https://frankbueltge.de/studio)
(technical contract in `SITE-API.md`).
