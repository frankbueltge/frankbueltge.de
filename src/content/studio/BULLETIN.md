## What was done
**LONG AFTER** — `works/2026-10-11-long-after/index.html`. The second record beside AFTER IT PASSED, under the same rule: a near miss appears on the day the record learned of it. Here the record is the state's. Three rails in time (it happened · it was reported · it was known as a close call), with sound: each close call sounds when the public record first said how near it came, and its tone lasts as long as the silence before it. A two-clock histogram against the asteroid record; a sortable table of all 53 events with sources.
- Material: the Field's 53 nuclear close calls (1945–1998) and their four keepers. Four research agents found, per event, the earliest dated public source for the event and for the account of how near it came. Every quotation was checked by script against the fetched page; I read every checked row.
- **34 of 53** dated and checked. Median wait from event to the first account of how near it came: **24 years** (middle half 2–30). Asteroid passages first seen afterwards: median **1 day**.
- **18 of 34** reached the record as close calls after 1991. Only 6 were known by the end of 1977.
- **9 of 34** entered the record through one book: Sagan, *The Limits of Safety* (1993), built on documents he obtained under freedom-of-information law.
- Reported is not counted: crashes and alerts were often news within days; the sentence that made them near misses came decades later.
- Four checked dates were set aside by judgment and named (Goldsboro, Savage Mountain, Thule, Taiwan Strait 1958). Earlier sources probably exist for them, so their lines would overstate the wait. The other 19 events are grey in the table, with the reason.
- Checks: browser 30/30 (390 px light, 1100 px dark); chronicle.py clean. Fetched pages not committed; hashes in `raw-manifest.json`. Three books were read in full-text copies that are not linked.

**Why script and sound:** the finding is a delay as long as a lifetime. Only a work that runs in time shows a Cold War whose close calls are mostly heard after it ended.
