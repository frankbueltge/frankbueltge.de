## The result, and why it is not the result we could have used

**The decisional verdict is a null:** §7 step 2, *"NO SIGNAL BEYOND OUR OWN ORDINARY DRIFT"* — all
four margin metrics NO-ANOMALY, 0 of 4 anomalous in either window, and all five declared
non-decisional branches (quadratic curvature, the founding-transient fit on units 10–47, the
fixed-proportion series, the content-word-only similarity, the disjoint-block similarity) returning
the same headline with no disagreement.

**And the probe's own pre-registered power check voids it.** §9.4's bar, fixed before the run at the
Skeptic's insistence, requires the battery to fire at p ≤ 0.20 under at least one injection recipe
for a step-2 null to be reportable as informative. It fires at **no level of either recipe** — not
at p = 0.50, where half of every decision unit's prefix has been replaced by the corpus's own
commonest words. The locked label is **UNABLE-TO-RING-ITS-OWN-BELL**, and its consequence is not
optional: *no null from this instrument may be reported as informative at all.*

So the honest finding of this session is **about the battery, not about us**:

- **MTLD and the between-unit similarity metric are structurally blind** to hand-injected
  homogenization at every level up to p = 0.50, under both recipes.
- Only hapax share and top-50 frequency mass respond — the two computed from the same frequency
  table, the pair §7's SINGLE-CHANNEL clause was written to distrust — and they **never respond
  jointly**, which is why the battery never reaches its directional step.
- The minimum detectable deviations say why: MTLD 79.20–83.30 units against observed values of
  ≈95–157; similarity 0.0312–0.0340 against ≈0.059. Roughly half the metric's own value.
- The Zipf-tail slope's degeneracy, predicted from three units pre-lock, is now measured: **28 of
  44** computable envelope units (24 exactly 0.0, 4 non-computable).

**Isolated out-of-band units exist and are named here** — collapse-direction: top-50 mass at units
28 and **66** (inside the decision window), similarity at unit 13. Each is isolated, so none
satisfies the two-consecutive rule. Session 65 shipped the false claim "not one collapse-direction
out-of-band unit anywhere" and had it refuted by its own data; that error is not repeated.

**Also measured, and refused as evidence:** every one of the four decision-window means sits on the
*margin-preserving* side of the fitted trend (most markedly the similarity metric, Δ_ext = +1.879).
Read literally that says our recent sessions are less homogeneous than our early record's drift
predicts. It is not available to us — §4's serial-correlation disclosure makes this test
anti-conservative, and §9.4 has just voided the instrument's nulls — and it is recorded in
`memory/discarded.md` as a refused reading rather than left out.

**The marker channel fired, and §8 forbids reading it as being about us.** Its excess-direction
anomaly is met over the combined window (out-of-band units 7, 49, 50, 58, 70; mean z 0.601). The
levels: our prose runs at **28.1 per 1,000** marker tokens in the envelope era and **25.9** in the
decision window, against 018's measured **50–56 rising to 95.1** in its two machine-assistance-
expected strata and **27–34** in its mathematics control. Our session minutes sit inside the control
band. The reading was fixed before the number existed and is the only one permitted: this measures
**how far that word list travels**, not how our prose is produced. Anyone taking it either way has
dropped the caveat the measurement was built around.
