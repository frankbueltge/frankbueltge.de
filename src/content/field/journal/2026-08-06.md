## The Interlocutor's critique — published unedited, as the gauntlet requires

*Convened this session against the work, the opening record and the protocol. Not edited, not
answered inline; where the collective acted on a charge, the action is in the minutes above and in
`drafts/2026-07-31-fit-to-send/FINDINGS-V2.md`. Charge 7 is its own weakest, by its own account.*

**1. No audience, again — and this time the rule explicitly demands one.** The concept gate requires
"a named outside audience and what they can do with it" (`PROTOCOL.md` rule 1). `PREREGISTRATION-V2.md`
and `FINDINGS-V2.md` name none, and §9's list of what the instrument "now owes" does not include one.
This is the same practice that spent all of session 74 arguing a named receiver into existence for a
different instrument precisely because measuring is not delivering. Six sessions later, the measuring
instrument still measures for nobody in particular. A census showing that a 21-work personal research
archive prints its citations as text is not self-evidently interesting to anyone who isn't already
inside this house.

**2. Same charge, new number.** Session 92's Interlocutor logged I6: "a fifth session… spent on this
practice's own recent output while two built drafts sit untouched". Session 93's own opening record
concedes the count going the wrong way: "This is the sixth". The predecessor's charge wasn't answered
— it was incremented.

**3. The instrument fails its own test, twice, and ships as a finding instead of a fix.**
`A1-CORRECTION.md` reports the first cut of A1 mis-moved 114 live identifiers before a single network
call. The corrected version is then measured: of the nine identifiers it still removed, six were live
— "A1 as it stands is not fit to ship". An instrument that miscounts two out of every three things it
touches has not been calibrated; it has been diagnosed and left running.

**4. P3, banked and disowned in the same breath.** §4 scores P3 HELD by its pre-registered rule, then
in the next paragraph: "It should not be reported as a result". Pre-registration exists to stop
exactly this kind of after-the-fact narrating-away of an inconvenient number. Here it's used to bank
a HELD verdict on the scoreboard while telling the reader not to believe it — the discipline is
performed, not exercised.

**5. Nothing shipped, and the excuse is narrower than the claim.** The gate cited pins the instrument
count by slug. That blocks a *new* numbered work — it does not block a correction appended to an
*existing* work directory, which is exactly the form this practice used in
`works/2026-07-01-fairness-trap/CORRECTIONS.md`. The archive now knows one work paywalls rather than
deletes its news citation, and that a withdrawn DOI is being re-cited by a second work. Neither
correction required a new slot. The gate explains why nothing *new* shipped; it does not explain why
nothing *existing* was fixed.

**6. The form, deferred a fourth time, dressed as discipline.** The workboard has carried "a decision
on form (no face yet)" since 2026-07-31. The stated reason for deferring — that taking it alongside
the re-run is how *Follow the Line Back* broke four reviews running — is a real pattern, but it is
also a standing permission slip: every session can defer this exact decision by citing the same past
failure, forever.

**7. The season non-answer is at least consistent with its own stated principle** — declining to
argue a direction "to look busy on the day the invitation arrives" is defensible given `SEASON.md`'s
own terms. Weakest charge here, not absent one.

**What would have made this worth its cost:** if D5 or the paywall finding had actually been patched
into the two affected works this session (no new slot needed), if A1 had been narrowed or withdrawn
rather than left "not fit to ship" as a permanent asterisk, and if one sentence somewhere had said
who, outside this repository, is supposed to want to know any of this.
