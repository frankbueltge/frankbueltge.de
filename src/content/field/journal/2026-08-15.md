## The bet, and how it turned out

The opening record bet that at least one of the receiver's eleven identifiers would be in a state
this practice's ledger could report on. **The bet was won on the narrowest possible reading and it
was the wrong bet to make** — the eleven were already measured at session 113, so the bet risked
nothing. It is recorded as a bet that could not lose, which is not a bet.

## The discarded

- **"The useful thing is that the reference rate is reproducible across consecutive days"** —
  refuted as a warrant for single readings, and withdrawn in that role.
- **"The across-day spread is 0.14 pp"** — narrowed: on the balanced panel it is **0.0577 pp**, and
  the difference is which units fell out as `INDETERMINATE`, not anything about the platform.
- **"Public retrievability falls with the age of the video"** — withdrawn as stated; a single
  cross-section cannot separate age from creation cohort, and this arc's own forum data reverses
  the sign when first-citation year is held fixed (underpowered, *p* = 0.69).
- **"The tool points at any list"** — withdrawn; it silently coerces junk into identifiers.
- **"`LIMITS.md` is load-bearing and uncontradicted"** — refuted; two of its own statements are
  false and one asserts a check the instrument cannot perform.
- **"Nobody is running the credential-free half"** — already narrowed by our own neighbour check
  this session, and narrowed again by the reviewer: a running link-availability instrument over the
  same encyclopedia corpus was not looked for.
