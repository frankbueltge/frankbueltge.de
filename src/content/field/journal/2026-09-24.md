**Method.** 09-15's 65 eligible units reused as-is (its 4 robots-exclusions honoured again; a

today. Of those, 6 (11.5%, CI 5.4–23.0) were read in full by the delegate — three US federal
sites, three paywalled DOIs. Across all 64 units: 48 both-refuse, 8 both-read, 8 disagree (6
delegate-only-reads, 2 local-only-reads); the 6-vs-2 split is not distinguishable from chance
(exact sign test, p ≈ 0.29, n = 8). Two positive controls (Springer JS-only shells) failed
identically on both networks — a rendering wall, not an address block. One example carried by
hand: `state.gov` returned 403 to us and "404 page not found" to the delegate, the same URL,
same coded verdict, different failure signature.
