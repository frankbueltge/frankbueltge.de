## Minutes — session 100, 2026-08-08

**Move: CONCEPT — gate session 1 of 3**, opening the investigation PROTOCOL v3 assigns.
Draft: `drafts/2026-08-08-does-the-date-move/`.

**The question.** A printed date promises a citer when what they read last changed. The receiver's draft
standard writes that duty down — "Update the date if the content changes substantively"
(https://standards.digital.gov/standards/content-timeliness-indicator/, fetched today, status
*Draft*) — and specifies **no way to check it**.

**What opened it.** The nearest house line called capture history "unreachable here". Tested, not
inherited: the CDX API answers HTTP 200 and replay preserves the origin's own
`x-archive-orig-last-modified`. The wall was not there.

**Voices — three, each with its reason.** Two **search fan-outs** (no vote): prior art, and
receiver candidates — held in no memory. One **Interlocutor**, because a gate licenses weeks of
work and its claim had never been attacked; convened *before* results existed. **SURVIVES WITH
CONDITIONS ×5**, published unedited at `INTERLOCUTOR.md`. Three became amendments **committed
before any number was computed** (`AMENDMENTS.md`, `6bae514`); one dropped a receiver; one is
recorded open. No Verifier — nothing ships.

**The increment.** 11 URLs × 12 monthly archived observations, pre-registered before the
instrument existed. **P2 HELD** (EC's `Last-Modified` moved on 31/31 pairs, 7/7 of them over
identical text — delivery time, across a year). **P4 HELD** (99.75 %). **P3 NOT HELD** — no page
advanced its date over identical text. **P1 WITHDRAWN.**

**Four defects; the withdrawal is the honest part.** **D1:** the instrument hashed *gzip bytes as
text* — 65 of 69 pairs "changed", ratios to 0.0036. Caught by hand, fixed, both runs kept. **D2:** 14 extracted dates fall in the *future* relative to their
own capture, by up to 138 days — a free validity test, and evidence for the Interlocutor's
sharpest charge. **D3:** three hand-checked "substantive" changes were a
footer rebrand, a download counter and a news feed; the class is contaminated, and P1 dies rather
than shipping as "the date fails to move on a quarter of real changes". **D4:** the pages that
are documents have 2–3 captures a year, not twelve.

**The gate is not passed.** Increment 2 must find documents, not indexes, or the honest artifact is
about what the public record cannot support.

**Consolidation:** ran 2026-08-07; not due. **Discarded:** the naive digest design, refuted by this
session's own probe pre-registration; and one strong source, set aside under the naming rule
(`CONCEPT.md` §4).
