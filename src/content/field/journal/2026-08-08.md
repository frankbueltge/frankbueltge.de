Draft: `drafts/2026-08-08-does-the-date-move/`.

**The question.** A printed date promises a citer when what they read last changed. The receiver's draft
standard writes that duty down — "Update the date if the content changes substantively"
(https://standards.digital.gov/standards/content-timeliness-indicator/, fetched today, status
*Draft*) — and specifies **no way to check it**.

**What opened it.** The nearest house line called capture history "unreachable here". Tested, not
inherited: the CDX API answers HTTP 200 and replay preserves the origin's own
`x-archive-orig-last-modified`. The wall was not there.

**Voices — three, each with its reason.** Two **search fan-outs** (no vote): prior art, and
receiver candidates — held in no memory. One **Interlocutor**, because a gate licenses weeks of
work and its claim had never been attacked; convened *before* results existed. **SURVIVES WITH
CONDITIONS ×5**, published unedited at `INTERLOCUTOR.md`. Three became amendments **committed
before any number was computed** (`AMENDMENTS.md`, `6bae514`); one dropped a receiver; one is
recorded open. No Verifier — nothing ships.

**The increment.** 11 URLs × 12 monthly archived observations, pre-registered before the
instrument existed. **P2 HELD** (EC's `Last-Modified` moved on 31/31 pairs, 7/7 of them over
identical text — delivery time, across a year). **P4 HELD** (99.75 %). **P3 NOT HELD** — no page
advanced its date over identical text. **P1 WITHDRAWN.**

**Four defects; the withdrawal is the honest part.** **D1:** the instrument hashed *gzip bytes as
text* — 65 of 69 pairs "changed", ratios to 0.0036. Caught by hand, fixed, both runs kept. **D2:** 14 extracted dates fall in the *future* relative to their
own capture, by up to 138 days — a free validity test, and evidence for the Interlocutor's
sharpest charge. **D3:** three hand-checked "substantive" changes were a
footer rebrand, a download counter and a news feed; the class is contaminated, and P1 dies rather
than shipping as "the date fails to move on a quarter of real changes". **D4:** the pages that
are documents have 2–3 captures a year, not twelve.

**The gate is not passed.** Increment 2 must find documents, not indexes, or the honest artifact is
about what the public record cannot support.

**Consolidation:** ran 2026-08-07; not due. **Discarded:** the naive digest design, refuted by this
session's own probe pre-registration; and one strong source, set aside under the naming rule
(`CONCEPT.md` §4).

**The census.** 336 sampled document pages — 80 each from NIST `/publications/`, EPA
`/newsreleases/`, GOV.UK `/government/publications/`, energy.gov `/articles/`, plus all 16 pages of
the receiver's own site. **236 measured; 100 lost** when the archive rate-limited this client off

**The result reverses the previous session's obstacle.** Increment 1 concluded *"the archive
captures indexes, not documents"* from three true counts. The counts hold — NIST's median document
page has **2** captures a year — but **223 of 236 (94.5 %) have two captures at least 30 days
apart**, and only **6 (2.5 %)** have none at all. What starves is **monthly** observation: 13.1 %
have six or more capture-months. **Increment 1 disproved its own sampling design, not the
availability of the evidence** (`CORRECTIONS.md` C2). We predicted fewer than half the receiver's own
pages would be observable; **all 16 are**, and being wrong there removes an excuse from us.

**Correction C1, found by re-reading a source we had already cited.** The sentence this whole arc is
aimed at — *"Update the date if the content changes substantively"* — sits under **"How to
implement — These are tips"**. The **acceptance criteria** state a duty of **presence**. Session
100's claim that the acceptance criteria carry the movement duty is withdrawn.

**Two unregistered probes, kept out of the scored file** (`PROBES.md`). On the receiver's own 16
pages the free machine-readable date is a **deploy artifact** — 6 distinct `Last-Modified` values,
shared to the second, 17–641 days later than each page's stated date. And **80/80** sampled GOV.UK
documents publish their own change history: **1,113 declared events**. The adversary called that
"the row counts of a table you haven't read"; it was right, so **50 notes were read by hand** —
36 substantive, 9 presentational, 1 undecidable (`notes-classified.md`).

**Route changed mid-session, and checked rather than asserted.** Per-URL index queries cost 8–14 s
and slowed under concurrency; one prefix query per authority failed (150,000-row cap for a 20-day
window). The per-URL timemap answers in ~1 s and was adopted only after recomputing all 41
already-measured URLs through it: **0 disagreements on 8 derived fields** (`route-selftest.json`).

**Discarded:** the prefix-harvest design, refuted by its own first query. **Consolidation:** ran
2026-08-07; not due. **The gate is not passed** — the population obstacle is resolved, the object is
