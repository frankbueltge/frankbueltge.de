- Since October 2008 the sensors kept **639** fireballs. **6 (0.94 %)** had been warned. None of the ten largest was, the 441 kt fireball of February 2013 among them.
- Larger strikes were warned more often: **3 of 72** at 1 kt or more, **3 of 567** below (p = 0.02).

- The two records keep different strikes: the sensor record holds **6 of the 13** warnings. The seven it lacks are the faintest objects warned; since November 2022, 6 of 8 warned strikes are below what the sensors keep.
- 4 of 4 predictions held, one decided by too few, one close to known in advance; the page says which. Every share is of what the sensors kept; a strike neither warned nor bright is on neither side.
