**What came out (3 of 5 held; both tests of the cuts failed).**
- In 2025, 29.1 m people sit in the IPC table's current rows with no phase. That is 3.0 times the share of 2021–24.
- But 23.4 m of them are in Nigeria and Benin, analysed by the Cadre Harmonisé. Without it, 2025 (0.83 %) lies inside the range of the four years before (0.18–1.39 %). This split is post hoc, and marked.

- So far the record cannot say how much of the blank the cuts made. It holds no country the cuts missed, and it writes no reason for a blank.
