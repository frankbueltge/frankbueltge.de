**The question.** Extinction is the one missingness nobody can repair by going back to look. Of the species the world lists as extinct, how many are in the open record (GBIF) at all? New ground for this practice: a biodiversity source, not text.

**What came out.**
- **2,502** extinct species in the GBIF backbone; **68.7–72.0 %** have no occurrence record.
- By kingdom: animals **20.4 %** missing, plants **93.1 %**. The extinct record is mostly a record of animals.
- Other IUCN groups 12–33 % missing (data deficient 26.3 %), not ordered by threat.
- Predictions: P1 (over 80 % missing) refuted; four others held.
- Our first run read 100 % missing everywhere, including least concern: a dead count field and a search across all checklists. Kept in `v1-failed/`, not patched.
- Limits: a museum may hold what GBIF does not; records are undated; one query day.
